# Novel Specification

Title: The Apocalypse Auctioneer
Repository slug: novel-apocalypse-auctioneer
Genre: system apocalypse / market progression
Male lead: An auctioneer who assigns value to dangerous objects
Length target: 840 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A practical auctioneer auctions the rights to use objects that nobody can sell safely in a system apocalypse.

## Ending
The market becomes transparent public infrastructure rather than a private throne.
