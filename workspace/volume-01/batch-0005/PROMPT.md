# Volume 01 Batch 0005 Writing Prompt — The First Hammer, Chapters 41–50

## Mission

Write the last ten chapters of Volume 01. This is the volume climax and the volume close: **a witnessed public auction at Lot 17 that sells one defined use of the Last Fare** — not the object, not a permanent road, not a private gate — with the St. Orra staging line kept visible to residents from the first minute to the last.

Chapters 41–45 pay for what Batch 0004 left open. Chapters 46–50 are the auction, the crossing, the term's end, the damaged token in public custody, and a lot book left open for somebody else to amend.

**This batch opens the route. ## Repair notes carried forward from `reviews/batch-0003-review.md` and this batch's own flags

**Do not reintroduce any of these, and do not write around them.**

## Batch goal

Three things, in this order and not the other way round.

**First, pay for Batch 0004's open promises in public.** Teo's question has been published with no answer attached since the twenty-sixth: *who tells four thousand households, by whom, and for how long, if the crossing never opens?* Selik's review of his own order of the ninth has never sat. The paper-levy vote has never happened. The far-side holder on the goods-shed floor has still not been told what the job is and has still not decided. A woman at the far receiving ramp has not been in a household since she was nineteen and asked for one thing to be entered because she said nothing. A man with a turned ankle is in a room in the lower terrace. **All of these must be answered, refused, or carried into the yard as unresolved on the page, before the road opens, or the climax will be a road with no district behind it.**

**Second, sell one use, and sell it to people who can refuse.** The auction must be a *sale process* with a floor, a refusal window that closes only when the case is physically opened, a lot description, a toll disclosed in the words the travellers will hear, a named bearer, a named remembering passenger, a term with a stated end, a release condition read aloud at the opening **and** at the closing by a different person each time, two fresh witnesses who have heard all of it and are not travelling on it, and a remedy that is known to fail and is called anyway. **Every one of those items was paid for in Batch 0004 and every one of them must now be performed on the page rather than cited.**

**Third, the cost.** The outline says the physical danger is not a monster: the route opens, a crowd wants to keep it open, a returning group forgets why it is traveling, and the token begins to fail before the stated term. **Adrian must choose between extending the route for a private cargo and accepting the public limit, and he must choose the limit and lose the extra shipment.** The convoy and a larger shelter group cross. The return memory is lost and is not erased. The token takes visible damage and becomes evidence of what the sale cost. **A child does not die. The batch's cost is the term, the extra shipment, the token, and whatever a person loses that is not a life — and that has to be real, specific, and named.**

## The two beats that must survive

**1. The crowd wants to keep the route open, and the public limit holds.** The staging line is kept visible from the first minute. A pressure to extend the term arrives from a direction nobody is prepared for — not a villain, not Halloway, and **not Selik**: it arrives from the crowd itself, from a parent, from a trader, from the people the road is for. Adrian ends the use at the stated limit. He loses something specific for it and the yard sees him lose it.

**2. The token is asked, and nobody has ever asked it before.** `THE OBJECT WAS NOT ASKED` is the last line Chapter 40 put in Mara Quill's mouth as *the first clause in this book she did not get from anybody.* **Somebody asks the brass a question, in public, in front of witnesses, as a question and not as a summoning.** The answer is short, plain, and does not solve the crossing. The asking is the volume's last genuinely new act and it must be Adrian's, it must cost him, and it must not be a spell.

## Starting state

- **No crossing of any kind exists.** The official span is shut to all passenger carriage including supervised cargo exceptions, its certificate suspended by the water office under its own clause. The closure order of the ninth states that **one office is responsible for the crossing at St. Orra** and is closed-ended; a **standing order for a permanently staffed crossing with no expiry** is being drafted in a room Adrian is not in and has never been read by anybody in Alder Reach. The public road at Lot 17 is suspended. The middle plank of the old footbridge is in the service cut, and the river stairs are a three-hour walk and not a crossing.
- **The Last Fare** has been activated **four times** and is cracked through the plate, in a locked case in the office behind the sale floor, on a seven-day custody term renewed four times and always in public: **the twenty-first, the twenty-ninth, the fifth, and the twelfth**, each on Nessa Pell's own request, on the record, on the ground that a term which expires in a crisis is a term that can be ended by anybody who is uncomfortable. **The twenty-first's own term lapsed on the twenty-eighth and stood unrenewed for a month before the twenty-ninth, and three chapters say so: Nessa is the only person holding a key on an expired term in Chapter 31, Chapter 35 says the lapse was a month, and Chapter 38 says she renews it in public when she thinks of it. Do not write that the term has never lapsed, and do not write that it is renewed every seven days without a gap.** **It may never open again and repeated use may strand the route permanently. It has answered six unasked questions in short plain lines and the sixth is that it was never asked anything.**
- **Three numbers, closed and not to be moved:** Chapter 19 offered 29, travelled 28, took 28 tolls outward, and had no return leg. Nineteen were stranded on the far bank; nine were unaccounted for; all nine were found at the near-side maintenance spur **on the nineteenth at nine minutes past seven**; eight lived and one child **died in the small hours of the twentieth, at an hour nobody in the room was keeping and an hour the registrar refused to invent**, with her name on a sealed sheet and nowhere else; all nineteen were walked home over the footbridge on the eleventh and twelfth. **Any new crossing in this batch creates a new set of numbers, and they must be counted by leg, stated aloud in a room, and entered the same way the old ones were.**
- **The nine-line return term is in the public book as offered for a future use and not attached to any use.** It has been checked by a barrow-hauler and a tea seller and found to have no fold in it. It says that if the marking is not where the pair says it is, **the joint is the return and the arm is not.**
- **The cause of the failed return is the movement of the marking.** A hand was in the bell-post socket, inside a day or two of the crossing; the rust arc is clean and the paint under the arm unweathered. **Who, how, and why are not established and must not be.** One nailed-heel print in the platform gravel, made before the search, going toward the river wall, belongs to nobody in the search party.
- **The unpaid return toll has spilled onto records.** Four documents changed: the Lot 17 notice board, a clinic receiving slip, two disagreeing pages of the public lot book, and **Mara Quill's sealed-page register**, in which exactly one cell has changed. The registrar has said in public that she cannot certify her own instrument.
- **A child is dead with no name in any public record.** The remedy was called and **not discharged**, the protected category entered as the reason. It has now been called three times, failed once over the child, and failed once on the twelfth over a man with an ankle. **The no-name remedy is the batch's instrument and its standing failure.**
- **Lot 17's standing money:** the yard priced the workshop at **six hundred and twenty-nine coppers** (press 380 by ward market less a 9-copper leak, plus 258 for a season of three named hands off the ward market's rate sheet). Adrian paid **one thousand one hundred and nineteen** on the twenty-ninth at figures he set, with the shortfall entered as uncomputed and **not nil**. **The inspection press has been given away and cannot be posted.** On the twelfth Adrian put a **held asset** into the sitting steward's custody: the lease on the building and the yard, the standing salvage, and a two-season named repair-crew term of two names and a mark, at **1,536 on his own figure, 1,481 on the ward market rate sheet, 1,580 in the yard**, forfeit to the remedy on a breach of any charter the board has recorded, **not returnable by anybody including himself**, entered as **neither a payment nor a security because a reader cannot post security**. The sign over the door is excluded for the second time in public.
- **The public board** can refuse to record a closing, publish a result, call a review, uphold a challenge in part, and set or renew the short public custody term. It cannot grant a use, cannot waive a toll, cannot audit a security, and **cannot strike a line off a notice board.** Seats: Nessa Pell as sitting steward, Sabin Tull and Marda Osk as the two unclaimed seats, with Mara Quill as registrar and not a vote. **Name all four aloud and put them in the room whenever the board acts.**
- **The five visible things** are the standing terms of every charter now: use, toll, term, release condition and appeal path in one place in that order on one sheet; the **release condition read aloud at the opening of the case and at the closing, by a different person each time**; a public refusal by saying the word with no reason owed, closing only when the case is physically opened; **no successful crossing may be treated as a transferable ownership right**; and the book records what failed as a failure of the instrument with no entry struck. An **eighth line**: no goods, no freight, and no carriage of any kind except the person who is carried as a person. **The eighth line is a problem for this batch, because the convoy is cargo. It is a problem the batch must solve in public and not by striking the line.**
- **A person is accounted for by a count, not by a household.** Four lines, entered on the second. A steward may not count the people in a group who are not in a household unless they ask; anybody may say no; saying no is not a refusal of service; the count is published with the decline in it. **A wrong count is not struck.**
- **A mark is a person's refusal and cannot be a job.** One mark was given two jobs — the fourth entry of the proposal of the twenty-ninth, and a clause written at two in the morning on the second and published in the terms of the sixth — by two people in two rooms who never saw each other's document, and the two men were of the same protected household and had never met. **No day-count goes on the interval.** **Nessa Pell's index of every use of a mark since the fourteenth is the only piece of administration anybody ever asked for and everything now depends on it.**

He has said in public that the counterfeit is **more credible after the failure**, that he is not lying, and that Adrian has built the only thing he has been afraid of in six years. **He has not appeared since Chapter 28 and he must not be humiliated. He must be better, and he must say so himself, and the crowd must want what he is selling.**
- **Joss Amery**, a carter, lost **three whole days** and a barrow-load of clinic stores, and a coal ration board in the wrong quarter was altered. He cannot be examined. **His loss has never been paid out and the batch should pay it, or say publicly why it cannot be paid.**
- **Selik's permanent-office proposal was tabled and not adopted**, and the vote that stopped it was a room of traders, not a registrar's procedure. **He asked on his own motion for a public review of his own order of the ninth, on the day the ward market votes on the paper levy, and it has not happened.**
- **The ward market's paper levy** is four coppers a quarter off every household for eleven years for paper that does not exist, and has been **proposed** to fund the warden's inspection and a public reader at a market counter at about **forty-one coppers a quarter — and the ward market has not voted on it.** The reader is running; the rate is not in force. Chapter 41 is the vote. Ivo Stenn cannot read a nine-line claim and says so, and the market's money is better spent on a room than on a bridge, and that is a defensible institutional conclusion the batch must not quietly overturn.
- **A yard of six hundred could not agree on the word** — *urgent*, *scheduled*, *priority* — for a medicine case, and the inability is institutional rather than emotional. **It is still unseeded and the batch should give it a room.**
- **The broken circle is a claim about a system, not a signature.** One impression on unrelated documents. No office, clerk, place, or person identified; the regional heading still scraped away.

## Chapter cards

### Chapter 0041 — The Day the Ward Market Votes

**POV:** Ivo Stenn.

**Location/time:** the ward market's counting room and the terraces, in the days after the twelfth.

**Immediate goal:** Put a rate to a thing nobody has been told will be paid for, on a day when the crossing may or may not open, and find out whether a market can vote for a hope.

**Resistance:** The Warden's own review of his order of the ninth is on the same day and the same yard, and the two things will be read as one by everybody who is not a clerk. Oveta Kraus's renewal motion is now the ward market's own motion. A trader who has read Adrian's held-asset entry asks what happens to a held asset if the road opens and the charter is never breached, and the answer is *it comes back*, and nobody in this district has ever put anything back. **Stenn is not a villain and not a coward and he is about to be the man who raises a rate on a promise**, which is either the whole of his character or the whole of his shame.

**Major turn:** **The market votes the inspection and the reader, and refuses the third thing** — a levy on the hope of a crossing. Stenn is asked by eleven trades to fund a rate for a road that has not been given a day, and he refuses, in public, with figures, and the refusal is the first thing the ward market has ever said yes about. **Selik's review of his own order sits the same day and Selik is examined by the room he is paying for, and the finding is that the order is correct and the district does not want it.**

**Emotional consequence:** Stenn discovers that the market he has spent six years saying no to is capable of saying yes to something, and that the thing it can say yes to is a **room**, and not a road, and that he has no idea what to do with that.

**Ending type:** decision. The inspection days are funded and published. The review is over. And a clerk at the ward market counter reads a paragraph out of Adrian's held-asset entry aloud to nine people, because the sentence *not returnable by anybody including himself* is the first sentence in three weeks anybody has brought news.

### Chapter 0042 — What the Job Is

**POV:** Fen Ambrose, with the far receiving ramp reached by the river stairs.

**Immediate goal:** Go and tell the man at the chain, in words, what the far-side holder is, and get a decision out of him without touching his name, and without the steward's discretion becoming the manifest.

**Resistance:** She has her own Chapter 36 clause and she is about to be caught by it. The job, when you say it aloud on a goods-shed floor, is not what anybody in a book thinks it is: it is deciding that a person arriving is somebody's and not a stranger's, for the whole of the time a vehicle is on the far side, on a floor where eleven people have been living since the plank went and where **one of the four uncounted people is a woman who has said she will not be counted by anybody.** A say-no is on the table and it is not a refusal of service, and it is recorded as a decline in the published count.

**Major turn:** **He says yes to a job he does not have a name for, on the condition that the count is published with the four in it and that the woman by the kettle is not asked again.** He is therefore the first bearer in this volume to have been asked, and the reason the charter can now be performed is a condition a protected person set. **The mark is retired** — the cross comes off the pin, because a person has been asked, and the index records the retirement and the date, and Nessa Pell reads the index entry aloud.

**Emotional consequence:** Fen has spent nine years arguing that a count is a public thing and has never once had to use it to protect herself from a count. She goes down a stair and comes back up one with a yes, and the yes cost a woman a re-asking she did not want, and Fen says so in front of eleven people.

**Ending type:** discovery. The two of them work out, on a goods-shed floor, what a return crew of three on foot from a far ramp to a near apron actually does when a bus does not come back, and the answer is that it does nothing, and that the charter must therefore say what they do instead.

### Chapter 0043 — The Sale

**POV:** Adrian Vale.

**Immediate goal:** Sell one defined use of the Last Fare in front of the city, with a floor, a refusal window, and a term with a stated end.

**Resistance:** **The eighth line.** *No goods, no freight, and no carriage of any kind except the person who is carried as a person.* The clinic has cold boxes and they are cargo and the line says no to them, and the line was written in a yard on the sixth by a trader and seconded by a barrow-hauler out of a counterfeit's second page. **The batch must not strike it.** The resolution has to be a real one and it has to be found in public: either the medicine goes as people who carry it and are carried as persons, which is a fraud, or the clinic's own line is something the clinic has to give up, or the eighth line has to be read narrowly and the reading has to be entered as a reading with a cost. **Adrian must not be the man who quietly redefines his own clause to save a delivery.**

**Major turn:** The lot is described. The use is one route across St. Orra for twelve minutes, coming down from the far bank. The toll is disclosed in the travellers' own words and read aloud twice. Tarin is the bearer and Dessa is the named remembering passenger and **says so herself, in the room, and states what she is and is not able to hold.** The term is one activation ending at the return point or at twelve minutes, with no automatic renewal. **There is no bidding**, exactly as in Chapter 19, and the reason there is no bidding is that every term of it can be checked against a book, and that is the whole of the difference between this and the nineteenth.

**Emotional consequence:** Adrian gets to say the thing he said on the nineteenth *correctly this time*, and it is not triumphant, because the person who has to say the toll to a room of six hundred is the woman who lost the reason she drives.

**Ending type:** escalation. The refusal window opens. Somebody in the yard says the word. The count changes. The sale continues because the sale continues when somebody refuses, and that is the first time a refusal has not stopped a room in this book.

### Chapter 0044 — The Question Nobody Asked

**POV:** Mara Quill.

**Immediate goal:** Get the object itself into a room as a question, having spent eight years building a process that never once asked it anything.

**Resistance:** Nobody has a procedure for it. A charter's terms are agreed by people; the anchor is not a party. **Orren Pike's warning is the front of the book: write the list of what is not in it and read that too, and the thing that is not in every charter in this volume is a question asked of the brass.** Fen's clause bites again: asking the object is not the same as asking a person, and a record that is kept of the object is a record nobody in this room is protected from. **Mara's own instrument is in the spill, and she is about to put her name to the first record in Lot 17's book that she cannot certify in advance.**

**Major turn:** The room constructs it in public, the way the board constructed the terms-closing: as a **question with a stated form and a stated limit**, put by a person who does not own the object, on the crate, in front of two fresh witnesses and about four hundred people, and it **is not a summoning.** The limit is written in before the question is: *one question, asked once, and the object is not asked to agree to anything, and there is no second question until there is a new closing.* **The question is not about the road.** It is the question Teo's open entry has been sitting on since the twenty-sixth, put to the only party in the city that has never been asked it: **what is to be said to the households, and by whom, and for how long, if this is the last time it opens.** The answer is short, plain, and does not solve the crossing.

**Emotional consequence:** Mara is the registrar and she has spent eight years making people agree to things and has never once made a room ask a thing a word, and she is frightened of it in a way she cannot enter in the book, and she enters the fear instead.

**Ending type:** discovery. The answer is in short plain lines, and one of them is a fact nobody in the room had, and **it does not name a person, an office, or a place**, and the yard's first instinct is to read it as a revelation and the board's first act is to refuse to let it be one.

### Chapter 0045 — The Staging Line

**POV:** Tarin Kest.

**Immediate goal:** Walk the line he has been asked to hold, with the people who will be on it, before the case is opened, and find out whether the far-side condition is written down for everybody or only for him.

**Resistance:** A far-side condition is a thing the driver is authorised to do. **A return crew of three on foot from a far ramp to a near apron has no far-side condition and no clause at all**, because they are not the bearer and the rules have never had anything to say about them. The clinic's responsible party is on this bank and has been responsible for boxes and not for people. **The signal crew's tenth hand has a copied page and will check the numbers and finds something.** And the crowd is already gathering, because a yard of five hundred has been promised a road and a road is a thing people bring children to see.

**Major turn:** The far-side condition is extended, in public, to **everyone who is on the route and not driving it** — which is a real extension of Selik's Chapter 15 clause and the first time the volume has taken his instrument and made it bigger, and he is in the room and has to say whether he accepts it. **He accepts it and adds a term of his own: a line-holder at the top of the service cut, and a rule about who may stand on the ballast, and a Warden's rule nobody may go past, and the twelfth of this month is entered in the minutes of a bridge review as the day that rule was argued with and held.** **Selik's fourth term from the nineteenth is now the standing safety rule of the Hearth Line and it is the rule that killed nobody and cannot save everybody, and that is the sentence the volume ends on.**

**Emotional consequence:** Tarin gives the bearer role back to nobody and keeps it, and the chapter is the one in which he says what it costs to be the only pair in a city that has just learned to want a road, and the cost is that every person who wants the road open longer is a person asking the wrong man for something.

**Ending type:** escalation. The refusal window closes at the opening of the case, the count is final, and the number is a number that has never been said aloud in a yard in this city, and it is not a round number, and somebody in the crowd has brought a child.

### Chapter 0046 — The Auction

**POV:** Adrian Vale.

**Immediate goal:** Close one bounded charter in front of the city and seat the token, and be the man who ends the term.

**Resistance:** Everything. The token may not open. The reading of it is the fourth or fifth of the new month and it costs an hour and there is a gap after it. **Where a toll takes the memory of purpose, a witness does not travel on the lot they witness** and the pair must be named and must be on this bank. **The remedy is a hearing and it will be discharged in public and recorded as not discharged, and everyone in the room knows it and is in the room.** And a crowd that has been sold a road for four weeks by three different men with a wax stick is going to be in the yard, and one of those men is going to sell tickets to it, honestly.

**Major turn:** The closing is made, the token is seated in the route-control slot above the old fare box **inside the vehicle**, and the route opens. **Halloway Reed sells a claim in the yard before it opens, honestly, on the first page, in front of everybody, and asks for it to be noted.** The convoy and the shelter group go.

**Emotional consequence:** Adrian is a man who has spent four weeks being the wrong one in every room, and for about eleven minutes he is the right one, and he is not allowed to enjoy it and the prose must not let the reader enjoy it either.

**Ending type:** action. The route is open and the far side is the far side and the far receiving ramp is where the charter says it is.

### Chapter 0047 — The Term

**POV:** Adrian Vale, on the rail.

**Immediate goal:** Hold the term at twelve minutes and bring the return party in, in the order the charter names.

**Resistance:** The token begins to fail before the stated end. Somebody in the yard asks for the road to be kept open — **and the request is reasonable, is made by a parent, and is made of Adrian personally, and it is the most dangerous thing that happens in the volume.** There is a private cargo on the apron that will go over if the route is extended by four minutes. **The extension is available, somebody has standing to ask for it, and it can be granted without breaking a single term in the book, because the only thing that forbids it is a line Adrian wrote on the twelfth that says the release condition closes the case and a person who says the word has the right to stop it.** He asks the person who wants it. They do not know that stopping it ends the road for the day.

**Major turn:** **Adrian ends the use at the stated limit and loses the extra shipment, and says the reason out loud in a yard, and the reason is that the limit is the only thing anybody in this city can check.** The returning group comes in on the terms: named count, read aloud, walked to the platform, and **every one of them pays the memory toll again**, and the count of tolls is said aloud, because tolls are counted by leg and travelers and tolls are counted separately. **The return memory is lost and is not erased.** One traveler cannot say why she set out, and says so in a yard, and it is not a tragedy and it is permanent.

**Emotional consequence:** Adrian keeps the limit and is told by somebody he respects that he kept it for the wrong reason, and he does not defend himself, and the difference between the right reason and the wrong reason is that the wrong reason is that he cannot afford to be wrong twice.

**Ending type:** cost. The route closes. The token comes back to the public case on its own, without the bus, and the wax is broken again, and nobody in the yard says anything for a while.

### Chapter 0048 — What Is Left of the Hearth Line

**POV:** Tarin Kest.

**Immediate goal:** Find out what the road is now that it has been run once by people who were not in the carriage, and say whether it can be run again.

**Resistance:** **A successful crossing may not be treated as a transferable ownership right** and three people in the yard try to treat it as one within an hour. One of them is a trader who has paid a good deal for the right to be early.
