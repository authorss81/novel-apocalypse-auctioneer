## The batch goal

**The walk happens, and it produces one thing the district cannot use.**

The twenty-ninth walk goes from the fourth place to the ninth, on foot, on the ballast, in daylight, by four named people: **Tarin Kest, the tenth hand, Ivo Stenn, and a woman of the ninth place who has not been asked what she is going for and is asked in the yard and dictates her own clause.** They measure the twelfth stall, re-measure the fourth-place stall, and take the counts going and coming.

Three things must happen, in this order and not another:

1. **The boundary.** The west end is walked to a boundary and marked by people who do not own it. The district's own rule — *a mark may not carry a job* — blocks the only instrument it has, and the answer is **two named people, a stated term, and a published entry.** This is the batch's new instrument and it is deliberately worse than the case.
2. **The first attempt at a multi-bearer charter fails**, and it fails for the correct reasons and not for a stupid one. Adrian is a Stage 1 Reader. He **may draft a split and may not close one.** He has no power to post security. And **a transferable slot is not a pair** — standing is per bearer, and a slot nobody stands in is not a bearer at all. Say all three, in a room, in his own words, and let him be asked twice to close something he cannot close and say no both times.
3. **The ward market learns to say yes to a road, once the road has a date.** The nine coppers Ivo Stenn refused in the third week of last month **come back to his own room on their own**, and he does not get to be pleased about it, because a rate that comes back by itself is a rate that was never his decision. One trade abstains again.

## Power discipline — this is the part that will be checked

**Adrian is a Stage 1 Reader and the volume does not promote him in this batch.**

- He **cannot** read the object again. His last reading was the eleventh; the limit ran after the eighteenth and he refused on the eighteenth. **He has read the brass once, in this volume, and it gave him one word and no place.** Do not give him a second reading to make the plot move.

- **A refusal to read is a reading too, and it is entered.** It is neither cowardice nor discipline.
