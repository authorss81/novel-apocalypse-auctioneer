## The batch goal

**Selik's bridge administration staffs a hut at a place it considers unsafe, and the district learns that the safest and the most hated are the same office.**

- The **harrow interchange** is under nine feet of water and the west end is dead. **The Harrow Tongue begins travelling between Faults**: a lot that was in a hut eleven miles west is now in a hut four, and a keeper cannot hold a pair she has to take to another village. **This is the outline's Chapter 71–80 item and it is unspent.** A cast-iron lever moves between huts, and every hut it enters is a hut that cannot turn a train, and the line is a sequence and not a number, so **losing the only place on the line that can turn a train is not losing an object, it is losing the west end.**
- **A Guild inspector appears for the first time in Volume 02.** A guild inspector's function is to certify a lot as clean and there is now a lot in a remote custody nine miles west, and the inspector is right about the object and wrong about the village, and **the inspector is not a villain and is not stupid and is cheaper to beat than to argue with.** The batch's canonical sentence about a guild inspector: **the lot is clean and cannot be sold to a village.**
- **The counterfeit gets a schedule faster than the ward market can print one, and Halloway Reed sells a timetable and is right about everything in it except the one thing the batch has already found.** His Monday print lands inside this batch, with the market's empty toll column set beside his figure at full width. He is not to be humiliated and not to be beaten by a printing press.
- **Tarin and Adrian's people protect the engine at Harrow without knowing who owns the signal's toll, and the answer is in a lease in a book in a room in Alder Reach.** The lease has been surrendered. The answer is that **the person who held the paper is not the person the paper names, and the paper named a road house, and the road house is not there any more.** Do not resolve what the toll lands on.
- **Selik staffs the hut, does it, and says so every published day, and the district finds out that the safest and the most hated are the same office.** He will not be thanked, he will not be converted, and he will be right.

- **Do not name Sarrow Water in a reading.** The object does not hold places and repeats itself when asked again.
- **Do not let the stalls become a new unexplained mystery.** They are the basin's own documented Fracture symptom, and the twelfth was found by walking and the reason it was never in the line book is that the book is a book of signals.
- **Do not find the second impression of the broken circle in this batch.** That is Chapters 81–90, and it must not shortcut the name.
- **Do not resolve:** the romance, Iven's identity, the blank lot's function, the origin of the Common Measure, the office in the origin column, or the three unexplained things of Volume 01.
- **Do not join the two lines** that hold the office's name.
- **Do not have a public market built out of one reader.** The proof of this volume is that the timetable, the toll boards, the lease, the count at the ninth place and the boundary are all held by people who are not Adrian. **The renewal on the twenty-ninth of April must be asked by somebody who is not Adrian and entered by somebody who is not Adrian.**
- **Selik is not defeated, not converted, and not wrong.** He says *that is not enough* from the rail on published days and nobody believes he will stop. **He is not on the twenty-ninth of April's walk and he is not to be put on it.**
- **The woman of forty-five is not a name in the book and must not become one.** She was asked to hold the boundary and said no, and her reason is that a name with nothing behind it is a name a man can use. She will say the word once more if it is needed and nothing else.
- **The protected category is not a bucket.** It has carried a passage twice, a loss once, a failure of a remedy once, a refusal to be asked again once, and a refusal to be named once, and the refusal to be named is not the same act as the refusal to be asked again and must not be added to the count as if it were.
- **A mark may not carry a job, and the boundary was built around that rule and not in defiance of it.** Do not retire the rule and do not make a mark carry a job.
- **The fifth instrument — a way of saying that a toll lands on a place — is still unnamed and unowned.** It is printed as an empty column at full width in the middle of the ward market's timetable. **A column that is printed narrow looks like a column that has been kept quiet.**
