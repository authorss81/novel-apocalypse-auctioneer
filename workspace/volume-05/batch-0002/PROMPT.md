## What this block is for

`outline/volume-05.md` gives it four things. All four must be on the page.

- **The nine dates.** A copy of the nine locks' standing orders comes down with a dray and gives nine dates. **A route keeper gives eight of the same nine differently and will not give the ninth.** The finding: **two lists of nine that agree on eight are not one list, and the one they disagree about is the one that matters.** The stranger's eight days are fixed and are in the canon card: **the first, second, third, fourth, sixth, eighth, tenth and eleventh of the fourth month.** A route keeper's eight must be a different set of eight, and the district must not be able to say which is right.
- **The first letter the Lock Council has ever sent to Alder Reach.** Polite, in writing, and it asks whether this district will send a man to a council. **Adrian reads it out in a yard.** It is a document this district does not own, it is read and not discussed, and the yard's finding is that a body which writes to a village is a body that knows the village has something.
- **The woman of fifty-eight gets her round back for eleven houses and not ninety, and she says so.** Not ninety. **She has to be asked, and being asked is not a resolution, and the figure for it does not exist and must not be invented.** Nobody can say what eleven houses is worth and that is the fifth of the five again, and a man of about thirty-four who digs loam will say so in the plainest words available.
- **The word for the people who keep them.** A keeper's word has come back into this district from a house thirty-four miles east and **Batch 0002 must be the block in which a keeper is named on a page, or in which it is established on the page that no keeper has ever been on one.** Either is a finding. Do not decide it in the outline; decide it in the chapter and write down what you decided.

## Rules that hold in the prose

- **No person is named.** The prose refers to everyone by description, exactly as Chapters 201 to 210 do: the man the figure of twenty-one years is against, the registrar of this district's records office, the man of about thirty-four who digs loam, the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing, the man of fifty-six, the man of about thirty-one who mends a dray, the woman of fifty-eight, the man with the portfolio, the man of about thirty at the counter, the man of about twenty-three who copies for nothing, the man of about thirty-four who keeps a scale, the man of about thirty-four who keeps a tally for six households, the woman of about thirty-four who keeps a goat, the man of about fifty-five who keeps a building, a man of about nineteen who counts, the man who keeps a road. **Cael Orin is a name on a stranger's sheet and has been said once in a yard; if you say it again, a clerk of nineteen years must enter what that does and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing must say the thing about a name said twice.**
- **No place is named on any document and none is named in narration.** The ninth place is what the documents say. The Registry's place is named on the lease. The Uplands are a place with nine things in it, four generations of keepers, and caves nobody has emptied. **The Lock Council is a body a letter comes from and it is not named as a place.**
- **The counting motif is load-bearing and every figure in it must reproduce.** Count the printed sentence, do not estimate it. Words separated by spaces; a hyphenated numeral is one word. **Batch 0001 found sixteen claims that had been estimated and every one of them was low. Do not repeat that.** An all-caps restatement at the foot of a chapter that repeats a count must repeat the body's figure, and a quotation of an earlier chapter's figure must repeat that chapter's figure. Verify before you save.
- **The counts that did not move must not move without a reason on the page:** things this district does not have **5**; instruments built and not named **6**; documents nobody owns **3**; protected things **5** and no sixth; conditions with no end on it **4**; refusals to join two things **13** and the registrar's own count **12**; refusals to read **9** with the departure unspent and no date in the ledger; refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given **6**; boards **5** and lines full **6** with the seventh at the ninth place ruled and empty; columns of not-askings **4**; a rate turning a year into coppers **none, and there never has been one**.
- **Binding cannot create consent. A certificate is not consent. A person who has not been asked has not refused and cannot be counted either way and the record says not asked. An address is not a bearer. A name put on a figure cannot be taken off it by the person it is put on. A year is not an asset. A toll is a thing a named person pays under a term. A yoke-load is a job. A mark may not carry a job. A resemblance noticed a second time is not a finding and a third time is a habit.**
- **Feet, inches, yards, miles, quarters, pounds, ounces, coppers, pints, degrees and the twelve-hour clock only. No metric, no twenty-four-hour clock, no weekday on any date.** Weekday names may be spoken by a character while a clerk of nineteen years enters that no weekday is attached to any date in this district; that pattern is in Chapter 203 and one weekday was removed from Chapter 207 by the last pass. Do not add one.
- **One System panel at most in a chapter, at most three in the block, and one may be disagreed with out loud in a yard.** A panel may not create consent or make a person a bearer. At most about thirty-five bold clauses a chapter; Chapters 209 and 210 of Batch 0001 came in low and the count is disclosed in the record.
- **Unresolved and to be left so:** the romance, the office, the fourth line of the offer, the city on the agent's ledger, the agent's name in any register of the west bank, the blank lot, Iven, the origin of the Common Measure, the First House, the removed signature, the three unexplained things of Volume 01, the boy's name in a column two days of road away, the two figures of five years and nine years. **No new final enemy. Selik Marne is a wary ally and not a friend.**
