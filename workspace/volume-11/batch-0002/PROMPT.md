## 1. What this block is

**This block's job is the first escalation and the midpoint reversal, and the reversal is on day 24 and it is a question, not an answer.**

- **Day 24. THE MIDPOINT REVERSAL, SPOKEN IN A MOUTH, IN THE ORDINARY VOICE, IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE, IN THAT YARD: somebody asks who said a thing has to be in writing to be true, and nobody in this district has ever said it, and the whole of what six volumes built is a book made out of things people said out loud in a yard.** A clerk enters it. ## 5. The relays and the method that keeps them alive

Five of the six relays go on once a chapter without being noticed: `the record about the not asking says not asked`; `read the number back to himself in a low voice`; `got it up about nine inches`; `by ten there were about nineteen people`; `the record about the not offering says nothing was asked and nothing was given`. `was not asked about the eleven miles` goes on fourteen of fifteen. `at the foot of that low wall with his coat folded on the stones` is a floor and not a target and may be silent on days when a different paragraph happens to that man.

**The duplication method, and this is the one thing worth carrying out of the last block.** Batch 0001's first sweep found nine duplicated sentences in ninety-one places, all in the daily frame, and the paragraph-size sweep found none of them. Every one was repaired by **appending a chapter-specific clause to the end of the sentence** — a fact about that morning — with no scene, figure, time, name or beat moved. **A frame sentence repeated on fifteen mornings is not a defect to be deleted and is not a figure to be raised. It is a sentence that needs a different tail.** Run both sweeps before you finish: the sentence-size one is the one that finds things.

**Both of them are blind below twelve words, and this block had an eleven-word run that both of them passed. If you sweep at one size you have decided what size to look at.**
