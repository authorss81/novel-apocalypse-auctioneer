The sentence is counted with words separated by spaces, a hyphenated numeral taken as one word, and the trailing full stop not counted. A claim in the form *in N words* is a second class and is swept separately. COUNT THE PRINTED SENTENCE. NEVER ESTIMATE IT. SET EVERY FIGURE BY MEASURING THE PRINTED SENTENCE AND WRITING THE CLAIM AFTERWARDS, AND IF A SENTENCE MOVES AFTERWARDS, RE-MEASURE EVERY CLAIM THAT SITS ON OR NEAR IT RATHER THAN RE-AIMING IT.**
