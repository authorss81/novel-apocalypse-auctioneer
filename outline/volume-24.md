## 2. The central pressure, in one sentence, and the five reasons it cannot be moved

**The piece.** A foot and about four inches of sound pallet with a square cut end has been carried in that yard for two volumes, is now lying in the open on a table where anybody walking past can see it, fits a corner that is a quarter of an inch low, and has not been laid in.

A reader could name that without a figure: **there is a piece of wood in this yard that belongs somewhere, and the one man who knows where it belongs has not put it there, and nobody is allowed to ask him why.**

### 2.1 The five reasons it cannot be moved, each with the thing that would move it

1. **Nobody may ask him what it is for.** Not the clerk, not the boy, not the man of about thirty-two who works the same table all morning, not the woman of fifty-two, not the man of thirty-eight, not anybody. The asking is not stopped and not withdrawn and has not gone quiet; it is simply not there, and the readout may not print that in any form, including as a count.
2. **The piece is not a figure.** It is not on the page at the back of that lot book, not in the lot book, not on a wall, not down that lane, not on a board, and no instrument may be pointed at it.
3. **There is nothing to thank him for.** The table is his own. Putting a corner of his own table right is not a favour to anybody in that yard, and the measure has never taken a line for anything he did and does not start now.
4. **He has given no reason and is not going to be asked for one.** He does not say out loud what the piece is for on any morning of this volume, and no other person supplies one.
5. **A corner of a table in that yard is not a thing the yard keeps records of.** The only record there takes a line when somebody says a reason out loud, and a piece of wood laid into a corner is not a reason said out loud.

### 2.2 The cheaper moves, and why each fails

Having somebody else lay it in fails — that is a new pair of hands on another man's table and it needs the man of about thirty-four to give the piece up, and giving it up is a new authority. Having the page at the back of that lot book take a line for it fails — that page has never taken a line for anything he did and does not start now, and the payment morning is fixed as a morning it takes no line on. Having him say why fails — he has not got one out loud, and inventing one is a new institution, exactly as it was for the turner. Having him be thanked for it fails — nobody in that yard has anything to thank him for and the thanking would be a new custom. Having the man of about thirty-two's five notched boards explained fails — see prohibition 65. And having somebody at the top of that lane be given the piece, or the table, or the corner fails — a person going up that lane is not a page and no second space is filled.
