## 2. THE CENTRAL PRESSURE, EVERY REASON IT CANNOT BE FIXED IN PLACE, AND THE CHEAPER MOVES THAT FAIL

### 2.1 THE PRESSURE, IN ONE SENTENCE

**THE YARD HAS A QUESTION IT IS ALLOWED TO ASK AND NOBODY IN IT IS ALLOWED TO ANSWER, IT HAS BEGUN ASKING IT ANYWAY, THE ASKING IS NOW A THING THAT HAPPENS IN THAT YARD MOST MORNINGS, AND THERE IS NO RULE, NO PAGE, NO OFFICE AND NOBODY WITH THE STANDING TO STOP IT.**

**AND IT IS NOT A NEW OBJECT. IT IS THE PERMISSION THE VOLUME BEHIND ME OPENED ON ITS LAST MORNING.** On Chapter 828, the one hundred and twelfth morning, a man of thirty-eight asked out loud, in about nineteen people's hearing, what the four figures on that wall are for, and a man of fifty-six said he did not know, and from that afternoon the man of fifty-six has said every morning at about four that he does not know, and anybody in that yard may ask him about it. **VOLUME 17 RESOLVED A QUESTION. VOLUME 18 IS ABOUT WHAT A DISTRICT DOES WITH A PERMISSION, AND A PERMISSION WITH NO PROCEDURE IN IT IS NOT A RULE, IT IS A HOLE WITH A MAN IN IT.**

### 2.2 THE FIVE REASONS IT CANNOT BE FIXED IN PLACE, EACH WITH THE THING THAT FIXES IT AND WHY IT DOES NOT

1. **THE ROOM NINE MILES UP THAT HILL ALREADY HAS THE QUESTION AND DID NOT SEND FOR THE PERSON WHO COULD ANSWER IT.** **Sabra Holt, fifty-two, nineteen years an appointed administrator, the third of the nine oldest keys**, is the one person in this district a room can be asked about a figure, and that room has known it for nineteen years and did not send for her. **AND A QUESTION PUT TO A ROOM COMES BACK DOWN THAT LANE AS A PAGE, AND A PAGE HAS NO MOUTH.** The turner came up the eleven feet on Chapter 840 and said *as sent* out loud in that yard for the first time in nine years and nobody turned round.
2. **THE DISTRICT'S OWN APPARATUS FOR A NAME ALREADY HAS ONE NAME IN IT AND THE HEADING STILL SAYS *WHOEVER*, AND THE ONE NAME DOES NOT SATISFY THE RULE, AND THE SECOND SPACE ON THAT FORM IS EMPTY.** The rule behind the column is that a person's own asking is what goes in, and it is in a book of a Council's. **TO WRITE THE ASKING DOWN, THIS YARD WOULD HAVE TO NAME WHOEVER ASKED, AND NO PERSON IN THIS YARD IS GOING TO NAME THE YARD.** The man at the end of the second table, about thirty-seven, unnamed, fifteen years at that table, on no page, said it out loud on the one hundred and twenty-first morning and this file does not improve on his sentence: **a room does not ask the person it wants, it asks the yard.**
3. **THE INSTRUMENT THAT COULD MAKE A QUESTION INTO A THING HAS BEEN USED ONCE, ON A PIECE OF GROUND, FOR A TOLL OF NINETY-EIGHT, AND THAT TOLL IS SPENT.** Sixteen people gave five and three gave six and the sum came to ninety-eight. **The toll may not be raised, lowered or paid twice, and the condition on the seat is unnamed, and a man of sixty-one said out loud that he was not going to name it, and so nobody in this yard is able to point the thing at a question, because there is no saying in it to point with.**
4. **THE MAN WHO CAN ANSWER IS NOT ANSWERING, AND SAYING SO IS THE ONE THING HE DOES EVERY MORNING, AND IT CANNOT BE PRESSED.** He gets the four twice a morning and has said every morning at about four that he does not know what any of them is for, and anybody in that yard may ask him about it. **A PERMISSION CANNOT BE WITHDRAWN BY A YARD OF ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE WITH ONE QUESTION AND NO OFFICE, AND HE IS NOT A FUNCTIONARY AND HAS NEVER ASKED FOR ANYTHING.**
5. **THE ONE THING THAT WOULD SETTLE IT — WORKING OUT HOW FAR FOUR HUNDRED AND ELEVEN IS OUT — IS NOT DONE BY ANYBODY IN THIS BASIN, IS NOT COMPUTED IN ANY DOCUMENT IN THIS TREE, AND IS NOT DONE IN THIS VOLUME.** **AND THE SIZE OF THAT ERROR IS NOT A FIGURE THIS FILE MAY PRINT, IN A LEDGER, IN A FIGURE BLOCK, OR IN A MOUTH, AND A CHAPTER THAT NAMES IT AS A MOVE THIS VOLUME COULD HAVE MADE IS A CHAPTER THAT HAS SAID THE THING OUT LOUD.** This file names it once, here, as the reason the pressure holds, and does not name it again.

- **PUT IT TO THE ROOM NINE MILES UP THE HILL.** Fails at reason 1. It also fails because the column is for whoever **read a thing out loud**, and the yard has not read a thing out loud, it has asked one, and the man of fifty-six refused the column out loud and the asking was entered and the one name in it does not satisfy the rule.
- **USE THE FOUNDER'S CLAUSE AGAIN.** Fails at reason 3. ## 3. THE RESOLUTION, AS A THING, WITH ITS MEASURE AND THE FIGURE THAT MEASURE HAS NO INTERCEPT FOR

### 3.1 THE THING

**FROM CHAPTER 886, `c = 46`, ON EVERY MORNING ON WHICH SOMEBODY IN THAT YARD ASKS THE MAN OF FIFTY-SIX A QUESTION OUT LOUD, ONE LINE GOES ON THE PAGE AT THE BACK OF THAT LOT BOOK, AND THE LINE CARRIES THE MORNING AND NOT THE QUESTION AND NOT THE ANSWER, AND THE CLERK READS THE MORNING OUT LOUD BEFORE SHE WRITES IT.**

**AND THAT IS THE WHOLE OF IT. IT IS NOT A RULE AND IT IS NOT A SCALE AND IT IS NOT A TRUST SENTENCE, AND EACH OF THOSE THREE REFUSALS IS A REQUIREMENT AND NOT A RESTRAINT:**

- **NOT A RULE.** Nobody may say that anybody has to ask, or that anybody may not ask, or how often, or on whose behalf, or what happens to an answer. The permission that has existed since the one hundred and twenty-fourth morning is unchanged and this does not widen it and does not close it. **The line on the page records that a morning happened, and a morning is not a rule and cannot be obeyed.**
- **NOT A SCALE.** It does not weigh anybody, it does not rank anybody, and no person in that yard is paid, given, put in a column, or put on ground by it. **The man asked is not thanked and the person who asked is not thanked, and the man who fitted the rail is still not to be thanked, and nothing on that page is a payment of the fifth of the five, which stays five and unpaid on all fifty mornings.**
- **NOT A TRUST SENTENCE.** Nobody is trusted by it and nobody vouches for anybody. It is a clerk writing a morning in her own hand and reading it out loud first, and that is the whole of the standing it has. **She is a clerk and not a person who finds out, and she says so out loud in that yard, and it has been said before.**

### 3.2 THE MEASURE

**THE MEASURE IS THE NUMBER OF LINES ON THE PAGE AT THE BACK OF THAT LOT BOOK THAT WERE PUT THERE BECAUSE SOMEBODY ASKED THE MAN OF FIFTY-SIX SOMETHING, AND IT IS ONE ON A MORNING SOMEBODY ASKED AND NOTHING ON A MORNING NOBODY DID, AND THE MORNINGS IT DID NOT MOVE ON ARE NAMED ON THE PAGE IN HER OWN HAND.** Chapter 888, `c = 48`, is one of them and the morning is named on the page.

### 3.3 THE FIGURE THAT MEASURE HAS NO INTERCEPT FOR, AND WHY THAT IS THE POINT

**THE FIGURE IS THE NUMBER OF MORNINGS ON WHICH SOMEBODY ASKED BEFORE THE PAGE BEGAN TO TAKE THEM. IT HAS NO INTERCEPT. IT MAY NOT BE GIVEN ONE. IT MAY NOT BE ESTIMATED, RECOVERED, INFERRED, CARRIED FORWARD, OR PRINTED IN A FIGURE BLOCK.**

**AND THE REASON IS PRINTED IN WORDS IN THE FIGURE BLOCK ON EVERY MORNING THE PAGE DOES NOT MOVE, AND IT IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT THIS VOLUME RESOLVES: THE FIRST ASKING WAS NOT ON THE PAGE. IT WAS NOT ON ANYTHING. ADRIAN VALE'S WAS ON THE ONE HUNDRED AND TWELFTH MORNING AND HE WAS NOT COUNTING AND NOBODY WAS COUNTING, AND BEFORE THAT A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX READ FOUR FIGURES OFF A WALL TWICE A MORNING FOR NINE YEARS AND WAS NEVER ASKED ANYTHING, AND THE MAN WHO PUTS THE TABLES UP, THE MAN WHO KEEPS A TALLY, THE MAN WHO DIGS LOAM, THE MAN WHO DRIVES THE CART, THE MAN WHO MADE THE STONE AND THE MAN WHO FITTED THE RAIL WERE ALL IN THAT YARD THE WHOLE TIME, AND NONE OF IT WAS COUNTED, AND IT CANNOT BE RECOVERED BY ANYBODY.**

**AND THE CONSEQUENCE IS STRUCTURAL AND A WRITER MUST NOT WALK PAST IT: THERE IS NO LINE THAT WOULD LET A SUCCESSOR WORK OUT WHAT THIS NUMBER IS TOMORROW, BECAUSE THE NUMBER IS NOT ONE A MORNING AND NEVER WAS, AND A FIGURE THAT SOMETIMES MOVES AND SOMETIMES DOES NOT IS NOT A LADDER AND MUST NOT BE TREATED AS ONE. A CHAPTER MAY NOT PRINT A PROJECTED VERSION OF IT. A FIGURE BLOCK MAY NOT CARRY IT AS A CELL. THE ONLY FIGURE THE BLOCK CARRIES IS THE WHOLE OF THE LINES ON THAT PAGE, WHICH IS A COUNT AND HAS BEEN A COUNT SINCE THE PAGE HAD NOTHING RULED ON IT.**
