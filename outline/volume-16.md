## 4. THE CENTRAL PRESSURE, AND WHY IT IS THE FIFTH OF THE FIVE THINGS

**THE CENTRAL PRESSURE OF VOLUME 16 IS THE PAGE THAT CAME DOWN THAT LANE, AND IT IS A LETTER, AND IT IS NOT A LOT.** On the third morning a stranger comes down that lane with a page in his own right hand, as four strangers did in Volume 15, and this one leaves it face down on that second table and goes back up without saying a word.

**THE PAGE ASKS THIS DISTRICT TO NAME ONE PERSON WHO CAN BE PAID FOR SITTING IN A ROOM FOR A TERM.** The First House is convening a sitting on the founder's clause. Any local market that intends to hold a local charter after that sitting must put forward one named person, and that person must be *payable* — a figure, a term, a bearer.

**AND THIS DISTRICT CANNOT PAY ONE PERSON BECAUSE THE FIFTH OF THE FIVE THINGS THIS DISTRICT DOES NOT HAVE IS A WAY TO PAY A PERSON WHO IS NOT IN A HOUSEHOLD. IT HAS BEEN UNPAID FOR FIFTY MORNINGS AND THE COUNT OF THE FIVE IS STILL FIVE AND IT IS NOT PAID IN THIS VOLUME.**

**THE TURN OF THE WHOLE PRESSURE, AND IT IS THE ONLY THING IN THIS OUTLINE THAT MUST NOT BE SOFTENED: the district's fifty-morning shame is now its credential, and the man the fifth line names and cannot pay is now the reason the district cannot sit at the table, and nobody in that yard has ever been told that the two are the same thing.** The unpaid man does not become a plot device. He is not rescued, he is not given the job, he is not paid, and Adrian does not make him a hero. He says no to being named and his no is correct.

**AND THE ANSWER THE DISTRICT SENDS UP THE LANE ON THE TENTH MORNING IS ONE NAME, AND IT IS ADRIAN'S, AND IT STATES NO FIGURE AND NO TERM, AND THE REASON IT STATES NEITHER IS THE POINT OF IT: the letter says in terms that this district can send a person and cannot send a promise, and it gives the reason in a full paragraph: that paying the fifth of the five would take an instrument, and an instrument is a thing a district makes, and the district holds nine coppers with a person attached to every one of them and no form to put them against a year. **THE CLOTH AND THE BAG ARE NOT ON THE PAGE. THE WOMAN OF FIFTY-EIGHT SAYS THE CLOTH OUT LOUD IN THAT YARD ON THE SAME MORNING AND IT IS NOT WRITTEN DOWN, AND THIS FILE USED TO SAY THE PAGE CARRIED IT IN NINE WORDS AND THAT WAS WRONG.** **THE BLOCK DOES NOT CLOSE ON THE BOY ADDING A COLUMN, AND THE CHAPTERS ARE CANON AGAINST THIS LINE: it closes on a rule *against* a column being entered in the book, written by the clerk and read out at four o'clock, both on the forty-fourth morning, and on a stranger coming down that lane at the top of it with the only copy of a note a man took in a corridor in Room Four.
