## 2. THE CENTRAL PRESSURE, AND WHY IT CANNOT BE FIXED IN PLACE

**A SHEET OF PAPER WITH THREE LINES ON IT — THE SAME THREE LINES, THE SAME WORDS, THE SAME FIGURES — IS LYING ON THE END OF THE SECOND TABLE IN THE YARD OF LOT SEVENTEEN, AND THE THIRD LINE OF IT IS EMPTY, AND THE THIRD LINE OF THE ORIGINAL IS A DATE, AND A STRANGER STANDING IN FRONT OF BOTH OF THEM CANNOT TELL WHICH ONE THIS DISTRICT OWNS.**

**IT CANNOT BE FIXED IN PLACE FOR THREE REASONS AND ALL THREE ARE ON THE PAGE.**

1. **THE THIRD LINE IS A FIGURE OF A PERSON.** The original carries a date, and the date is the day the water stopped being a foot, and the only person in this district who knew that day is the man who was standing in the ditch when it stopped. A filled third line is a public record of a person. A blank third line is a public record of nothing. **This district has spent six volumes saying that a public record in this basin is a record of a thing and not of a record of a person, and the two sheets in that yard are the same shape and one of them is a person and the other of them is not, and neither sheet says which is which.**
2. **THE SECOND LINE OF BOTH OF THEM IS WRONG IN THE SAME WAY.** The figure on the second line of the original is a day out and always has been. A person who copied the book faithfully copied the error. **A copy that carries the error is indistinguishable from an honest copy, and the district's only defence against a forgery has been that this district does not forge, and that defence is about conduct and not about the page.**
3. **THE DISTRICT'S ONLY UNCHECKABLE FIGURE IS IN THE SAME YARD AND CANNOT BE COMPARED WITH EITHER.** The wear in the underside of the stone lying face up on the end of the second table cannot be a day out, because it names no day, and cannot be checked by anybody, including the man who put his thumb in it. Volume 10 ended with both figures in the open and neither chosen. **A copy brings a third thing to a table that already holds two answers to one question, and a table in this district can hold two and this file does not know that it can hold three, and the volume's job is to find out on the page.**

**THE FIGURE THE PROTAGONIST IS AFRAID OF IS STILL A DAY. HE SAID IT ONCE, IN A YARD, IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE, ON THE FOURTEENTH MORNING AFTER THE COUNT, AND HE WAS NOT ASKED FOR IT AGAIN AND NOBODY AGREED WITH HIM. THIS VOLUME PUTS A DAY IN FRONT OF HIM THAT HE DID NOT PUT THERE.**

## 3. THE RESOLUTION, AND IT IS A THING AND NOT A RULE AND NOT A POST AND NOT A FOURTH COLUMN

**THE FOURTH LINE.**

**THE LOT BOOK ON THE END OF THAT FIRST TABLE HAS THREE LINES. IT HAS HAD THREE LINES SINCE THE SECOND OF THE TWELFTH MONTH AND IT HAS HAD NO FOURTH LINE ON ANY DAY OF TEN VOLUMES, AND THAT ABSENCE IS NAMED IN THE INHERITED LEDGER EVERY MORNING. THE RESOLUTION OF THIS VOLUME IS THAT THE BOOK GETS A FOURTH LINE, AND IT IS PUT THERE IN THE OPEN, IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE, BY A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS, ON A NAMED DAY OF THE LAST BLOCK, AND IT IS WRITTEN IN HER OWN HAND AND NOT AT ANYBODY'S DICTA.**

**AND THE FOURTH LINE IS NOT A NAME AND NOT A FIGURE AND NOT A DATE, AND THAT IS WHY IT IS THE RESOLUTION AND NOT A DEFEAT. IT IS NINE WORDS. IT SAYS THAT THE THIRD LINE ABOVE IS A FIGURE OF A PERSON AND THAT THE PERSON IS NOT NAMED IN THIS BOOK. A STRANGER CAN NOW STAND IN FRONT OF A COPY WITH AN EMPTY THIRD LINE AND A BOOK WITH A FULL ONE AND KNOW WHICH IS THE COPY, BECAUSE THE COPY DOES NOT HAVE THE FOURTH LINE AND THE COPY CANNOT HAVE IT, BECAUSE THE FOURTH LINE IS AN ADMISSION RATHER THAN A FIGURE AND A PERSON WHO MAKES ONE IS MAKING IT ABOUT THEMSELVES.**

**THE COST, ALLOCATED TO BODIES, BECAUSE A RESOLUTION WITH NO COST IS A RULE:**

- **THE DISTRICT LOSES THE ONE PUBLIC EXPIRY IT HAS.** Before the fourth line, the third line was the only thing in this district that went out of date on its own. A fourth line admitting that the third line is a figure of a person means the district has said out loud, in a book a stranger may walk up to, that its own best instrument is a record of a man. **The count of things this district has made moves from twelve to thirteen, and the thirteen is smaller than the twelve.**
- **A PERSON WHO COPIES THE BOOK AFTER THE FOURTH LINE IS COPYING AN ADMISSION**, which is easier to copy than a figure and costs the copier nothing.
- **THE DISTRICT CANNOT NOW PROVE THE COPY WAS A COPY OF THIS BOOK**, because it has admitted in public that the third line is about somebody it will not name, and a copy with an empty third line is what that admission looks like from nine hundred yards off.
- **THE MAN WHO DIGS LOAM IS THE PERSON THE THIRD LINE IS ABOUT, AND HE IS NOT ASKED TO AGREE, AND HE IS NOT ASKED WHETHER THE NINE WORDS ARE TRUE, AND HE PUTS HIS OWN THUMB IN THE HOLLOW IN THE UNDERSIDE OF THAT STONE ON THE DAY THEY GO ON, AND HE DOES NOT SAY ONE WORD ABOUT IT, AND ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE AT THAT TABLE HAVE SAID SINCE THAT THEY DO NOT KNOW WHETHER HE AGREED.**
- **NO COLUMN IS RULED FOR THE FOURTH LINE AND NO CLERK WILL RULE ONE**, on the ground a clerk of nineteen years gave out loud in a yard and entered: **that a fourth line is not a column and that a column is something a person looks down and a line is something a person reads.**
