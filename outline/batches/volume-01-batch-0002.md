# Volume 01 Batch 0002 Outline: The First Hammer

Batch number: 0002

Chapter range: 11–20

Beat structure: **Recovery (Chapters 11–12) → Investigation and boundary (13–14) → Practical work and institutional test (15–16) → Escalation (17–18) → Climax and turn (19–20)**

## Pressure rotation

- **Ch 11 — character and political work:** demand arrives before procedure does.
- **Ch 12 — political pressure and a physical interruption:** a false-alarm evacuation makes Selik's tightening credible.
- **Ch 13 — investigation:** the counterfeit is a network, not one document.
- **Ch 14 — character work and boundary:** the Unlisted make the protected category concrete.
- **Ch 15 — training and work:** standing is learned from a driver, not from a reading.
- **Ch 16 — political, institutional, and return work:** the board runs its first real closing, the one that brings the far-bank party home.
- **Ch 17 — escalation and temptation:** a crisis larger than the honest process.
- **Ch 18 — character failure:** Adrian postpones the return term and hides his security.
- **Ch 19 — action under public terms:** one full auction, an outward success.
- **Ch 20 — aftermath turning to dread:** the return bus is missing.

Chapters 11–12 open with consequence rather than a new object. Two later chapters carry physical action: Chapter 16, a bounded return crossing run by the board, and Chapter 19, the full auction. They are separated from each other and from the Chapter 12 evacuation by investigation, character work, training, political pressure, and character failure.

## Batch goal

Convert the first successful but fragile Last Fare crossing into a public service that can be inspected, challenged, and renewed, then let a larger medicine crisis make that service politically irresistible. Adrian must begin to organize a full public auction before the batch ends. He will treat the return leg as an administrative extension of the outward bargain and use a private workshop promise to make the sale look safer than it is. Do not complete the full backwash reversal in this batch. That reversal is reserved for Chapters 23–26. By Chapter 20, the convoy should appear to have crossed successfully while the return group is already late or visibly wrong.

## Starting condition

- Year 17 after the Long Fracture, several days after the first public crossing.
- "Ward Nine" names a district and a platform on opposite banks. The ward, its clinic lines, and the destination are across the water. The Ward Nine old platform and its one-armed bell post are on the near side, on the Lot 17 apron, so the first charter's marked return brought the bus home to Alder Reach. The route crosses; the return lands beside Lot 17.
- The Last Fare is cracked after two activations and sits in a documented public case at Lot 17. It is not freely reusable, and repeated use may strand the route permanently.
- It opens one route across St. Orra for twelve minutes. Every traveler loses the memory of why they are traveling; anyone who travels back pays again.
- It needs a driver who knows pre-Fracture Line Six, a marked return at the Ward Nine bell-post platform, and a passenger who can remember the destination. Tarin Kest was the sole bearer. Dessa Rill was separately named as the return traveler.
- Lot 17 has a public lot book, a seven-day steward term, a protected-name rule, and a public challenge path.
- The public lot board is a constituted four-seat panel: the registrar, the sitting steward, and two seats held by whoever in the yard has no claim on the lot. It can refuse to record a closing and call a review. It cannot grant a use.
- Adrian has signed a public undertaking in the lot book: before any next use he will state the full known toll, the return toll, the unresolved terms, and the remedy, and he will not use a private promise to make a public charter appear complete.

## Starting relationships

- Adrian and Mara have conditional professional trust built on disclosure and correction. Neither confuses it for forgiveness or romance.
- Tarin accepted the charter because the terms were visible. Bryn, his son, is personal pressure, not a plot rescue.
- Orren is an uneasy apprentice who values a page over an authority and keeps Sela Pike's name out of the public book.
- Lina will help the public route but will not hand over her community's names.
- Selik is credible on safety and Adrian is credible on access. Halloway is neither a secret villain nor a defeated one.

## Batch midpoint (Chapter 16)

Chapters 11–16 turn one successful crossing into an actual service. Mara creates a public schedule for proposals, the counterfeit dispute becomes public work rather than a warning, the Unlisted build a witness channel that holds a public duty without publishing a household, and Adrian pays for a route repair in labor rather than hiding the cost. The batch midpoint is Chapter 16, where the board runs a closing of its own and that closing brings the eight children home from the far bank. They arrive able to name the platform and unable to say why they were sent, and the boy who refused is the only person in the room who can account for the whole cost. The Hearth Line has now crossed twice under public terms and is still not dependable.

## Batch climax (Chapters 19–20)

Chapters 17–20 raise the scale until the honest process cannot keep up. A larger shipment, repair crews, and a school shelter group have to move in one crisis window. Adrian convenes the first full public auction, discloses the outward memory toll, frames the return as an administrative extension rather than a separately negotiable toll, and keeps a private workshop guarantee out of the public book. The outward crossing is a visible civic success. The return bus is not at the marked platform.

## Required character changes

- **Adrian:** begins able to state costs and unwilling to be limited by the room; ends having broken a public undertaking he signed, and knowing that the difference between concealment and postponement is only a matter of timing.
- **Mara:** begins with a procedure that works; ends unable to reach him through procedure alone and forced to treat his signed promise as evidence rather than reassurance.
- **Tarin:** moves from accepting a visible charter to stating what standing actually is, and to a refusal that is a form of safety.
- **Orren:** converts a stolen record into a public service and keeps the household out of it.
- **Lina:** stops treating Adrian as someone who will inevitably file her and starts treating Mara's process as a tool that must answer to people.
- **Selik:** tightens the official bridge after a false alarm and becomes more credible, not less. He must not become a secret villain or win the argument by decree.
- **Halloway:** his counterfeit is publicly exposed by a carrier, not by a verdict. His argument about certainty and price stays understandable.

## Required power changes

- Adrian remains **Stage 1 Reader** through Chapter 20. He cannot read continuously, predict the route, identify lies with certainty, split a use among bearers, transfer or renew a charter, or post security.
- He does not gain a new ability in this batch. What he gains is worse: a public promise he will break, and a sale that works.
- The batch must leave a visible cost: a breached undertaking, a strained relationship with Mara, and a cohort of returning travelers whose whereabouts are unknown at the end of Chapter 20.

## Required world reveals

- Selik's bridge closes more tightly after a false-alarm evacuation, and the tightening is a real safety decision with a real cost to Unlisted travelers.
- The counterfeit is distributed through several brokers using one copied form, and the broken-circle mark is used to make a document look like it belongs to a shared closing system.
- The protected category can be made administrable: a group, a count, a steward, a destination, a remedy, and a sealed page for anyone who must be identified.

## Open questions after the batch

- Where is the return group, and what did the missing return term actually cost them?
- What was in the private workshop guarantee, and who else was told about it?
- Why do three separate brokers carry the same copied form with the same mark?
- Can a public board hold a public undertaking, or does it only hold objects?
- Does Selik's tightened bridge become a temporary measure or a permanent Warden route?

## Chapter Cards

### Chapter 0011 — The Next Morning's Price

**POV:** Adrian Vale

**Location/time:** Lot 17, early morning several days after the first crossing; the repaired floorboards smell of wet wool and lamp oil.

**Immediate goal:** Get a new medicine shipment ready without turning the first successful charter into an unchallenged schedule.

**Resistance:** Families want the route reopened immediately. The public lot book has no procedure for deciding who gets the next opening. Adrian's hand still misbehaves after a reading. Mara refuses to let him write a promise that treats the next shipment as an entitlement.

**Information revealed:** Three legitimate claimants arrive with three incompatible needs, and none of them is dishonest. The scarcity is not the route; it is the fact that one twelve-minute opening cannot be fairly divided by the person who can read it.

**Action or decision:** Adrian refuses to rank the three claimants on his own authority and brings the ranking to the board instead, accepting that his reading can describe a route but cannot decide who deserves it.

**Emotional change:** Adrian discovers that he wants to be the one who ranks the claimants, and understands for the first time that wanting it is the reason Mara built the process. The relief of putting the decision somewhere he cannot overrule arrives only after the wanting has been admitted out loud.

**Power or resource change:** No new ability. Adrian accepts that a public reading is an input to a decision rather than the decision.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** The toll echo is a cost of reading, not a memory toll. Adrian's Chapter 6 gaps came from the bare-claimant toll he paid as default bearer without ever boarding, not from travel, and he can still inspect, write, and negotiate.

**Ending type:** discovery

**Next-chapter pull:** A courier brings news that Selik's bridge review has been shortened, while a private buyer has already paid Halloway for the next crossing. The board must be defended before the medicine arrives.

### Chapter 0012 — The Manifest Question

**POV:** Mara Quill

**Location/time:** Brass Verdict Hall and the Wardens' review room, late morning; wet coats, bridge permits, and a table covered in duplicate forms.

**Immediate goal:** Turn Selik's supervised bridge review into a real test of whether visibility is the only safe condition.

**Resistance:** Selik has a legitimate record of bridge failures and will not release a manifest rule without evidence. Halloway sends a witness who says disclosure always becomes a weapon against the poor. A feverish guardian and her child need care, and the child's household is not cleanly registered.

**Information revealed:** Mara finds a bridge record where the manifest was used to identify a missing person after a fault, and the same rule elsewhere used to expose an Unlisted household. The rule is not a plot. It is a tool that cuts both ways and has never been examined by the people it cuts.

**Action or decision — required beat, restored from the Volume 01 escalation:** partway through the review, a Warden gauge on the span faults and the span reports movement. Selik evacuates the deck: everyone off, counterweight locked, no exceptions, and the line closed until the reading is explained. A guardian who had just been granted a provisional berth is turned back with her feverish child. The alarm proves to be a false alarm — a chalked depth mark mistaken for a hairline crack, or a gauge with a bent float — and the span is sound. The evacuation is still correct, and the cost of it lands on the people with the least margin. Selik tightens the manifest requirement in the same breath, because the near-miss proves the span can report a fault it cannot explain, and he will not carry passengers on a structure he cannot interrogate. Mara records the false alarm and the evacuation together, and does not let the tightening pass as free caution.

**Major turn:** Mara proposes a supervised group designation, a named responsible party, and a public appeal, rather than either universal naming or silent exclusion. She acts before she is certain, which costs her the room.

**Emotional change:** Mara publicly disagrees with Selik without pretending his safety history is worthless, and accepts responsibility for the consequences of acting early.

**Power or resource change:** The review's written question survives; Mara's preferred remedy does not. Selik keeps operational authority and a tighter manifest. The false alarm becomes public evidence that his caution has a price, which protects his credibility for Chapters 27–33.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** Selik is not defeated and not exposed as a fraud. His tightening must remain understandable to a reader who has watched the span move.

**Ending type:** earned hook

**Next-chapter pull:** Selik accepts the review's written question but not Mara's remedy. Adrian must decide whether to use the official bridge's delay or press the public route again.

### Chapter 0013 — The Paper Network

**POV:** Orren Pike

**Location/time:** Ward Nine kitchen exchange, night; paper copies are hidden under floorboards while Halloway's people move crates outside.

**Immediate goal:** Trace the counterfeit's second distribution before another carrier uses the false return point, and learn what the broken-circle mark is doing on unrelated papers.

**Resistance:** Orren's stolen records are incomplete. A Unlisted household wants the paper destroyed. Halloway's crew is competent, paid, and more useful to the neighborhood than the public registry. Orren's anger about Sela makes him want to publish everything at once.

**Information revealed:** Several apparently separate brokers are using the same copied form. The broken-circle mark is not only a signature; it is being used to make a document look like it belongs to a shared closing system, which is a claim about legitimacy rather than about identity.

**Action or decision:** Orren preserves the dangerous evidence, keeps the household location out of the public record, and insists on a copy in the kitchen exchange and a public challenge path. He does not become obedient.

**Emotional change:** Orren chooses accuracy over both exposure and silence, and feels the cost of holding evidence nobody has thanked him for.

**Power or resource change:** The counterfeit becomes a public service problem rather than a moral warning. No new antagonist appears; the brokers are paid, ordinary, and connected to Halloway rather than to him directly.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** The mark is a clue only. Do not name a person, place, or institution behind it in this batch.

**Ending type:** discovery

**Next-chapter pull:** A courier reports that a private carrier has already accepted Halloway's claim and intends to cross before dawn. Adrian must prepare a public process before fear makes the counterfeit easier to believe.

### Chapter 0014 — The People Who Refuse Names

**POV:** Lina Vale

**Location/time:** A kitchen exchange in Alder Reach, morning; a public complaint board has been copied onto a wall. Eight of the shelter's nine children are across the water at Ward Nine and one is here at the yard where he refused to board, and the exchange is deciding what to do about a family in two places.

**Immediate goal:** Protect the shelter's children from the next manifest while securing a route that does not depend on a municipal list.

**Resistance:** Lina's community distrusts public lots because the last registry erased people. Mara's protected-name rule may be too vague to identify a bearer or remedy a breach. Some shelter families want total secrecy; others want the road even if it means a name is written.

**Information revealed:** A protected category can be made administrable. A sealed page held by the registrar can name a responsible party who must be identified, and it is opened only if the remedy is called. This is the rule the first crossing already needed and only wrote down afterwards.

**Action or decision:** Lina and Mara agree that the public book records a group, a count, a steward, a destination, and a remedy, while a paper witness network confirms who has chosen to participate. A person who refuses a name still receives care and can still challenge the closing.

**Emotional change:** Lina stops treating Adrian as someone who will inevitably file her and begins treating Mara's process as a tool that must answer to people and not only to clerks.

**Power or resource change:** The Unlisted gain a real channel inside the public process. It is not a loophole, and Mara makes the cost of administering it her own.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** Protected absence must not deny water, shelter, or medicine, and must not become a private monopoly on secrecy.

**Ending type:** earned hook

**Next-chapter pull:** The board receives its first challenge from a household that refuses to be named but accepts the route. The challenge is valid, forcing the next crossing to name a remedy before it opens.

### Chapter 0015 — What the Driver Knows

**POV:** Adrian Vale

**Location/time:** Tarin's repair bench beneath the Needle Road House, afternoon; the bench is crowded with axles, route cards, and a bus with a freshly changed east axle.

**Immediate goal:** Learn enough about Line Six and the return point to state a second charter honestly, without pretending his reading can replace a driver's knowledge.

**Resistance:** Tarin distrusts any "safe" route and refuses to let Adrian reduce driving to a chart. Adrian's reading is unstable, and Dessa's memory loss makes every practical question about return dangerous. The bus repair is expensive and the Road House needs Adrian's inspection work.

**Information revealed:** Standing is a relationship between a person, a route, and a return point, not a property of the token. A driver can hold a route in the hand and lose it in the reason.

**Action or decision:** Adrian pays for the axle repair with labor rather than hiding the cost, and Tarin agrees to consider another public charter only if the return condition is read before anyone boards.

**Emotional change:** Adrian's desire to lead is redirected toward preparation and disclosure. He accepts that Tarin's refusal can be a form of safety.

**Power or resource change:** Adrian gains practical knowledge he paid for and no new reading ability. The Hearth Line is shown to be a relationship that has to be maintained, not an asset.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** Tarin is not converted into a follower. He is a professional who will stop the route.

**Ending type:** discovery

**Next-chapter pull:** A second route card shows a maintenance cut the official map omitted. The next crossing may need a different return marker, and the Hearth Line cannot be a single fixed route forever.

### Chapter 0016 — The First Public Service

**POV:** Mara Quill

**Location/time:** Lot 17 and the public board, late afternoon and into evening; the first charter is copied into the lot book while a crowd argues about a second opening, and the eight children on the far bank are still waiting on the other side of the Fault.

**Immediate goal:** Establish the public lot book as an actual service with a challenge path, not a ceremonial record written after Adrian's decisions — and give the board its first real job by running the second bounded crossing, which is the one that brings the eight children home.

**Resistance:** Selik's review has not concluded and his manifest is now tighter. Halloway's counterfeit is still cheaper for some families. The Ward Nine cooperative wants priority. Adrian wants to open the route before the next shipment. A public board can become another gate if its rules are too slow or too abstract. The return marker Chapter 15 found is not the one the first charter used, so the second closing has to be written rather than copied, and a returning group of children is a harder thing to put on a bus than an outbound one.

**Information revealed:** The board works on someone other than Adrian. Its limits are also visible: it can refuse to record and call a review, and it cannot grant a use, so it can be outmaneuvered by patience as easily as by money.

**Required beat — the far-bank return:** the second closing is a return crossing, and it brings the eight children, the two escort stewards, and the four medicine-crew members who stayed on the far bank. The children come back able to name the platform and unable to say why they were sent there; the boy who refused stays dry on the near bank for the whole chapter and is the one person who can tell the room what the crossing cost, which is the argument the book needs and cannot invent. The stewards answer for the eight out loud. Nobody is quietly written off as accounted for. Two canon requirements must be honored in the closing itself: the Last Fare needs a driver who knows the route and a passenger who can remember the destination, and Dessa has already lost the memory that made her qualify, so the return charter must name a new remembering passenger and say plainly what that person is being asked to carry. And the eight children have already paid the toll once; nobody may quietly treat a second payment as more of the same, because whether anything is left to take from them is the most interesting fact in the chapter.

**Action or decision:** Mara creates a public schedule for proposals — a route request, a disclosed toll, a named bearer, a term, a remedy, two fresh witnesses, and a way for affected people to refuse — and then runs the first proposal through it. She rejects a permanent grant and rejects Adrian's private guarantee. Adrian agrees to make the next proposal public before activation.

**Emotional change:** Mara's process works on someone other than Adrian for the first time, and she discovers what her own rule costs: eight children come home protected by a category she invented, and not one of them can say what was done to them. She has to choose between a book that hides households and a room where a loss can be heard. Adrian watches her choose the hearing, and understands that the two things he wants are not the same thing.

**Power or resource change:** The Hearth Line crosses twice and is still not dependable: the token takes more visible damage, the return marker has to be re-learned, and eight children come home with a hole where a reason was. Adrian has institutional standing he cannot act outside of.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** The board's composition stays visible: registrar, sitting steward, and two rotating seats with no claim on the lot.

**Ending type:** discovery

**Next-chapter pull:** Two crossings have worked, and the news travels faster than the token does. A second, larger shelter group arrives asking for the same road, because the first group told them it was real, and the clinic announces a shipment larger than the first one. Medicine, repair crews, and a school shelter group must all move within the same crisis window, and the board now has a demand larger than its charter can honestly carry.

### Chapter 0017 — The Larger Shipment

**POV:** Adrian Vale

**Location/time:** St. Orra staging line and Lot 17, dawn after the announcement; the yard is filling with carts, clinic workers, and families.

**Immediate goal:** Determine whether the larger shipment can be moved safely under one public charter, or whether the community must accept a divided, less satisfying route.

**Resistance:** The shipment is politically irresistible. Selik's tightened official bridge can move some loads but not all. Halloway offers a complete private package that appears faster. A second and larger shelter group — not the eight who came home, but people who saw them come home — needs the same access as the clinic, and so do the Unlisted households. Adrian's hand and memory make a long reading expensive.

**Information revealed:** Waiting for a perfect public process now has a measurable cost in people, and rushing has a measurable cost in people. Neither number is knowable in advance, which is exactly the situation Adrian is worst at.

**Action or decision:** Adrian realizes that waiting may cost lives but a private bargain will hide the return cost. He begins drafting terms for a full public auction, asking Tarin, Mara, Orren, Lina, Selik, and the affected groups to make the terms harder to misuse.

**Emotional change:** Adrian's want expands from protecting Lot 17 to holding a region together, while his fear of another evacuation makes him eager to control the room.

**Power or resource change:** Adrian's scope expands without his power expanding. The danger of this chapter is scope, not capability.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** The concealment begins here as a decision, not a lapse. Adrian knows what he is choosing.

**Ending type:** discovery

**Next-chapter pull:** Halloway tells Adrian privately that the public board will be sold to the people who can afford certainty. Adrian does not have a rebuttal yet.

### Chapter 0018 — A Sale That Looks Complete

**POV:** Adrian Vale

**Location/time:** Lot 17 back office, night; the public board is covered with a new auction sheet and the private workshop inventory is being counted. The second and larger shelter group is boarding in the yard, not the eight children from the first crossing.

**Immediate goal:** Write terms detailed enough to attract the medicine convoy, repair crews, shelter families, and Wardens while keeping the route inside one activation.

**Resistance:** Every group wants a different bearer or destination. Selik demands a manifest. Halloway offers a private guarantee. Adrian knows the return memory toll is real but cannot prove how the route will handle a larger group. Mara demands that the return leg be named before the sale.

**Required beat — breach of a public undertaking:** Adrian signed in the lot book, in front of the room, that before any next use he would state the full known toll, the return toll, the unresolved terms, and the remedy, and that he would not use a private promise to make a public charter appear complete. Chapters 18–19 must be written as a breach of that signature, not as an ordinary working decision. Someone should say so out loud in Chapter 18 — Mara, Tarin, or Selik — and Adrian should answer with the sentence he tells himself makes a signed promise non-binding: that the outward crossing is the emergency, the return term can be written within the hour, and no one is being deceived because nothing has happened yet. That argument is the exact shape of his flaw. It must be plausible enough that a reader understands it and ugly enough that nobody in the room accepts it.

**Action or decision:** Adrian treats the return as a technical extension of the outward term and postpones its full disclosure until after the outward crossing. He uses a private workshop guarantee — the inspection press, a promised repair-crew term, or equivalent inventory — to make the sale appear financially secure, and keeps it out of the public book.

**Emotional change:** Adrian tells himself that the private promise is temporary and that the public process will catch the error. He has learned the shape of the mistake and not yet its cost.

**Power or resource change:** Adrian gains apparent credit he has not earned. The inventory that makes the sale look safe is real, and so is his obligation to it; that obligation becomes public in Chapters 23–26.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** The concealment is partial, not total. He states the memory toll in general terms. He conceals the return leg specifically, which is worse, not better.

**Ending type:** decision

**Next-chapter pull:** The auction sheet is completed and copied. The public board reads complete except for a return condition Adrian has privately promised to write later. The first convoy arrives at the staging line before dawn.

### Chapter 0019 — The Full Auction

**POV:** Adrian Vale

**Location/time:** Lot 17 sale floor and the St. Orra staging line, dawn; rain, lamps, the public board, and a crowd larger than the room can safely hold.

**Immediate goal:** Close one public charter for the larger shipment — medicine, repair crews, and a second, larger shelter group, not the eight who already crossed once — and make the outward crossing look like a decisive civic victory.

**Resistance:** The counterfeit is circulating among the crowd. Selik's Wardens are watching for an overclaim. Tarin is the only credible bearer, and the return standing is not fully understood. Unlisted participants fear the manifest. Families are desperate enough to accept terms they have not read. The medicine's cold chain will not wait for perfection.

**Information revealed:** A public process can be technically complete and substantively misleading, because the parts left out are the parts nobody is standing in the room to check. The counterfeit is exposed here by a carrier refusing its labor clause, not by a verdict, which is the only way exposure will ever work.

**Action or decision:** Adrian and Mara conduct a public auction for one defined use, not ownership and not a permanent route. Adrian discloses the outward memory toll and the fact that the return may cost again, but frames the return as part of the same administrative term rather than a separately negotiable toll. The public crowd accepts the terms because the immediate need is real and Adrian's private guarantee makes the sale feel safe.

**Emotional change:** Adrian experiences the familiar relief of a problem obeying a visible action. He believes he has made a route answerable to the public while still keeping the most dangerous term in his own hands.

**Power or resource change:** Adrian's public standing grows on the strength of an incomplete record. This is the last point in the batch at which his authority is uncontested.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** Mara's objection is on the record. Do not let her be absent, compliant, or magically reassured.

**Ending type:** earned hook

**Next-chapter pull:** The bus enters the route with the medicine convoy, repair crews, and the larger shelter group. The outward side succeeds. The return side does not return on time. Do not reveal the full backwash or Mara's accusation yet; the reversal belongs to Chapters 23–26.

### Chapter 0020 — The Late Return

**POV:** Adrian Vale

**Location/time:** Lot 17 and the St. Orra return line, morning after the larger outward crossing; the public board is still open and the crowd is waiting for the return bus.

**Immediate goal:** Bring the named return travelers back to the marked platform and keep the second activation from becoming a private success story.

**Resistance:** The return bus is late. Tarin and the returning group are moving on a line that no longer answers to the public board. The crowd wants the route kept open for the next load. Selik's Wardens want to close the line. Adrian's private guarantee and the missing return term are beginning to matter to people who never saw the negotiation.

**Information revealed:** The outward success created a public promise that the public terms do not describe. A crowd that watched medicine cross did not consent to the return cost, because the return cost was never in the document they accepted.

**Action or decision:** Adrian reads the term back from his own handwriting and finds that the return was folded inside the administrative term, exactly where he put it. He said out loud that the return would cost again, and he never said what that cost was or who would be asked to pay it separately. He does not yet know where the bus is. He must decide whether to extend the route for another load, reprice it, or open a public question while people are still outside and the document that admitted them contains no return term of its own. He chooses to open the question, and discovers that admitting the gap in public costs him the sale he just made.

**Emotional change:** The relief of the outward crossing curdles into dread. Adrian's first instinct is to extend or reprice the route; Mara forces him to say what is known and unknown before acting.

**Power or resource change:** Adrian loses the ability to speak for the route. The board becomes the only thing that can still act, and it can only record, refuse, and review.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** Do not resolve the missing group, restore the memory, or name the false destination's cause. The reversal is reserved for Chapters 23–26.

**Ending type:** decision

**Next-chapter pull:** The return bus is not at the marked platform. A public complaint is already being written, and the next medicine case is due before anyone has agreed how to describe what happened.
