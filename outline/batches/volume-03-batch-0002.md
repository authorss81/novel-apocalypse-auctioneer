## The rules this batch produced, all of them short and all of them in the book

1. **A DATE IS NOT A DECISION. A DATE IS THE LAST DAY SOMEBODY CAN DO SOMETHING, AND ON THE DAY THE DATE IS GONE THE THING IS EXACTLY AS IT WAS.** A rule that has never been tested is not a rule.
2. **A MECHANIC PUT THE BAR DOWN AND WAS NOT STOPPED, AND THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN A PERSON WHO IS STOPPED AND A PERSON WHO HAS NO STANDING TO STOP HIM IS THE DIFFERENCE THE WHOLE BATCH IS ABOUT.**
3. **A QUESTION THAT HAS NOT BEEN ANSWERED IS NOT A REFUSAL, AND THIS DISTRICT HAS ELEVEN REFUSALS AND NO QUESTIONS, AND THAT IS THE WORSE OF THE TWO.**
4. **A PARTY OF NINE PEOPLE GOING SOMEWHERE TOGETHER IS NOT AN INSTRUMENT AND IS NOT A JOB AND IS NOT A CHARTER.** Nine is nine agreements and not one.
5. **A PERSON ASKED TWICE IS NOT A PERSON ASKED ONCE AND NOT A PERSON ASKED THREE TIMES.**
6. **A DOCUMENT ABOUT PEOPLE SAID NINE AND EIGHT WENT, AND NOBODY OWES A CORRECTION, BECAUSE A DOCUMENT DOES NOT OWE ANYBODY A CORRECTION.**
7. **A LOT HAS SEVEN PARTS AND NOT FIVE, AND SIX OF THE SEVEN CAN BE WRITTEN DOWN AND THE SEVENTH IS A PERSON.** A lot with no bearer is not a lot, and a thing that is not a lot cannot be sold.
8. **BINDING CANNOT CREATE CONSENT AND IT CANNOT CREATE A PERSON.** A transfer needs a person to transfer from and a person to transfer to.
9. **AN ITEM'S PRICE CANNOT SEPARATE THE PERSON WHOSE BODY MAKES IT WORK, AND THE PRICE WAS NEVER THE THING.** A price is a number a person can hand over. The body is the thing.
10. **A LOOM IS DESCRIBED THE WAY A PERSON DESCRIBES A JOB AND NOT THE WAY A PERSON DESCRIBES A THING.** And **a rate sheet is described the way a person describes a thing**, and the two came from opposite banks of the same river and neither has met the other.
11. **A YEAR DOES NOT GO TWICE.**
12. **THE YEAR IS NOT THE SELLER'S.**
13. **THE COST OF A READING IS A PART OF THE LOT'S TOLL, AND THE TOLL ON THIS ONE IS A YEAR OUT OF A PERSON HE HAS MET TWICE, AND HE IS NOT GOING TO SPEND A PIECE OF SOMEBODY ELSE'S ON A QUESTION HE CAN ASK WITH HIS MOUTH.**
14. **A MARK IS A PERSON'S REFUSAL OF A NAME AND A MARK IS NOT A PERSON.** A mark may not be used as a bearer, a holder, a witness, a responsible party, or a person who is asked. And **a person who signs with a mark he has used since he was nine has said yes, and the mark cannot be counted, and that is a defect in the sheet and not a defect in the man.**
15. **A SIGNATURE HAS ALWAYS MEANT THE PERSON WAS IN THE ROOM, AND THIS IS NOT A FAULT IN THE FORM, AND IT IS THE ONLY THING THIS DISTRICT HAS FOUND IN NINE WEEKS THAT IS NOT A FAULT IN ANYBODY.**
16. **A CERTIFICATE IS VALID AND TRUTH IS A DIFFERENT THING, AND THE ONLY WAY TO BE WRONG ABOUT A CONSENT IN THIS BASIN IS TO BE RIGHT TWICE.** Nothing was forged.
17. **A STRIKE IS A THING AN INSTRUMENT DOES, AND A BINDER HAS FIVE POWERS, AND A STRIKE OF ANOTHER BODY'S INSTRUMENT IS NOT ONE OF THEM, AND A MAN WHO CANNOT STRIKE A THING IS NOT A MAN WHO HAS BEEN REFUSED.** A strike of a thing is also a thing a certificate can be.
18. **MY INSTRUMENT IS A BOOK. IT IS NINE COPIES ON ELEVEN BOARDS AND IT WORKS IF A PERSON COMES TO THE YARD.** Being the only person who can say no is not the same as being able to stop a thing.
19. **THE STRONGEST INSTRUMENT THIS DISTRICT OWNS AND A DRAWER THIRTY-FOUR MILES AWAY ARE THE SAME INSTRUMENT POINTING IN OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS.**
20. **A DISTRICT THAT RUNS OUT OF DATES IS IN THE SAME POSITION AS A DISTRICT THAT NEVER HAD ANY, AND IT TAKES ABOUT NINE WEEKS TO TELL THE TWO APART.**
21. **A NAME IN A ROOM WHERE THE PERSON IS NOT IN THE ROOM IS A THING YOU CARRY TO A DOOR.**
