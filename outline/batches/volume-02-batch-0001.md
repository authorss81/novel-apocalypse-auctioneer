## The numbers, fixed

- **The Harrow Tongue:** taken out of the flooded Harrow interchange **in the fourth year**, **thirteen** years in use, **five** of them in the hut at the ninth place and **eight** in eleven huts, three sheds and a box in a wagon. Present is Year 17. Four plus thirteen is seventeen; five plus eight is thirteen. Do not write "nine years in use."
- **The siding lease:** **nine years, signed by Tarin Kest in the ninth year**, expiring **in the fourth month of next year**, unrenewed and untold. The water column has been drawn off for the same nine years. A nine-year lease signed in Year 9 expires in Year 18, which is next year. **Do not write "signed in the fourth year" of this lease** — the fourth year belongs to the Tongue's salvage and to nothing else on this line.
- **The train on the siding:** arrived on the **eleventh of February**, nine vehicles. That is **twenty-four days** on the 7th, **twenty-six** on the 9th, and **thirty** on the 13th. Recompute from the eleventh of last month; do not carry a figure forward.
- **The stalls:** the line book has **eleven entries**. Two of them are one stall entered twice, so the line has **ten**. Add the floor in the hut at the ninth place and the keeper's figure is **twelve**; the tenth hand's is **eleven**. **Both numbers are in the book and neither is struck**, and that is the finding, not an error. The stall at the fourth place measured **344 yards on the 9th** and **361 on the 18th**, on an afternoon when nothing went through it.
- **The line book:** **906** movements indexed since the flood year, of which **141** name the ninth place as the origin held. The tenth hand has **211** of the 906. Sef Anyard's hut book has **61**, four of them in a second ink.
- **Reversals:** **nineteen** since the water came, **eleven** of which left a stall. Sef Anyard has held the hut **eleven years** and the Tongue **five**.
- **The origin column:** **four** entries that are not places in Sef's book and **four** in Orren's, **and they are not the same four**, so there is a fifth somewhere in a column of sixty-one the line book has not got. One of them is spelled out in full: **THE STANDING OFFICE OF CROSS-DISTRICT PASSAGE, ESTABLISHED, AND NOT AT THIS PLACE.** **In two documents, in two rooms, in two lines, not joined.** The registrar has agreed to keep them apart and the reason is entered.
- **The village:** **thirty-one people**, four houses and a chapel with a bell that is not rung. Asked of thirty-one on the 15th: **twenty-three answered, eight were not asked, and the count of yeses is one.** **Twenty-nine** people worked the line in the first year, not thirty-one.
- **The count rule:** a charter has a lot, a use, a bearer, a toll, a term and a remedy, and all six point at a person or an object. A place is none of those. The rule's four lines are **Adrian's**, written at a kitchen table in the second week of last month and approved by a yard of about six hundred.
- **The refusal:** Adrian has **not read since the eleventh of this month** and came back on the eighteenth, the day his own limit permitted a reading, and did not read. **The interval is six days.** He has read the brass once before, on the eighteenth of last month, and it cost nineteen minutes and left him unable to tell whether a thing was nearer or further. The non-event is entered with **no cost and no time**. **Do not write twenty-five days. Do not write a season.**
- **The money:** the rate is **fifty coppers a quarter** over **four thousand and one households**, in five columns, one trade abstained. The payment on the unstated claim is **1,190 / NO SHEET / 1,430**, entered as a payment and not a security, shortfall uncomputed and not nil. **1,190 coppers is about six-tenths of one per cent of the quarter's take.** The stair day was **44 / NO SHEET / 58**.
- **The board's five powers,** in the order painted on the wall in Chapter 51: **refuse to record a closing; publish a result; call a review; uphold a challenge in part; set or renew a short public custody term. And it cannot name a person.** **Publish a result is the second.** Say it as the second.

## The key rotation, fixed and continuous

- **Hester Vane** held from the twenty-sixth of February for **six days**, expiring at midnight on the 3rd. She refused to name her own successor in a yard of about sixty.
- **Wren Ostler** named on the 2nd, key handed over at ten past twelve on the 2nd, **four days**, expiring midnight on the **6th**. Renewal was to be on the 5th.
- **The lapse.** The board named nobody on the 5th or the 6th. The **7th to the 11th had no holder at all**: the case stood shut in a locked office on a term that had run out, the object sat on a lapsed term for four days, and it is entered **as a lapse and not as a theft**. **It is the first lapse in the history of the public lot book and the first thing the standing instruction has ever actually cost anybody.**
- **Wren Ostler** again from the **12th**, four days, expiring midnight on the **16th**. Renewal on the 15th.
- **Mrs Halder** named on the 15th at about ten in the evening, holds from the **17th**, four days, and will not hold it alone. Nobody has the power to give her a second person. Entered as a standing failure.
- **A term of four days is the shortest this board has ever set.** Do not let it drift back to seven.

## Who is on the walk, and who is not

The **twenty-ninth** walk from the fourth place to the ninth is **Tarin Kest, the tenth hand, Ivo Stenn, and a person of the ninth place who has not been asked what she is going for.** Stenn is on it because he said in a yard on the eighteenth that a rate sheet starts with somebody walking onto the ground and counting.

**Selik Marne is not on the walk and must not be put on it.** He said in Chapter 59 that he will not be the man who does it, because it is a Warden's order and this is a public lot, and he said *that is not enough* from the rail on the 21st. His staffing of a hut at a place he considers unsafe belongs to **Chapters 71–80** and is not spent early.

**Adrian Vale is not on the walk either**, and the sheet says why: the twelfth stall is to be measured **by somebody who is not the reader**, and the tenth hand has entered that he will not walk a line with a man who cannot tell where the river is.

## The instruments this batch made

1. **A name in a book is a handle.** A name is not a promise to turn up. A name is entered only after the asking, with the asking's time.
2. **A step in a plan that asks somebody a question is not a step until it has an hour and a person.**
3. **A reading with no lot.** The sheet, the sleeve number, the reader, the hour, the duration, and two people who compared the hand. Not evidence, not a relic, not a charter, confers nothing, seats nobody.
4. **A lot in daily use: the remote custody.** Deliberately a worse record than a case, entered as such. Keeper named, not an officer, asked and asked again on the 29th including no. No case, no counter, no key. **The nine miles is the point.**
5. **A rule that transfers is a habit with a new address.** The second rail on a straight is four feet from a man; on a curve, a foot and a half, with the ballast at his chest.
6. **The scarcity word is a direction and not a queue.** Five words proposed, none adopted. What worked is a working page with no heading whose first line is **A TRAIN THAT CANNOT BE MOVED IS NOT WAITING. A TRAIN THAT CANNOT BE MOVED IS THE END OF THE LINE.**
