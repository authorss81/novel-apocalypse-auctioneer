## 3. The numbers, closed by this block

## 4. The System panel spent by this block, and the power not granted

- **One System panel in the block, in Chapter 175, in the yard at about half past twelve in the afternoon, unasked, and it is a rule, a warning and a choice. It repeats none of the seven panels spent in Chapters 103, 116, 125, 137, 145, 156 and 168, and no panel may appear in Chapters 181 to 190 without that check being made and said.**

> **A THING A STRANGER MAY READ AND NOT CARRY IS HALF AN INSTRUMENT, AND THE HALF THIS DISTRICT IS SHORT IS THE HALF THAT GOES.**
>
> **A SENTENCE SAID AT A COUNTER BY A PERSON WHO WAS NOT ASKED IS A SENTENCE AND NOT A DOCUMENT. A DOCUMENT IS A THING A PERSON HAS DECIDED TO KEEP.**
>
> **TO PUT A SENTENCE IN A PUBLIC BOOK IS TO MAKE IT A THING YOUR OFFICE STANDS BEHIND. YOU MAY DO THAT TO A HOUSE. YOU MAY NOT DO IT TO A PERSON.**
>
> **A DISTRICT THAT CAN ONLY ACT ON WHAT A PERSON CARRIES HOME CANNOT ACT ON WHAT A PERSON SAID. THAT IS A LIMIT AND NOT A FINDING, AND A LIMIT IS STILL A THING TO WORK IN.**
>
> **YOU MAY NOT ASK HER A SECOND THING. THAT IS NOT A CHOICE YOU GET TO MAKE LATER.**

- **Underwriter is not begun in this district and the reason is entered and the reason is not a delay.** The reason has changed its shape a fourth time since the second of January and has not moved: **a man who cannot list what he has posted cannot pool a single toll, the fifth of the five is a person, and the fourth of the five is a receipt in a building thirty-four miles east that a stranger may read and may not take away.** A power change has to be earned through a cost and this block has not paid one. **Nothing in Chapters 171 to 180 is a screen for the power.**
- Adrian did not, in this block, maintain a public lot, coordinate more than one bearer, arrange a dispute hearing, or make the consequences of an object visible to a whole settlement. **He refused in Chapter 173 to put six households in a public book in four seconds and said why in forty-one words, and in Chapter 176 he said that the whole of what a Binder can do with a piece of paper about a child's name is nothing.** He could not close a transfer because the transfer has no named holder — **the farm with the absent owner is still unsold and the sluice is open and is not a lot** — and he could not make the figure of years a priority for anybody, and Chapter 172 gives the aggregate as a floor and not a total.
- **The echo of an origin toll is not reached for. The iron is not touched, read or referred to. The Last Fare is not touched, read or referred to.**
- **A year is not an asset, is not a security, and nobody can post one against it; a year and a half is not a figure; twenty-one years is not a price and four hundred years is not a total; two floors are not a sum; and there is no rate in this basin that turns one year into coppers and there never has been.** **The aggregate unpaid toll is four and two and eleven and four, which is twenty-one, and it is a floor and not a total, and a year and a half sits on a line of its own and is not added, and two people in a column are not known and not asked, and one frame took nothing.**
- **Binding cannot create consent; a certificate is not consent; a mark is not a person; a signature from two days' walk away is a signature and not a person who was in the room.** A right to withdraw is not a consent and is not a release. **A person who has not been asked has not refused and cannot be counted either way, and the record must say *not asked*.**
- **An address is not a bearer, a holder, a witness, a responsible party, or a person who was asked, and a name put on a figure cannot be taken off it by the person it is put on.** A name a house does not copy cannot be entered by anybody. **Four households are not four addresses and four addresses are four places to send something to.**
- **A house that writes everything on the face of a sheet has still taken the one thing a person cannot check**, and a district that cannot print a sentence has found the other end of the same rule.
