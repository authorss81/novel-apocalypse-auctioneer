A cordwainer of about forty-eight in a doorway at the west end of Alder Reach did not appear. **Batch 0005 added five and kept them apart: a woman of about thirty-six who keeps a scale at the sluice end of the loams and has stood at it nine years; a man of about forty-one who drives a salt cart and has done the thirty-four miles eleven times this year; a man of about thirty-seven who sells salt by the load out of a yard on the Verge; a man of about forty-eight who keeps a tally for six households at the sluice end of the loams, who is not the sign-writer of sixty-one; and a woman of about twenty-six who is at the fourth place because the platform is thirty feet long and it is dry.** **Give every one of them a trade, a doorway or a stall in the same paragraph and never let a pronoun carry the identification alone.**
