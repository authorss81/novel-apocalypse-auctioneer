**AND THE COST OF ALL THREE IS THE SAME COST: A SHELTER CANNOT BE REHEARSED, AND A DOCUMENT BUILT FOR PERSONS WHO HAVE NOT BEEN ASKED HAS NOT BEEN TESTED AGAINST ANYBODY AND IS BEING SIGNED ANYWAY. THAT IS WHAT A CONVENING IS, AND A CONVENING IS NOT THE SAME THING AS A SAFE THING, AND THE TWO WORDS ARE NOT THE SAME WORD.**
