## 3. The numbers, closed by this block

### The counting house, thirty-four miles east

- **A man of about forty behind the counter is a clerk of the house and is not a person in this district.** He asks what you want and does not wait to be told who you are. He refused the receipt in twenty-eight words: **the receipt is given to the man who brings the thing in, and the man who brought it in is in this district, and you are not him.** He said in nineteen words that a man may take his name off the sheet and may not take the figure off. He said in nineteen words that a named person is told once a season by letter and that about nine of those letters a year are returned and that the house does not send them twice. **The word REQUIRED is painted on the board outside the door and is not on the counter.**
- **The five terms, and they are fixed:**

> **THE HOUSE TAKES THE FIGURE A PERSON OWES AND NOT THE LAND THE FIGURE IS ON.**
>
> **THE HOUSE WILL NAME A BEARER FOR A FIGURE AND WILL FILL THE COLUMN ITSELF.**
>
> **THE NAMED PERSON WILL NOT BE ASKED WHETHER THEY WILL STAND.**
>
> **A FIGURE IS DISCHARGED WHEN THE HOUSE HOLDS A RECEIPT FOR IT. THE HOUSE KEPT THE RECEIPT AND WILL SHOW IT AT THIS COUNTER TO A STRANGER WHO ASKS.**

> **THIS SHEET IS THE TERMS OF THE HOUSE. THE HOUSE IS NOT A PERSON.**
