## 3. The three printed documents of this block, the two panels, and the test

**The test this volume settled: a document is introduced as a thing somebody reads out and is read back afterwards because it is the rule of the counter; a panel arrives unasked and is then argued with or not argued with.** This block carries three of the first kind and two of the second, and **the cap of two panels is met and the two are both disagreed with out loud, once in a barn by a man of about thirty-four who digs loam and once in a yard by a man of fifty-six.**

**THE FIRST DOCUMENT, the first local charter, was read out and read back in Chapter 244 and must reproduce exactly:**

>
> **IT IS FOR THE GROUND, THE DRAINS AND THE WATER OF ONE SETTLEMENT. THE NUMBER OF THAT SETTLEMENT AND THE NAME OF IT ARE NOT ON THIS SHEET, AND A HOUSE IS NOT A BEARER, AND A LIST OF HOUSES IS NOT A BEARER.**
>
> **THE BEARERS ARE THE FIVE NAMES UNDER THE LINE. A NAME GOES UNDER THE LINE ONLY IF THE PERSON IT BELONGS TO PUT IT THERE, IN THEIR OWN ROOM, WITH SOMEBODY STANDING IN THE DOOR, AND A NAME PUT UNDER THE LINE BY ANYBODY ELSE IS NOT ON THIS SHEET.**
>
> **THE TERM IS FIVE SEASONS. THE TERM BEGINS ON THE FIRST DAY OF THE NEXT MONTH AND IS COUNTED FROM THAT DAY AND NOT FROM THE DAY THE SHEET WAS SIGNED.**
>
> **ANY ONE OF THE FIVE MAY GIVE UP THE WHOLE OF IT ON THE FIRST DAY OF ANY SEASON AFTER THE FIRST, BY SAYING SO OUT LOUD TO ANY ONE OF THE OTHER FOUR. THE GIVING UP IS DONE ON THAT DAY. A GIVING UP IS NOT A REFUSAL AND IS NOT A COLUMN.**
>
> **A GIVING UP DOES NOT GIVE BACK A SEASON. NOTHING SOWN IS PULLED UP. THE COST OF A GIVING UP IS NOT MONEY. IT IS THAT THE ARRANGEMENTS ABOVE THAT SETTLEMENT GO BACK TO BEING HELD BY NOBODY'S AGREEMENT, AND THAT WORK ALREADY DONE IS NOT RETURNED, AND THAT THE SPENDING A GIVING UP DOES NOT RETURN IS NOT PRICED HERE BECAUSE THERE IS NO RATE IN THIS BASIN THAT TURNS A YEAR INTO COPPERS AND THERE NEVER HAS BEEN ONE. NOBODY OWNS THE WATER. A BEARER HOLDS A SHARE IN AN ARRANGEMENT, A SHARE IS NOT THE WHOLE OF IT, AND THE WHOLE OF IT IS NOT ANYBODY'S.**

**THE SECOND DOCUMENT, the covenant, was read out and read back in Chapter 246 and was carried by hand to three of the four counters in this district. It must reproduce exactly:**

> **THIS IS A COVENANT AND NOT A CONSTITUTION, AND IT SAYS SO AT THE TOP OF ITS OWN FACE.**
>
> **NINE STRUCTURES STAND IN THE WATER ABOVE FOUR DISTRICTS. THEY ARE NINE AND NOT TEN. THEY ARE FINITE. NO PERSON AND NO BODY IS THE HOLDER OF ANY OF THEM, AND NO PERSON SHALL HOLD THIS ALONE.**
>
> **THE STEWARDS ARE FOUR. THEY ARE CHANGED EVERY SEASON BY A SHOW OF HANDS IN THE PLACES THEY COME FROM. A STEWARD CARRIES THE FIGURES FOR ONE SEASON, IS NOT A HOLDER, IS NOT A BEARER OF THE WHOLE, AND MAY BE ASKED A QUESTION BY ANYBODY AT ANY TIME INSIDE THAT SEASON AND BY NOBODY AT ALL OUTSIDE IT.**
>

>

>
> **IF A PLACE DISAGREES, THE DISAGREEMENT GOES BY ITS OWN ROAD TO ANOTHER REGION AND IS PUT IN A BOOK THERE THAT A STRANGER MAY WALK UP TO. THE GOING IS CARRIED BY A PERSON AND NOT BY THE TOLL, AND THE COST OF THE GOING IS NOT PRICED HERE.**
>
> **THE TABLE HAS THREE COLUMNS. THE FIRST IS HEADED BY WHAT IS HELD. THE SECOND IS HEADED BY WHO HOLDS IT. THE THIRD IS HEADED BY A WORD AND NOT BY A NAME, AND THE THIRD IS EMPTY ON THE FACE OF THIS SHEET, AND IT IS EMPTY BECAUSE THE PEOPLE WHO WOULD GO IN IT HAVE NOT BEEN ASKED, AND A PERSON WHO HAS NOT BEEN ASKED HAS NOT REFUSED AND CANNOT BE COUNTED EITHER WAY.
