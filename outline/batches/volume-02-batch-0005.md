## The numbers, closed

- **The line book.** Nine hundred and six movements in it since the flood year, plus one. **A hundred and forty-one of the nine hundred and six named the ninth place as the origin held before the twenty-ninth of June and one hundred and forty-two movements name it now.** A column for the number of a vehicle has existed since the flood year and was written in for the first time on the thirtieth of June: **NINE.** A clerk of the ward market wrote the ninth place into the book as a place a thing stops at and not a signal on the twenty-second of June, which is the first line in nine hundred and six in which a place appears on its own. **The book is in three copies: a shelf in the hut at the ninth place beside a stove, the ward market counting room, and the board of eleven in the yard of Lot 17, and a stranger took that one cold on the sixth of July and the room let him.**
- **The boundary at the west end.** Two named people, the keeper of the only pair and the woman at the column, for a month, renewed on the twenty-ninth of June and **ending on the twenty-ninth of July**, entered in the public lot book, in the water office's own book and in the public lot book a second time, and chalked on the kerb rail of a platform. **There is no mark on the ground.** There is a hole a foot across and a foot and a half deep with a string pegged across it, and sixty yards of string west to a second peg, and **the hole is not a mark and is not the boundary and is a place where a person stands.** The ground is held by nobody. **The distance from the column to the drainage cut is two hundred and sixty yards, of which two hundred is footpath, and the cut is the end of the boundary because in the fourth year a man put a train over the cut and it went in.** Nobody extended it past the cut.
- **The three askings.** April: fifty-one minutes, eleven of them the fifth thing, asked by the tenth hand, who cannot be paid. May: **thirty-nine minutes, nine of them**, asked by a clerk of the ward market who said in a room that it would not be her again. - **The toll on the place.** **One hundred and forty-one out of one hundred and forty-one was what the ninth place could produce at eight o'clock on the evening of the twenty-ninth of June, and one hundred and forty out of one hundred and forty-two is what it can produce now.** The one it cannot produce is the one they made, and neither of the two women knows anything happened. **One departure in a hundred and forty-two is the first toll anybody in this basin has ever been able to state, and the fraction is smaller than any of the hundred and forty-one before it, and it is stated because the place it landed on was asked first.**
- **The run.** Iron into the bracket at about twenty minutes to midnight on the twenty-ninth of June; points thrown at about a quarter to; **forty minutes round the loop; the ninth vehicle clear at twenty minutes to twelve; the fourth place at ten past midnight; the east end at half past midnight; back to the ninth place.** Two movements, **the toll charged once**, the origin the ninth place, and the ninth place had been asked. ## The rules this batch produced, all of them short and all of them in the book

1. **A NUMBER IS NOT A BOUNDARY, IT IS A MEASUREMENT OF ONE.**
2. **A BOUNDARY IS TWO NAMED PEOPLE, A STATED TERM, A PUBLISHED ENTRY, AND A HOLE.** Nothing else.
3. **A HOLE IN THE GROUND IS A PLACE A PERSON STANDS AND IS NOT A MARK AND IS NOT THE BOUNDARY.**
4. **THE DAY IS THE PERSON.** A lamp that needs a day of the week is not a lamp; it is a thing a man has to be right about.
5. **A RAIL IS THE ONLY INSTRUMENT IN THIS DISTRICT'S HISTORY THAT KEEPS A PERSON ON A THING WITHOUT CARRYING ANYBODY'S NAME.** It is not a security, not a mark, not a job, and it is free.
6. **A PLATFORM IS A PLACE A THING STOPS AT, AND A PERSON IS NOT A THING, AND A PLATFORM IS NOT A MARK, AND A MARK MAY NOT CARRY A JOB.**
7. **A SECURITY WHICH IS SPENT ON THE THING IT SECURES IS NOT A SECURITY ANY MORE**, and a spent security is a thing somebody else has looked at.
8. **A TRANSFERABLE RIGHT IS NOT A NEUTRAL RIGHT.** The week moves; whoever holds it did not ask for it and the people it lands on were not asked.
9. **TWO PEOPLE WHO KNOW IN ADVANCE WILL AGREE WITH YOU.** Therefore the two witnesses are named to the man who acts at the moment and not before.
10. **A WITNESS IS A PERSON WHO WILL BE SOMEWHERE ELSE WHEN THE REASON IS READ OUT.**
11. **A STAGE IS NOT A MOMENT.** Written in a margin in a clerk's hand and underlined twice at a request.
12. **A PERSON WHO CAN BE REPLACED HAS NOT BEEN WORKED OUT AND NOBODY IN THIS DISTRICT KNOWS HOW TO DO IT**, and that is entered as not known and not as a delay.
13. **A SIGNAL REMEMBERS A SHAPE. THE ORDER IS THE SHAPE.**
14. **A DATE IS THE ONLY LINE ANYBODY HAS EVER PUT ON A TOLL BOARD THAT NOBODY IS ALLOWED TO ARGUE WITH.**
15. **A CORRECTION IS OWED BY A ROAD**, and this district does not own a press and has bought one run.
16. **A TOLL LANDS ON A PLACE AND THE AMOUNT OF IT IS A FRACTION, AND THE FRACTION IS SMALLER EACH TIME**, and the first toll anybody in this basin has been able to state is one departure in a hundred and forty-two.
17. **A REHEARSAL IS A WALK DONE BEFORE THE DAY IT IS FOR, ON THE SAME GROUND, BY SOME OR ALL OF THE PEOPLE WHO WILL DO IT — AND IT CANNOT FIND OUT WHETHER A GROUND WILL DO WHAT A PERSON SAYS IT WILL DO.** A yard cannot rehearse a loop. Four rehearsals have been held: the twenty-second of March in the rain, the twenty-first of May up a bank, the twenty-first of June in a yard on the tide, and the twenty-seventh of June on the ground, which is the first to finish in the dark.
18. **A SECURITY IS A THING SOMEBODY ELSE CAN GO AND LOOK AT**, and the inspectability clause is *inspectable by any person at any time without asking anybody's permission*.
19. **A LINE OF STONES IS NOT A MARK, BECAUSE A MARK IS CUT AND THAT IS LAID**, and the standing test case is not extended, and there is nobody who is to lay one.
20. **A REHEARSAL, A PLATFORM, A RAIL, A CORRECTION, A QUESTION AND A SECURITY ARE SIX INSTRUMENTS THIS DISTRICT HAS BUILT AND NOT NAMED**, and a name is the last thing an instrument gets and not the first.
