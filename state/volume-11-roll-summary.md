## 0. The four things a Volume 12 writer needs before anything else

**One is the volume's own finding and it is paid. Two are liabilities and one of them is a page and not an argument. The fourth is a decision nobody has made and that no writer may make.**

### 0.1 A public record that goes out of date on its own is a record of a person, and the district has now said so in public

**The third line of that lot book is a date, and the date is the day the water stopped being a foot, and the only person in this district who knew that day is the man who was standing in the ditch when it stopped. A person nine hundred yards off copied the shape of that book and left the third line out. On the sixteenth of the third month, at about ten, in the open, in daylight, in front of about nineteen people, in her own hand and at nobody's dictation, a clerk of nineteen years wrote nine words on a fourth line of that book: `The line above is a person, not named here.`** The count of things this district has made moved from twelve to thirteen on that morning and the thirteen is smaller than the twelve.

**THE FOUR COSTS ARE ALL ON THE PAGE AND NONE WAS SOFTENED.** This district lost the one public expiry it had. A copy made after that morning is copying an admission, which is easier to copy than a figure and costs its copier nothing. This district can no longer stand in front of a stranger and say that the other sheet is a copy. And the man the third line is about was not asked to agree and was not asked whether the nine words are true.

### 0.1a THE DAY-48 READING IS NOT THE FIRST TIME THAT SHEET WAS READ OUT LOUD, AND A VOLUME 12 MUST NOT INHERIT THE CLAIM THAT IT IS

**`outline/batches/volume-11-batch-0004.md` SECTION 1, `state/volume-11-batch-0004-summary.md` SECTION 2 AND THE VOLUME 11 HANDOFF ALL SAY THAT THE READING ON DAY 48 IS THE FIRST TIME THAT SHEET HAS BEEN READ OUT LOUD BY ANYBODY. THREE CHAPTERS SAY OTHERWISE AND ONE OF THEM SAYS IT IN A LEDGER.** On day 4 a woman who takes in washing read that sheet standing up twice. On day 15, at about eleven, in front of about nineteen people, a boy of about nineteen read the two third lines out loud, one after the other, standing between that first table and that second table, and said of the copy's third line: `And that one is nothing, and I have read it out loud, and it is still nothing, and it is going to be nothing this afternoon and it was nothing yesterday morning.` A clerk entered that an empty line was read out loud in that yard in front of about nineteen people and that reading it out loud did not make it a figure, and entered that she is not going to enter that it did not make it a figure either. The ledger of the same chapter says a boy read its empty third line out loud in front of about nineteen people that morning. On day 17 the man the third line is about read a copy of that date in a shop.

**WHAT IS STILL TRUE ON DAY 48 AND IS THE VOLUME'S BEAT: it is the first time the man who made that copy has read it, and he had put it there on the first morning and had not read one word of it in forty-eight mornings; it is the first time that sheet has been read out loud as a whole document, all three lines, with the man who made it holding it; and it is the first time anybody has said out loud, in front of about nineteen people, that the third line of it is not there.** Reading an empty line out loud and saying out loud that it is not there are two different sentences and the difference between them is the whole of what the fourth line bought this district. **The chapters are canon and were not edited and the three documents carrying the false claim are not a close's to edit. The full finding is at `state/volume-11-close.md` section 7.9.**

### 0.2 The copy-versus-book question was answered three times and the answer is that it is not answered

**This is the volume's answer and a later volume may not read it as settled.** A man who mends fencing said out loud on day 42 that the only way to tell a stranger which of two sheets is this district's own is for the person who made it to stand beside it and say something about himself, and a clerk entered that no person has done that. **The man who made the copy stood beside it on day 48 and said a thing out loud about the sheet and not about himself, and said so himself, and finished `it is still not the book`.** On day 50 the yard said out loud that about nine people can now tell you which of the two sheets has a line with nothing on it, and that not one of them can tell you which of the two this district made, and that this is the first thing that yard has ever said out loud about a page, and it is not a way of telling a stranger. **The four places in this district where those three lines can be read are on a `no person did that` list and not on a resolution list.**
