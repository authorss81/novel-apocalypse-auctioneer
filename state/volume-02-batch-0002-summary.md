# Volume 02 Batch 0002 Summary — The Borrowed Road (Chapters 61–70)

## Batch outcome

**The walk happened and it produced one thing the district cannot use.** Four people went out on the twenty-ninth of March from the fourth place to the ninth, on foot, on the six-foot, in daylight, and they came back with: **a line of chalk sixty yards past the water column, held by two named people for a calendar month, on a published entry, and printed in a document that says in its own words that it is not the fifth thing.** The fifth thing — a way of saying that a toll lands on a place — still has no name and no owner and is still the one that matters.

The batch also does the work Batch 0001 left paid and unpaid. **Stenn's fortnight is paid off** and the rate sheet for ground nobody has claimed goes up with a first line that is not about money, and its first finding is the reason nobody ever asked Sarrow Water anything, and it is not prejudice: **a place that cannot pay for its own instruments cannot be put on a sheet without making the sheet wrong**, and the cost of having been asked twice is already at two hundred and four coppers a quarter against a rate of two hundred. **The ward market answers the standing order of the ninth in public and loses by seven to three, and it knows exactly what it declined and why, in a figure, and the figure is eighty-eight coppers a quarter against a take of two hundred thousand and fifty, so the market is refusing something it can afford more than two thousand times over, and the reason is that a seat which may be filled and cannot be emptied is a condition and not a service.** **The first attempt at a multi-bearer split of a use already performed fails, in a room, for three correct reasons and a fourth that comes off the floor from the ground. It is not the first four-bearer instrument of the series - Volume 01 staged one in Chapters 35, 38 and 44 - and the difference is the whole of it: a charter somebody was proposing against a use this district has already performed nineteen times,** and Adrian Vale is asked twice to close a thing he cannot close and says no twice. **The market then says yes to a road, once the road has a date, and the rate on the line goes from nine to fourteen and nobody proposed fourteen.**

The batch's costs are: a boundary that cannot be seen from the end it is for; a man of fifty-six who holds a key for four days and cannot read figures; a nine-year lease surrendered in the fourth month before it runs out, which takes a Road House out of a line it has been on since he was thirty-four; a levered storm drain in the middle of a bank a man of seventy looks at every evening; a sheet of paper that costs nine hundred and six coppers and is carried twice on a chandler's trade; a fourth denial in nine days, and a fifth; and **one hour and eleven minutes, which is the first figure this district has ever put on the price of a refusal, and which is the price of the room and not of the thing.**
