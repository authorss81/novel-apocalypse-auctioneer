# Character State — After Chapter 0040

## The public lot board

Four seats and no owner: Mara Quill as registrar, the sitting steward, and two rotating places held by whoever in the yard has no claim on the lot. It can refuse to record a closing, publish a result, call a review, uphold a challenge in part, and set or renew the short public custody term. It cannot grant a use, cannot waive a toll, does not audit a security, and **cannot strike a line off a notice board — it can only record what happened.**

**Anything new in this batch had to be built out of powers the board already has, and it was.** The **terms-closing** is a *publication* that takes two fresh witnesses and calls a review, and the entry says the board confers no use and seats no token. **Recording the state of the room's knowledge beside an instrument** is now a standing practice the board adopted on its own motion because a book which records a thing that was known and not said cannot be used against anybody in it. **The board published an open question with no answer attached** — Teo's question about who tells four thousand households if the road never opens — and called a review, which is the only thing it could legally do with a question.

Seats in this batch: **Nessa Pell** as sitting steward (her seven-day term from the twenty-first lapsed on the twenty-eighth and was renewed in public on the twenty-ninth, on the fifth and on the twelfth, each time on her own request and on the record), **Sabin Tull** (barrow-hauler, bad hip, four refusals in nine days, and in this batch seconded the eighth line, the non-transferability clause and the held asset), and **Marda Osk** (tea at the tram arch, no claim on any lot, who in this batch is the person who moves a counterfeit's argument into a clause).

## Adrian Vale

- **Age/role:** 38; independent auctioneer at Lot 17. **Still a Stage 1 Reader, and still not a Binder.** He cannot split a use among bearers, transfer it, renew it, close a multi-bearer charter, or **post security.** He has said this in a yard five times since the twenty-ninth of last month and the twelfth's entry says it again in the same line as the figure.
- **Physical state:** every real reading costs a toll echo — exhaustion, a metallic taste, flattened sound, a right hand that stays open, and a bad hour he has learned to spend at a desk pricing salvage. **Five readings are entered in the month of the crossing and one in the new month**, and Nessa Pell counts them and writes down what each cost: a lost afternoon on the seventeenth; nine minutes and twenty seconds on the nineteenth; an hour on the twenty-first; an hour on the twenty-seventh on a feed pump; **an hour on the twenty-ninth on his own four-bearer draft**; and **twenty-two minutes on the twelfth off an open page**, entered as *the first entered in this month*, with the reader not to read again before the fifteenth. **Do not count four, five or six.** He has never been a traveler. The unexplained Chapter 6 fragment of Tarin's memory of purpose has not recurred and must not be used as a mechanic.
- **What this batch gave him, and it is not a power:** a document that was complete and checked and about nothing; **a held asset**; a man in a hospital room with an ankle; and the discovery that **the room can use him for something other than a reading.** Nessa Pell hands him an hour of work and times his silence and takes the credit for the instrument he wanted, and for the first time in seventeen years the city needs him to be answerable for a document rather than to be fast with a damaged object.
- **Current want:** a road with the whole of it written down before the door opens. He has written the whole of it down and there is no road.
- **Current need:** to stop applying a schedule to a thing that is not a schedule. **He entered Tarin Kest's name over a refusal given in the same room** because the box needed four names and there were three, and he says so in daylight, and the reason is a competence and not a lie. He is not having a breakdown; he is having the wrong competence and it is aimed at the work for the first time.
- **Flaw in action, named in public:** he wrote a clause at two in the morning that put a named party on a rail, and the rail gave a foot, and a man who cannot be named in a public book is hurt for the rest of his life. **He said it first, in the yard, before anybody else, and refused to have it entered as bad luck.** He also wrote a slot where a person had to be, and the slot is the reason a mark did two jobs.
- **Cost paid:** the inspection press is gone and the lease was nearly gone and is now in a steward's hands; a thousand five hundred and thirty-six coppers of his own inventory held against a breach; his yard's two long-standing lots and the ward market's cooperation from last month; **the trust of a man who said no to him in a room**; and the public knowledge that his best clause of the month put a person on a rail.
- **Authority:** one published failed charter; one unattached nine-line return term; one payment entered with its shortfall; **one suspended multi-bearer terms-closing**; one held asset; a review called on the fifteenth; and the standing sentence of Chapter 40: *a reader was told the rule and obeyed it and did the next thing instead, and the next thing is not a power and has no name in this book.*
- **Relationship state:** Mara cannot reach him by procedure and has stopped trying and started reading his documents out loud instead; Tarin is the bearer and has said no to him; Orren's warning is still the front of the book and Orren took his one sentence; Lina has told him in front of a room that he is the right man in the wrong one; Selik is a rival who has waited nine years for this and says so; **Nessa Pell is the person who has begun to use him correctly.**

## Mara Quill

- **Age/role:** 34; municipal registrar, author of the public lot book, the proposal schedule, the no-name remedy, and now the split-use remedy.
- **What this batch gave her, four things she had to be first to say in public:** that a permanent office is a permanent exception; that the registrar who has spent eight years building instruments that work by pointing **has been asked to write one that works without pointing at anybody**; that **a remedy can be offered knowing it will fail**, with the cost set before the agreement and the failure written on the front of the entry; and that **a book which records its failures loudly is a book that will be used against the people in it**, which Rell Bode said and she conceded in a yard.
- **The four sentences she does not put in the book.** In a kitchen on the fourth she says out loud that the people nearest the fault boundary are the ones nobody counted, that she has known it since the fourteenth, and that the reason entered in the remedy is going to be true and incomplete. **Bett Ruhl writes them on the wall under the three lines in charcoal**, and they are the first words in Lot 17's public record entered by anybody who is not a registrar. This is the standing fact about her: **she is the only person in the district who knows the reason the remedy will fail and has put it where she cannot edit it.**
- **Current want:** a settlement people consent to rather than a process people comply with. She has discovered, in a school gym, in a yard, and in a kitchen, that **it is mostly reading**, and she is not sure whether that is a discovery about the road or about herself.
- **Cost:** her own instrument is still in the spill; she entered her expectation of a failure in advance so that nobody could say they did not know; she read a whole book aloud for three hours and ten minutes and stopped four times; and **she keeps the limit a cook imposed on her and has now honoured it once in four weeks and is going to be asked about it.**
- **Relationship with Adrian:** no romantic resolution, no softening, and no scene in which either says a word about it. **They are further apart in law than they have ever been — she holds a book he cannot post into, and he has signed a thing the book has no name for — and closer in practice than they have ever been**, passing a slate across a crate at a quarter to nine at night while ninety people listen.

## Tarin Kest

- **Age/role:** 43; mechanic, route keeper, founder of the Needle Road House.
- **Current state: home, and the only bearer there is.** In this batch he **refused to be one of four** and said why: a road that does not stop when the man stops is a queue with a timetable, and a day will come when the rota has a man in it who has never been up out of the cut, and he will not know what to do and will not know that he does not know. - **And Adrian entered his name in the box anyway**, with the refusal in the same line and the reason entered. Tarin has not been asked about it since and has not raised it, which is worse.
- **Current want:** a road with the whole of it written down before the door opens, and a person in every clause who is a person.
- **Bryn:** one page, one refusal, and a reason. **Out of this batch entirely and must stay out of it.**

## Selik Marne

## Lina Vale
