# Character State — After Chapter 0030

## The public lot board

Four seats and no owner: Mara Quill as registrar, the sitting steward, and two rotating places held by whoever in the yard has no claim on the lot. It can refuse to record a closing, publish a result, call a review, uphold a challenge in part, and set or renew a short public custody term. It cannot grant a use, cannot waive a toll, does not audit a security, and **cannot strike a line off a notice board — it can only record what happened.** **Any chapter that gives the board authority must name all four seats aloud and put them in the room before it acts.** Seats used in Batch 0003: **Nessa Pell** (sitting steward, term renewed for seven days in public on the twenty-first on her own request), **Sabin Tull** (barrow-hauler, four refusals in nine days, seconded the motion that entered the private undertaking as offered and not verified, and named Adrian's repetition aloud off the pages) and **Marda Osk** (tea at the tram arch, seconded the nine-line return term, and one of the three named strip-writers of the protected passage). Prior seats: Coll Ferrin and Marda Osk in Chapters 11 and 16; Ilma Trent and Bevon Rahl in Chapter 19.

## Adrian Vale

- **Age/role:** 38; independent auctioneer at Lot 17; **Stage 1 Reader, and still not a Binder.** He **cannot post security at all**, and he has now said so in a public yard in the same entry as a payment, so that a later clerk cannot read his payment as a posted asset.
- **Physical state:** Every real reading costs a toll echo — exhaustion, a metallic taste, flattened sound, a right hand that stays open. **Four readings are entered in the public book this month and Nessa Pell enters them as four**: a lost afternoon in Chapter 17 (three consecutive readings in two days), **the brief reading of the brass on the morning of the nineteenth in Chapter 20** (nine minutes and twenty seconds of contact), **an hour in Chapter 24** when the token answered the fifth time, and **an hour in Chapter 27** on a damaged feed pump, which she enters as *a reading, the fourth entered this month, and the first since the twenty-one*. Do not count three. **He has never been a traveler.** The unexplained Chapter 6 fragment of Tarin's memory of purpose has not recurred and must not be used as a mechanic.
- **What this batch gave him, and it is not a power:** a published failure, a real payment that is not enough, a child with no name in a public book, and the discovery that the room stops asking him things when he stops pricing them. **He lost the ability to speak for the route without being silenced.** The market read his own terms and can now check his next one; the board is the only thing left in the yard that can act, and it can only record, refuse, and call a review.
- **Current want:** to be reachable by a piece of paper rather than by his own character. He has got it, and it is a public reading of his own handwriting in front of six hundred people, and he is not sure he would go back.
- **Current need:** to find the sentence that is not *the emergency is now and the paper comes after*. He looked for two days and did not find one, and the batch's answer — which he himself gave in a yard — is that the paper is the point and the road is a separate problem he has not solved.
- **Flaw in action, named in public:** he has reached for a good number to stop people asking three separate times, and on the twenty-ninth he entered a figure of his own setting that made his own page look insufficient, on the stated ground that a shortfall entered as nil would be a number he chose.
- **Cost paid:** a workshop emptied and a lease offered, six hundred and twenty-nine coppers of standing, the ward market's cooperation, two long-standing lots, the inspection yard, most of the credibility of his reading, a child he could not price, and the public knowledge that his best sentence in three weeks was a list of what he cannot do: he cannot read the route or say where it will come up, he cannot post security, and he cannot prove that the nine lines he has just written would hold.
- **Authority:** one published failed charter, one unattached nine-line return term, one payment entered with its shortfall, no mandate, no posted security, and a public undertaking he broke in his own handwriting and has not repaired. **He still cannot open a road, and the yard knows it.**
- **Relationship state:** Mara has stopped being able to reach him by procedure and has started reaching him with a document; Tarin is home and is the bearer Adrian cannot get; Orren's warning is the front of the public book; Lina has named the cost of his publishing and refused to let him have a triumph; Selik is a rival he has conceded to in public.

## Mara Quill

- **Age/role:** 34; municipal registrar, author of the public lot book, the proposal schedule, and the no-name remedy.
- **What this batch gave her:** two things she had to be the first to say in public. She said that **the suspension is the board's and not hers**, in a yard of six hundred, in order to stop herself from being a registrar with a thumb on a scale. She said that **the registrar cannot certify her own instrument**, and that the spill may have come from her own sealed-page register, and she said it before anybody else did. She entered in the minutes of a bridge review that she came to stop a closure and could not construct an argument against it, and called her own failure the review.
- **Current want:** to make the undertaking enforceable by something other than her memory of what he promised, and then to make the thing she built withstand being run by a person who is tired.
- **Current need:** to accept that an imperfect public process is more just than a private exception **and** that it has to be answerable to people who are not clerks. She has agreed to a limit imposed by a cook: the entry is read back in the room, before the table opens, with two people in it who are not clerks.
- **Cost:** she held the remedy over a dead child and wrote down that it was not discharged. She put a document she did not write and cannot audit into her own process. She is tired, it has cost her about four things this month, and three of them were visible. **Her process now works on a document she is least equipped for, and she knows it.**
- **Relationship with Adrian:** the rupture has completed and become working proximity. She reads his failure out in the middle of a day; he publishes. **No romantic resolution and no softening, and he has not begun to know what he has to give up.**

## Lina Vale

- **Age/role:** 27; Unlisted memory courier and organizer; Adrian's younger sister.
- **Current state:** **she has put her own name in the public book as the steward of a passage of nineteen people**, and she said in a kitchen that this is the entire price of the arrangement and that she is paying it because on the night of the **nineteenth** there was nobody in any book in this city who could be sent to look for the nine. - **Relationship with Adrian:** she names the cost of his publishing and refuses to let him have a triumph out of it, and she told him to go round the back of a kitchen because a man who comes through the front door again is a man who is comfortable.

## Selik Marne

- **Age/role:** Civic Warden and administrator of the official St. Orra service approach.
- **Current state: he has won, and he won on the merits.** The rebuilt gauge housing drew a mark on its pilot channel at **twenty-three minutes to eight on the morning of the nineteenth**, on a shut and empty span, on a trace with no instrument attached to it. He read the day's tapes at eight that evening, Vasey came at ten, and **at about eleven that night** he instructed in writing that the pilot be left attached and marked, and said he would carry supervised cargo and no passengers on it; the copy went out at midnight. He did not argue that point; **he had the certificate suspended under the water office's own clause, by the two people who signed it, before the review sat, and then said so in the first four minutes of the review.** The span is shut to all carriage including supervised cargo exceptions, pending a replacement housing, with a public inspection on stated days paid for by the ward market and read out by a clerk the review table appoints.
- **What he says:** a structure that can report a fault it cannot account for is not a structure he carries passengers on, **which is his own sentence from the March evacuation, applied to his own paperwork, before anybody else could apply it.** A road you cannot account for is not a road. A crossing without a manifest is not a crossing, and he will keep a marshal and a lamp at the footbridge and will put no name in a book about it.
- **What he did for the public road this batch:** refused a fifth cargo box twice on the record, sent the eleven-claim queue a copy of the night's log at his own cost, **cut the manifest to four boxes a day on the seventeenth, which is what produced the queue, and refused a fifth box twice on the record**, sent the eleven-claim queue a copy of the night's log at his own cost, **read all twenty-two letters at the review on the ninth, eleven from the queue and eleven from outside it, and named the five who said they could manage without the bridge and could not** (this is Chapter 29, not Chapter 22), stood a flag on the spur in Chapter 19, and put Adrian's published failure into the minutes as the reason his own instrument got examined.
- **His position now:** the closure order of the ninth states that **one office is responsible for the crossing at St. Orra**, and a **standing order for a permanently staffed crossing with no expiry is being drafted in a room Adrian is not in.** **He must be argued with in Chapters 34–42, not defeated, and his safety record stays real.**
- **What he has not done:** gloated. He shook hands with Adrian in the middle of a yard in front of everybody, which was deliberate, and said the thing about the minutes so that four people heard it and two of them wrote it down.

- **Current state:** the counterfeit is a printing plate in three brokers' hands and is at **twenty-one claims** and rising, and **it is winning on speed rather than on concealment.** The new trade is nine flat lines with no fold in it, sold openly to people who watched a public road fail, and the return point on it is a real name on a real pre-Fracture document that happens to be a flooded trench.
