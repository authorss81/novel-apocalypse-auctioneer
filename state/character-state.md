## The public lot board

Four seats and no owner. It can refuse to record a closing, publish a result, call a review, uphold a challenge in part, and set or renew the short public custody term. **It cannot grant a use, cannot waive a toll, does not audit a security, and cannot strike a line off a notice board — it can only record what happened.** It acts more than once in this batch and each time it works with the five it has.

**Seats at the end of Volume 01:** **Mara Quill** as registrar, not a vote and not independent; **Nessa Pell** as sitting steward; **Sabin Tull** and **Marda Osk** in the two unclaimed places. All four are named aloud every time the board acts, which is now written into the prose as a reflex rather than a formality.

**What the board did in this batch, and it is a list of everything it can do:** sat its own called review of the suspended terms-closing and **sustained seven items, refused one entry, and refused a sentence about a place**; **upheld a challenge in part** on the far-side condition and let Selik Marne make it bigger; **published a result** — that a damaged object in a public case is a lot with a condition and not evidence; **called a review** and sat it; **set and renewed a custody term twice, in public, on the record**; **took four sets of fresh witnesses** — Rutt and Mrs Halder, the tenth hand and Ferrin Usk, and two at the staging line; **performed a closing with a condition unmet and entered the fact, in four reasons, in its own words**; and **refused to let an answer to a question be entered as a finding, and refused to let it be entered as a confession either**, which is the finest thing the board has ever done and it took about ninety seconds.
