# Volume 03 Batch 0002 Summary — The Hearth Commons (Chapters 111–120)

## Batch outcome

**A district with a railway and five toll boards and a charter that ended on a date went thirty-four miles east and found out that a thing it could not price and could not name has a person standing in it, and that the person said no four times in four sentences, and the reason was not the price. The twenty-ninth of August came and went and nothing was decided on it, and the finding of that day is that a thing nobody is allowed to decide is not decided by a date. A guild inspector certified a consent that four people signed and one person did not attend, the certificate is valid, the registrar could not strike it, and the registrar said in a room that her own instrument had failed, and nobody argued with her.** This is the volume's second batch. It spends the volume's central pressure — the Salt Verge's looms — and it turns the volume's method against itself at the midpoint.

**The batch's engine is a distinction the district could not previously afford: a thing that has a price and a thing that has a person.** Adrian wrote the most complete set of terms this district has ever seen for a thing that cannot be a lot, and every one of the five parts of a lot in it is correct, and the sixth of the seven is a person, and the seventh cannot be written down at all. **An item's price cannot separate the person whose body makes it work.** That is the volume's midpoint reversal and it is the worker's own finding about herself, said in a field in about eleven minutes, and Adrian is not the one who says it and is not the one who found it.

**The twenty-ninth of August is the volume's reversal of a rule it made four weeks earlier.** Batch 0001's finding was that a thing nobody is allowed to decide is decided by a date, and it was printed on a four-copper sheet on the second of August and was in every way correct. On the twenty-ninth the date arrived, a mechanic of about forty-three stood on a platform for two hours with a ten-foot bar and nobody in the yard had standing to say no to him, and he put the bar down, and the finding is that **a date is not a decision; a date is the last day somebody can do something, and after that the thing is simply still there.** The rule has never been wrong, because the only two dates that have arrived in nine weeks have not had to decide anything. A rule that has never been tested is not a rule.

**And there is now no date in this district after the twenty-ninth of August except two, and both of them belong to other people.** A boy of seventeenth's index carries five dates on one line and no names against them, and on the twenty-fifth of August he ruled a line under the fifth of them in his own hand, and the finding is that a district that runs out of dates is in the same position as one that never had any, and it takes about nine weeks to tell the two apart.

## New canon, written into the canon card before Batch 0003 uses it

**A date is not a decision. A date is the last day somebody can do something, and on the day the date is gone the thing is exactly as it was.** A rule that has never been tested is not a rule, and this district's rule about things nobody is allowed to decide had never been tested until the twenty-ninth of August.

**A mechanic of forty-three put a bar to it and put it down, and the minute says he put it down and not that he was stopped.** The distinction between a person who is stopped and a person who has no standing to stop him is the distinction the whole batch is about.

**A question that has not been answered is not a refusal, and this district has eleven refusals and no questions, and that is the worse of the two.** The registrar entered the salt man's question on the eighteenth of August with a heading and nothing under the heading.

**A party of nine people going somewhere together is not an instrument and is not a job and is not a charter, and it is the first thing in nine weeks that went out of this district with no column anywhere for it.** Nine is nine agreements and not one. And **a document about people said nine and eight went, and nobody owes a correction, because a document does not owe anybody a correction.**

**A person asked twice is not a person asked once and not a person asked three times.** One answer changed and one did not, and the one that changed is in the book and the one that did not is in the minute.

**A lot has seven parts and not five, and six of the seven can be written down and the seventh is a person.** A lot with no bearer is not a lot, and a thing that is not a lot cannot be sold, and the man who found that out had written the terms four days before he found it out and said so in a room and asked for the entry to be under the terms and not above them, because people read the top of a page.

**An item's price cannot separate the person whose body makes it work, and the price was never the thing.** Four offers in four minutes and four refusals in the same four sentences. **A price is a number a person can hand over. The body is the thing.**

**Binding cannot create consent and it cannot create a person.** A transfer needs a person to transfer from and a person to transfer to; the frame is held by nine households and not by a person, the bed is a hole, and the year is in neither of them.
