# Volume 02 Summary: The Borrowed Road — the roll for Chapters 51–100

**Volume 02 is complete at Chapter 100.** This is the roll for the whole volume and it is the first document a Volume 03 writer should read. It is not a summary of chapters; it is a statement of what the volume built, what it cost, and what is still open. The per-chapter record is `state/chapter-summaries.md`; the five batch records are `state/volume-02-batch-0001-summary.md` through `state/volume-02-batch-0005-summary.md`; the running state is `state/current.md`; the live threads are `state/open-threads.md`.

## The sentence the volume turned on

**A destination can be a victim too.** Adrian said it in a yard in the middle of the volume and the volume spent its remaining forty chapters proving it and then costing it. The Needle Tramway's Harrow Tongue can send one vehicle back along the section it came from, out of a place with no other way out. It costs the origin. The origin settlement loses the memory of its own departures. **A reversal saves a train and leaves a stall, and the stall walks west, and the village at the ninth place had lost the memory of one hundred and forty-one movements out of nine hundred and six and not one person among thirty-one could account for a single one.** The whole volume is the discovery that a railway can be run in public and that the running of it is a thing that has to be asked of the people it lands on, and that the people it lands on had never been asked anything by anybody.

## The five batches and what each one did

- **Batch 0001 (Chapters 51–60), the walk of the twenty-ninth of March.** The custody key turned for the first time. A river-stair day was walked and the count of seventeen was right in number and wrong in identity. **The standing order with no expiry was read aloud in the room with a door on it** — four drafts over nine years, none written by anybody who read the last, competent, correct in intent and unanswerable because nobody had read it, and it cites a pre-Long-Fracture public office nobody in the basin can find in a register. The Harrow Tongue was introduced by a signal keeper in her own words, out of a hut, in weather, and not by a reading. The line was walked end to end and a ninth place that is not a stop was found in it. Adrian read the tongue and the reading was correct and **the object did not hold places, and the reading could not say where the toll lands.** A boy who had refused in public twice walked nine miles to a village that is not a stop and found that nobody there could account for a single departure.
- **Batch 0002 (61–70), a destination can be a victim.** Adrian admitted it in public and could not price it, and **three instruments built in five weeks failed on the same person for three different and correct reasons:** the proposal schedule is about a use that is not being proposed, the no-name remedy is for people who refused a name and the ninth place refused nothing, and the count rule makes a village into a household, which is precisely the thing the count rule was written to stop being true of people. The first attempt at a multi-bearer charter fails because a Stage 1 reader may draft a split and may not close one. The market learns to say yes to a road once the road has a date. **One hundred and forty-one, and the figure is the number in the prose and is not to be changed back.**
- **Batch 0003 (71–80), a train that carries the anchor.** The Tongue began travelling between Faults, from a hut eleven miles away to a hut four, and a keeper cannot hold a pair she has to take to another village. A bridge administration was forced to staff a hut at a place it says is unsafe, and the district found that **the safest and the most hated are the same office** and did not take the man out. The counterfeit got a schedule faster than the ward market could print one, and a man who sells nothing is right about four things and wrong about one. Tarin and Adrian's crew protected the engine at Harrow without knowing who owned the signal's toll, and the answer was in a lease in a book in a room in Alder Reach. **The second reading of the lot and what it cost: memory of which gate he went out of his own city by, on the seventeenth of February at six in the morning.**
- **Batch 0004 (81–90), three stations.** The rotating multi-bearer charter was built by five other people and **the reader said in four seconds that he cannot close it, before the room got to it, and asked for the fact that he said it first to be entered, and the entry says on its face that he is not one of the four bearers, not a keeper of a board and not on the committee, and that this is the first document in this district's history where that is true of him on the page.** Three toll boards went up with a keeper at each and the first thing written on the first one was a name nobody can find in a register. The second impression of the broken circle was found on a hut board by a boy copying a line book. **The standing order was answered in public in a market with the count each trade carried read out for the first time, and the office lost five to five and the timetable carried six to four**, and the district could not agree about an office and agreed exactly and inversely about a sheet. The twenty-ninth of May was asked by a clerk and entered by a bridge clerk and was better than April in three ways.
- **Batch 0005 (91–100), the borrowed road.** The west end was walked to a boundary and the rule that a mark may not carry a job blocked the only instrument the district had, and the answer was two named people, a stated term, a published entry **and a hole**. **A train reached three stations instead of one** in the rain on the night of the twenty-ninth of June. **Adrian posted a real security and closed the split and is a Binder**, with no panel, in a hut, and a clerk wrote *the stage is not a moment* in the margin and underlined it twice at his request. The Fault did not disappear; **it stopped walking, and where it stopped is written down with the date.** The volume closes on a signal's memory pattern that is a broken circle, on an entry whose origin is not a place, and on the name of a pre-Long-Fracture public office that is not a guild and that nobody in the Morrow Basin can find. **It was not found.**

## What the volume built, in the order it built it

- **A method, and it is on a wall.** The five ways of asking a person whether they will do a job, and a sixth thing that has no name and belongs to a clerk of nineteen years, and the finding that **the board cannot ask a person anything and the yard can, and that the difference between those two things is the whole of what has gone wrong in this district since the eighth of March.** It was used on the reader, on a signalman in a hut, on a board keeper, on a fourth bearer, on a paperer, and on two women in a doorway. It was used forty-one times on the boundary and the reasons are in the book in the people's own words.
- **A boundary that is two named people, a stated term, a published entry, and a hole.** Asked in March, in April, in May, in June, and due again in July. **The first asking cost a man a day he was not paid for; the second cost a woman a day and a night; the third cost a paperer nothing at all, and the finding is that a thing which turns at no cost is not a rotation.** A woman said no in March, was asked again in May, said no again, is not named, and is not asked for a fifth month; **not asked is in the count and a count is a column and not a protection.**
- **Four toll boards, four unpaid keepers, and the fifth of the five instruments this district does not have, which is still unnamed.** Nine feet by three, a four-foot column ruled in pencil at its two ends and empty between them, the reason printed inside it at the height of a man's eye, a keeper's line, a date, and one line anybody may write a sentence on. **The answer to the four-day problem is a person: a column with nothing in it is worth more than a column with a price in it for about four days, and the fourth day is the day the keeper starts.** The fourth board has no heading because there was no money for letters, and a man of sixty-one painted the place's name on it at the height of a hand so a person in the dark can find it with their fingers. **Do not confuse that list with the six instruments the volume built and did not name — a rehearsal, a platform, a rail, a correction, a question and a security. Six is the count of the things built; five is the count of the things missing; the fifth of the five is the toll instrument.**
- **A railway that is a coalition route**, with three stations, a platform that a village of thirty-one was asked about and said yes to in six separate figures, a printed timetable, a correction sheet, a line book in three copies, and a west end closed by a dated entry.
- **A four-bearer charter that closes.** The use is split among four people in four places, each with a place, an approach and a stated week, the toll named as the origin, the origin named as a place that has been asked, the term ending on a date, and two fresh witnesses who do not travel. **The fourth bearer's only standing is the ending, and he was found by a person doing a different job, and he ends the use by saying a reason out loud, and a person who cannot be replaced is not the same as a person who is permanent.**
- **A rule that a mark may not carry a job, which the boundary was built around and not in defiance of, and which was used four times in the last ten chapters and retired nowhere.** A line of stones is not a mark, because a mark is cut and that is laid, and that hole is recorded and not covered.
- **A security that is a thing somebody else can go and look at**, and five of them, none of them a sum, one of them spent on a platform, one of them a hostage because the district has no word for what it is, and the last one made of a day a week and three bound copies of a book.
- **A power change, and it is not a screen.** He can write a bounded charter, split a use, close a transfer, make a lot portable or renewable under a stated term, and post a security. **He cannot maintain a public lot, coordinate more than one bearer, arrange a hearing, or make the consequences of an object visible to a whole settlement, and this district has no public lot, four bearers and no hearing, and the entry says that neither of those two facts is a coincidence.** He does not become replaceable until Volume 05 and nobody in this district knows how to make a man replaceable, and that is entered as not known and not as a delay.
- **A count of things this district cannot do, and it can name them.** A way of saying that a toll lands on a place; a column that is a place; an office; **and, found in the last batch, a station with nobody at it.**

## What the volume cost, in the order it cost it

- **Memory of departure, three times, and each time a different departure and each time smaller.** In March, which way a river went, for about fifty minutes. In April, the memory of which gate he went out of his own city by, on the seventeenth of February at six in the morning, and it had not come back twenty-three days later. In June, the twenty-second of last month at about six in the morning, the walk he did not finish. **He has one left, and the cost is a countable number of the specific thing it takes, and a man who counts it can say no with a number in his mouth instead of a principle.**
- **Five refusals to read, and none of them written as cowardice or as discipline, and a clerk of nineteen years who said she would take the word out and has never taken a line out of anything in nineteen years.**
- **A hundred and eighty-three coppers that went on nothing, and a trader of fifty-one who paid out of her own till for weeks and asked twice that it be said nobody asked her either.**
- **The ground at the ninth place, the salvage, the stabling yard, the standing.** A mechanic of forty-three signed a lease in the ninth year in about eleven minutes and surrendered it four months before it ran out, posted four hundred and ten coppers of standing salvage so that a man could walk up a bank and look at it, watched it become a platform, and said in a yard that a thing a man gives up and a thing a man is given are not the same size. **He is not let off and he did not ask to be let off, and he has seven vehicles a week and no yard.**
- **A boundary that is two women saying yes every month to a nine-word sentence that stops about nine people a year, and neither of them likes it.**
- **A memory the ninth place lost without knowing: one departure in a hundred and forty-two, on the morning of the thirtieth of June, while about forty people watched and not one of them can say afterwards what the nine vehicles looked like going round.**

## The vocabulary, which is the volume's actual subject

**This district's problem was never a shortage of instruments. It was that nobody could say what a thing was for, and it solved that once, in a yard, in nine minutes, when somebody asked how a person is asked whether they will do a job.** Everything else in fifty chapters is that method applied to harder subjects, and the sentences it produced are all short and all in the book: a mark may not carry a job; a line of stones is not a mark because a mark is cut and that is laid; a security is a thing somebody else can go and look at; a name at the top is a heading and a name in the middle is an amount; an office that lets a term lapse has not ended anything, it has stopped saying when; a public office will tell you when and will not tell you who and a public board will tell you who and cannot tell you when; a second impression is not a second answer; a rate that is not asked for is a rate that has not been agreed to; a yes is not a hold and a hold is not a term; a no that is asked twice is not a refusal that expires but a column; a stage is not a moment; a date is the only line anybody has put on a toll board that nobody is allowed to argue with; a signal remembers a shape and the order is the shape; a transferable right is not a neutral right; and the fifth instrument, which is a way of saying that a toll lands on a place, is still unnamed and unowned and has four boards and five hundred printed sheets and nobody's name on it.

## The relationships, in one paragraph each

- **Adrian and Mara Quill:** further apart in law than they have ever been and closer in practice than they have ever been, and neither has said a word. A slate went past in a kitchen under the Ward Nine tram line on the second of May, the third of June and the fourth of July, and a reader at a counter wrote down that six people have now seen that kitchen and that she is not going to ask, because the answer would be in a room and not in a book. **A page in the public lot book carries two hands for the first time in nine weeks, and the registrar wrote the inspectability clause under his name and read it out at a counter.** They still do not say it. Volume 03 may begin to, and may not resolve it.
- **Adrian and Tarin Kest:** operational partners with a real unresolved argument that came due in cash. Kest signed the lease and surrendered it and gave up the salvage and the yard and the standing, and he is the man who parked a railway on a village out of ordinary competence and good intentions, and the volume did not let him off. **He and Adrian closed a charter together and neither of them thanked the other and the yard saw both of them lose something.**
- **Adrian and Selik Marne:** argued with for nine weeks, never defeated, never converted, not wrong. He staffed a hut at a place he says is unsafe and said so on every published day. His permanent office was answered and lost five to five and the figure of what the district declined is now public. **He asked in writing for his own man to be paid, was refused in about four minutes, was not thanked, and did not ask to be thanked, and that is the whole of why he is a wary ally and not a friend.** He is not on the boundary's walk.
- **Adrian and Orren Pike:** Orren is seventeen, is paid four coppers a quarter for an index, is still not Adrian's apprentice, found the second impression of the broken circle, found the fourth bearer, measured the stall four times with a tape, and was a witness because he does not travel. **He is right about what a thing is and wrong about what it means, and a registrar has refused to enter the wrong half four times and told him the sentence he wants is worse than the sentence he needs and that he may have it for nothing.**
- **Adrian and Lina Vale:** partly unrecorded, not thanked and will not be, with a booked autumn read-back. She says a courier network cannot be enrolled and the reason is worse than a delay.

## The mystery, and how far it got

**The question is what a public office that a signal can remember and a basin cannot find was for, and Volume 02 did not answer it and could not.**

The name is **THE STANDING OFFICE OF CROSS-DISTRICT PASSAGE, ESTABLISHED, AND NOT AT THIS PLACE.** It is a public office and it is not a guild. It is in four entries in a column of sixty-one that the line book has not got and in four entries in a keeper's column headed ORIGIN HELD, and **the four in one book are not the four in the other, so there is a fifth somewhere.** Five people spent about two hours failing to find it in a register in May; a fifth search on the fourth of July was for the function rather than the name and found that eleven trades cannot agree on what a public office does that none of the eleven trades does. Four people in four rooms said it was a place a person could refuse, a place a name could be charged to, a place a cost could be put in front of before it was paid, and a fifth man did not know, and **all four answers are about carrying something somebody would not carry themselves, and they were not joined.** The name is now written in five places and is in no register, and a sign-writer of sixty-one has the phrase twice in his own hand in thirty-one years of lettering jobs and no office and nobody who gave him the words.

**The broken circle is a system and not a signature, and it is in three places and not two.** The mark on the iron; the impression on a water office notice dated the second of the sixth year with no name above the fold line where a name would go; and **the signal's memory — sixty-one entries in a column the line book has never had, laid out on a boards floor in the order they were copied in, which make a broken circle.** A signal remembers a shape and the order is the shape. **The shape and the mark are not joined, the shape did not produce the name, and the registrar has refused to join them six times and has said out loud that she has given the same reason six times and that nobody has ever asked her why.** A second impression is not a second answer, and what the sixth impression changed is one thing: this district now knows where the first one is not.

**Untouched and to be left untouched:** the blank lot, Iven, the origin of the Common Measure, the First House, the removed signature, the three unexplained things of Volume 01, the romance, and the protected category's count of five.
