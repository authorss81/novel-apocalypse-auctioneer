# Batch 0001 Summary — The First Hammer

## Batch outcome

Chapters 1–10 move the St. Orra problem from an unknown brass object to one witnessed public crossing. Adrian Vale begins as a practical broker, performs a bare activation without a valid closing, becomes the Tally's default bearer, and pays with exhaustion, sensory disturbance, memory gaps, lost inventory, and public trust. Mara Quill forces a public process. The Last Fare carries the medicine convoy and a small school shelter group across St. Orra once, then returns Tarin Kest without his reason for carrying the medicine and Dessa Rill without her reason for traveling. The route works, but it is expensive, damaged, and not yet a dependable Hearth Line.

## Beats completed

- Lot 17's ordinary sale is interrupted by the Last Fare, a distressed fever shipment, and Halloway Reed's first appearance.
- Adrian's contact produces an unstable three-part reading: one twelve-minute route, a memory-of-purpose toll, and standing requirements for a driver, return point, and remembering passenger.
- Mara audits Lot 17, seals the token and manifest, distinguishes a charter from a sale, and refuses to let municipal inspection substitute for fresh voluntary witnesses.
- Selik Marne's controlled bridge is legitimate and effective but requires named manifests. Adrian accepts a supervised inspection and identifies the Ward Nine bell-post return point.
- Adrian breaks the seal and activates without a valid closing. Two medicine boxes cross; Tarin and the return group are diverted to a false maintenance destination. Backwash alters a clinic slip and public notice board. Adrian settles part of the claim with the repaired blue-collar injector and promises Tarin full disclosure next time.
- Mara opens a public hearing. Lina makes the Unlisted boundary concrete: the shelter group can be described without publishing a household. Orren traces Halloway's counterfeit, recovers the original manifest, and keeps Sela Pike's name out of the public book while bringing the route evidence to Lot 17.
- Adrian writes the first simple public charter: one route, one activation, Tarin as sole bearer, Dessa as the separately named return traveler, Alma Dren and Ewan Latch as fresh independent witnesses, a Ward Nine return, a public remedy, and no renewal. Neither witness travels, because a witness who pays the toll can no longer describe what she accepted, so the far-bank duty goes to two escort stewards named on sealed sheets held by Mara and by the school, and to Renn Fisk for the boxes. One of the nine children refuses and makes Alma state that her name does not speak for him, and a relief driver off the St. Orra staging line gives back a place held open for him. The medicine and shelter group cross; eight of the nine children travel, and sixteen people pay the toll. Tarin returns without his purpose, Dessa without hers, and the token is visibly damaged and returned to public custody. Adrian keeps his hand on the fare box and pays only the echo.

## New canon and state

- Adrian has earned Stage 1 Reader: he can read use, immediate toll, and standing on one contacted lot, but pays a toll echo, cannot predict, cannot read continuously, and can become a default bearer after a bad reading. The echo is the price of reading. The memory toll belongs to people who knowingly travel. He is not a Binder and has posted no security.
- Where a toll takes the memory of purpose, a witness does not travel on the lot they witness. The remedy rests on appeal, and the toll takes the memory the appeal asks about, so the required pair stays where it keeps its memory. A witness who is also a beneficiary's responsible party hands that duty to a named substitute. Recorded in `bible/power-system.md`, `bible/terminology.md`, and `bible/world.md`.
- A responsible party who must be identified but may not be published may be named on a sealed page held by the registrar. It opens only if the remedy is called. This rule was used in Chapter 9 and written into the lot book in Chapter 10.
- The public lot board is a constituted four-seat panel: the registrar, the sitting steward, and two rotating seats held by whoever in the yard has no claim on the lot. It can refuse to record a closing and call a review. It cannot grant a use.
- Geography is fixed: Lot 17 and the St. Orra staging yard are on the eastern bank; the Ward Nine fever ward and the receiving stations are on the older central bank. The medicine starts stranded on the wrong bank.
- The Last Fare's memory toll applies on the outward crossing and again to a returning traveler. Sixteen people traveled on the first public crossing and all of them paid: eight children, two escort stewards, and four of the five medicine crew crossed and stayed; Tarin Kest and Dessa Rill returned. One of the nine children named as beneficiaries refused and did not board. Tarin is the sole bearer; Dessa is a condition on the use, not a second bearer.
- **Renn Fisk is the clinic porter of Chapters 1–6.** He sets the six-hour coolant clock, boards the failed activation, is recovered from the flooded service wall, is named in Mara's Chapter 6 affected list, and crosses again in Chapter 9 as one of the four who stay. One person, crossing twice, never a second porter, and his second crossing is a choice made knowing what the first one cost.
- A previously closed inactive custody charter authorizes physical movement of an unactivated anchor to a receiving bonded depot for a stated custody term, with two witnesses and an unclaimed-delivery remedy. It grants custody only, never activation, ownership, or a bearer role. The surviving strip names Orren Pike and Lot 17's bonded salvage table.
- The first public crossing is complete but not repeatable without new consent. Lot 17 now has a public lot book, a documented custody arrangement, a public challenge path, and a promise from Adrian to state the full known toll before another use.
- The broken-circle closing mark appears on the Last Fare, the counterfeit, the recovered manifest, and an unread regional record. It is a mystery clue only; no place, person, or Common Measure answer is revealed.
- Iven Tallow and the blank lot are now named in Chapters 1 and 2 rather than only implied. Adrian's platform memory — a clean hand in his writing that got eleven people off and left four — is the stated root of his pricing compulsion. Iven remains presumed dead; the blank lot's function is still unknown.
- Selik keeps temporary authority over the official bridge and accepts a supervised review of the manifest. Halloway's counterfeit remains attractive to desperate people, and his complaint about false return points and concealed labor remains active.

## Relationship movement

- Adrian and Mara move from audit and suspicion to conditional professional collaboration. Trust is not forgiveness or romance; Adrian admits he wants her process because he fears his own improvisation.
- Tarin accepts a visible-toll charter but insists on a named return point and the right to stop the route. He remembers the road, not why he drove it, after the failed activation.
- Orren begins an uneasy apprenticeship: he brings evidence to the public process, refuses Halloway's private offer, and refuses to publish a household or treat Sela's name as restoration.
- Lina protects the Unlisted boundary without abandoning the shelter's need for a route. She and Orren establish paper copies so a public failure cannot erase every account.
- Selik remains a credible safety administrator. Halloway remains a competent, generous-to-his-own-crew antagonist whose counterfeit problem is a service failure as well as a lie.

## Unresolved handoff

The next medicine shipment is due at dawn. The public board must decide whether a second crossing is possible without repeating the false-destination failure. Selik's bridge review, Halloway's counterfeit network, the Unlisted witness channel, the strained token, and the broken-circle mark remain active. The Tally's claim on other unnamed people remains a recorded unknown, not an invented roster. Adrian still has no public authority beyond one witnessed charter and no posted security. Iven remains presumed dead; the blank lot remains unexplained.

## Plan deviations

The approved cards in `outline/batches/volume-01-batch-0001.md` were edited during the batch to match the prose. The prose is canon, and the card text has been aligned to it so the next writer is not handed a plan the manuscript already contradicts. Every change is listed here, because a back-edited plan is a plan whose history has to live somewhere else.

- **Chapter 9, second witness.** The card originally read "a Road House supply driver." The prose uses Ewan Latch, a contracted ward-market freight driver. Reason: a Road House driver could be read as connected to Tarin's own operation and therefore not independent. The change fixed a real independence problem.
- **Chapter 9, Orren's exclusion.** The card originally excluded Orren because "he agreed not to list the Unlisted shelter." The prose excludes him because he is the source who found the hidden addendum and has not accepted the role. Reason: the source of the evidence is not independent of it.
- **Chapter 9, term definition.** The card originally read "ending when the last traveler reaches the platform or at twelve minutes." The prose ends the term when the second of two named return travelers, Tarin and Dessa, reaches the platform. Reason: "the last traveler" is ambiguous once a group is on the far bank, and the two named returners are the ones the remedy depends on. The card has since been aligned to the prose.
- **Chapter 9, witness travel.** The card specified "a school shelter steward who is not a passenger." The prose had Alma Dren board the bus, which broke the appeal path. Corrected in the review pass: Alma does not travel, and the far-bank duty goes to two escort stewards named on a sealed page. The card text has since been extended to state the substitute, because the rule in `bible/power-system.md` requires one.
- **Chapter 9 and 10, the public board.** The cards referred to a closing board and a board appointment without ever seating the body. A four-seat composition was added in Chapters 9 and 10 so that a board with appointing power exists in front of the reader.
- **Chapter 5 and 6, the map.** The red circle drawn in the Chapter 2 back office was on the old Line Six map, but Chapter 5 puts Tarin's patched map on the inspection block and Chapter 6 has Adrian forget drawing the ring. Corrected in the review pass: the ring is drawn on the patched map in Chapter 5, and Chapter 6 refers to that map.
- **Chapter 1 and 3, the clinic's bank.** The cards and early prose left the receiving ward's bank ambiguous, so a reader could conclude the medicine had already arrived. Corrected in the review pass: the Ward Nine fever ward is named as being across the water from Lot 17.
- **Chapter 9, Adrian's cost.** The prose gave Adrian the memory toll while he stood at the fare box, which contradicted the toll's scope and undercut his later undertaking. Corrected in the review pass: he pays the toll echo only.
- **Chapter 9 and 10, toll accounting.** The observed result recorded only Dessa's loss. Corrected in the first review pass: the crossing is recorded as sixteen travelers, the two refusals, and the second payment on the return leg. A first pass at that fix asserted seventeen travelers, which contradicted the recorded refusal of one of the nine children. The count still did not follow what actually boarded, because the charter placed a fifth medicine-crew member on the far bank alongside the four it named; that was corrected in the second review pass. Note the *observed result* entry is in **Chapter 10**, not Chapter 9 — Chapter 9's spoken count records the sixteen and the two refusals but not the second payment.
- **Chapter 9, rule explanation replaced with scene.** The two paragraphs that restated Alma's and Ewan's roles in the register of a memo were cut and replaced with the sealed-page negotiation, the boy's question, and Ewan's separate and different promise. This is the only substantive body rewrite the first review pass made to Chapter 9.
- **Chapter 9 and 10, the return platform's bank, and Chapter 5 and 6, the porter's name.** The third review pass moved two facts out of the state files and into the text a reader actually reads. "Ward Nine" names a far-bank district and a near-bank platform, so the Chapter 9 spoken use, term, and remedy and the Chapter 10 USE, TERM, and BENEFICIARIES entries now name the bank in the charter and the book instead of relying on a continuity note; the Chapter 9 card's continuity fact was realigned to match. Separately, the unnamed clinic porter of Chapters 1–6 is now named **Renn Fisk** in Mara's Chapter 6 affected list and identified in the Chapter 9 scene as the man who set the coolant clock and already crossed once, so the batch's clock-setting porter and its far-bank porter are one person. The Chapter 9 card's "a sealed page" was also aligned to the two sealed sheets the prose created. The remaining third-pass repairs — the lost antecedent in Chapter 5, the escorts' and crew's identical coats in Chapter 9, and four contentless nods in Chapters 8–10 — were prose defects with no card behind them.
