## 1. The position the block opened on

**The thirtieth of the sixth month of the eighteenth year after the Long Fracture is the last day of Batch 0003's prose, and the first of the seventh month is one day after it.** The block opened on a district holding two pieces of paper in a book a stranger may walk up to and read, one of them a hole with a reason printed under it and one of them wrong on its face; on a man who said in a lane that a wrong sheet is a worse thing than no sheet and that he was not going to be the man who took it back out of that book; on a bell that had been rung once on the night of the twenty-fourth for about nine minutes and was not going to be rung again; on a child of about eight in a room with nothing over the door who had heard it and had not been asked; on a table with four legs against a wall outside a building with two doors, a piece of chalk on it, and a curtain on a nail in an end wall; and on a figure of four hundred and eleven arrived at by counting the people who answered a door, on a sheet nailed to a gatepost, for the first day of the eighth month.
