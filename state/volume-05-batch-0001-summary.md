## 1. What the block is

Ten chapters in eleven days, the first of April to the eleventh of the fourth month of the eighteenth year after the Long Fracture, in a yard and a lane and a hill and an office four hundred yards apart. A lease clerk of about forty-four walks up the lower terrace road on the second of April with a flat case the size of a hymn book, and walks past a yard with nine people in it to go to a door with a fanlight over it, and what he came for is a page and not a bearer, and nobody in this district asked him what he came for until the seventh day. A Registry renews a lease on a road whose toll is paid at a place the sheet will not name; the holder is a name a body has printed. A schedule of nine rows has a column headed OPEN, eight days in it and a word in the ninth, and the word is IT IS NOT A ROAD. A man of about thirty-one who mends a dray goes two days of road to a counter and comes home with a strip of paper saying his own name came off a sheet: he has been found and not released. He offers a dray. A dray cannot go up two hundred yards of soft ground, and the only yoke in the district is a carrying yoke on a stone on a hill, and a man of about thirty-six who keeps a scale says out loud that it is not the thing that would pull a dray. **It is taken off the stones anyway, at about eleven in the morning, without asking the woman who owns it. She is brought up the bank at half past one with two empty buckets she could not fill, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing carried them up the hill and was not asked to. The man of about thirty-four who digs loam says the plainest sentence in the block: a piece of wood on a cart that came out of a yard is a job, and the job is hers because it came off her stones, and she is not in the cart and she is not in the yard.** On the tenth the yoke goes on a tailboard in the open where anybody in four hundred yards can walk round it, and she stands at the bottom of the lane for a minute and a quarter and says nothing, and the man who took it says out loud in front of nineteen people that he has not got a way to pay her for the two days. On the eleventh, at about half past five in the morning, a dray goes up the road with two people in it and the yoke lashed to the tailboard with about nine feet of line.

**The block's finding, stated once: this district counted one door and not the other. A book a stranger can walk up to is a door, and the district built it on purpose and told nineteen people it was the best instrument in the yard. A yard that about nineteen people can stand in and decide something about a woman who is not in it is also a door, and the district has been standing in it for eighteen months and never once wrote that down. The door that got used this month was not the one anybody was proud of.** The volume's method held and did not once produce a remedy. It produced two refusals in four seconds, one refusal in fifty-six words that was given a second time in fifty-eight, a no that a man of fifty-six said in seventy-two words is a thing that happened and is not a thing that holds, and a piece of wood on a cart.

## 4. The figures that carry the volume

- **The eight days.** Read out of a hand copy in Chapter 209 and entered in a book in Chapter 210, in this order: **the first of the fourth month, the second, the third, the fourth, the sixth, the eighth, the tenth, the eleventh.** Eight days inside eleven days, one night a night. **The sheet does not say whether a day under the heading OPEN is a day the thing was open or a day it is to be open, and this district does not settle it.** Four of the eight had already happened when the sheet was read out on the fifth of April and four had not, and nobody in that yard asked for the eight days on the fifth of April although a clerk of nineteen years was standing in it. **The last of the eight is the eleventh of April, which is the last day of this block.**
- **The ninth row carries a word and not a day, and the word is IT IS NOT A ROAD.** A word in a column headed OPEN is a finding and not a blank. **The ninth place is where the toll on the lease is paid, and the ninth holding on the delivery sheet at the second reservoir is the one nobody can account for, and the two are a rhyme that was refused as a join on the page in Chapter 205 and again in Chapter 209.** They are not joined.
- **The two words for the same things.** The Registry's word is gates and the keepers' word is locks, and this yard used a borrowed word for five days until a man of about thirty at a counter gave the district the house's word on the ninth of April and said that his house has a word for the man who keeps one and a word for the night he turns it. **Nobody in this district has seen a keeper. Four generations of them have never been on a page of anybody's, and that is the first stranger's figure anybody in this yard has entered, and no date and no year and no place came with it.**
- **The stranger's days on the ground: five days on a bench outside a door with a fanlight over it, from the second to the sixth of April, and nine days in the district in all.** He arrived on the second and left at about half past five on the eleventh. He was never asked to leave and never asked to stay. He refused the place three times and never once refused outright. **He never answered the third of the six things, which is the year.** He said his body enters a no and dates it, and that the asking is the answer. **He went up a road on a dray with a yoke he did not ask for and a man who mends drays, and nothing in this block says where the road goes or when the dray comes back.**
- **The lease term ended on the last day of the second month and the renewal is dated the first of the fourth month, and nobody has ever seen the day it was due on.** A term, a renewal, a date that is late and a date that is due are four different things and there is no column in this district for any of the four.
- **Two hundred yards of soft ground at the top of the only road in this district that climbs.** The man of about thirty-one has tried it once and failed. The road goes out past the loams and up past the top field of a farm four miles out, and the top field is the fourth holding on the delivery sheet, and the fourth holding has not taken a drop since the first of the second month. **A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing and a man of about thirty-four who digs loam say the same thing from two directions and the yard does not join any of it to a road a person opened in the night nine miles away.**

## 6. The relationship movement, and what is not resolved

**Adrian and the registrar of this district's records office move four times in this block and none of the four is a declaration.** On the second of April she refuses him a page to a stranger in thirty-seven words and a clerk entered that it was the right refusal and it is still the right one. **Adrian and the man of about thirty-four who digs loam** is the relationship the block leans on. **Adrian and the man of about thirty-one.** Offered a dray on the seventh, took the yoke off the stones on the ninth without asking, said it out loud in fifty-four words and asked for it to be written in his own words, put the yoke on a tailboard in the open on the tenth so that its owner would see it on a cart, said in fifty words that he has not got a way to pay her for the two days, and drove the dray up the only road that climbs at about half past five on the eleventh. **He is the fifth of the five posted securities and the fifth of the five things this district does not have, and he is a man and not a bearer, and he asked on the fifth of April what a person at the back of a book is for and answered in forty-four words: I do not know.**

## 9. Corrections carried by this run

**Twenty-three repairs in the prose of the block. No chapter was rewritten, no scene was removed, no plot moved, no beat was dropped, no chapter's date moved, and the ending of the block stands. All six numerals are in figures a reader can check, which is the standard this block holds itself to.**
