## 1. What the block is

Ten chapters in eleven days, the first of April to the eleventh of the fourth month of the eighteenth year after the Long Fracture, in a yard and a lane and a hill and an office four hundred yards apart. A lease clerk of about forty-four walks up the lower terrace road on the second of April with a flat case the size of a hymn book, and walks past a yard with nine people in it to go to a door with a fanlight over it, and what he came for is a page and not a bearer, and nobody in this district asked him what he came for until the seventh day. A Registry renews a lease on a road whose toll is paid at a place the sheet will not name; the holder is a name a body has printed. A schedule of nine rows has a column headed OPEN, eight days in it and a word in the ninth, and the word is IT IS NOT A ROAD. A man of about thirty-one who mends a dray goes two days of road to a counter and comes home with a strip of paper saying his own name came off a sheet: he has been found and not released. He offers a dray. A dray cannot go up two hundred yards of soft ground, and the only yoke in the district is a carrying yoke on a stone on a hill, and a woman of about thirty-six who keeps a scale says out loud that it is not the thing that would pull a dray. **It is taken off the stones anyway, at about eleven in the morning, without asking the woman who owns it. She is brought up the bank at half past one with two empty buckets she could not fill, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing carried them up the hill and was not asked to. The man of about thirty-four who digs loam says the plainest sentence in the block: a piece of wood on a cart that came out of a yard is a job, and the job is hers because it came off her stones, and she is not in the cart and she is not in the yard.** On the tenth the yoke goes on a tailboard in the open where anybody in four hundred yards can walk round it, and she stands at the bottom of the lane for a minute and a quarter and says nothing, and the man who took it says out loud in front of nineteen people that he has not got a way to pay her for the two days. On the eleventh, at about half past five in the morning, a dray goes up the road with two people in it and the yoke lashed to the tailboard with about nine feet of line.

**The block's finding, stated once: this district counted one door and not the other. A book a stranger can walk up to is a door, and the district built it on purpose and told nineteen people it was the best instrument in the yard. A yard that about nineteen people can stand in and decide something about a woman who is not in it is also a door, and the district has been standing in it for eighteen months and never once wrote that down. The door that got used this month was not the one anybody was proud of.** The volume's method held and did not once produce a remedy. It produced two refusals in four seconds, one refusal in fifty-six words that was given a second time in fifty-eight, a no that a man of fifty-six said in seventy-two words is a thing that happened and is not a thing that holds, and a piece of wood on a cart.
