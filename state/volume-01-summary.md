# Volume 01 Rolling Summary — The First Hammer

## Current position

Chapters 1–10 are complete. Alder Reach has one successful but costly Last Fare crossing and no dependable Hearth Line yet. The immediate medicine shipment is delayed, the official bridge remains under Selik Marne's authority, and Halloway Reed's counterfeit claim is still moving people through private bargains.

## Established pressure

- The Last Fare opens one route across St. Orra for twelve minutes.
- Each traveler loses the memory of why they are traveling; a returning traveler pays again. Sixteen people paid on the first public crossing, and one of the nine children named as beneficiaries refused to board.
- The route needs a driver who knows Line Six, a marked return at the Ward Nine bell-post platform, and a passenger who can remember the destination.
- Where a toll takes the memory of purpose, a witness does not travel on the lot they witness. The remedy rests on appeal, so the required pair stays where it keeps its memory, and a witness who is also a responsible party hands that duty to a named substitute.
- The token is strained after one bare activation and one witnessed activation. Repeated use may strand the route permanently.
- Backwash is real: it can alter nearby records and rules, and it cannot be erased by destroying or hiding the object.
- Geography: Lot 17 and the St. Orra staging yard are on the eastern bank; the Ward Nine fever ward and the receiving stations are on the older central bank. Medicine starts stranded on the wrong bank.

## Adrian's position

Adrian has moved from Stage 0 practical broker to Stage 1 Reader through failure, an affected-person promise, and a physical toll echo. He can state one contacted lot's use, immediate toll, and standing. He cannot predict a result, read continuously, or operate a Fault object without disclosure, fresh independent witnesses, a term, and a remedy. He is not a Binder, has not posted security, and is answerable for the failed claim. The echo is the price of reading; the memory toll belongs to people who knowingly travel. Adrian has lost no memories as a traveler, and the Chapter 6 gaps came from the bare-claimant toll he paid as default bearer without ever boarding. He has signed a public undertaking to disclose the full return cost before any next use, and Chapters 18–19 are where he breaks it.

## Public institutions

Lot 17 has a public lot book and a documented public custody arrangement for the Last Fare. The public lot board is a constituted four-seat panel — registrar, sitting steward, and two rotating seats with no claim on the lot — that can refuse to record a closing and call a review but cannot grant a use. Mara, Adrian, and a board-appointed steward must be present to open the case; Nessa Pell holds the first seven-day steward key, granted in an audible vote. Selik's bridge is a lawful alternative awaiting supervised review of its manifest requirement. The Unlisted have a provisional protected channel: beneficiary households may refuse public names, and a responsible party who must be identified may be named on a sealed page the registrar holds, while the steward, count, destination, bearer duty, and remedy remain accountable. Orren and Lina keep paper copies outside the municipal record.

## Active threats and promises

- The next medicine shipment is due at dawn; a second crossing would need new terms and a new closing.
- Eight children, two escort stewards, and four of the medicine crew are on the far bank, unable to say why they traveled. The stewards named on the sealed page accepted duty for nine and answer for the eight that went.
- Halloway's counterfeit has a false return point and a concealed seven-day repair-labor term. It cannot create a valid charter, but it can send carriers into bare-claimant danger.
- Selik may use the failed public route to justify a permanent bridge closure or a stricter manifest, and Chapter 12's false-alarm evacuation is the event that makes that tightening credible.
- The broken-circle mark connects the Last Fare to records from an unidentified region outside current basin maps.
- Adrian's blank lot remains a familiar mystery object; Iven remains presumed dead and his living custodianship is unrevealed.

## Next planned movement

Chapters 11–16 should harden the first public road: establish the Hearth Line as a proposed public service rather than a private bargain, put the counterfeit dispute into public work, test the Unlisted witness channel, bring the far-bank party home, and let a false-alarm evacuation force the market to choose between waiting for Selik's safer route and accepting another bounded public attempt. Chapters 17–20 should begin the larger medicine crisis and the first full auction, written as a breach of Adrian's signed undertaking, with the concealed return toll and private security reserved for the Volume 01 midpoint reversal at Chapters 23–26.
