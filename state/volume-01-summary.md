# Volume 01 Rolling Summary — The First Hammer

## Current position

Chapters 1–40 are complete. Alder Reach has crossed St. Orra three times with the Last Fare, failed on the fourth, found the nine, buried a child with no name in a public book, published the whole of it, and then spent four weeks arguing about what a paper is for.

**There is still no road.** The official span is shut to all carriage with its instrument certificate suspended by the water office over Selik's own written instruction to leave the pilot attached. The public road is suspended. The middle plank of the only remaining footway is at the bottom of the service cut. The token is in a locked case on a seven-day custody term renewed four times in public, cracked through the plate, and nobody has said since the nineteenth that it will open again.

**What is new is that the district now has the terms, and has never had the road.** Eight terms written by eight different frightened people have been collected into one place and read aloud to the people who will pay them. A multi-bearer terms-closing was built by a board out of powers the board already had, published, and **suspended in its first hour** on the ground that it could not be performed and had never been checked, and its review is set for the fifteenth. A man of a protected household in the service-cut yards was hurt on a rail by a clause Adrian wrote at two in the morning, and **he is not in any public book by name**, and the remedy for him is a hearing that failed in public and is recorded as not discharged. A registrar who knows exactly why her own remedy will fail has written the reason in charcoal on a kitchen wall where she cannot edit it.

## Established pressure

- The Last Fare opens one route across St. Orra for twelve minutes and takes the memory of why each traveler is traveling, **per traveler per leg**. It is **cracked through the plate after four activations** and **it is not known whether it opens again.** It returns to the public case at the closing whether or not the vehicle does. It has now answered **six** unasked questions, the last of which is that it was never asked anything at all.
- **Tolls are counted by leg, and travelers and tolls separately.** Ch 9 outward: 16 travelers, 18 tolls. Ch 16 return: 16 distinct people, 18 tolls. Ch 19 outward: **29 offered, 28 travelled, 28 tolls, no return leg at all.** **19 stranded + 9 unaccounted for = 28. All 9 were found on the nineteenth at nine minutes past seven; 8 lived and 1 child died in the small hours of the twentieth. All 19 were walked home over the footbridge on the eleventh and twelfth. Nobody came home before that, and nobody came home in Batch 0004.**
- Geography: Lot 17, the St. Orra staging yard, the marked spur, the old Ward Nine platform and its bell post, the service-cut yards, and the far abutment are on the eastern bank; the Ward Nine district, its fever ward, the receiving stations, the far receiving ramp and the far school house are on the older central bank. **Every charter and book entry carries the bank in the text.**
- The four places stay distinct: the **loading spur / marked spur** is the near-side track behind the old school shelter; the **maintenance spur** is the near-side place the bus was found; the pre-Fracture **maintenance bay** is beyond the river wall on Tarin's working card; the **service approach** is Selik's span, shut.
- **The cause of the failed return is the movement of the marking.** A hand was in the bell-post socket, inside a day or two of the crossing. **Who, how, and why are not known.** One nailed-heel print going toward the river wall belongs to nobody. The nine-line return term now in the book answers it procedurally: *if the marking is not where the pair says it is, the joint is the return and the arm is not.*
- **Three unexplained things, none joined.** A bus came out of a Fault with nobody driving it. A certified gauge housing drew a mark at twenty-three minutes to eight on the nineteenth on a shut empty span. A foot of the crown of a service cut went down at four minutes past eleven on the seventh with a man standing on it — **and that one is accounted for, by a clause Adrian wrote, and the accounting is a fact about paperwork and not about the ground.** The other two were said out loud by Selik Marne in a counting room, written on two separate lines by Ivo Stenn, and not joined.

## Adrian's position

**Stage 1 Reader, and still not a Binder.** He cannot read continuously, predict, transfer, renew, split a use, or **post security** — and he has said so in a public yard five times since the twenty-ninth. **Five readings are entered in the month of the crossing and one in the new month.** He has never boarded. His standing in the district is now the standing of a man who is answerable for a document: a clause he wrote at two in the morning put a protected man on a rail, and he said so first, in daylight, and refused to have it entered as bad luck, and the room used him accordingly. **The held asset of the twelfth is not a promotion.** It is the lease, the salvage and a two-season named repair-crew term, in a steward's custody, forfeit on a breach, not returnable — entered as neither a payment nor a security, with the reason given out loud in a yard and the words *it is not heroism* written down by the clerk because Nessa Pell writes down what people are.

## Public institutions

- The **proposal schedule** (seven items) is the standing rule. A document made in a back office is not a proposal.
- The **no-name remedy** answers Rell Bode's challenge, and the **split-use remedy** of the fourth is it made general: a hearing, a pooled count published before and after with any difference entered as a finding about the counting, and a payment rule that is never nil because nobody came. **It is entered known to fail, it was called on the twelfth, and it was not discharged, and the cost was set before the agreement.**
- The **public board** is four seats and no owner. It can refuse to record a closing, publish a result, call a review, uphold a challenge in part, and set or renew the short custody term. It cannot grant a use, waive a toll, audit a security, or strike a line. **In this batch it built a new instrument out of those four powers, adopted the practice of recording the state of the room's knowledge beside an instrument, and published an open question it cannot answer.**
- A **payment on an unstated claim** is a working instrument: entered as a payment and never as security, at a figure the yard is invited to check, with the shortfall entered as uncomputed and **not** nil. **A payment conveys nothing**, which is why a lease offered in a payment was never sold.
- **A held asset** is a third kind, and it is what a reader can actually do.
- **The claim-comparison table** and **disclosure that travels to the person** both exist; the second is built so that no courier is ever in a room where a reading happened.
- **A person is accounted for by a count, not by a household.** Four lines, entered on the second, and the volume's real constitution.
- **A mark is a refusal, not a person, and may not carry a job.** Nessa Pell's index of every use of a mark since the fourteenth is the only piece of administration anybody asked for and everything now depends on it.
- **The ward market's paper levy** — four coppers a quarter for eleven years for paper that does not exist — now funds the warden's inspection and a public reader at a counter at about forty-one coppers a quarter.

## Active threats and promises

- The return toll is **unpaid**, the spill is on four records, and Adrian's payment is on a claim nobody has stated to anybody.
- A **child of the second shelter group is dead with no name in any public record**, and the remedy is entered as not discharged.
- **A man of a protected household is in a room in the lower terrace with an ankle that will turn on wet ground for the rest of his life**, and the remedy for him failed in public.
- **Two protected people at the far receiving ramp have not been asked and one has not decided**, and one of them is a bearer in a suspended charter.
- The counterfeit is at twenty-one claims and rising, the new technique is nine flat honest-looking lines with no fold in it, **and Tove Alard has just printed a plain translation of the ninth line under every claim in the city, which teaches a carrier to read in a minute and therefore creates a faster counterfeit.**
- **Joss Amery has lost three whole days** and a barrow-load of clinic stores, a coal ration board in the wrong quarter was altered, nobody can put a claim on a person they cannot point at, and **his loss has still not been paid.**
- **The far bank has no crossing of any kind** and the river stairs are a three-hour walk.
- **Selik's permanent-office proposal was tabled and not adopted**, and he has asked, on his own motion, for a public review of his own order of the ninth, on the day the ward market votes on the paper levy. **Neither has happened.**
- **Teo's question is open and unanswered**: who tells four thousand households, by whom, and for how long, if the crossing never opens.
- **Rell Bode's objection that the book will be used against the people in it has no answer**, and Mara Quill's answer to it — the book has to be fat, because a selective book is the one nobody can check — is an obligation rather than a solution.
- The broken circle is a **claim about a system, not a signature**, on a Warden fuel return, a school ration ledger, a pre-Fracture salt permit, a bus manifest, and a counterfeit claim. One impression, no office, no name, the regional heading still scraped away.

## Next planned movement

**Chapters 41–50 are the last ten chapters of Volume 01 and the volume's climax.** The witnessed public auction sells **one defined use** of the Last Fare — not the object, not a permanent road, not a private gate — with Tarin as bearer, a rotating return crew, a named convoy and shelter group, fresh witnesses who accept the whole toll including the return, and a term that expires and requires a new closing. The staging line stays visible to residents. The batch's own new clauses stand in the way of the easy version: the release condition must be read aloud at the opening and the closing by a different person each time, a successful crossing may not be treated as a transferable ownership right, the far-side holder has not decided, the fourth bearer is a mark, the count is wrong by a household, and the remedy is known to fail. **The route must open and something must cost, and the cost cannot be the child again.** Adrian ends the use at the stated limit rather than extending it to save a private cargo, keeps the damaged token as physical proof in public custody, and leaves the lot book open for somebody else to amend. **Do not join the three unexplained things. Do not name Iven. Do not restore Dessa. Do not convert Lina. Do not give Tally a stage.**
