# Volume 01 Rolling Summary — The First Hammer

## Current position

Chapters 1–20 are complete. Alder Reach has crossed St. Orra three times with the Last Fare and is now waiting to find out what happened to the third crossing's return. The official bridge is lawful, tighter, and under review. The counterfeit is a printing plate in three brokers' hands. The public lot book has a proposal schedule, a no-name remedy, and a board that has acted twice. Adrian Vale has broken a public undertaking he signed himself, in his own handwriting, in front of two hundred people, and the batch ends with a missing bus and a complaint by a nine-year-old on his own notice wall.

## Established pressure

- The Last Fare opens one route across St. Orra for twelve minutes. Each traveler loses the memory of why they are traveling; a returning traveler pays again.
- **Tolls are counted by leg, and travelers and tolls separately.** The Chapter 9 outward crossing carried sixteen people and eighteen tolls. The Chapter 16 return carried sixteen distinct people and eighteen tolls. The Chapter 19 outward crossing carried **twenty-eight travelers and twenty-eight tolls**, out of twenty-nine offered, because one child refused and the refusal is deducted aloud in the count; its return leg has no record at all because there is no record to make one in.
- The route needs a driver who knows Line Six, a **named-pair return point** — a place, an approach, and the person who can hold them — and a passenger who can remember the destination. Since Chapter 15 the marked return is **the second rail joint east of the old Ward Nine bell post, under the lamp, where the ballast drops and the plate is stamped, in the bus of Tarin Kest, coming down from the far bank**, with a far-side condition for the service cut.
- The token is cracked through the plate after **four** activations — one bare and failed in the flood, and three witnessed. It may not open again. It has begun answering unasked questions in plain lines: `A TRAVELER WHO HAS ALREADY PAID: NOT STATED` and `RETURN: NO CONDITION IN THE RECORD`. Escalate that carefully; it is a behaviour, not a mechanic.
- Geography: Lot 17, the St. Orra staging yard, the marked spur and the old Ward Nine platform are on the eastern bank; the Ward Nine district, its fever ward, the receiving stations, the receiving ramp and the far school house are on the older central bank. **Every charter and book entry carries the bank in the text.**
- The four places stay distinct: the **loading spur / marked spur** is the near-side track behind the old school shelter; the **maintenance spur** is the near-side false destination from Chapter 6, a few streets west of the depot, **still unsearched at the end of Chapter 20**; the pre-Fracture **maintenance bay** is on Tarin's working card beyond the river wall; the **service approach** is Selik's official span.

## Adrian's position

Stage 1 Reader, and still not a Binder. He cannot read continuously, predict, transfer, renew, split a use, or **post security** — which is exactly why Chapter 18's private workshop promise was reachable and Chapter 20's foreman could be told a press was standing behind a season's wages that no registrar could inspect. The echo is the price of reading; the memory toll belongs to people who knowingly travel; Adrian has never boarded. He is not a candidate for Binder until he survives the failed public auction, works through Mara's corrections, and posts a real asset as distinct security — which is Chapters 34–42.

## Public institutions

- **The proposal schedule** (seven items) is the standing rule of the Lot 17 public lot. A closing cannot be recorded from a document made in a back office at night.
- **The no-name remedy** answers Rell Bode's challenge: a public hearing after which the record states that the remedy was called and **not discharged**, and why, with the protected category named and no household named. It works by failing loudly and often. That is deliberate.
- **The public board** is a constituted four-seat panel — registrar, sitting steward, and two rotating seats held by whoever in the yard has no claim on the lot — that can refuse to record a closing, publish a result, call a review, uphold a challenge in part, and set or renew the short public custody term. It cannot grant a use. **Name all four seats aloud and put them in the room whenever it acts.** Nessa Pell's seven-day term has been renewed twice in public and has two days left.
- Adrian's private undertaking — inspection press, four hundred and ten coppers, one season of repair-crew labor with three named hands — is real, inspectable in principle, and **not in the public book**. It is not security, because he is Stage 1 and cannot post any.
- The Unlisted have a protected category: a group, a count, a steward, a destination and a remedy in the book, a paper note of participation held outside it by the people the household chooses, and a sealed page for anyone who must be identified. Refusing a name costs nothing in care and does not remove anybody's remedy or duty.

## Active threats and promises

- **Nine people are missing between the banks:** Tarin Kest, Dessa Rill, one of the three escort stewards, and six of the ten shelter children who boarded. **Nineteen are stranded on the far bank** — four clinic crew, nine signal hands, four children, two stewards — with the public road suspended and no announced way to fetch them. Nineteen and nine are the same twenty-eight, and the number is in the book.
- The bell post's one arm has been turned to point along the spur instead of across it, four yards past the joint. Nobody was near it. The maintenance spur has not been searched.
- Selik's span runs a tightened manifest and moves four small cargo boxes a day. The review continues on the ninth. He intends, in Chapters 27–33, to argue from the Chapter 19 failure for a permanent Warden route.
- Halloway's counterfeit is a printing plate carried by three brokers. His own term is now fifteen days and falls on him, on the first page, in public. He has conceded the room and the first sale and cannot shut it down.
- The broken circle is a **claim about a system, not a signature**, found on a Warden fuel return, a school ration ledger, a pre-Fracture salt permit, a bus manifest, and a counterfeit claim. One impression, no office, no name, the regional heading still scraped away.
- Three boxes of clinic stores and a draught are due at the near abutment by four in the afternoon of Chapter 20, and a third of the fever ward's cots are empty.

## Next planned movement

**Chapters 21–30 carry the search and the first half of the reversal, and must not complete it.** The shape is: the search widens and somebody has to decide who goes into the Fault; the diverted returners and the false destination are found; the backwash exposes the concealed return term and the private workshop guarantee, and the public asks why he could describe every benefit and not the cost; Mara stops the route and says out loud, as a moral and professional accusation rather than a discovery, that he is making the same emergency exception Iven made and calling it temporary. Do not resolve the missing group, do not restore memory, do not name Iven, do not introduce a new antagonist, and do not let the recovery begin paying for itself before Chapter 27.
