# Volume 01 Rolling Summary — The First Hammer

## Current position

Chapters 1–10 are complete. Alder Reach has one successful but costly Last Fare crossing and no dependable Hearth Line yet. The immediate medicine shipment is delayed, the official bridge remains under Selik Marne's authority, and Halloway Reed's counterfeit claim is still moving people through private bargains.

## Established pressure

- The Last Fare opens one route across St. Orra for twelve minutes.
- Each traveler loses the memory of why they are traveling; a returning traveler pays again.
- The route needs a driver who knows Line Six, a marked return at the Ward Nine bell-post platform, and a passenger who can remember the destination.
- The token is strained after one bare activation and one witnessed activation. Repeated use may strand the route permanently.
- Backwash is real: it can alter nearby records and rules, and it cannot be erased by destroying or hiding the object.

## Adrian's position

Adrian has moved from Stage 0 practical broker to Stage 1 Reader through failure, an affected-person promise, and a physical toll echo. He can state one contacted lot's use, immediate toll, and standing. He cannot predict a result, read continuously, or operate a Fault object without disclosure, fresh independent witnesses, a term, and a remedy. He is not a Binder, has not posted security, and is answerable for the failed claim.

## Public institutions

Lot 17 has a public lot book and a documented public custody arrangement for the Last Fare. Mara, Adrian, and a seven-day board-appointed steward must be present to open the case; Nessa Pell holds the first steward key. Selik's bridge is a lawful alternative awaiting supervised review of its manifest requirement. The Unlisted have a provisional protected channel: beneficiary households may refuse public names while the steward, count, destination, bearer duty, responsible parties, and remedy remain accountable. Orren and Lina keep paper copies outside the municipal record.

## Active threats and promises

- The next medicine shipment is due at dawn; a second crossing would need new terms and a new closing.
- Halloway's counterfeit has a false return point and a concealed seven-day repair-labor term. It cannot create a valid charter, but it can send carriers into bare-claimant danger.
- Selik may use the failed public route to justify a permanent bridge closure or a stricter manifest.
- The broken-circle mark connects the Last Fare to records from an unidentified region outside current basin maps.
- Adrian's blank lot remains a familiar mystery object; Iven remains presumed dead and his living custodianship is unrevealed.

## Next planned movement

Chapters 11–16 should harden the first public road: establish the Hearth Line as a proposed public service rather than a private bargain, put the counterfeit dispute into public work, test the Unlisted witness channel, and force the market to choose between waiting for Selik's safer route and accepting another bounded public attempt. Chapters 17–20 should begin the larger medicine crisis and the first full auction, with the concealed return toll and private security reserved for the Volume 01 midpoint reversal at Chapters 23–26.
