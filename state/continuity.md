# Continuity State

Canon baseline plus the events of Chapters 1–10. Iven remains presumed dead from Adrian's point of view.

## Timeline and mystery

- The **Long Fracture** is the single Year 0 collapse; “Fracture” is its common shorthand. The failed Common Measure triggered the collapse and created Faults.
- **Geography across St. Orra:** Lot 17, the St. Orra staging yard, and the Alder Reach market are on the eastern bank. The Ward Nine fever ward, the clinic's receiving stations, and the school shelter's destination are on the older central bank, reachable by Selik's official span, the river stairs, and the old footbridge over the service cut. The Last Fare's route runs beneath the river wall. Medicine that has to be delivered is stranded on the wrong bank until somebody moves it.
- **"Ward Nine" names a district and a platform, on opposite banks.** The ward, its clinic lines, and the people waiting there are across the water on the older central bank. The Ward Nine *old platform* and its one-armed bell post are on the near side, on the Lot 17 apron, which is why the marked return of the first charter brought the bus home to Alder Reach rather than to Ward Nine. **The disambiguation now lives in the charter and the lot book, not only in this file: the Chapter 9 spoken terms and the Chapter 10 entries both name the return as the old Ward Nine platform "on this side of the water" / "on the near apron of this bank", and name the beneficiaries as the Ward Nine clinic line on the far bank.** Any new charter or book entry must carry the same bank language; a bare "the Ward Nine platform" is ambiguous by itself, because the district and the platform are on opposite banks. Tarin's loading spur behind the school shelter and the platform are on that near side too. The old shelter by that platform was emptied into the east ward after the first flood, so the platform carries rails, a canopy, and people waiting when a bus is due — no children and no cots (Chapter 4). The nine children named as beneficiaries in Chapter 9 were **housed before crossing** in the old gym on the near bank; eight of them crossed and one stayed. Any later return crossing must be written on this geography: the route crosses, the return lands beside Lot 17. The bus itself is a near-side wreck, out past the underpass inside the ring on Tarin's road map (Chapter 2).
- **Terminology for the two approaches.** The *loading spur* is the near-side track behind the old school shelter up to the Ward Nine old platform and its bell post; the charter calls it the *marked spur*. The *maintenance spur* is a different place — the false destination the bent route delivered the bus to in Chapter 6. The *service approach* is Selik's official bridge crossing, which carries the supervised small-cargo exception. Keep these four apart.
- Iven Tallow was Adrian's mentor during the final pre-Long-Fracture months, gave him the blank lot he carries, and vanished during the collapse. Adrian names him in Chapter 1 and again in Chapter 2, where he sets the blank lot beside the token on the strength of an old instruction and gets nothing. Iven has remained inside the First House for seventeen years as Custodian, but this is not known to Adrian and is not revealed in Batch 0001. Adrian's fear of repeating an evacuation he made "look orderly" with clean language is stated in Chapter 1 and grounds his compulsion to price everything.
- The Tally is a limited accounting presence built from emergency records and witnesses. It records declared use, tolls, standing, outcomes, and backwash. It cannot judge fairness, know the future, or create consent.
- The broken-circle closing mark appears on the Last Fare, Halloway's counterfeit, the original bus manifest, and an unread regional record. It is a mystery clue, not proof of Iven's identity or an answer to the Common Measure.

## Rules that must remain consistent

- A valid consensual charter requires lot and condition, territory, use, bearer, beneficiaries, toll, term, release or return condition, at least two independent witnesses, and remedy. Witness assent must be fresh after full disclosure; affected people must retain a real chance to refuse.
- Where a toll takes the memory of purpose, a witness does not travel on the lot they witness. The remedy rests on appeal, and the toll takes the memory the appeal asks about, so the required pair stays on the side that keeps its memory. A witness who is also a beneficiary's responsible party hands that duty to a named substitute. On a lot whose toll is not memory of purpose, this restriction does not apply on its own. Where a responsible party must be identified but may not be published, a sealed page held by the registrar may name them; it opens only if the remedy is called. In Chapter 9 Mara sealed **two** sheets — one she holds, one the school keeps, each marked rather than signed — so the stewards hold their own record of the duty. Treat the school copy as canon.
- **Reading-cost bleed, observed once.** Adrian's Stage 1 echo is defined as a fraction of the object's immediate toll, which would mean his own memory of purpose. In Chapter 6, as bare claimant, he also briefly receives a fragment of *Tarin's* memory of purpose — a memory of the man laughing, detached from any occasion — and he notices it is not his. This has happened once, was never explained, and is not established as a repeatable mechanic. Do not use it as a general rule; if it returns, treat it as a mystery to escalate rather than a tool.
- A previously closed **inactive custody charter** authorizes a named finder to carry an unactivated anchor to a receiving bonded depot for a stated custody term, with two witnesses and an unclaimed-delivery remedy. It grants custody only, never activation, ownership, or a bearer role. The surviving strip on the Last Fare manifest names Orren Pike, Lot 17's bonded salvage table, and the term; contact is not activation.
- A founder-mark forced record is mechanically enforced but is not consent. It bypasses exactly the fresh-independent-witness and linked-term safeguards and cannot add a new use. A valid consensual closing that expressly identifies the override, states the replacement remedy, and receives acceptance from at least two fresh independent witnesses supersedes it for the named lots.
- A one-time toll is consumed when due. Security is forfeited only on breach. If one pool of holdings funds both, the closing must identify the exact consumed asset and a distinct security asset; no other person may be conscripted.
- Adrian grows through the fixed seven-stage progression. Reading requires history or contact, and every stage leaves the prior cost active.
- Stage 1 permits a full three-part reading and a simple one-bearer, short-term charter with a visible toll. Multi-bearer splits, transfers, renewals, and security-backed public terms remain Stage 2 work.
- The Last Fare requires a named driver, a return point, and a named passenger whose standing is to remember the destination; the passenger is a condition on the use, not a second bearer unless a later Stage 2 charter explicitly assigns one. The memory toll applies to each traveler on each crossing, including the return.
- Overdraft counts enforceable recognition-backed claims, including emergency lots for which Adrian made his recognition shared security under a public mandate. Political authority alone does not cause it.

## Events through Chapter 10

- Adrian found the Last Fare under the Lot 17 inspection block, put there by a boy of seventeen or near it who slipped away unnamed. An old inactive custody charter on the torn manifest names Orren Pike as the finder — so the custody chain points to him even though Chapter 1 never names him — together with Lot 17's bonded salvage table, a custody term, two witnesses, and an unclaimed-delivery remedy. It allowed the unactivated token to be moved but did not authorize use.
- Adrian's first contact produced an unstable notice: one route across St. Orra for twelve minutes, a memory-of-purpose toll, and a missing return point. Repeated use may strand the route.
- Mara sealed the token and manifest, audited Adrian's private ledger, and required a complete proposal. She refused to substitute municipal inspection for fresh voluntary witnesses.
- Selik's official bridge was shown to be lawful and effective under supervision, but its manifest requirement exposed people who cannot be entered in a municipal record. A supervised review of the bridge's terms is pending.
- Adrian activated the Last Fare without a valid closing. He became a bare claimant. Two medicine boxes crossed before the route diverted Tarin and the return group to a false maintenance destination. Selik's Wardens recovered the two boxes and brought them **back across the official bridge** over the supervised service approach, using the written cargo exception from the bridge inspection, which carried no passengers and no full convoy. Because those boxes were recovered, **nothing had reached the clinic yet** at the end of Chapter 6, and all six cold boxes were still on the Lot 17 side when Chapter 9 opened. Backwash altered a clinic slip and public notice board. Adrian surrendered the repaired blue-collar injector as a concrete settlement against part of the claim, not as payment for memory. Mara resealed the token and recorded the custody handoff.
- Adrian promised Tarin to state the full return toll before any next attempt. Mara suspended the use and opened a public complaint and charter process.
- Lina established that the school shelter group could be described without publishing a household or children's names. The protected channel keeps the steward, count, destination, bearer duty, and remedy accountable.
- Orren recovered Halloway's counterfeit master and the original manifest, refused Halloway's safe-passage offer, and brought the evidence to Lot 17. The counterfeit uses a false salt-loading return and a concealed seven-day repair-labor term. It cannot create a valid charter.
- The first public charter names the cracked Last Fare; one route; Tarin as sole bearer; the medicine convoy and the school shelter group of nine children as beneficiaries; Dessa Rill as the separately named return traveler; a Ward Nine bell-post return; a memory toll on outward and return travel; one activation ending when the second of the two named return travelers reaches the platform or at twelve minutes; release to the public case; and a public remedy and appeal path. Two people refused to board — one of the nine children and a relief driver off the St. Orra staging line, whose place had been held open for him — and neither was argued with. Neither witness travels: Alma Dren and Ewan Latch stayed on the near bank, and the two escort stewards named on Mara's sealed sheets answer for the children on the far bank alongside Renn Fisk for the medicine crew.
- The medicine convoy and shelter group crossed once. Sixteen people traveled and every one of them paid the toll on the outward leg; the two who returned paid again. The eight children who crossed, their two escorts, and four of the five medicine crew are on the far bank and cannot say why they are there, and the stewards answer for them anyway. The ninth child stayed dry on the near bank because he refused. Tarin knew the route but not why he had carried the medicine; Dessa remembered the route but not why she traveled. The token cracked further and was returned to public custody. The token was strained after one bare activation and one witnessed activation.
- Lot 17 now has a public lot book, public custody, paper copies, a protected-name rule, a sealed-page rule, and Adrian's signed promise to disclose the full known cost before another activation. The public lot board is a closing board of four seats: Mara as registrar, the sitting steward, and two unclaimed seats held by whoever in the yard has no claim on the lot, which turn over often enough that the board cannot be bought by buying its members. It can refuse to record a closing, publish a result, call a review, and set or renew a short custody term; it cannot grant a use. Nessa Pell holds a seven-day renewable steward key, and the vote is taken aloud in front of the open book. The inspection press and repair-crew term are available but not posted as security.

## Fixed ending constraints

- Iven's Great Closing is a linked forced record intended to make every open charter permanent.
- Adrian refuses the founder's seat, voluntarily pays the final covenant's one-time founding toll with his private recognition, claims, ownership interests, and exceptional mark, and loses his unique reading.
- Local communities replace the forced record with limited, revocable charters governed by a Civic Tally. Adrian and Mara retain separate professional authority and later choose a shared life; Tarin, Orren, and Lina remain independent.
- The Common Measure trigger, Iven's custodianship, the blank lot's function, and the limits of the Tally are answered. The first proposer, the removed founding signature, the precise cascade sequence, and records beyond the Morrow Basin remain intentionally unresolved.
