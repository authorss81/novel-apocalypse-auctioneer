# Continuity State

Canon baseline is established for the pre-chapter story. No prose events have occurred.

## Timeline and mystery

- The **Long Fracture** is the single Year 0 collapse; “Fracture” is only its common shorthand. The failed Common Measure triggered the collapse and created Faults.
- Iven Tallow was Adrian's mentor during the final pre-Long-Fracture months of the Common Measure, gave him the same blank lot he carries, placed the founder mark in its record, and vanished during the collapse.
- Iven has remained inside the First House for seventeen years as Custodian. He is alive, but Adrian believes him dead throughout the opening volumes.
- The Tally is a limited accounting presence built from emergency records and witnesses. It records declared use, toll, standing, outcomes, and backwash; it cannot judge fairness, know the future, or create consent.

## People and relationships

- Adrian Vale, 38, is a practical auctioneer whose central flaw is turning uncertainty into a price and hiding shared liability.
- Mara Quill, 34, is the skeptical municipal registrar and Adrian's single slow-burn partner. They begin in professional conflict and retain independent authority.
- Tarin Kest is Adrian's friend, rival, and route keeper; Orren Pike is the record thief who becomes a public registrar; Lina Vale is Adrian's Unlisted sister and courier.
- Halloway Reed, Selik Marne, Nera Voss, Cael Orin, the Lock Council, and finally Iven form the fixed antagonist ladder. Iven is the final antagonist; no replacement final enemy may be added.

## Rules that must remain consistent

- A valid consensual charter requires lot, use, bearer, beneficiaries, toll, term, at least two independent witnesses, and remedy. Witness assent must be fresh after full disclosure; affected people must retain a real chance to refuse.
- A founder-mark forced record is mechanically enforced but is not consent. It bypasses exactly the fresh-independent-witness and linked-term safeguards and cannot add a new use. A valid consensual closing that expressly identifies the override, states the replacement remedy, and receives acceptance from at least two fresh independent witnesses supersedes it for the named lots.
- A one-time toll is consumed when due. Security is forfeited only on breach. If one pool of holdings funds both, the closing must identify the exact consumed asset and a distinct security asset; no other person may be conscripted.
- Adrian grows through the fixed seven-stage progression. Reading requires history or contact, and every stage leaves the prior cost active.
- Overdraft counts enforceable recognition-backed claims, including emergency lots for which Adrian made his recognition shared security under a public mandate. Political authority alone does not cause it.

## Fixed ending constraints

- Iven's Great Closing is a linked forced record intended to make every open charter permanent.
- Adrian refuses the founder's seat, voluntarily pays the final covenant's one-time founding toll with his private recognition, claims, ownership interests, and exceptional mark, and loses his unique reading.
- Local communities replace the forced record with limited, revocable charters governed by a Civic Tally. Adrian and Mara retain separate professional authority and later choose a shared life; Tarin, Orren, and Lina remain independent.
- The Common Measure trigger, Iven's custodianship, the blank lot's function, and the limits of the Tally are answered. The first proposer, the removed founding signature, the precise cascade sequence, and records beyond the Morrow Basin remain intentionally unresolved.
