## 1. The position the block opened on

**The tenth of the seventh month of the eighteenth year after the Long Fracture is the last day of Batch 0004's prose, and the eleventh is one day after it.** The block opened on a district holding two pieces of paper in a book a stranger may walk up to and read, one of them a hole with a reason printed under it and one of them wrong on its face; on a yard a stranger can walk into that had been asked twice in ten days by two sides to be an address and had answered neither time, and on a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing saying in a lane behind a bank that a market is a place where somebody asks somebody a question and that he did not know what that gate was yet; on a table against a wall about four feet high eleven miles off that a man with two hands that do not work could not set up on his own; on a woman who keeps the books in a room four hundred yards from that gate who had refused a man's help in front of about nine people on the eighth of that month and told him not to come and ask her again this month; on a woman of about thirty-one who keeps a route who had refused the same man in a lane on the ninth and told him he was not going to get anything out of that lane; and on a figure of four hundred and eleven arrived at by counting the people who answered a door, on a sheet nailed to a gatepost, for the first day of the eighth month.

**The starting figures, all derived and none carried: board 198. Train 514 days. Unentered days 228 since the twenty-fourth of November. A hundred and eighty-nine days from the second of January.
