## New canon, written into the canon card before the volume close uses it

**A ledger is a record of what a thing did. It is not a remedy, it is not a price and it is not a claim, and a year entered in it is entered once, and which column it is in does not decide when it went.** **A right to withdraw is a door in one direction and does not reach backwards.** **A person who has not asked for a right and has not been given one has not withdrawn and may not be entered as having withdrawn, and there is no column for it and a clerk may not make one in front of a stranger.** **The man named on a figure is not a row; he is the place a person looks when they want to ask who owns the years.**

**A sheet with twenty-one years on it is not a ledger and a ledger is a different thing, and the ledger has to be built by the people who counted and by the council and not by the man who can be found, and the reason is nineteen words and they are his own: a book the man who can be found builds is a book about him and not about the year.**

**The sixth column is the only one of the six worth having and it is the one nobody can fill, and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said in a yard that he would take it anyway.**

**A ledger cell can be empty on purpose and the page cannot say so, and nine copies of a page go out on nine hands and none of the nine carries the reason.** **A struck row says a thing was entered here and was taken back out of it, and a row with a hole in it says a thing was never entered, and the page has one of those two.**

**A worker who says no on the last day of a season is a worker who has already paid, and the last day of a season is a day the person is standing in the thing, and that is why the no is the wrong day.** **The eleven weeks between a notice and the first of April are not a formality, they are the time it takes the wrong person to find out.**

**The first use in this district of a right to withdraw withdrew a man from a season that never started, and the notice was good work and it was worth nothing, and the year and a half is on a line of its own in a party's minute and the notice does not touch it.**

**A frame is oak and a frame is the last thing that happens to a person. A notice against a frame is a door in the right direction into a thing that is not the frame, and this district has one instrument and it is pointed at the wrong one, and a district with no instrument at all knows it has nothing.**

**A ledger is what happened. A right is what somebody did not let happen, and there is no column on the page for what somebody did not let happen, and the man who fills the page did not rule one.**
