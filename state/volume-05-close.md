## 2. Does the volume's ending agree with `outline/ending.md` and with the fixed series ending?

**The ending is fixed and may be adjusted in scene order and local consequences, not in substance. Five things, as they stand in the prose at Chapter 250, with the chapter each is in.**

### 2.1 The final external conflict — what the volume must NOT have spent

`outline/ending.md` says a man dead from this district's point of view for eighteen years uses the founder's mark to begin the Great Closing across the basin; that the Tally enforces a permanent forced record that is not a valid consensual charter; that the override substitutes a Custodian for independent voluntary witnesses and bypasses the term limit without creating consent; that it is the emergency provision of an unfinished measure invoked after seventeen years of unresolved obligation; and that **it is not a natural eruption and not a new System.**

**Chapter 250 has none of this and is not supposed to. WHAT THE VOLUME HAD TO BE TRUE OF IS THAT IT SPENT NOTHING RESERVED FOR THE GREAT CLOSING, AND IT DID.**

- **Iven Tallow, the founder's mark, the Custodian, the forced record, the Tally's replacement rule, the emergency provision of the unfinished measure: a whole-word scan of all fifty chapters returns ZERO.** He does not appear, is not named and is not foreshadowed.
- **Nothing in the fifty chapters resolves the fourth line of the offer, and the offer is the same offer the ending's second climax beat depends on.** The refusals to read are nine, the departure is unspent and there is still no date in the ledger.
- **Nothing supplies a seventeen-year obligation, a substitute for independent voluntary witnesses, or a term limit that can be bypassed.** The volume builds the opposite: a term that is printed on the face of a sheet and a right to give the whole thing up on the first day of any season after the first.
- **No natural eruption and no new System.** The nine locks are nine pieces of cast iron in oak in a bank, and a plank in a notch, and a frame holding water because a frame holds water.
- **No new final enemy.** Every faction in Chapters 201 to 250 is in `outline/series.md`'s Volume 05 list: the Lock Council, the Upland assemblies, the Guilds, Orin's Registry, independent memory keepers. The Council has no face in four blocks.

**Verdict: the Great Closing is unspent. Volume 06 opens with it unspent.**

### 2.2 The protagonist's powers at the close

`outline/ending.md` requires, for the final internal choice, that he be able to auction **the right to administer the Tally**, that he be **the only person able to reopen, revise or revoke a charter** at the moment of the choice, and that his final loss be **authority and not death**.

**What Volume 05 gave him: the Convener's power, and the loss of the neutrality of his name.** Chapter 244 convenes nine separate local charters. Chapter 246's covenant says no person shall hold this alone. Chapter 247 he is put in a column in a book a stranger may walk up to, by show of hands, in a barn, in front of about nineteen people, and **he said the price out loud before he took it, in a hundred and forty-nine words, and a stranger came four miles to a gate the same evening and asked for him by name instead of asking for a page.** He said the rule himself: **a name put on a figure cannot be taken off it by the person it is put on, and he did not ask anybody to take it off.** Chapter 249 a letter names him twice, calls him the founder of a covenant that has no founder, and goes out on nine walls; he does not deny the word and does not claim it, and a clerk entered that a man who refuses to deny a word does not thereby claim it and that nothing anybody said that afternoon made him a founder and nothing anybody said that afternoon took it off him either.

**What that means for the ending's shape, stated plainly because a reader of the ending will want to know it was noticed: a man who can convene nine settlements and cannot be convened out of a column is a man the Great Closing can reach through a claim.** The Great Closing, in `outline/ending.md`, is begun with the founder's mark and every permanent forced record is written in Iven's name, and the founder's seat is a seat with a name on it. **Volume 05 has spent five months teaching this district that a name in a column is findable, and has taught the man in the column that he cannot get the name off. That is not a contradiction of the ending. It is the ending's cheapest price arriving early, in the wrong shape, on a sheet about water instead of a forced record, and it is a real consequence: the man who is most useful in the final lot is also the man a mark can most easily be laid on, and he will know that before anybody tells him.** The ending's own requirement — that the loss be authority and not death — is already half-spent in a form Volume 06 inherits and does not have to invent.

**He is alive, he is socially recognised, he is not a founder, not a holder, not a bearer of a toll and not a party of the fourth of four, and he lost no life and no limb in doing any of it.**
