# Volume 02 Batch 0005 Summary — The Borrowed Road (Chapters 91–100)

## Batch outcome

**The west end is marked by two named people and a hole, a train reaches three stations in the rain, a reader posts a day a week and three books and closes a split, and the volume closes on sixty-one pieces of paper on a floor and a name nobody can find.** This is the volume's last batch and it contains the volume's climax, the Stage 2 change and the close, and nothing after it exists inside Volume 02.

All five of the volume's Chapters 91–100 items happen, in the order the outline sets them, and none was spent early.

**The rule that a mark may not carry a job blocked the only instrument the district had**, and the answer was two named people, a stated term, a published entry and a hole in the ground that is a place a person can stand next to and is not a mark. **A line of stones was not extended.** The rule was not retired.

**A train reached three stations instead of one**, at about a quarter to midnight on the twenty-ninth of June, in the rain, on a loop eleven yards shorter than the standing train, under a charter that rotates three bearers by the week with a fourth person whose only standing is the ending. There is a toll board at each of the three places and **a remedy that can be reached by a person the record cannot point at**, because the fourth bearer was found by a paperer who asked a man with a lever what the lever was for. **The Fault did not disappear. It stopped walking, and where it stopped is written down with the date.**

**Adrian posted a real security and closed the split, and it is the Stage 2 change.** The security is a term of labour and a term of stock: one day a week at the ninth place from the twenty-ninth of June to the twenty-ninth of July, and three bound copies of the line book at eleven coppers each out of his own hand. It is not his recognition and it is not his mark. **The entry says in the same line that a transferable right is not a neutral right, and that he has gained a responsibility and not an authority, and that the charter can be revoked by four people who do not like him.** There was no panel and no ceremony, and the clerk wrote *the stage is not a moment* in the margin and underlined it twice, and he asked for both underlines to stay.

**Selik is a wary ally.** His span is still shut. His permanent office was answered and lost five to five, and the district knows in a public figure exactly what it declined. He asked in writing for his own man to be paid, was refused in about four minutes, was not thanked, did not ask to be thanked, and says *that is not enough* on published days and will go on saying it.

**The volume closes on the signal's memory pattern, on an entry whose origin is not a place, and on the name of a pre-Long-Fracture public office that is not a guild and that nobody in the Morrow Basin can find.** Sixty-one entries in a column the line book has never had, cut out of nine drawers in nine houses and laid out on a boards floor, make a broken circle. **The two lines are not joined and the sixth refusal is in the book.** The office's name is spelled out on three toll boards, on five hundred printed sheets, on a security schedule and in the water office's own book, it has been looked for in five registers this basin can reach, and it is not in any of them.

## Beats completed

- **Chapter 91 — The Hole (the fourth, fifth and sixth).** Opens on a man with a shovel beside a hole he has been told twice not to fill, and the minute. A hole dug to put a post in is not a post, and a post is a mark, and a mark may not carry a job, and the district's one instrument for a boundary on the ground is the one instrument it may not use. The ground is held by nobody, which is the only reason any of them can mark anything on it, and the mechanic who signed the lease says so and asks for it in the book before anything else. Four things are tried in about two hours and none can be used, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing says the sentence that ends it: a number is not a boundary, it is a measurement of one. **The answer on the sixth is four lines long and is the first instrument in this district's history made of nothing but people and a date.** A man of thirty-nine who has been in a hut forty-two days on a term of thirty says the nine-word word out loud and gets the fifth one supplied by the woman of the column, and the finding is that a man who has been somewhere forty-two days instead of four is a different man and the difference is in the fifth word.
- **Chapter 92 — Nine Vehicles (the seventh to the tenth).** Opens on a man of sixty-eight with a lamp counting buffers on a siding, for forty minutes, unasked, and says the rail is not a mystery before anybody asks. **The three reasons a train on that siding cannot go east are put in one place for the first time and they are not one reason**: the loop, the frame, and the toll. The woman who keeps the book of departures gives a hundred and forty-one in four seconds. **The hut at the fourth place has no keeper and has not had one in nine years**, which is the only reason the iron is under a coat on a bench. A paperer of about thirty-eight asks the question four people could not answer in nine weeks — *has anybody ever put a platform at this place* — and the answer is no, and it took a woman with a hook to find out. Four things are needed and three cost money and the fourth is a question, and a market has never bought a question. **The finding of the tenth is that a security which is spent on the thing it secures is not a security any more**, said by the man who posted it in about eleven words, and the registrar enters that the district has one fewer security and one more platform. A stranger writes **4** in chalk in the empty four-foot column and the keeper wipes it off and says nine words: it is not what it costs to ride, it is what it costs to arrive.
- **Chapter 93 — Twice Is Not a Rotation (the eleventh to the thirteenth).** Opens on a reader at a counter reading out a date and then reading out that there is nothing under it. **He says no in one word to the permitted hour and gives a number instead of a principle for the first time, and Nessa Pell enters the non-event with no cost and no time, and the four refusals in the volume stay four.** The finding of the eleventh is that the reason he gave is a number, and the number is the only new part of it. A room finds the person who can read a rate sheet aloud in about eleven minutes, and the person is a paperer whose trade is other people's documents, and she says yes before she is asked for the sixth time in nine weeks. **The sheet of the twenty-ninth goes into the public lot book with a delay published for the first time for a document that was not late.** The tenth hand's finding is that twice is not a rotation: a rotation that has turned twice has been shown this district will pay for the walk twice, and a subsidy is a thing you stop.
- **Chapter 94 — The Fourth Bearer Is Not a Slot (the fourteenth to the eighteenth).** Opens on three people in a signal cabin with a sheet from the tenth of May, and the first thing they do is find a mistake in their own document that had been in the public book for five weeks: a board keeper in the fourth line of a rotating charter, struck by herself, in her own name, in front of two people. ## New canon, written into the bible before Volume 03 uses it

**A hole in the ground is a place a person stands and is not a mark and is not a boundary and is not a mark that carries a job.** A mark is cut and that is laid, and there is no rule about stones and nobody is to lay one; the rule is not retired and the standing test case is not extended. **The rule that a mark may not carry a job was the reason the instrument exists and is the heading the instrument fits under.**
