## Chapter 0001 — The Bus Token

Adrian runs an ordinary Lot 17 sale on the eastern bank of St. Orra while the clinic's fever shipment sits on that same bank and the Ward Nine ward that needs it is across the water. He refuses to sell a cracked surgical lamp as safe, then a boy of seventeen or near it slips a brass token and torn manifest under the inspection block. Adrian's first contact produces a Tally notice: the Last Fare can open one route for twelve minutes, but no return point is named. It also leaves a metallic taste and a bodily warning. He marks it unsafe, refuses to sell it, and takes it to the back office. In the office he establishes the two threads that run under the batch: the blank lot card Iven Tallow gave him before the Fracture, never once filled and never once readable, and his own memory of the Alder Reach platform where a clean hand in his writing got eleven people off and left four behind. Curiosity and responsibility replace his ordinary control.

## Chapter 0002 — A Twelve-Minute Road

Adrian tests the token against an old Line Six map without activating it, and also sets the blank lot card beside it on the strength of an old Iven instruction; both give him nothing. Repeated contact costs him dizziness, a metallic taste, and a memory gap. The notice clarifies the use, memory toll, driver standing, return point, remembering passenger, and the danger of repeated use. Tarin traces the route, identifies the Ward Nine bell-post platform and its loading spur, and refuses to promise he will remember why he is driving. Adrian chooses not to sell the object or ask an uninformed crowd to vote on it. Mara arrives to audit.

## Chapter 0003 — The Auditor's Question

Mara Quill finds the token absent from the municipal registry and outside the public sale book. She distinguishes custody, sale, and charter, lists the required fields including condition, territory, release, witnesses, and remedy, and refuses to let her inspection count as consent. Halloway Reed argues that delay will kill people. Mara seals the token and manifest at Lot 17, records Adrian's contact, and gives him until first light to produce complete terms. The shipment is on the St. Orra bank and the ward that needs it is in Ward Nine, across the water. Her brother's record contained the phrase “accepted risk,” which she identifies as a label used to end a real question.

## Chapter 0004 — The Safe Bridge

At dawn, Adrian, Tarin, and Mara inspect Selik Marne's official crossing. Selik's bridge is a legitimate, carefully supervised public system, but its named-manifest requirement excludes a feverish child whose guardian cannot be entered cleanly. Mara secures a supervised physical inspection with the bridge terms staying in force during it; **Selik** is the one who identifies the Ward Nine bell-post return point and its one-armed post, and Adrian writes the description on the back of Mara's custody strip. The old shelter by that platform was emptied into the east ward, so it carries rails and a canopy but no children, though people still wait there when a bus is due. The medicine convoy and school shelter group wait at the St. Orra staging yard. Adrian refuses to surrender the token to Selik and cannot yet say whether an honest public opening is possible.

## Chapter 0005 — The Price of the Return

At Lot 17, the crowd demands a crossing. Adrian unfolds Tarin's patched map on the inspection block and draws a fresh small red ring around the east platform, inside the driver's wide one, then explains the outward memory toll and the repeated-use warning but does not disclose the return cost clearly. Mara refuses to close a charter with an unverified return point. Halloway offers a private claim with concealed labor terms. Adrian breaks the municipal seal and activates the token without a valid closing, becoming the bare claimant. Two medicine boxes cross before the return group is diverted toward a false destination. Tarin asks Adrian why he is driving.

## Chapter 0006 — The Memory After the Return

The returning bus is trapped behind a service wall and pulled toward a flooded maintenance bay. Adrian, Mara, Selik's Wardens, and a rescue crew recover Tarin, Dessa Rill, the clinic porter — entered in Mara's affected list by name as **Renn Fisk**, the man who set the six-hour coolant clock in Chapter 5 — and the escort. Adrian suffers exhaustion, numb hand, distorted sound, and memory gaps, and cannot remember drawing the red ring he put on Tarin's patched map. He also briefly receives a fragment of Tarin's own lost memory and notices it is not his; that bleed is unexplained and happened once. Backwash rewrites a clinic slip and a public notice board. He offers Lot 17's repaired blue-collar injector as a concrete settlement, not a replacement for memory. The two boxes that crossed are recovered and brought **back across the official bridge** over the supervised service approach, so nothing has reached the clinic yet. Mara suspends the use, reseals the token, and records the handoff. Adrian promises Tarin to state the full return cost next time. Stage 1 Reader is earned through the failure, promise, and toll echo.

## Chapter 0007 — The Price of a Name

At the Brass Verdict Hall, Mara creates a public complaint and charter-hearing process. Selik demands named manifests; Halloway argues disclosure lets the wealthy buy access. Lina Vale insists the school shelter can be described without publishing a household or the children's names. Mara agrees to a bounded protected-absence channel that keeps the steward, count, bearer duty, and remedy accountable. Adrian admits he wants Mara's procedure because he fears his own improvisation. A message reports a copied Fare claim in use at Halloway's warehouse; Orren arrives with the source trail but not the master.

## Chapter 0008 — The Counterfeit Fare

From the Ward Nine kitchen exchange, Orren traces Halloway's counterfeit. The claim uses a false salt-loading return point and hides a seven-day repair-labor term. He learns Halloway is offering him safe passage in exchange for the master. Orren recovers the **whole** bus manifest — the larger sheet the torn strip at Lot 17 was cut from, lifted during the surge that opened the road — sees a margin note about Sela Pike, and escapes through the Unlisted courier route. He brings the manifest and counterfeit to Adrian and Mara while agreeing to keep Sela's name and the shelter household out of the public book.

## Chapter 0009 — Twelve Minutes of Public Road

Before the public board opens, Halloway's private buyer is stopped with a counterfeit claim. Orren reads the hidden labor term aloud; the carriers refuse movement. Mara states the board's four seats and its limits, then records the claim as invalid without substituting for witnesses — and Mara, not the board, is the one who does the recording, so do not give the panel a refusal or a review it never performed. Adrian writes a one-activation public charter with Tarin as sole bearer, the medicine convoy and nine-child shelter group as beneficiaries, Dessa Rill as the separately named return traveler, a Ward Nine return point named as the old platform **on this side of the water** so it cannot be confused with the far-bank ward, a stated repeat-use risk, release to the public case, and a public appeal remedy. When Alma Dren accepts the witness role and also claims the right to take the nine children over, Mara stops the closing: a witness does not travel on the lot they witness, because the appeal depends on her remembering the terms. Alma stays; two escort stewards take the far-bank duty and give their names on **two** sealed sheets, one held by Mara and one kept by the school, each marked rather than signed, instead of in the book. Ewan Latch accepts as the second witness, and the two acceptances are recorded separately. One of the nine children refuses and asks Alma to state that her name does not speak for him, and a relief driver off the St. Orra staging line gives back a place held open for him. Adrian keeps his hand on the fare box but is not a traveler, so what he pays is the toll echo and the growing distance from his own terms. Sixteen people travel and every one of them pays: eight children, two escorts, and four of the five medicine crew cross and stay, with Tarin and Dessa returning. **Renn Fisk, the porter who set the six-hour coolant clock in Chapter 5 and was pulled out of the wreck in Chapter 6, is identified on the page as one of the four who stay — the same man, crossing a second time.** Tarin and Dessa return; Tarin cannot say why he carried the medicine, Dessa cannot say why she went. The token cracks further, the release is recorded, and the loss walks away from the bus.

## Chapter 0010 — The Public Lot Book

Several days later, Mara formalizes the public lot book at Lot 17 after a review opened at the Brass Verdict Hall. The book records use, toll, bearer, beneficiaries, responsible parties, term, witness roles, release, remedy, and an observed result that accounts for all sixteen travelers, both refusals, and the second payment on the return leg. **Its USE and TERM entries name the return as the old Ward Nine platform on the near apron of this bank, and BENEFICIARIES names the Ward Nine clinic line as on the far bank, so the book itself resolves the two-banks ambiguity rather than leaving it to the reader.** The four-seat public board appoints Nessa Pell steward for seven days in an audible vote. Selik accepts supervised review of his bridge rather than an immediate permanent seizure. Halloway offers to manage future crossings and remains a live pressure. Lina and Orren establish paper copies, and the protected-name rule gains a sealed-page clause for a responsible party who must be identified. Alma Dren says what she can and cannot testify to, which is why the two who did not cross are the two who can carry an appeal. The broken-circle mark on the token, counterfeit, and recovered manifest matches an unread regional record beyond current maps. Adrian signs a promise to disclose the full cost before another activation; Lot 17's inspection press and repair crew remain available, not posted as security. Dessa returns to the depot knowing the route but not her purpose, and the next medicine shipment is due at dawn.

## Chapter 0011 - The Next Morning's Price

Three honest claimants arrive for one twelve-minute route: the Ward Nine clinic's fourteen cold boxes, the ward market's request for a published three-day service, and Alma Dren's fourteen people stranded on the far bank. Adrian says "the clinic goes first" in front of nine people, hears himself, and stops. He states aloud that he wants to be the one who decides who gets the road, and that the wanting is why there is a board. He refuses the ranking and gives it to the board, which is seated aloud (Mara, Nessa Pell, Coll Ferrin, Marda Osk), refuses to record a preference, and records three requests with no priority. The token's condition is read in public under supervision: **cracked, a second route may not be available**, and the echo leaves Adrian's right hand slack. A boy who refused to board in Chapter 9 offers to be the one who testifies to what the crossings cost. A courier brings two bad pieces of news: Selik's bridge review has been moved up, and a private buyer has paid Halloway for the next crossing and means to take it before dawn.

## Chapter 0012 - The Manifest Question

The supervised bridge review at the Brass Verdict Hall, at a table covered in duplicate bridge forms. Members named aloud: Mara (registrar, not independent), Ansel Roak (route keeper), Ewan Latch and Oveta Kraus (ward market). Selik offers a narrower condition; Mara proposes a supervised group designation with a sealable registrar's page, and Selik refuses it. Delia Fenn brings a year-eight permit to show that the manifest saved her brother's body. **A Warden gauge faults, the span reports movement, and Selik evacuates the deck - everyone off, counterweight locked, no exceptions.** Petra Sill, granted a provisional berth minutes earlier, is turned back down the near ramp with her five-year-old daughter Bel, and returns to the end of the queue unasked. The reading proves to be a false alarm: a fuel can dropped at seven bent the sump float, and a chalked maintenance depth mark on the second pier read at lamplight looked like a hairline crack. The span is sound; the evacuation was still correct. Selik tightens the manifest in the same breath. Mara writes the false alarm and the evacuation in one paragraph so the tightening cannot pass as free caution. Selik accepts the review's written question and refuses Mara's remedy; the review continues on the ninth. Alma Dren takes Petra and Bel into the school gym.

## Chapter 0013 - The Paper Network

Orren traces the counterfeit to a **printing plate, not four forgers**: identical miscut type across claims offered by three brokers in three parts of the city - Ossie Prent (lamp-trimmer, arcades), Jory Slade (rope-maker, river stairs), Hask Vell (writes claims at a tea table on the terraces). He also learns that **the broken circle is not a signature but a claim about a system**, pressed onto unrelated documents - a Warden fuel return, a school ration ledger, a pre-Fracture salt permit - to make paper look like the output of a settlement. He cuts the Sela Pike margin note off the evidence along the sheet's existing crease and gives it to Bett Ruhl, so no household can be found on the same page, and keeps the rest. He prints eleven copies of the third page overnight and gets Sabin Tull to see it before he moves. The chapter ends with a signed claim already in circulation and a crossing intended for first light.

## Chapter 0014 - The People Who Refuse Names

Lina's kitchen exchange, where Mara's seven public columns have been copied onto the wall in charcoal plus three added lines (**WHO CAN SAY NO / WHO PAYS IF IT FAILS / WHO ANSWERS WHEN NOBODY CAN BE FOUND**). Overnight the courier network has made the counterfeit unbuyable. Mara comes in through the stair door past the third barrel and refuses to take a roll. The protected category is argued into shape: a group, a count, a steward, a destination and a remedy in the book, and a paper note of who chose to take part held outside it by people the house chooses. Wenna Keel wants her name written for her missing son; Bett Ruhl wants nothing written. Lina admits out loud, in front of eleven people, that she wants Adrian in the room and would rather he were not needed. **Rell Bode lodges the first challenge against a public lot**: a remedy reached by naming her is not a remedy, because the after is the only part that lasts. The board upholds the challenge in part and will not record any closing whose remedy depends on finding a person who refused to be named.

## Chapter 0015 - What the Driver Knows

Tarin's bench beneath the Needle Road House. Standing is a relationship between a person, a route, and a return point - a named pair, not a property of the token - and a bell post on the ground is a landmark rather than a return point, because anybody can move it. The new return is the second rail joint east of the bell post, under the lamp, where the ballast drops and the plate is stamped, **in the bus of Tarin Kest, coming down from the far bank**, plus a far-side condition for the service cut. Adrian pays for the axle in labor: forty shop hours, wheel nights, and an honest re-rating of nine carts and a hundred and forty salvage lots in the Road House yard, entered in both books before the crossing. Dessa Rill cannot be the remembering passenger - what survived her toll is an old rule for a city she is not in (green is the water, red is lamps) - **and Adrian sees the hole in that argument and does not raise it, because whatever made her dangerous to hold a route went out with the reason. Chapter 19 is where that comes back: Tarin names her, reverses himself in the yard, and takes the liability himself.** Bryn asks to ride and is refused on the grounds that wanting it is not allowed. A second route card shows the pre-Fracture Line Six emergency maintenance bay beyond the river wall, on no public map, which is where the road can come up on the wrong side.

## Chapter 0016 - The First Public Service

Mara writes the **seven-item proposal schedule** (route request with use, destination and count; the toll in the travelers' own words; a named bearer and remembering passenger and what that passenger carries; a term with a stated end; a remedy that works for a person the record cannot point at; two fresh witnesses who are not travelling; a public way to refuse that closes only when the case opens) and runs the first proposal through it herself. She refuses Stenn's permanent grant and refuses Adrian's private guarantee. **The board runs its first closing of its own: a return crossing.** Tarin and Wenna Keel go out and two pay; the eight children, two escort stewards and four crew come home. **Sixteen distinct travelers, eighteen tolls.** The object reports `A TRAVELER WHO HAS ALREADY PAID: NOT STATED`. The eight can name the platform and not one can say why they were sent; Renna Ivers, seven, reports that the toll takes the *why* both times and that she looked for a third thing and it is not there. Renn Fisk, on his third crossing, asks that it be written that the toll is not pain - "the thing it does is politeness." **Mara chooses the hearing over the sealed page:** the remedy is called and recorded as *not discharged*, with the protected category named as the reason and the loss heard but not repaired. The crowd's next demand - fourteen boxes, a second and larger shelter group, four repair gangs, one crisis window - is larger than the charter can honestly carry.

## Chapter 0017 - The Larger Shipment

Selik's housing is certified but the slots stay halved; the official crossing can move four small cargo boxes a day and the named work crew, and cannot move the eleven children without a roll. Halloway offers a complete private package: medicine, crews, and shelter, on a **fifteen-day labor term that falls on him**, written on the first page - and states in the yard that the seven-day counterfeit came out of a room he rents to three brokers, that he sold the first one, and that he cannot shut it down. Adrian reads the token three times in two days, gets less each time, and loses an afternoon; he writes the failure in the public book. He drafts a list of **ways the terms can be misused** and takes objections from Tarin, Mara, Orren, Lina, Selik and Fen Ambrose; Orren's is the return - "say what it costs and who pays it separately." Adrian answers three of seven. Then at four in the morning, with the yard cold and the medicine loaded, **he ranks the yard** - medicine, riggers, children, and the Unlisted and cooperative last - and it is a good ranking, and nobody stops him, which is the change. Halloway's private warning closes the chapter: the public board will be sold to the people who can afford certainty, in daylight, by a fair process, with no dishonest act anywhere in it.

## Chapter 0018 - A Sale That Looks Complete

The breach. At three in the morning, in the back office, with the public board turned to face the wall, Adrian drafts the auction sheet. **The RETURN field reads: the return follows the outward term as an administrative extension of the same use, no separate toll is negotiated, anybody who returns pays the memory toll again.** He also lies to one group in plain language: one marked return means one destination, so the riggers lose daylight walking from the receiving ramp, and the sheet says so. Mara comes in at twenty past three with the lot book and reads his own undertaking aloud from page eleven. Adrian answers with the sentence: *the outward crossing is the emergency, the return term can be written within the hour, and nobody is being deceived because nothing has happened yet.* Nobody accepts it. Tarin: he agreed to a return point, not to a return with no terms in it. Selik: a rule that cannot be read aloud by the people it governs has already failed the people it governs, and he will not hold a flag on a confidence. With the room empty, Adrian writes a **private workshop undertaking** - the inspection press at three hundred and eighty, the repair-crew term at a season, three named hands, four hundred and ten plus a season, forfeit to the remedy on breach - stamps it with his auction stamp, keeps it out of the public book, and puts a line on the sheet reading *security offered in the room, entry in the public book to follow the return term*. He knows exactly what that is and tells himself it is temporary.

## Chapter 0019 - The Full Auction

Two hundred inside, a hundred and forty at the glass, and a hundred more standing in the open yard behind them. Adrian chains the doors, ropes the floor, and runs the seven-item schedule. **There is no bidding:** the clinic posts the cold chain and two weeks of clinic hands, the signal crew post nine hands and a season and their tools, the shelter posts its count and a line letting the steward add a child out loud up to the opening of the case, the ward market posts fuel and lamp rights and a gate clerk, the Wardens post the flag and the route keeper's power. The counterfeit is exposed by a carrier opening the fold - Gerta Lund reads the third page aloud and walks her barrow out - and Halloway reads his own longer labor term in public and concedes where the seven-day version came from. Adrian discloses the outward toll and says the return may cost again, framed as the same administrative term, and says the security in the room but not in the book. **Tarin says no** until the board records that the return is unwritten and that he said so before the case opened, and then offers two honest alternatives: go out with the hole recorded, or do not go out at all. The sale closes as a one-activation charter with the return unwritten. **Twenty-nine are offered and twenty-eight travel:** one of the eleven children refuses and the refusal is deducted aloud in the count, and a repair hand who was never in the count walks to the far bank by the old footbridge under a Warden escort. **Tarin names the passenger himself and then reverses himself in the yard, on the record, and twice:** he ruled in Chapter 15, on a bench with the woman it was about standing there to hear it, that Dessa Rill could not hold the destination, and now says in front of two hundred people that it should have been said in front of two hundred people then - a person with no reason left is not the same as a person who knows the way, and if he is wrong the wrong is his and not Mr. Vale's and not the registrar's. **Bevon Rahl** has the count, the passenger, and the toll read back to him before the token leaves the case. The token reports `RETURN CONDITION NOT IN THIS RECORD` and the board's page records the same. The outward side succeeds: fourteen boxes counted across the opening, nine signal hands walking to the cut, and Fen Ambrose dividing the far side on the ramp - four children in to the school house with two of the stewards, six children back on the bus with the third. **The bus turns with nine people on it** - Tarin, Dessa, one steward, six of the ten children - and the route closes at twelve minutes before it comes out, leaving nineteen on the far bank. The board's page reads, in Mara's hand: *the return is unwritten.*

## Chapter 0020 - The Late Return

The same morning, from twenty to eight onward. Adrian's first three moves are to extend, reprice, and reread; the second one sounds, in an empty yard, like a man repricing his own house. He reads the RETURN field four times and understands that this was not a silence but a sentence put in the place a condition goes, pre-consenting twenty-eight people to a toll they were never told what they were agreeing to. The token itself reports **`RETURN: NO CONDITION IN THE RECORD.`** Mara forces a known-and-unknown list in the yard before he can act. Selik asks for the charter, the security, and the return term in that order and states in front of two hundred people that there is no security on this charter; a repair-crew foreman says Adrian told him the press stood behind it, in a room, and asks whether there will be anything behind those season's wages. Adrian says the breach out loud in daylight, and says the sentence again so the yard can hear what kind of sentence it is. Stenn withdraws the ward market's fuel and reports that the refused three-day service is now what sixty people are asking for. A cooperative clerk asks whether the road will be open again this afternoon for three boxes and a draught. **Mara suspends the public road.** The search finds the marked spur empty, the platform empty, nine sets of wet prints going out and one coming back - and the bell post's one arm **turned to point along the spur instead of across it**, four yards past the joint Tarin has never missed. Nobody has been near the post. The party does not get past the river wall. A public complaint goes up on the notice board in a school hand, written by Teo, the boy who refused, before anyone has agreed what to call any of it.

## Rolling handoff, after Chapter 0020

Adrian is still Stage 1 Reader. He has not gained an ability; he has gained a broken public undertaking, a private asset nobody else can inspect, and a sale that worked. The public road is suspended pending a new proposal. The Last Fare is cracked through the plate. Nine people are unaccounted for between the banks: Tarin Kest, Dessa Rill, one of the three escort stewards, and six of the ten shelter children who boarded. Nineteen are on the far bank: four clinic crew, nine signal hands, four children, two stewards. Three boxes of clinic stores and a draught are due at the near abutment by four in the afternoon. The bell post's arm has been turned and nobody has been near it. Selik's bridge is tighter and more credible than it was; Halloway's counterfeit is a printing plate in three hands; the broken circle is a coat, not a signature; and Rell Bode's challenge is still the question underneath all of it - how a remedy works for a person the record cannot point at.

## Chapter 0021 - The Search Is a Public Thing

The afternoon of the crossing day. Adrian turns a crowd into a search and discovers the search must be a public process: **a route, an end, a person who answers, and a way to stop it** - the last being the Chapter 16 refusal-window rule applied to a room hunting a bus. Mara Quill enters it as *the rule was never about charters, it was about rooms*. **The board cannot grant a search, only Selik can, and Adrian goes and asks for his own terms**, which is the inversion of the batch. Selik's four additions: a Warden and his marshal go past the wall; a walking party does not go onto the ballast past the second rail ever; the river wall is said in Selik's own words, not Adrian's; the party walks the gravel once and the book records what it did to the place. The search returns at twenty to five with nothing, and nothing was destroyed. **The three clinic boxes go on the supervised cargo exception; Adrian tells the clerk he does not know the position of the draught.** The second party brings a single nailed-heel print out of the platform gravel - small, left foot, worn flat outside, three nails gone, not one of nineteen men's feet, made before the first party walked the gravel, and pointing away from the platform and up the spur toward the river wall. **Nell Sarr then finds a clean arc of broken rust inside the bell-post socket and unweathered paint under the arm: a hand has been in there and swung it, inside a day or two of the crossing.** Adrian enters both facts and refuses to let anybody write a cause.

## Chapter 0022 - What the Span Recorded

Selik Marne, the same day into the night. The three boxes cross and **the draught is refused at the scale as a cylinder outside the clamp's certified load and goes by the river stair by hand, four hours, with two of his men off the abutment on a day when the near-side queue is eleven deep.** The far-bank trades are eleven claims against four boxes a day, a queue produced by the manifest he cut on the seventeenth; he refuses a fifth box on the record, enters the refusal, and sends all eleven a copy of the night's log at his own cost. **The twenty-two letters beat is not in this chapter** - it is the review on the ninth in Chapter 29. The three clinic boxes cross at ten past five with a marshal; the draught is refused at the scale as a cylinder outside the clamp's certified load (a hundred and seventy pounds of flat load, cylinder forty-two) and goes by the river stair by hand, four hours, with two of his men off the abutment on a day when the near-side queue is eleven deep. The certified gauge housing - sealed and signed by two people who are not him - **logged a movement at twenty-three minutes to eight on the morning of the nineteenth, on a closed empty span, on the pilot channel: the water office's fourth trace with no instrument attached, there to prove the tape was running.** It has never moved in nine years. He does the tapes at eight that evening, Vasey comes at ten, and **at about eleven that night Selik tells him to leave the pilot channel attached under a written mark rather than break the certificate, and says he will carry supervised cargo and no passengers on it** - a decision that the copy he sends to the public board at midnight makes unrecoverable, and that a second water-office fitter reverses on the twenty-first, on her own initiative and not at his request. He copies the whole reading to Lot 17's public board at midnight **in his own words, in advance of any request, because a reading nobody can account for is about to be shaped by whoever talks about it first.** He declines to put a Warden hand on the bell post. He applies his own March sentence - *a structure that can report a fault it cannot account for is not a structure I carry passengers on* - to his own paperwork before anyone else can, and knows the March evacuation was right and cost a woman her provisional berth.

## Chapter 0023 - The Maintenance Spur

Late day into night, and the hardest physical chapter. Two young Wardens hear an engine idling behind a service gate on the west side of the depot. **The bus is standing at the bottom of the cut with its nose against the service wall, doors open, engine idling after eleven hours and forty minutes with nobody in the driver's seat, route-control socket empty, the fare-box cover hanging on one screw.** The barrow with the broken wheel from the platform is outside the door. **Nine found: Tarin Kest on foot at the top of a service cut four streets away; Dessa Rill at the spur; one escort steward, marked and on a sealed sheet and unnamed in the prose; and six of the ten children who boarded.** The steward came out backwards because she did not trust herself to turn round, put the six around the barrow with the two smallest under the sacking, had no blanket and no fire for eleven hours, and said so out loud *before anybody is kind to me*. The roll was folded inside her coat. **Dessa has Line Six, has the rules, has the skill, and has lost the reason she is the one who drives the medicine, and nobody can say whether the road took that or the cold did.** One of the six children will not wake and is carried to the depot on a handcart at a quarter past ten. **The far-side condition works and is charged for:** the bus came up out of the cut on the wrong side at 7:38, Tarin could not find the pair at all, he stopped the vehicle, got out, and walked back toward the wall for four streets, and nobody followed him onto it, and the bus went on empty-handed and the eight got off it alive. He says both halves out loud and puts it on himself: the clause saved them and it cost eleven hours, four streets, six children in the cold, and a child. **The cause of the broken pair is the marking moving: a hand was in the socket, inside a day or two of the crossing. Who, how, and why are not established.** **The child dies in the small hours of the twentieth, at an hour nobody in the room was keeping and an hour the registrar has refused to invent; her name goes on a sealed sheet in Mara's coat and nowhere else.** At ten past four in the morning the depot notice board has rewritten itself: four lines in Adrian's own unstamped hand naming Lot 17's inspection press at three hundred and eighty, a season of repair-crew labour with three named hands, and the sentence *entered in the public book to follow the return term* - a sentence that existed in exactly two places, one of which was his inside pocket.

## Chapter 0024 - The Bill Comes Due

Mara Quill's chapter, the next morning. The board sits at half past nine with all four seats named aloud - Mara Quill (registrar, not a vote, not independent), **Nessa Pell (sitting steward, two days of term left, asking in public so nobody can say she was given it)**, **Sabin Tull (barrow-hauler, four refusals in nine days)** and **Marda Osk (tea at the tram arch, no claim on the lot)** - and renews the term for seven days in eleven seconds, on the ground that a term that expires in a crisis can be ended by anybody uncomfortable. Nine copies of the book are in the city by nine o'clock, put there by Mara on purpose, including one carried to a tea table by a man with a barrow. The child's death is entered in the yard as a line with no name in it, and the remedy is **called and not discharged**. ## Chapter 0037 - A Remedy for a Person Who Is Not There

Mara Quill, Lot 17, the hearing room and the kitchen exchange, the third and the fourth. **Rell Bode is entitled to the floor and takes it**: a remedy that works by pointing at a person is not a remedy for a person who refused a name, and **a split makes it worse, because the false-destination loss of a split use lands on whoever is standing nearest the fault boundary, and that is a fact about geometry and not about consent** - and the people nearest the boundary are the service-cut yards, four streets west of the depot, in two blocks the ward market has never had a rate sheet for. Mara agrees with all of it in public and offers a remedy she knows will fail: **a hearing on a stated day, a pooled count published before and after with any difference entered as a finding about the counting, and a payment rule that is never entered as nil because nobody came.** She says out loud that she has spent eight years building instruments that work by pointing and does not want this one. The cost is set before the agreement - **two pounds and eleven coppers, entered as nine with the lie in it in the reader's line**, which is the first sum in the book that is wrong on purpose. The remedy is entered **known to fail on the day it is called**, with the words at the front. The board is asked whether it may record a closing on a remedy known in advance to be dischargeable and not otherwise, takes a day, and answers in eleven seconds per seat: **it will record the closing, and it will record beside it the fact that the remedy was entered knowing it would not be discharged**, and that this is the first time the state of the room's knowledge has been entered with the instrument. Nessa Pell adds, on her own account, that this is the first instrument in eleven years that was costed before it was agreed to and the first she has been asked to hold that she cannot check, and that the board may replace her and the remedy will not care. In the kitchen that night, **Mara says the four sentences the entry does not contain** - that the people nearest the fault boundary are the ones nobody counted, that she has known it since the fourteenth, and that the reason entered is going to be true and incomplete - and **Bett Ruhl writes them on the wall under the three lines in charcoal**, and they are the first words in Lot 17's public record entered by anybody who is not a registrar.

## Chapter 0038 - The First Split Auction

Adrian Vale, one day, the sixth. Five hundred people in a yard and nothing to open: no token, no bus, no road. Adrian says it in the first minute and prices salvage for forty minutes to get his hands steady. **The board, all four seats named aloud, constructs a terms-closing out of powers it already has**: it cannot grant a use, so it *publishes a result* whose first line may not be read two ways, takes two fresh witnesses after full disclosure, and calls a review of its own publication. **Nessa Pell built the instrument and gets the entry that says so**, because the man who wanted it did not build it. Seven items in the schedule's order plus **an eighth line, moved by Marda Osk and seconded by Sabin Tull: no goods, no freight, and no carriage of any kind except the person who is carried as a person** - the first clause in the book anybody asked for because a counterfeit's second page made them think of it. The witnesses are **the tenth hand**, who accepts on the record that he is the tenth and not one of the nine, that his nine have been home since the fourteenth, that he keeps a page with every number anybody has said out loud in a yard and has not been able to check one of them, and that if the count is short he will stand at the rail and say so and will not be argued with, and **Bett Ruhl**, who cannot read and says so first and accepts on the condition that the ninth line is read aloud and that she does not have to pretend to have understood it, and asks for a third reading. **Dessa Rill says the batch's first honest sentence about the future**: the paper is the only thing that is real, and she would rather be a line in a book than a driver on a road that does not open, and she has paid five tolls and can price every one and account for none. The record is made at twenty past five, published in nine copies. **At half past six a reader at the ward market counter stops reading aloud** - *Rutt*, a signal clerk of about fifty - because there are four bearers and she can only find three, and one of the three is entered over his own refusal, and the fourth is a mark. She stops. **The board suspends the closing in its first hour, all four seats named, on the ground that it cannot be performed as written and was not checked.** Nessa Pell's closing sentence: **a schedule that says the terms are complete is the ninth thing that is not in the charter, and it will be in every one of them, and it is going to keep being in them until somebody with a bad shoulder and a good eye walks out to a service cut and asks the people who live there what they are holding.** Nothing is struck. The paper stays up.

## Chapter 0039 - Four Streets and a Wrong Count

Adrian Vale, the marked spur, the river wall, the top of the service cut and the yards, the seventh and the eighth, in bad weather. **No bus, and what happens is a foot.** The party of six goes out to walk the line because the published terms say the fourth bearer holds the marked line at the top of the service cut, and the line is a six-foot tarred twine between two iron pins above the ballast. **Selik Marne chalks the rule on the rail and stops them: nobody past the second rail, ever** - the fourth term of the nineteenth, with the flag out of the flood year and the twine four years old, all three of them the same rule - and agrees to a look from the twine and no further. What Adrian actually wants is in four documents, and the walk is the least useful part of the morning. **A mark is doing two jobs**: the fourth entry of the twenty-ninth, a strip Fen carried to the far receiving ramp, the published terms of the sixth, and **a paper on a gate in the service-cut yards that says HELD. ASK AT THE YARDS.** Somebody went round on the second, at the same hour Adrian was writing his clause, and got five or six yeses to a household's mark without once asking *may I put your name in a book*. Nessa makes Adrian read out **every place a mark has been used since the fourteenth - twenty-three places, an hour and ten minutes**, and the twenty-third is a strip in a kitchen book that was never meant to be in a public record. **Selik's rule is the rule that saves a man and the rule that puts him in the wrong place:** Adrian's clause reads *and nobody goes past it*, and the *it* is eleven feet above the rail, and a foot of the crown goes down at four minutes past eleven the way a plank goes. **The lamp was still burning, so somebody could see where he had gone.** The Warden of twenty-five admits he was going to tell the man and did not, and Selik tells him he should have, and says to Adrian that he has waited nine years for a man to argue with him on the ballast and it is his own handwriting. **The wrong bearer is of the same protected household as the fourth entry, and the two had never met, and one mark had been given two jobs four days apart by two people who had never seen each other's document.** The man said one thing in Adrian's yard on the sixth, to Orren Pike, in a voice carrying nine feet: *I was sent to hold a line.* **Adrian says first, in the yard, before anybody else, that the schedule is his and the clause is his and it is not bad luck**, and that the man hurt is not in the book by name and the four things he is about to hand over will not help him either. The room uses him for the first time in seventeen years as the man answerable for a document. He is read to the end of his hand. The chapter ends at about ten at night in the kitchen under the tram line, with **Bett Ruhl, Mara Quill and Lina Vale looking at a strip that does not match** - two books, two counts of the same six people, and Lina's rule that **both numbers go in the same book, not the correct one**, and that Mara must stop and ask her in front of Bett Ruhl rather than explain it.

## Chapter 0040 - The Book Opens Again

The token's seven-day custody term, renewed on the fifth of this month, expires on the twelfth and is renewed again in public, on Nessa Pell's own request and on the record, and she says out loud that she is going to keep asking for it. On the eleventh, **Mara reads the whole of the seventh out loud in the kitchen before the table opens, with two people in the room who are not clerks** - the first time in four weeks she has done what she promised - and stops and asks Lina what the two books are, and Lina answers in her own words. On the twelfth the **hearing is called at nine, all four seats named, and is not discharged**: the pool is published before and after at **thirty-five, with no difference**, because a strip chain was matched out loud in a room and the matching found the two numbers that differ, and the two numbers are now both the count. **Rell Bode takes the floor and says the thing nobody has an answer to** - a book that records its failures loudly is a book that will be used against the people in it - and Mara concedes it in public and gives the only answer there is, which is that the book has to be fat, because a selective book is the one that cannot be checked, and says herself that this is an obligation and not a solution. **The rewrite lands in five sentences**: use, toll, term, release condition and appeal path in one place in that order on one sheet; the release condition read aloud at the opening and at the closing by a different person each time; any affected person may refuse in public by saying the word and is owed no reason; **no successful crossing may be treated as a transferable ownership right**, and anybody offering a claim on a successful crossing is offering a thing that does not exist; and the book records what failed as a failure of the instrument and no entry is struck. **In the middle of it Adrian posts the held asset**: the lease on the building and the yard, the standing salvage, and a two-season named repair-crew term of two names and a mark, at **1,536 / 1,481 / 1,580**, held by the sitting steward under a seven-day renewable term, forfeit to the remedy on a breach of any charter the board has recorded, **not returnable by anybody including himself**, **entered as neither a payment nor a security because a reader cannot post security**, with the sign over the door excluded for the second time in public. His reason is given out loud and is not heroism: *I have run out of ways to hurt people by accident and this is the last one that is mine to give away.* Mara then reads the whole book in one sitting, from the eighteenth of last month to the twelfth of this month, three hours and ten minutes, six hundred people staying. Adrian reads the open page and gets `ONE USE IN THE RECORD. NOT PERFORMED. / THE TERMS ARE ENTERED. THE OBJECT WAS NOT ASKED`, entered as the first reading of the new month, and Mara says it is correct and that **the object was not asked** is the first clause in this book she did not get from anybody. The book is now the first thing in Alder Reach a stranger could use to argue both that a public route is possible and that the public man is not to be trusted, and it holds both in the same hand, and neither of them says a word about the fact that they are further apart in law and closer in practice than they have ever been.

## Rolling handoff, after Chapter 0040

Adrian is **still a Stage 1 Reader**. He cannot split, bind, transfer, renew or post security, and the held asset of the twelfth is **not** a security and **not** a stage; the entry says so in the same line as the figure. Five readings are entered in the month of the crossing and one in the new month, and Nessa Pell is the clerk who counts them. The public road is suspended, no fifth activation is on the record, and the token is behind a locked door on a seven-day term renewed four times in public, on the twenty-first, the twenty-ninth, the fifth and the twelfth. The terms-closing of the sixth is suspended; its review is called for the fifteenth. Selik's own order is under review by his own motion, on the day of the paper-levy vote, alongside a permanent-office proposal that was tabled and not adopted. A man of a protected household in the service-cut yards is in a room in the lower terrace with an ankle; the far-side mark-man on the goods-shed floor has still not decided what the job is; a woman at the far receiving ramp has not been in a household since she was nineteen and asked that the only thing in the book entered because she said nothing be that she said nothing. **A mark was given two jobs and Nessa Pell's index is the only thing standing between this city and a second version of the same failure.** The ward market funds an inspection and a public reader out of a paper levy that has been charged for eleven years for paper that does not exist. Hask Vell is exposed by a rent receipt and is not broken and is still selling honest fast claims to people who cannot read. **Teo's question is open and unanswered: who tells four thousand households, and for how long, if the crossing never opens.**
