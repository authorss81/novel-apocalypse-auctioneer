# Chapter Summaries

Batch 0001, Chapters 1–10, complete. These are compact continuity records for the next batch, not substitutes for the chapter text.

## Chapter 0001 — The Bus Token

Adrian runs an ordinary Lot 17 sale while the clinic's fever shipment is stranded beyond St. Orra. He refuses to sell a cracked surgical lamp as safe, then Orren Pike slips a brass token and torn manifest under the inspection block. Adrian's first contact produces a Tally notice: the Last Fare can open one route for twelve minutes, but no return point is named. It also leaves a metallic taste and a bodily warning. He marks it unsafe, refuses to sell it, and takes it to the back office. Curiosity and responsibility replace his ordinary control.

## Chapter 0002 — A Twelve-Minute Road

Adrian tests the token against an old Line Six map without activating it. Repeated contact costs him dizziness, a metallic taste, and a memory gap. The notice clarifies the use, memory toll, driver standing, return point, remembering passenger, and the danger of repeated use. Tarin traces the route, identifies the Ward Nine bell-post platform, and refuses to promise he will remember why he is driving. Adrian chooses not to sell the object or ask an uninformed crowd to vote on it. Mara arrives to audit.

## Chapter 0003 — The Auditor's Question

Mara Quill finds the token absent from the municipal registry and outside the public sale book. She distinguishes custody, sale, and charter, lists the required fields including condition, territory, release, witnesses, and remedy, and refuses to let her inspection count as consent. Halloway Reed argues that delay will kill people. Mara seals the token and manifest at Lot 17, records Adrian's contact, and gives him until first light to produce complete terms. Her brother's record contained the phrase “accepted risk,” which she identifies as a label used to end a real question.

## Chapter 0004 — The Safe Bridge

At dawn, Adrian, Tarin, and Mara inspect Selik Marne's official crossing. Selik's bridge is a legitimate, carefully supervised public system, but its named-manifest requirement excludes a feverish child whose guardian cannot be entered cleanly. Mara secures a supervised physical inspection and identifies the Ward Nine bell-post return point. The medicine convoy and school shelter group wait at the St. Orra staging yard. Adrian refuses to surrender the token to Selik and cannot yet say whether an honest public opening is possible.

## Chapter 0005 — The Price of the Return

At Lot 17, the crowd demands a crossing. Adrian explains the outward memory toll and the repeated-use warning but does not disclose the return cost clearly. Mara refuses to close a charter with an unverified return point. Halloway offers a private claim with concealed labor terms. Adrian breaks the municipal seal and activates the token without a valid closing, becoming the bare claimant. Two medicine boxes cross before the return group is diverted toward a false destination. Tarin asks Adrian why he is driving.

## Chapter 0006 — The Memory After the Return

The returning bus is trapped behind a service wall and pulled toward a flooded maintenance bay. Adrian, Mara, Selik's Wardens, and a rescue crew recover Tarin, Dessa Rill, the clinic porter, and the escort. Adrian suffers exhaustion, numb hand, distorted sound, and memory gaps. Backwash rewrites a clinic slip and a public notice board. He offers Lot 17's repaired blue-collar injector as a concrete settlement, not a replacement for memory. Mara suspends the use, reseals the token, and records the handoff. Adrian promises Tarin to state the full return cost next time. Stage 1 Reader is earned through the failure, promise, and toll echo.

## Chapter 0007 — The Price of a Name

At the Brass Verdict Hall, Mara creates a public complaint and charter-hearing process. Selik demands named manifests; Halloway argues disclosure lets the wealthy buy access. Lina Vale insists the school shelter can be described without publishing a household or the children's names. Mara agrees to a bounded protected-absence channel that keeps the steward, count, bearer duty, and remedy accountable. Adrian admits he wants Mara's procedure because he fears his own improvisation. A message reports a copied Fare claim in use at Halloway's warehouse; Orren arrives with the source trail but not the master.

## Chapter 0008 — The Counterfeit Fare

From the Ward Nine kitchen exchange, Orren traces Halloway's counterfeit. The claim uses a false salt-loading return point and hides a seven-day repair-labor term. He learns Halloway is offering him safe passage in exchange for the master. Orren recovers the original manifest stolen during Adrian's failed activation, sees a margin note about Sela Pike, and escapes through the Unlisted courier route. He brings the manifest and counterfeit to Adrian and Mara while agreeing to keep Sela's name and the shelter household out of the public book.

## Chapter 0009 — Twelve Minutes of Public Road

Before the public board opens, Halloway's private buyer is stopped with a counterfeit claim. Orren reads the hidden labor term aloud; the carriers refuse movement. Mara records the claim as invalid without substituting for witnesses. Adrian writes a one-activation public charter with Tarin as sole bearer, the medicine convoy and nine-child shelter group as beneficiaries, Dessa Rill as the separately named return traveler, Alma Dren and Ewan Latch as fresh independent witnesses, Oren Fisk and Alma as responsible parties left on the far bank, a Ward Nine return point, a stated repeat-use risk, release to the public case, and a public appeal remedy. One passenger refuses. The route opens once; medicine and children cross, Tarin and Dessa return, Tarin no longer knows why he carried the medicine, Dessa forgets why she traveled, and the token cracks further. The token is returned to the case and the release is recorded.

## Chapter 0010 — The Public Lot Book

Several days later, Mara formalizes the public lot book at Lot 17 after a review opened at the Brass Verdict Hall. The book records use, toll, bearer, beneficiaries, responsible parties, term, witness roles, release, remedy, and observed result. Selik accepts supervised review of his bridge rather than an immediate permanent seizure. Halloway offers to manage future crossings and remains a live pressure. Lina and Orren establish paper copies and a protected-name rule. The broken-circle mark on the token, counterfeit, and recovered manifest matches an unread regional record beyond current maps. Adrian signs a promise to disclose the full cost before another activation; Lot 17's inspection press and repair crew remain available, not posted as security. Nessa Pell becomes the first seven-day public steward for the case. Dessa returns to the depot knowing the route but not her purpose, and the next medicine shipment is due at dawn.

## Rolling handoff

Adrian is Stage 1 Reader, not Binder. Tarin is the sole bearer of the last charter and remembers the route but not his original purpose. Dessa is the separately named return traveler and has lost her reason for traveling; Oren Fisk is the named clinic porter and responsible party left on the far bank. Mara and Adrian have conditional professional trust, not romance. Lina and Orren protect the Unlisted boundary. Selik's bridge is lawful but under review. Halloway's counterfeit remains active. The token is damaged, resealed after the failed activation, and publicly held under a seven-day board steward term. The Tally's claim on other unnamed people is a recorded unknown. The broken-circle mark is unexplained. Iven is presumed dead and the blank lot's function is unknown.
