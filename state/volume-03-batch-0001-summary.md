## New canon, written into the canon card before Batch 0002 uses it

**A form is made by an office; an office is a thing that can be asked; every form this district has run in nine years was printed by somebody who needed it for themselves.** And nobody builds a column for a thing they do not have.

**A shape is not an answer.** Four books with the same hole in them is a shape, and the only thing standing between a stranger and a system is a line that says the four of them arrived separately.

**A gap gets copied along with the book that has it in it,** and a clerk who copies a book twice a quarter is the instrument, and nobody has to decide anything for it to keep going.

**A price can be published in a yard a quarter of an hour before a man is asked to pay it,** and it happens because of two ordinary pieces of administration and not because anybody decided to do it.

**A refusal to read is a reading and is entered,** and the cost of a refusal is that nobody had anything that was going to come out of it. **A man who has published the hour of a reading is entitled to wait for it.**

**A person answered where she lives is a person who was not brought to the answer.** **A rotation is not free; it is only ever free to the person who is asked.** **An instrument which either room can end is an instrument that neither room has to.** **A thing that nobody is allowed to decide is decided by a date.**

**A security which ends on the same day as the thing it secures was never a security, and a thing somebody else can go and look at does not need the man who posted it to agree.**

**A column with nothing in it is worth more than a column with a price in it for about four days, and the fourth day is the day the keeper starts.**

**A toll that lands on a place is not a figure and not a name and not a day. It is a place, a day, and either an amount or the fact that the place cannot say one.** **A board is not a document and nobody keeps a register of boards, and a place can be written on a thing that is not a document.** **A board which says where a toll landed is not a place a toll lands.**

**A thing with an owner is a mark, and a mark may not carry a job, and the difference between a thing I own and a job I do is about four feet of board and a cloth. That is why it works. It is not his.**

**A description is not a name, and a slip with a description on it is a thing a stranger can carry to a door, and a name is a thing a person hides behind.** **A line with no name against it is a column and not a payment.** **A figure in a public book can be read by a stranger and can be carried to a door.**

**A sentence somebody said out loud is not a finding and it is not nothing, and the column for things that are not findings is the only place in this book where a person can be wrong out loud and stay wrong.**

**A responsibility is not a job. A job can be refused and a responsibility can be taken away in a sentence, and a man who reads a number is not a man anybody can be sent to a place.** **A term is not a quarter. A quarter comes round again by itself and a term does not.**

**A document that has become wrong for a reason nobody caused is a document nobody owns,** and it fits in neither of the two lists this district keeps. **A man does not end. He either comes or he does not.**

**A limit of mine is not a history of anybody's, and people read the top of a page.**
