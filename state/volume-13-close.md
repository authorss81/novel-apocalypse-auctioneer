## 3. What this volume was, and the finding that is arithmetic and not a plot

### 3.1 The verdict

**Volume 13 is about a sentence this district wrote in Volume 12 that works, and about what working costs, and the cost is a person.** The fifth line of that lot book tells a stranger to go down a lane and ask a man who digs loam whether a page is that yard's. It was written by the man himself, at his own dictation, in a clerk's hand, over nine words of a fourth line, and it was written for one question. It does not say what it is for, and therefore it works on anybody. A stranger who reads it goes and asks that man a thing, and the thing is not about a page, because the line never said it was, and the man who wrote it never looked at a sheet of paper in his life.

**The volume put that man in a position to be asked, in front of about nineteen people, in the open, in the ordinary voice, and on the twenty-fifth of the sixth month, Chapter 649, day 49, a stranger came down that lane on the words of that line and asked him a thing that was not about a page and he said no. And then he said out loud that he was not taking it back. And a clerk of nineteen years entered a no on her own page and entered the reason, which is that a person said no in that yard and a page is where a person says a thing when it is going to be said twice, and said out loud that she was not going to strike it out in the morning, and entered that now so that it is in the book before she does it, and said out loud, in the same breath, that she has been right about a great many things this month and that a clerk who is never wrong is not a clerk.**

**The reading of that lot moved from the fourth of the five to the fifth of the five in the same afternoon, in a clerk's hand, on the same page as the no, and it did not move again. It is the fifth of the five on day 50 and it is not the sixth. The page is still on the table. The fifth line is untouched and is still working exactly as it was written. The man is still findable in the morning and is still not paid. A person saying no does not scale, and the volume's own learning is that it is the only thing in thirteen volumes that has ever protected a page from being used for the wrong thing.**

### 3.2 The opposite verdict, which is also on the page

**The remedy worked once and the yard knows it worked once, and it is on the page that the yard knows.** The man of about thirty-one who digs loam said out loud on the morning after the no that the reading of that lot is the fifth of the five and that it was paid on the morning before by a person saying no and that it is not the sixth, and a clerk entered the reason, which is that a remedy is what happens when that person says no and that the page is still on the table this morning and the fifth line is untouched and the man is still findable in the morning and is still not paid. A man of about thirty-seven who cuts reeds said out loud the same morning that those two sentences have never been in the same morning before in that yard, and that a man who says both of them on one morning has made a thing this district has no word for, and that he has been cutting reeds on that water since he was about eleven years old and has got a word for a good many things and has not got one for that.

**And the finding that keeps it true.** On the morning of the no the count of people who have come down that lane with a page in their hand stood at nine and did not move, and it had stood at nine the morning before, and it began this volume at nothing on the eighth of the fifth month, and it moved on nine of the fifty mornings and every time with a person and a reason in the room before the hand moved. **A page that sends a stranger works nine times in fifty days and does not stop working, and a man saying no once did not stop it either.** What the no stopped was the next question, in that yard, on that morning. The volume's own answer is on the page in a ledger sentence and it is not defended anywhere: the only thing in thirteen volumes that has ever stopped a page being used for the wrong thing is a person saying no, and a person saying no is not a thing that scales. **That is a real finding and it is also the whole of the protection this district has, and a reader who takes the first half without the second will think the volume ended better than it did.**

### 3.3 The third thing the volume found, which is arithmetic and not a plot

**THE DISTRICT COUNTED THE WRONG DIRECTION FOR FOUR DAYS, AND IT WAS THE FIRST FINDING OF THE VOLUME AND NOT A COMPLICATION ADDED TO IT.** The fifth line tells a stranger to go *down* that lane, through the gate, past the gate to the water, and along the water to where a man is digging.
