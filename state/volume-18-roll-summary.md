## 3. THE POINTERS THAT ARE NOT FIGURES, AND WHY THIS FILE PRINTS SO FEW

**THE LADDER HAS EIGHTEEN ROWS AND NINE HUNDRED CELLS ON THESE FIFTY MORNINGS AND NOT ONE OF THEM IS PRINTED HERE. THEY ARE AT CLOSE SECTION 2, AND EVERY ONE OF THEM CONFORMS TO ITS DECLARED INTERCEPT PLUS `c`, AND THE CLOSE SAYS WHICH ONES ARE CONSTANTS AND WHICH ARE ORIGINS AND WHICH ARE GONE. A SUCCESSOR WHO NEEDS A CELL READS CLOSE SECTION 2 AND NOT THIS FILE, BECAUSE A FIGURE PRINTED IN TWO PLACES IS A FIGURE THAT WILL DISAGREE WITH ITSELF IN ONE OF THEM.**

**THE COUNTS HAVE EIGHT ROWS AND AN INDEX LINE THAT GAVE A COUNT'S FIGURE WOULD BE A SECOND PRINTING OF THAT FIGURE. THE INDEX GIVES THE MORNING, THE BLOCK, WHAT THE MORNING DID AND THE POINTER. THE COUNTS AND THEIR SCOPES ARE AT CLOSE SECTION 3, THE LANE COUNT AT CLOSE SECTION 3.2, THE PAGE AT THE BACK AT CLOSE SECTION 3.3 AND IN FIXED FINAL FACT 3.**

**THE HOUSE FIGURE FOR A BLOCK AND THE HOUSE REGISTER FAMILIES ARE ABOUT OTHER BLOCKS AND ABOUT SCENES AND NOT ABOUT A MORNING, AND AN INDEX LINE THAT PRINTED THEM would be publishing a figure about the whole volume in a file about one morning. THEY ARE AT CLOSE SECTION 4. THE HEDGE FAMILY, BOTH CONVENTIONS, IS AT CLOSE SECTION 4.2.**

**THE ONE EXCEPTION IS THE COUNT OF COUNTED MONTHS, WHICH IS NAMED IN LINE 44 ABOVE BECAUSE **WHAT THAT MORNING DID** WAS THAT IT MOVED, AND A LINE THAT says a clerk counted a month out loud and does not say that the count is a count has lost the morning. THE FIGURE ITSELF IS AT CLOSE SECTION 3.3 AND FIXED FINAL FACT 2 AND IS PRINTED HERE AS A POINTER IN WORDS AND NOT AS A FIGURE, WHICH IS ALSO WHAT THE CHAPTER DOES ON THE STONES IN THE AFTERNOON.**

## 4. WHAT THE FIFTY MORNINGS DID, IN ONE PARAGRAPH, WITHOUT A FIGURE

**A YARD OF ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE LEARNED IN FIFTY MORNINGS THAT IT COULD NOT HAND AN ANSWER ON, AND THEN FOUND A PLACE TO PUT THE ASKING WHEN SOMEBODY ASKED, AND THEN PUT THE ASKING THERE THREE TIMES IN FIVE MORNINGS AND NAMED THE TWO MORNINGS IT DID NOT, AND THEN ENDED ON A MORNING WHERE NOBODY ASKED ANYTHING AND THE MORNING WAS WRITTEN DOWN WITHOUT A LINE BEING TAKEN FOR IT. A QUESTION WAS PUT DOWN OUT LOUD IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE's HEARING AT THE MIDPOINT AND WAS NOT ANSWED. A MONTH WAS COUNTED IN A MOUTH AND IS NOT ON ANYTHING. A FIGURE ON A SHEET AT A GATEPOST IS STILL FOUR HUNDRED AND ELEVEN AND NOBODY IN THIS BASIN HAS COMPUTED BY HOW MUCH. THE FIFTH OF THE FIVE THINGS THIS DISTRICT DOES NOT HAVE IS STILL FIVE AND IS UNPAID. AND THE ONE FIGURE THE CLOSE DID NOT PRODUCE IS THE SIZE OF THE ERROR IN THAT SHEET.**
