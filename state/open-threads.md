# Open Threads

No in-story event has occurred yet. These are the author-facing promises that begin with Chapter 1 or develop across the fixed series outline.

## Volume 01 entry threads

- **St. Orra and the Hearth Line:** reopen medicine and supply routes without turning temporary access into private control.
- **The Last Fare:** its twelve-minute use and memory toll must be tested in public, not explained only in author-facing material. Its standing must keep the named bearer separate from the return passenger who remembers the destination.
- **Adrian and Mara:** begin with an audit, distrust around hidden security, and one clear professional disagreement; do not start the romance already resolved.
- **Halloway Reed and Selik Marne:** counterfeit exploitation and a legitimate monopoly must offer different routes through the opening crisis.
- **Unlisted households:** route, shelter, and repair records can expose people even when no dangerous lot names them directly, making omission an immediate political issue.
- **Iven and the blank lot:** Iven is presumed dead. Adrian already carries the same lot; later volumes reveal its origin and history rather than introducing a second blank lot.

## Mystery schedule

- **Answer by the ending:** why the Tally exists, why anchor lots remember the failed Common Measure, why it recognizes Adrian, what Iven's custodianship concealed, and how the Great Closing is broken without private rule.
- **Reveal in progression:** the Last Fare's shared closing mark; the First House and removed founding signature; the blank lot as the Common Measure's distributed settlement seat; the difference between mechanical recognition and valid consent.
- **Intentionally unresolved:** who first proposed the Common Measure, the identity and purpose of the removed signature, the precise order of the Year 0 contract failures, and whether comparable records exist beyond the Morrow Basin.

## Fixed endpoint

Iven remains the final antagonist. His linked founder-mark forced record must be replaced through the Tally's replacement rule: the final term-limited covenant must expressly name the override, state its remedy, and receive acceptance from at least two fresh independent witnesses. Adrian pays a voluntary one-time toll rather than losing security through breach, gives up exceptional reading, and leaves a Civic Tally that works without him. No new System or final enemy may replace this resolution.
