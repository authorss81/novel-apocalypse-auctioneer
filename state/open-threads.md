# Open Threads — re-scoped to Volume 02

Author-facing promises at the end of Volume 01. The fixed series ending remains intact. **Volume 01 ran Chapters 1–50 and is complete. The next phase is the Volume 02 outline or the first Volume 02 batch, and the controller dispatches it; no further Volume 01 batch may be created.**

## The position Volume 02 opens on

- **A public crossing happened once.** The eighteenth of this month, one activation, twelve minutes, twenty-four travellers, twenty-six offered, thirty-one tolls, seventeen people left on the far bank, and a token that is cracked through and parted at the rim.
- **The Hearth Line is administratively possible, finite, and answerable to more than one steward. It is not dependable.** The span at St. Orra is shut to all carriage with its instrument certificate suspended. The plank is in the service cut. The far bank has no crossing of any kind and the river stairs are a three-hour walk in daylight, one at a time. **Every future crossing is a new proposal, a new closing, two fresh witnesses, a public yard, and a day somebody has to stand in front of.**
- **The token may never open again.** It has five openings on the record. It has lost seven grains. It said on the sixteenth that it does not know whether the route will close at twelve minutes or before it. **Volume 02 must treat the possibility of a last opening that never comes as a live fact, not as a threat, and somebody in the district has to plan for the day nobody finds out.**
- **Lot 17's public lot book is open and is the first thing in Alder Reach a stranger can take cold.** It records use, toll, term, release condition, appeal path, a remedy known to fail and recorded as not discharged, an asset held by a steward, a question asked of the brass, a count with a refusal in it, and a custody with a condition. **It is also the first thing a hostile stranger can use, and Rell Bode's objection that a book which records its failures loudly is a book that will be used against the people in it is still only half-answered by the answer that the book has to be fat.**

## Volume 02 live threads

- **The custody key and its first expiry.** **Hester Vane holds it from the twenty-sixth for seven days. It expires on the third of the new month.** The board can set and renew the term and can do nothing else; it cannot create a rotation and the request stands on the minute. **The standing instruction is that the person holding the key on the day of expiry must be named in the book before it is held, or it is not held — and the first thing Volume 02 does with that instruction is watch it fail or nearly fail, because a rule nobody has had to keep is not a rule.** The first batch should sit on that date.
- **A custody term that expires quietly.** The term of the twelfth lapsed at midnight on the nineteenth and nobody noticed for twelve hours, with the object in a case in a building with four hundred people in the yard, and the lock was Nessa Pell's and the authority was nobody's. The hour of a renewal is now published in advance. **The generalisation is the volume's first job: a clerk's memory is not an instrument, and nothing this district builds is finished until somebody other than the person who built it is scheduled to do the next part of it.** A term without a named holder in advance is a paper levy with no printed matter.
- **Seventeen people on the far bank with no way home, and a passage that takes two and a half days.** The return crew of three is a count and not a rescue, the light at the top of the stairs is a clerk who can see nine feet and cannot see the turn, and the count is published in the morning whether it closes or not. **The first Volume 02 batch should walk one of those days.** Nobody has yet been in a count at the top of a three-hour stair.
- **The no-name remedy failed on the twelfth of last month and has now been called again at a crossing that actually happened.** It is entered as not discharged and the reason entered is the protected category, and the registrar knows the reason is true and incomplete and has put it in charcoal on a wall she cannot edit. **Volume 02 has to decide whether a remedy which fails loudly on a stated day is the thing this district wanted, and either answer changes what the Civic Tally is.**
- **A child is dead with no name in any public record** and a man in a room in the lower terrace has an ankle that turns on wet ground, and the remedy for both was called and not discharged. The remedy of the fourth is a machine that fails on a schedule. **Nobody has yet been paid for either of them and the payment rule has never been tested on a real loss at a real crossing.**
- **The service-cut yards, four streets west, in two blocks the ward market has never had a rate sheet for**, are the people nearest the fault boundary, and the reason a split route is worse than a single one, and they are in no count in any book. **They are the first place Volume 02 should go looking for who is actually going to be hurt by a public route, and a public route that opens twice a month will find them.**
- **The counterfeit is still winning on speed and is now better armed.** Tove Alard has printed the plain translation of the ninth line under every claim in the city, knowing it teaches a carrier to read in a minute and therefore creates a faster counterfeit. **Halloway Reed sold forty-one places at eleven coppers in an afternoon with the toll on page one and got the argument right: the public road will fail, and on the morning it fails the only purchasable thing is speed.** The honest version is now in competition with the counterfeit on the same ground and the counterfeit is faster, and the comparison table read nine claims in a night and five of them were honest. **No arrest resolves this and no Volume 02 batch should try.** Hask Vell is exposed by a rent receipt and is not broken and is still selling.
- **Rell Bode's drawer.** A book that keeps a piece of evidence in a drawer is a book with a drawer, and the object is now in a case in public view with a published inspection, a rotation of keys and a reader at a counter. **She has been given the only answer there is and she says the answer is not enough, and the standing task is the fatness of the book: every refused draft, every number anybody set and anybody else checked, every reading and what it cost.** The volume's constitution is complete and it is unfinished, and the two sentences belong side by side on the page.
- **Teo's question has been answered once.** *Who tells four thousand households, and by whom, and for how long, if the crossing never opens* was put to the only party in the city that had never been asked it, and the answer was that **what is owed is not owed to a man, it is owed to the people who paid it; say it in the room where they paid and not in the room where the paper is kept; and if nothing else is sayable, say the thing that is already in the book.** That is an answer about duty and not about duration, and nobody in the district has a day. **The first Volume 02 crossing has to be announced, and announcing it is the first time this district tells anybody a date, and the date is a date somebody could be wrong about.**
- **Selik's permanent-office proposal is tabled and not dead**, his order has been reviewed and found correct, the district does not want it, and **his standing order with no expiry in a room with a door on it has still not been read by anybody in Alder Reach and is still not named by anybody in Alder Reach.** He is going to keep saying *that is not enough* in a public yard on every published day. **The first Volume 02 batch has to sit in that room and he has to be in it.**
- **Selik's own argument against the whole arrangement, entered on the eighteenth and not withdrawn: a limit checked by one man is a man and not a limit, and Adrian should be replaced in about a year by a room with four seats in it and a notice nailed up in eleven places.** Adrian agreed it was fair and declined to say which of his two reasons was the true one. **The seed of Volume 02's institutional argument is already in the book and it is not a fight about Selik.**
- **Adrian can still be made the wrong one and has said so himself.** The answer the volume ends on is *the limit is the only thing anybody can check*, and the standing objection is that a limit checked by one man is a man. **Neither of those is a resolution and the next chapter has to make somebody act on the second one.**
- **Orren Pike has eleven lines and a sixth line that is his mother's name**, has told nobody he moved the erasure record, and told a registrar on the sixteenth that he was faster than she was and that nobody has offered him a job. **He is a copy clerk with an index and a name he still has not said, and he is not Adrian's apprentice and is not going to become a safe one.**
- **Lina Vale is partly unrecorded and has not been thanked and will not be**, has written a fifth line on her own wall in chalk, and has booked the autumn read-back and said that a thing in both the book and the wall ends up in neither. **The protected category has now carried a passage twice, a loss once, a failure of a remedy once, and a refusal to be asked again once, and it must not become a bucket and must not become a conversion.**
- **A yard of six hundred still cannot agree the word.** *Urgent* eleven, *scheduled* seven, *priority* four, all entered, none adopted, and the line is in the order the responsible party put it in because the reason is a clock and not a word. **The vocabulary of scarcity is still missing and a district about to run a road twice a month is going to need it, and the vocabulary is an institutional failure and not anybody's temper.**
- **Joss Amery has been paid and the reason he could be paid is that he can be found.** Every other person whose loss cannot be pointed at is not a person the book can reach. **How a payment reaches a person who is not in a household is the next volume's central problem and this volume's last one, and the answer in this book is currently that most of them are not paid.**
- **A man was asked four days late and asked for the delay to be entered, and the book entered the delay and did not apologise in so many words.** The far-side responsible party's name is on a sealed page and is not in the book. **A person who consents to a job and is not asked whether there is a charter is a category this district invented in the last five weeks and has met once.**
- **The two unexplained things, still not joined.** A certified gauge housing drew a mark at twenty-three minutes to eight on the morning of the nineteenth on a shut empty span on a trace with no instrument on it, and a bus came out of a Fault with nobody in the driver's seat and then carried twenty-four people through a route and came back with its engine running. **The second one is now in daily service as the district's only vehicle and the first one is still on a shut span, and nobody has joined them and nobody may.** The bus's *use* is now ordinary and its *arrival* is still unexplained, and the difference is the whole of the mystery's method.
- **The broken circle.** One impression on a Warden fuel return, a school ration ledger, a pre-Fracture salt permit, a bus manifest and a counterfeit claim. A claim about a system and not a signature. No office, no clerk, no place, no name, the regional heading still scraped away. **Volume 01 did not answer it and Volume 02 should not either; what Volume 02 should do is find the second impression on a document Adrian has never seen.**
- **The three numbers of the nineteenth of last month and the one that has no name in any public book** are settled and must not be restated wrongly, and the two symptoms of the nineteenth that are not — the bus that came out by itself and the line that moved — are not joined to them or to each other.
- **The Quiet Registry** is undeveloped, unacted, and has no named officer, and must stay that way.
- **The blank lot and Iven.** Untouched in this batch and to be left untouched for now. The accusation of the twenty-fifth turned out to have a second application, to Selik, and Volume 01 used it once and did not make it a finding about anybody else. **Do not deepen the father-shaped influence in Volume 02 beyond what a person would find in his own handwriting.**

- **Answer by the ending:** why the Tally exists, why anchor lots remember the failed Common Measure, why it recognises Adrian, what Iven's custodianship concealed, and how the Great Closing is broken without private rule.
- **Reveal in progression:** the Last Fare's shared closing mark; the First House and the removed founding signature; the blank lot as the Common Measure's distributed settlement seat; the difference between mechanical recognition and valid consent.

- **Open and unexplained, and none of them joined to any other:** **why a bus came out of a Fault with nobody in the driver's seat** — now the only vehicle in the district, and still unexplained; **why the pilot line deviated by a third of a division at twenty-three minutes to eight on the morning of the nineteenth**; **why the foot of the crown of the service cut went down at four minutes past eleven on the seventh** — accounted for, by a clause a man wrote at two in the morning, and the accounting is a fact about paperwork and not about the ground.

## Fixed endpoint

Iven remains the final antagonist. His linked founder-mark forced record must be replaced through the Tally's replacement rule: the final term-limited covenant must expressly name the override, state its remedy, and receive acceptance from at least two fresh independent witnesses. Adrian pays a voluntary one-time toll rather than losing security through breach, gives up exceptional reading, and leaves a Civic Tally that works without him. No new System or final enemy may replace this resolution.
