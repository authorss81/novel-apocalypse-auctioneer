# Open Threads

Author-facing promises after Chapters 1–10. The fixed series ending remains intact.

## Volume 01 active threads

- **The Hearth Line:** The first public crossing is complete, but there is no dependable schedule. The next medicine shipment is due at dawn, and any second crossing requires a new closing with a visible return condition, fresh witnesses, and a remedy.
- **The children on the far bank:** Eight children, two escort stewards, and four of the five medicine crew (including Renn Fisk, the crew porter) are still across the water, unable to say why they traveled, while the ninth child stayed on the near bank because he refused. The stewards named on Mara's sealed sheets accepted duty for nine and answer for the eight that went. They have to come home, and Alma Dren is the witness who has to hear what they say. Do not let the far bank become an off-screen holding area.
- **The Last Fare:** The token is cracked after two activations. Its twelve-minute use, memory toll, driver standing, Ward Nine return, and Dessa-style remembering passenger are established. Repeated use may strand the route permanently. The failed activation left a recorded Tally claim on other unnamed people whose source is unknown; do not turn that unknown into a fabricated roster or a convenient new antagonist. Do not make the token a universal route key, a permanent right, or a substitute for consent.
- **The public board:** Four seats, no owner, and the power to refuse a record and call a review but not to grant a use. It is the institution that keeps the Last Fare out of both Adrian's and Selik's hands. Any chapter that gives it authority must keep its composition visible and must not let it become a rubber stamp or a fifth antagonist.
- **Adrian's signed undertaking:** He promised in the lot book to state the full known toll, the return toll, the unresolved terms, and the remedy before any next use, and not to use a private promise to make a public charter appear complete. Chapters 18–19 break it. Write the breach as a breach, and let someone name it out loud.
- **Adrian and Mara:** The failed bare activation cost trust, but Mara's public process let Adrian act under visible terms. Their relationship is conditional professional collaboration, not romance. The next sale will test whether Adrian can disclose uncertainty without hiding security — and he has already signed a public undertaking that says so in writing.
- **Alma and Ewan:** The two witnesses kept their memories by not travelling, so they are the pair that can carry an appeal. Their first use of that position, and Alma's own limit on what she can testify to, are set in Chapter 10.
- **Selik's bridge:** Selik retains temporary authority over a legitimate official crossing. The manifest requirement and its exposure of Unlisted residents are under supervised review. Do not turn him into a secret villain or resolve the bridge monopoly before the public route has a fair test. The tightened manifest must come from a real safety event, not from his convenience.
- **Halloway's counterfeit:** The false salt-loading return and concealed seven-day repair-labor term remain active. Halloway's argument—that disclosure gives richer people an advantage—must stay understandable. The counterfeit can cause bare-claimant danger without creating a valid charter.
- **The Unlisted boundary:** Lina and Orren have established a protected-name channel for the school shelter and other households, plus a sealed-page rule for a responsible party who must be identified. The two escort stewards hold a second sealed sheet themselves, so the duty is not dependent on Mara alone. It must not become a loophole that denies water, shelter, or medicine, and it must not turn secrecy into a new private monopoly. The public case is held under a seven-day, board-renewable steward term so custody cannot quietly become Adrian's private property.
- **The Quiet Registry:** named in Chapter 7 as the body Lina's Unlisted households fear will find a household once it appears in a public record. It is offstage, unnamed in its structure, and established only as the threat behind protected absence. It belongs to a later volume's pressure on the Unlisted, not to Volume 01's antagonist ladder. Do not develop it, name an officer, or let it act in this volume.
- **Sela Pike:** Orren has a margin note and a record of misclassification. A name can be preserved as evidence without restoring the person or proving what the Tally erased.
- **Iven and the blank lot:** Iven Tallow is named in Chapter 1 as Adrian's pre-Fracture mentor, and in Chapter 2 Adrian sets the blank lot beside the token on the strength of an old instruction and gets nothing. Iven is presumed dead from Adrian's point of view. Later volumes reveal its origin and history; do not introduce a second blank lot or reveal Iven's living custodianship in Volume 01. Adrian's platform memory — a clean hand that got eleven people off and left four — is stated in Chapter 1 and is the root of his pricing compulsion; it is his, not a plot device to be resolved this volume.
- **The broken-circle mark:** The Last Fare, counterfeit, original manifest, and an unread regional record share the mark. The next investigation may push toward records outside Alder Reach, but Volume 01 must not identify the person, place, or Common Measure behind it.

## Volume 01 planned escalations

- Chapters 11–16: harden the first public road, formalize the provisional lot-book process, trace the counterfeit through public work, test the Unlisted witness channel, bring the eight children, their two escort stewards, and the four medicine-crew members home from the far bank, and let a false-alarm evacuation make Selik's tightening of the official bridge credible before the market decides whether to wait for a safer official route or accept another bounded public one.
- Chapters 17–26: a larger medicine crisis makes the Hearth Line politically irresistible. Adrian organizes a full auction, breaks his signed public undertaking by treating the return toll as an administrative extension, and uses a private workshop guarantee to make the sale look secure. Several returners are diverted into a false destination; backwash exposes the concealed term and the private security. This is the Volume 01 midpoint reversal.
- Chapters 27–33: Adrian publishes the failed terms, pays a real portion of the backwash, loses reputation and inventory, and helps make the counterfeit dispute a public service rather than a moral warning.
- Chapters 34–42: affected people write terms instead of bargaining for an exception. The first multi-bearer public auction fails. Mara forces a rewrite with visible release and appeal, and Adrian posts Lot 17's inspection press and a named repair-crew term as distinct security, beginning the Binder transition.
- Chapters 43–50: the witnessed public climax sells one defined use of the Last Fare, not the object or a permanent road. The convoy and shelter group cross, the return memory is lost, the token is damaged, and public custody replaces private ownership.

## Mystery schedule

- **Answer by the ending:** why the Tally exists, why anchor lots remember the failed Common Measure, why it recognizes Adrian, what Iven's custodianship concealed, and how the Great Closing is broken without private rule.
- **Reveal in progression:** the Last Fare's shared closing mark; the First House and removed founding signature; the blank lot as the Common Measure's distributed settlement seat; the difference between mechanical recognition and valid consent.
- **Intentionally unresolved:** who first proposed the Common Measure, the identity and purpose of the removed signature, the precise order of the Year 0 contract failures, and whether comparable records exist beyond the Morrow Basin.

## Fixed endpoint

Iven remains the final antagonist. His linked founder-mark forced record must be replaced through the Tally's replacement rule: the final term-limited covenant must expressly name the override, state its remedy, and receive acceptance from at least two fresh independent witnesses. Adrian pays a voluntary one-time toll rather than losing security through breach, gives up exceptional reading, and leaves a Civic Tally that works without him. No new System or final enemy may replace this resolution.
