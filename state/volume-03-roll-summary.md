# Volume 03 Summary: The Hearth Commons — the roll for Chapters 101–150

**Volume 03 is complete at Chapter 150.** This is the roll for the whole volume and it is the first document a Volume 04 writer should read. It is not a summary of chapters; it is a statement of what the volume built, what it cost, and what is still open. The per-chapter record is `state/chapter-summaries.md`; the five batch records are `state/volume-03-batch-0001-summary.md` through `state/volume-03-batch-0005-summary.md`; the running state is `state/current.md`; the live threads are `state/open-threads.md`; the canon card for the last ten chapters is `outline/batches/volume-03-batch-0005.md`.

## The sentence the volume turned on

**A place that has a railway and a charter and four toll boards has still not found a way to say what a toll lands on a place, and the first large thing anybody in this district is asked to do with a dangerous object since the ninth of March is put to a person whose body makes it work.** The Salt Verge is thirty miles east across a river that came up nine feet in the flood year and has not gone down. Salt is the one thing this basin cannot do without for eleven months. A loom is a wind frame, a channel and a bed of brine. It holds the channel exactly and the salt comes out of the water whole, and somebody pays its toll, and the toll is **a year of a worker's memory, taken at the loom and not asked for by anybody, with the worker bound to the channel for the length of the year.** Not the memory of a thing that happened. A year.

**The instrument the district lacks is the one that would let a stranger read a cost off a piece of paper instead of off a person**, and fifty chapters of this volume are the method — ask a person in the five things, and then write down what they said in their own words — applied to a subject on which the method is not neutral, because asking a worker about a year is asking a person who cannot remember the year.

## The opening position, restated for a Volume 04 writer

- **The month was July.** The first hard date was the twenty-ninth of July and four things ended on it. **The month turned five times in the volume** — in Chapter 107, 115, 124, 135 and 144 — and the volume closed in December.
- **Adrian was a Binder**, as of the twenty-ninth of June at about half past eleven at night, in a hut, with no panel. He could write a bounded charter, split a use, close a transfer, make a lot portable or renewable under a stated term and post a security. He could not maintain a public lot, coordinate more than one bearer, arrange a hearing, or make the consequences of an object visible to a whole settlement, **and this district had no public lot, four bearers and no hearing**, and the entry says that neither fact is a coincidence.
- **He has one departure left and it is unspent.** **Nine refusals to read are on the record across Volumes 02 and 03 and none of them is written as cowardice or as discipline.** **Ivo Stenn's separate sheet is nineteen words in every book in this district and is twenty as printed**, and the mismatch is carried, canon, and disclosed rather than repaired. The reading ledger is one for April, none for May, one for the twenty-ninth of June, one for the sixth of September, one for the ninth of October and one for the third of November, **and there is still no date in the ledger for the next reading.** The intervals have gone up on use for the fifth time in eleven weeks and he will not defend it and will not call it anything else.
- **A hundred and eighty-three coppers went on nothing at the ninth place** in the second week of a month in the middle of the year and nobody can say who is owed it, **and the answer is not to be struck.** It was read out at the ward market counter on the twenty-second of December for the first time in eleven weeks and nobody refused.
- **The span at the hut a bridge administration staffs is still shut.** Seventeen people are on the far bank with a stamped book and no way home. **Selik Marne is a wary ally and not a friend**: he was not asked in December, he is not on a bank asking, he said the two words he says on published days on the first of December and was not asked about them, and the agent of a guarantee house did not meet him.

## The five batches and what each one did

- **Batch 0001 (Chapters 101–110), the twenty-ninth of July.** A term ends a use without the man who built the right to stop it standing up, and the twenty-ninth has no climax in it and that is the finding. **A thing nobody is allowed to decide is not decided by a date; a date is the last day somebody can do something.** The sixth search for the office was for the work and found four gaps in four books in four rooms, and a shape is not an office. The permitted reading hour opened and nobody asked him, and he was asked on the sixteenth and refused, and the refusal is the sixth and it is entered. A hundred and eighty-three coppers go into a market book under a heading with no name against it. **A lot has seven parts and not five**, and the seventh is a person.
- **Batch 0002 (111–120), a man from the salt pans.** The first consequence of nothing running is a district with a railway and no schedule. A man from the Verge walks eleven miles to ask whether the water at the ninth place can be counted. **The looms are described by the people who work them and not by a reading**, in four rooms, and four people described one and none of them could be asked a question about it. **A year does not go twice.** A woman of thirty-one refuses four offers in four minutes and says nine words: the price is not the thing, the body is the thing. Two valid certificates in one drawer say opposite things about the same sheet and neither can be struck. **The only way to be wrong about a consent in this basin is to be right twice.**
- **Batch 0003 (121–130), a year, taken and not asked for.** The first channel fails, and the day it failed is entered as not known and is not a mystery. A food convoy chooses between a public loom and a private one, and the private one is cheaper and the public one is asked. **The aggregate unpaid toll becomes visible as one figure against Adrian's own name, and the number is not his fault and is his responsibility, and the entry says both.** A worker council forms because a loom-worker wrote down four things that had happened to four people and would not sign them. Nera Voss's first agent is courteous and does not hide what she is offering, which is worse. The romance moves one sentence across a room and does not resolve.
- **Batch 0004 (131–140), the ledger and the right to withdraw.** The memory ledger is built by six people and a clerk: six columns, a page ruled to thirty-one rows, eleven filled in four hours, twenty ruled and empty, on a board of eleven in a yard where a stranger took a copy cold and understood the sixth column without being told. **A right to withdraw before renewal fails once in public** — the first notice in this district withdrew a man from a season that never started — and exists at the end of the batch in a second form with a named hole: **a frame is oak and the channel is where the year comes out of.** Adrian's public status becomes a financial liability and he is told so in his own trade's words by a man who sells on a tram arch and is right.
- **Batch 0005 (141–150), the crop and the precedent.** **The crop survives** in a cart that came west on the twenty-second of November, and the channel it was going to survive on did not run, and both facts came in the same cart and were not joined. **The council's board, which had one day left on it, gets a fourth day — the twenty-fourth of December — written by a man who counts for a living in a hand anybody can read.** A third form of the notice is written that says on its face that nobody has established what a withdrawal costs. **The Salt Loom charter closes on four sheets, with rotating workers, a visible ledger and a withdrawal right, done by nineteen people and a clerk and entered by a clerk of the ward market, and Adrian's name is on it as a guarantor of nothing and as the man responsible for the aggregate unpaid toll.** **Alder Reach and the Salt Verge sign a food agreement on seasonal terms whose sixth sheet is the reason, and the reason is a year, and the two witnesses were not in the room when the reason was read out and nobody is to ask either of them why.** **A guarantee house's ledger shows the same memory tolls, a year in the fourth column with no heading over it and four hundred years of them, in a city that is not named.** And the instrument that says what a toll lands on a place is used four times in anger in one month and still has no name and no owner.

## What the volume built, in the order it built it

- **A ledger. Six columns, a page ruled to thirty-one rows, eleven filled in four hours by six people and a clerk, twenty ruled and empty, in nine copies on eleven boards in the yard of Lot 17, and one copy disagrees with another in one word.** The columns are **who stood in it, which frame, which year of theirs, when it happened, what they said, whether they were in the room.** The sixth is the one the volume is about: it is the only one of the six worth having and the only one of the six nobody can fill. **The third column is not totalled and cannot be, because a column of years is not a column of money and a year does not go twice.** The second column is the only one of the six that cannot be checked at all, because the four who counted on the second of October named the frames instead of numbering them. The seventh row is not a year and says so on its face. The eleventh row is four empty columns and the words NOT ASKED in the sixth, and four seasons went into its third column on the eighteenth of November and the sixth column was left alone, because the column is for what this district asked and changing the cell would make the page say we asked. **A line is ruled under the eleventh row and nothing is written under it.**
- **A right to withdraw before renewal, in a third form, with the hole written on the sheet.** It names a person, a frame and a date on one line in the middle of the sheet and it does not name a season. Its four lines under those three things say: the notice is not a release, is not a consent and does not give back a year; **the frame is oak and the year came out of the channel and nobody has established what a withdrawal costs and nobody is going to establish it by asking the person;** a person who withdraws has not consented to the season they withdrew from and a person who has not been asked has not refused. **The first two words of the top line are the same as the first form's, and the season in them has not started, and the man who wrote the third form did not write the first.**
- **A charter, on four sheets, closed and entered, in four different states.** A rotation of four people with a term, whose second line says **NOT A BEARER** four times. The ledger, named as **A PAGE RULED TO THIRTY-ONE ROWS**, with the number deliberately not on it. The third form of the notice. And terms, carrying the season, the withdrawal right with the hole on its face, the figure of twenty-one years in the same line as one name, and an entry that runs to the thirty-first of March. **A guild inspector of forty-four sent it back thirty-four miles with nine lines saying that a certificate can say four people were asked and cannot certify what any of the four said to the other three, and the word certified is on none of the four sheets.**
- **A food agreement between Alder Reach and the Salt Verge on seasonal terms, six sheets, with two figures that are not added and no rate between them and no coppers on either.** The sixth sheet is the reason. **The reason is a year, and a year is not a price, and the sheet says that on its face.** The two witnesses are a man of sixty-one with a frame who was two miles west and a woman of fifty-one who keeps a stall on the lower terrace and supplies the Harrow engine's oil out of her own till, and **the sheet says on its face that the two witnesses were not in the room when the reason was read out and that nobody is to ask either of them why.**
- **A method that is now a clerk's method and not a Binder's.** A person is asked in six things; a person is not asked and the record says **not asked**; a person who has not been asked has not withdrawn and cannot be entered as having withdrawn. A count of not-askings is a column and is not a bucket and is not a protection. **A man who makes a book and a man named in a book cannot be the same man, and the whole of that rule took four people in September to find out and did not take a Binder.**
- **The fourth place has a person in it for the first time in nine years, and the person is not a keeper and does not want to be one**, because the platform is thirty feet long and it is dry. **The boy of nineteen at the fourth place is nobody's keeper and is not her keeper and the two are in one line and are not joined.**

## What the volume cost, in the order it cost it

- **A public status.** On the nineteenth of October nine households and not Adrian entered the word steward, with the date it ends in the same line as his name, and the word cost him the right to be neutral, and a man of fifty-six entered that he wanted the loss of it entered as a credit to the four people who noticed he was wrong inside about nine minutes and not as a fault of his. **The second half of the word is a season and the season has not started, and on the second and fifth of December a clerk of nineteen years entered that this is not a delay, of her own accord, and was not asked to.**
- **The aggregate unpaid toll: twenty-one years**, four plus two plus eleven plus four, **with a year and a half on a line of its own that is not added, two people in a column as not known and not asked, and one frame that took nothing.** It is a floor and not a total. **A stranger asked what a year is worth and was told there is no rate in this basin that turns one into the other, and that a rate is a thing two people agree on and this figure has one name against it and no second person anywhere.**
- **A name that became a liability.** A man who sells on a tram arch priced it at **about a fifth**, could not tell a fifth of what, and said that he has never sold a year and cannot warrant one and cannot strike it out when it comes up short, because nobody can tell him whose it is. Adrian conceded in nine seconds that this is his trade's fault and then said the half that makes it true: a security is what a bidder pays for twice, he sold the district a name and did not price it, the price is a year, and he cannot give it back. **The fifth is not entered and there is no column for fractions in this basin and none was created.**
- **Nine refusals to read, and one departure still unspent**, and the intervals have gone up on use for the fifth time in eleven weeks.
- **A rotation that has turned once and one week of another rotation that ran out with nobody in it.**
- **A channel whose sill went, a pit with no loam in it, a bed of brine that goes back into the water in about nine days if six people all say no, and a second pan with a frame over it and nobody in it — and nobody joining any of it to anything.**

## The vocabulary, which is the volume's actual subject

**This district's problem was never a shortage of instruments. It was that nobody could say what a thing was for, and it solved that once, in a yard, in nine minutes, when somebody asked how a person is asked whether they will do a job.** Everything else in fifty chapters is that method applied to a year, and the sentences it produced are short and are in the book: a book the man who can be found builds is a book about him and not about the year; a mark may not carry a job; a security is a thing a stranger can go and look at; nothing said is not nothing happened; a struck row says a thing was entered and taken back and a row with a hole in it says a thing was never entered, and a hole in a row is a place a stranger will put something; a form is a thing people use and not a thing people read, and a blank at the top of a form is a place for the next person to write, and the next person is always a stranger; the sixth column is the only column worth having and the only one nobody can fill; a notice is handed to a person in a room and lived with by the house; a frame is oak and a frame is the last thing that happens to a person, and the channel is where the year comes out of, and a right to withdraw is a door in one direction and does not reach backwards; a person who withdraws has not consented to the season they withdrew from, and a person who has not been asked has not refused; a column of years is not a column of money and a year does not go twice; a year is not a price and there is no rate in this basin that turns one into the other; a name cannot be taken off a figure by the person it is put on; a guarantor of nothing is a door with a name on it; a board a person can read is a board a person can argue with, and a board in a hand that cannot be read is a board a person has to come and ask about; a board is a place a line of chalk goes and a person who writes on a board four times in a month is not using an instrument and is using a person; a rotation of four people is a lot with four bearers and the four are not bearers; a charter is four things that cannot be taken away one at a time; a document that says what it does not know is the first one a person can hold; a reason is for the people who were not in the room; a signature from a person who was not in the room is a signature and not a person who was in the room; a cart is not a finding; a copy is the document and the original is a room; a stage is not a moment, a stage is a length of time with a person standing in it and a date on it and somebody else's plan at the end of it; and a page that says a man is fine with a blank cell is a page about the man, and a page that says why the cell is blank is a page about the clerk.

## The mystery, and how far it got

**The question is what a public office that a signal can remember and a basin cannot find was for, and Volume 03 did not answer it and could not.**

The name is **THE STANDING OFFICE OF CROSS-DISTRICT PASSAGE, ESTABLISHED, AND NOT AT THIS PLACE.** It is in five places and in no register. Eleven trades cannot state what it did. Four answers were given in four rooms — a place a person could refuse, a place a name could be charged to, a place a cost could be put in front of before it was paid, and one man who did not know — and **all four are about carrying something somebody would not carry themselves, and they were not joined.** Four gaps in four books in four rooms were not joined to each other. Four documents mention no office. One sentence is in the column for things that are not findings. The question is in a public book dated the fifth of July. **The district's strongest instrument is a book that works if a person comes to the yard, and two valid certificates in a drawer thirty-four miles away say opposite things about the same sheet.**

**The refusals to join two things stand at thirteen and did not move in December. The registrar's own count stands at twelve and did not move in December.** A guild inspector of forty-four declined a fifth time in eleven weeks on the fourth of December, in writing. **Nobody asked the registrar why she holds two lines apart, and that was the ninth time in eleven weeks nobody has, and the block went near the question twice and was refused twice without a count moving, because a question asked in a lane is not a question asked in a room and a person who does not join a thing while she is writing it is not refusing it.**

## The two dates the district owns

**The thirty-first of March** is the day the name comes off the figure, the day the steward's address ends, the day the boundary's term ends and the day the season renews, **and the four of them are on one line in a charter and are not joined.**

**The first of April** is the first day a woman stands in a frame for a season that has not been certified, and **it is a date this district has not reached and is not a date this district is going to be told about.** Nobody stood a woman in a frame on the first of April, and no chapter in this volume contains a March scene or an April scene. **The volume's own method for a distant date is that a room says what it is for and the distance is a cost, and the volume's closing image is a ledger on a trestle table in a yard and not the first of April.**
