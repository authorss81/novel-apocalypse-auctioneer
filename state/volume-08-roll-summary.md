## 7. WHAT A VOLUME 09 WRITER MUST NOT RE-DERIVE, AND WHY

**THIS VOLUME TAUGHT FOUR THINGS AND ALL FOUR ARE ABOUT FIGURES AND NOT ABOUT PROSE, AND THE FIRST THREE ARE THE SAME THREE THE EARLIER VOLUMES TAUGHT, WHICH IS THE POINT.**
