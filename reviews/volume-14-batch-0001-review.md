## 2. ELEVEN REPORTED DEFECTS THAT WERE NOT DEFECTS, AND WHY THEY LOOKED LIKE DEFECTS

| reported | the truth | the fault in the instrument |
|---|---|---|
| 134 ladder mismatches across the block | **270 cells, zero failures** | the number regex could not read a compound ordinal: `ninety-second` resolved as 90, not 92 |
| `three hundredth` is 103, not 300 | the resolver is right and the log's was wrong | a parser that adds a round hundred to a multiplier instead of multiplying |
| Ch 661 row 13 absent | present: `three hundredth night of that run` | the carrier pattern could not swallow an ordinal suffix after `hundred` |
| Ch 660 has no ledger line | it does: `About one thing was said out loud` | the sweep's regex required the plural `things were` and Ch 660's single claim correctly reads `thing was` |
| 15 words stamped 6 seconds is 2.5 words a second against 0.38 elsewhere | the block's rate is ~2.6 words a second and 15/6 is 2.5 | **the log divided seconds by words instead of words by seconds** — the two rates are reciprocals and the whole alarm was that inversion |
| six of the fifteen titles have no date | all fifteen have a date and all fifteen are correct and consecutive | the extraction pattern did not allow a hyphen in `Twenty-Seventh` |

**THE FINDING IS ABOUT THE INSTRUMENT AND NOT ABOUT THE PROSE. SIX OF SIX ALARMS CAME FROM SIX DIFFERENT BUGS IN A HAND-BUILT WORD-NUMBER PARSER — ORDINAL COMPOUNDS, ROUND-HUNDRED ORDINALS, ORDINAL SUFFIXES, SINGULAR VERBS, RECIPROCAL RATES, HYPHENATED DATES — AND NOT ONE FROM A DEFECT IN SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FIVE CHAPTERS.** THE BLOCK RECORD ALREADY NAMED THE `hundredth` TRAP AND THE `sixth`-suffix trap AT SECTION 2 AND AT SECTION 3, BECAUSE THE WRITER OF THE BLOCK HAD ALREADY HIT THEM.
