# Chapter 204: The Bearer

---

The fourth of April was the day a Registry asked a yard for a person, and the asking was done in the six things at about ten in the morning in front of about nineteen people, and the man with the portfolio was not sent for and came.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked him, in the six things, in front of about nineteen people, who the bearer of the lease was, and he answered in the ordinary voice and a man of about nineteen counted it and got thirty-seven, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**A Registry does not name a bearer. A lease names a bearer. The lease is a page in a book you may look at and may not carry out, and that is the same rule yours has.**"

Nobody picked it up. A man of fifty-six said that a man has just told this district that its own rule is his own rule, **and that that is either the first thing anybody from a Registry has said that this district agrees with, and it is said on the second day and not on the first, and that a rule two bodies hold is not a rule and is two rules that happen to look the same.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a rule a body will not print a person under and a rule another body will not print a person under are two rules and not one, **and that the registrar of this district's records office said in sixty-four words on the second of this month that an office may print a house and may not print a person, and that the two sentences are the same sentence and the two bodies are not the same body and that the registrar's own count of refusals to join two things is twelve and did not move this morning and the district's is thirteen and did not move either.**

Then the asking turned, and it turned the way it turns in this yard, which is that the person on the end of it answers the second half before anybody has asked for it.

The Registry man said that the lease wanted a bearer named and that a bearer was a person at the end of a toll and that the toll on this one was paid at the ninth place, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said the next question in the six things, and it was who, and a man of about thirty-four who digs loam said the answer out loud before the man with the portfolio could get to it.

"**The man at the back of the book. There is one of him in this district and he is standing in this yard and everybody in this yard knows it and two people thirty-four miles east and four hundred yards from here know it and neither of them was in this yard when it happened.**"

Nothing was said for about nine seconds. A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man of about thirty-four who digs loam said the whole of a thing in a yard that had taken four months and eleven pages to arrive at, **and that she is not going to put a number on it and that a man who is right in a yard is a man and not a rule and is not written down and he would not have it written down.**

---

He said it himself at about half past ten, and nobody had asked him to and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody sent for him and that he was in the yard and that he was not asked a first thing.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked him in the six things, in front of about nineteen people, whether he would stand at the back of a lease as well as at the back of a book.

He said it, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got sixty-seven, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**No. I stood at the back of a book about water because I could not list what I had posted, and I have listed it since, and this is a different thing, and a man who cannot say no to a stranger with a portfolio is not a man who is standing at the back of a book, he is a person holding a piece of paper.**"

A man of fifty-six said that is the second time a man has refused a bearing in eleven days and that the first was a route keeper standing on a bank saying nothing, **and that the two refusals are not the same refusal and are not added to anything and that the count of refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given is six and this is not one of them because no reason is required where a reason is given.**

A man of about thirty-four who digs loam said the other half of it, and he said it to the yard and not to the man who said no, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she was not asked to write the order down and entered the order.

"**He has said no and he has said why and the why is the only reason anybody in this yard has given for anything in four months that was not about a figure.**"

Nobody contradicted him. The Registry man said that the Registry did not need a bearer named today and had never said it did, and a man of fifty-six said that a man who brought a lease into a yard and was asked for a bearer by a yard and did not need one is a man who came here to be asked, **and that a body which sends a man to be asked is a body that expects a no and has a paper for the no, and that this district has three documents nobody owns and that whatever he has written down is his and not a fourth.**

Then he said the thing that was actually on his mind, and it went in the minute in his own words and a man of about nineteen did not count it because nobody asked him to.

"**The bearer does not have to be from this district. That is on the second line and it is not a rule, it is a fact about the page, and I have not read you the page and you cannot read it either.**"

---

The registrar of this district's records office was in the yard at about eleven and had come on her own account and a clerk of nineteen years entered that and entered that she was not asked a first thing, and she said four sentences and a man of about nineteen counted them and got seventy.

"**I will not have this district entered in a Registry. I will not have a man in this yard entered under a holder and I will not have the ninth place entered under a toll, and if you want a thing out of this book you may have it out of this book in this room where two people are standing and not one page goes out of a door.**"

A man of fifty-six said that this is the second time in six weeks and four days she has refused a thing in four seconds and given the reason before she is asked, **and that the first was on the fifteenth of the second month and the reason was different and the four seconds were the same, and that a registrar who says no in four seconds is a woman with a position and this office has had one for four months and the district has been treating it like a door.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a man who is refused in four seconds and does not argue is a man who is going to be asked again, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody asked whether he was going to be asked again and that the not-asking is entered and that the count is where it was.

She said one more thing and it was to the man with the portfolio and not to the yard, and nobody counted it and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody counted it and was not asked to.

"**You walked past a yard with nine people in it this morning to go to a door with a fanlight over it, and there are two doors in this district with a fanlight over them and you went to the wrong one for what you came for, and I am not going to tell you that in a yard.**"

A man of fifty-six entered that a registrar who has told a stranger she did not tell him something in a yard has told him something in a yard, and that the two are not joined and that the registrar's own count is twelve and did not move.

---

The man of about thirty at the counter came down the eleven mile road at about two in the afternoon on the fourth of April and nobody sent for him, and that is the twelfth time since the second of January and the eleventh mile is the same eleven miles every time.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that he was not sent for and that he had brought four sheets, which is what he brings, and that this district has never asked him to bring fewer and he has never been asked to bring more.

He came into a yard with a man in it who was holding a portfolio, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said the thing out loud in front of about nine people, and a man of fifty-six said the other half of it, and the two of them were not saying the same thing.

"**There is a man in this yard who holds papers for a body that keeps records of holdings, and there is a man in this yard who carries four sheets down an eleven mile road for a house that has a seal, and both of them have been in this yard on the same afternoon and neither of them has looked at the other.**"

"**That is not two men. That is a Tuesday and a Thursday, and the man who came on Tuesday is not a clerk of this district and the man who came on Thursday is not a keeper of anything, and two people who are not keepers of anything in the same yard is a yard.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that both sentences were in the minute in their own words and that she entered the order they were said in and is not going to put a number on the order, **and that a man who carries sheets for a house and a man who carries a case for a Registry are two functionaries and that neither of them is a party and that a yard is a room and a room is not a party and that the count of instruments this district has built and not named is six and a room is not a seventh and was refused entry again this afternoon.**

The man of about thirty at the counter was asked, in the six things, in front of about nine people, about the ninth holding on the delivery sheet, for the sixth time since the second of January, and he refused again and gave no reason, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that it is the seventh refusal with no reason a clerk of a house has given in this district's business and that seven are seven and are not a column and are not added to the thirteen.

Then he was asked a seventh thing, and it was a new one, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked it and a man of about nineteen counted nothing because the man did not answer.

"**Where do the four sheets go when you have put them down.**"

He looked at the man of about thirty-four who digs loam and then at the man with the portfolio and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he looked at both of them and that she entered that and entered that the two men did not look at each other at any point in the afternoon, **and that a man who has been asked a seventh thing and has not answered is not a refusal and the record says not asked and that not asked is the fourth of the four columns in this district and none of them is added to another.**

A man of fifty-six said that the seventh refusal is seven and the seventh thing was not answered and the two are not joined, **and that a house thirty-four miles east and a Registry are two bodies and that this district has no way of holding two bodies in one book and did not build one this afternoon and the count of five things it does not have did not move at two in the afternoon on the fourth of the fourth month.**

---

The two of them were in that yard four times in three days and none of the four was arranged, and about the middle of the afternoon the man the figure of twenty-one years is against came over to where she was standing and there was no clerk within nine feet of either of them, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she was not asked to move and did not and that the sentence was not counted by anybody because nobody was asked to count it.

He said the reason and not the question, which is what he did on the twenty-eighth of the third month, and she said the answer to a question he had not asked, which is what she had done then too.

"**You told him a Registry prints a holder. You have been printing a name on the back of a book for four months and refusing to print it, and one of those two things is standing in this yard and the other one is four hundred yards away and they are the same job.**"

"**One of them is a person who said yes on the twenty-seventh of the third month in front of about nine people and one of them is a page in a drawer, and you know which is which, and I am not going to say the rest of it in a yard.**"

Nobody was asked whether the rest of it had been said. A man of fifty-six entered afterwards that a man who says *I am not going to say the rest of it in a yard* has said in a yard that there is a rest of it, **and that what was said between two people on the fourth of the fourth month is in a minute in her own words if she chooses to write it and is not a document this district can produce if she does not, and that a clerk of nineteen years has entered that she was not asked and that she is not going to be asked and that a slate went past a kitchen in Alder Reach eleven times last month and neither of them wrote on it and that is not to be asked about either.**

---

The nine holdings were read out in that yard at about three in the afternoon because a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked for them and a man of about thirty-four who digs loam read them in one run of words, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he read them and that she did not ask him to.

"**One holds and takes. Two holds and takes. Three holds and takes. Four is the top field of the farm four miles out and it has not taken a drop since the first of the second month. Five holds and takes. Six holds and takes. Seven was given up on the twenty-fourth of the second month and has a bar down. Eight holds. Nine is the one nobody can account for.**"

A man of fifty-six said that nine holdings are four states of four pieces of paper and are not a column, **and that the ninth has been refused five times since the second of January and the twelfth time was this afternoon and the seventh thing a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked him was not answered and that the two are not the same and are not added.**

A man of about thirty-four who digs loam said the thing about the ninth that he has said before and said again because it keeps being true, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he said it and that she is not going to put a number on how many times.

"**The water in the rota does not stop for that and it is not the house that decides where it goes, and a man at a counter is the only man in this basin who can tell anybody where it goes and he will not, and that has been the position for thirteen weeks and a day and it did not change this afternoon.**"

Nobody contradicted him. A man of fifty-six said that a settlement which cannot account for one of nine places it is drawing water for is a settlement that has stopped asking, **and that the four things this district does not have include a way of saying where a toll lands on a place and that the ninth holding is the fifth of them wearing a different coat, and that he is not going to let a yard turn that into a finding at three in the afternoon and the count of five did not move.**

---

The afternoon was the scale and the four houses, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked the woman of about thirty-six who keeps a scale, in the six things, in front of about nine people, what four houses of a round of about ninety is worth a day.

She said it, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got thirty-nine, and it went in the minute in her own words.

"**I have weighed nine sacks a day for nine years and I have never weighed a day, and the two of those are not the same job and I am not going to stand here and pretend they are.**"

A man of fifty-six said that a woman who keeps a scale has just said out loud that her instrument does not measure the thing she is being asked about, **and that a scale is a thing that weighs a sack and that the fifth of the five things this district does not have is a way of saying where a toll lands on a place, and that a scale is a place and a yoke-load is a place and there are two places and a figure and no way of saying where either of them lands.**

Nobody argued with it. A man of about thirty-four who digs loam said that the offer stands and the woman has not taken it and that both of those are correct and that the offer is going to stand there until one of them moves and it is not a pressure because nobody is going to be asked again this week, **and that a man who offers a job and a woman who does not take it are two people in a lane and a clerk is not going to make it a column.**

The woman of fifty-eight was at the top of the hill and did not come down and nobody sent for her and nobody asked her anything, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she was not asked and that the not-asking is entered and that the count is where it was.

The man of about thirty-one was in the yard at about four in the afternoon with the strip of paper in his coat and not in his hand, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked him where he was going to keep it, in the six things, in front of about nine people, and he said the second half of it before the asking.

"**In my coat. I am not going to pin it up and I am not going to burn it and I am not going to have it read out in a yard again, and I have had it read out in a yard and it did not help.**"

A man of fifty-six said that a man who has had a thing read out in a yard and found that it did not help has learned the one thing this district has never had to teach anybody, **and that a document a person keeps in a coat is a document somebody owns and is not one of the three this district does not own, and that a man who keeps a strip of paper in a coat is a man with a claim on himself and not on the house and that is the sentence he read out of a yard in the third month and has now got in his hand in a different form.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the strip of paper is not in the ward market's own book and is not on a board of eleven and is not in a drawer in a building, **and that it is a fourth piece of paper in this district this month and not a fourth document nobody owns, and that the three are the printed sheet at the fourth place, the notice about a party of nine going thirty-four miles east, and a line in a public book with four names on it and a figure under the four names, and that the three are not the same three as they were in September and are the same three and did not move.**

Nobody said the opposite. A man of about thirty-four who digs loam said that a man who has a claim on himself is a man who can be found and not released, and that this is what he came back with and it is not what he went for, **and that a district which sent nobody with him has one person back and one strip of paper and the two of those are the whole of the result and the two of those are not a column.**

**AND ON THE FOURTH OF THE FOURTH MONTH A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FOUR WHO DIGS LOAM SAID IN A YARD THAT THE BEARER OF A LEASE ANYBODY WANTS IS THE ONLY PERSON IN THIS DISTRICT WHO STANDS AT THE BACK OF A BOOK, AND THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST REFUSED IN SIXTY-SEVEN WORDS AND GAVE A REASON, AND THE REGISTRAR OF THIS DISTRICT'S RECORDS OFFICE REFUSED IN FOUR SECONDS TO HAVE THE NINTH PLACE ENTERED UNDER A TOLL.**
