# Chapter 220: Nine Days

---

The twenty-first of April was a day with nothing on the printed sheet and nothing in the road house's book, and about nineteen people were in the yard of Lot Seventeen at about ten in the morning, and the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing had been up the only road in this district that climbs since about five yesterday afternoon and had come down it in the dark and was in the yard and had not slept.

A man of about thirty-four who digs loam gave the figures out loud and a man of fifty-six read the board out afterwards and got both of them right.

"**A hundred and eighteen days on the board and four hundred and thirty-four days on the train, and the days nobody has entered since the twenty-fourth of November stand at a hundred and forty-eight, and the last day of this month is nine days off and there are two things falling due on it and this district can answer neither.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a letter from a body that is not a place asks for a man by the last day of this month, and that a man from a Registry said in this yard on the fifth of this month that he has to be at a place with no name on it on that same day and there is nobody at the place but him, **and that a man at a counter has refused the ninth holding seven times since the second of January and a body that prints a column headed OPEN has never once been asked to send anything, and that the refusals to join two things are thirteen and the registrar's own count is twelve and neither of them moved at about ten in the morning on the twenty-first of the fourth month, and that a second refusal of the same join is the same refusal and there have been four in twelve days and they are one.**

---

The man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said what he had gone up for, and nobody had asked him, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody sent for him and that he was not asked a first thing.

"**The channel at the head of the top field has about a foot of water in it and it has been dry since the first of the second month, and the fourth holding took a drop sometime in the night, and there was no dray on that road and no yoke on it and no man in this district but me.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the fourth holding on the delivery sheet at the second reservoir is the top field of a farm four miles out and that it had not taken a drop since the first of the second month, and that it has taken one, **and that a man of about thirty-four who digs loam opened a sluice for that holding on the night of the thirteenth of last month and that nobody has asked him to open it again and that he says he has not, and that a road somebody opened in the night nine miles off on the twenty-second of the second month is a third thing and the two are not joined.**

The man of about thirty-four who digs loam said it out loud before anybody could find it, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got eighty-two, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**That is the third time this month somebody has put a wet thing next to a night nine miles off and every one of the three was put there by a man with a bucket, a pail or a channel, and a third time is a habit, and I have said that in this yard two days running and I am going to keep saying it until somebody in here can tell me which of the three of them is a keeper.**"

A man of fifty-six said that nobody in this district can name a keeper and that a column headed with a word and not a name has carried a house nine times this month and a person no times, **and that a man of about thirty at a counter said on the ninth of this month that his house has a word for the man who keeps one and a word for the night he turns it, and that a man who keeps a road said the same two words this month out of a different house and added that neither of them is on any page, and that a district with two words for a man and no name for him has not got a man and has got a word and a word is not a figure and is not a schedule and is not a column and is not a document.**

---

The man of about thirty at a counter did not come, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he did not come and that this district has no schedule for him and has never had one, **and that a man who does not come has not refused and cannot be counted either way and the record says not asked, and that his fifteenth time down that eleven mile road was the fifteenth of this month and the sixteenth is not due from anybody because this yard has never once given a man of about thirty a day to be here on.**

The woman of fifty-eight was in the yard at about half past eleven and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked her, in the six things, in front of about nineteen people, whether she had started the eleven, and she said yes in four seconds and gave the reason, and a man of about nineteen counted the reason and got thirty-three, and it went in the minute in her own words.

"**I started at seven this morning and I will do eleven and I have not been asked what it is worth today and that is the first morning anybody has left it alone.**"

Nobody contradicted her. A man of fifty-six said that the fifth of the five things this district does not have is a way to pay a person who is not in a household, and that the count of things this district does not have is five and did not move at about half past eleven in the morning on the twenty-first of the fourth month, **and that there is still no rate in this basin that turns a year into coppers and there never has been one, and that a woman who says the whole of what she knows about a job is a woman who has been asked for it four times in a month and has priced it not one of those times.**

Then the registrar of this district's records office said one thing in the yard at about one in the afternoon and it was not asked for and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody asked for it.

"**That book now holds eight printed days off a stranger's sheet and nine printed nights off a body's own paper, and one of those two is a word where the other is a day, and the twentieth of this month is in one of them and not in the other, and a person may look at both of them in this office and may not carry either of them out of it.**"

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said out loud that a body which writes to a village is a body that knows the village has something, and that a registrar who has put the day a body printed into a book four hundred yards from a yard has made that body findable, **and that a book a stranger can walk up to is a door and this district built it and told nineteen people it was the best instrument in the district, and that on the second of this month a stranger walked past a yard with nine people in it to go to a door with a fanlight over it, and that the door that got used this month was not the one anybody was proud of, and that the count of instruments built and not named is six and the count of documents nobody owns is three and neither of them moved today.**

---

The man of about twenty-three who sells nothing and copies for nothing was in that yard at about half past two and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked him, in the six things, in front of about nineteen people, whether he would copy the standing orders, and he did the thing before anybody had finished asking.

He copied the nine rows and the column heading and the column headed KEEPER and the nine nights onto the back of a sheet of his own buying, in his own hand, in about forty minutes, and he gave the copy to a clerk of nineteen years and said twelve words, and a man of about nineteen counted them and got twelve.

"**Nobody took it. I gave it away. Put both of those down.**"

A clerk of nineteen years read the copy against the sheet and got nothing wrong, which is the rule of the counter, and entered that the copy is a copy and that a copy a man keeps in a coat is a copy and is not a fourth document nobody owns, **and that this district now holds two copies of two different documents in two different rooms four hundred yards apart, one of them made in fifty minutes on the sixth of this month and one of them made in forty minutes today, and that the two of them are copies and not documents nobody owns and the count of three did not move at about half past three in the afternoon on the twenty-first of the fourth month.**

A man of fifty-six said that the man is owed eleven coppers a week and that the district is not going to pay him and has said so four times for the same reason, **and that a man who has now made two copies for nothing in a fortnight and given both of them away is the reason a printed day can be found in two rooms in this basin, and that a reason is not an instrument and the six instruments this district has built and not named did not become a seventh because of him today.**

A man of about thirty-four who digs loam said that the man who copies is now the only person in this district who can put a body else's mistake back in front of that body, **and that a copy a man made in forty minutes is a thing four people in this yard can check, and that a man who is not paid for checking is not a clerk and a clerk of nineteen years is a clerk and there are two of those in this basin now and one of them is nineteen.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said at about four in the afternoon that a man of about twenty-three who copies for nothing had said twelve words in a yard and that a man of fifty-six who gives the figures out loud had never once said anything as short, **and that the district has two men in it who can be relied on to be brief and it has no money for either of them and it has nine days to answer a letter that asks for a man.**

The lane four miles down from the lower terrace had about four people on it at about half past four in the afternoon and nobody had sent anybody, and the man of about fifty-five who keeps that building was on his own step with a pail in his hand and was not mending the tank, and the tank was within a hand's breadth of its lip and the crack was running.

He was not asked anything and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he was not asked a first thing and that the record says not asked.

The man of about thirty-four who digs loam had walked down the lane and was not sent for, and he said the last of it there, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got eighty-six, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**The ninth of the nine was yesterday, and this district has got a pail, a crack in a tank and a channel with a foot of water in it at the top of a field, and not one of those three is a keeper, and the lease has to be answered in nine days at a place with no name on it, and there is nobody in this district who can get there and nobody in this district has ever met a man who keeps one.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that he said it and that nobody wrote it down, because a man of fifty-six is not a clerk and has said so about himself eleven times in four months, and that the asking was entered on the eleventh of this month and again on the nineteenth and this is the third time, **and that the conditions with no end on it are four and the boards are five and the lines full are six and the seventh line at the ninth place is ruled and has nothing on it, and that the columns of not-askings are four and the protected things are five and there is still no rate in this basin that turns a year into coppers, and that none of the seven of those moved at about four in the afternoon on the twenty-first of the fourth month.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-FIRST OF THE FOURTH MONTH THE FOURTH HOLDING ON THE DELIVERY SHEET TOOK ITS FIRST DROP SINCE THE FIRST OF THE SECOND MONTH, A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY AT A COUNTER DID NOT COME DOWN AN ELEVEN MILE ROAD AND WAS NOT ASKED, THE REGISTRAR OF THIS DISTRICT'S RECORDS OFFICE SAID OUT LOUD THAT HER BOOK NOW HOLDS EIGHT DAYS OFF A STRANGER'S SHEET AND NINE NIGHTS OFF A BODY'S OWN PAPER, AND THE LAST DAY OF THE FOURTH MONTH IS NINE DAYS OFF.**
