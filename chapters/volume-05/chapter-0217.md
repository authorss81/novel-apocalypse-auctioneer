# Chapter 217: The Road Went Up And Came Back

---

The eighteenth of April was a day with nothing on the printed sheet and nothing in the road house's book, and about nineteen people were in the yard of Lot Seventeen at about ten in the morning, and a man of about thirty-four who digs loam had been up the only road in this district that climbs and had come back down it before any of them arrived.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing gave the figures and a man of fifty-six read the board out after him and got both of them right, and neither of them had been up the road.

"**A hundred and fifteen days on the board and four hundred and thirty-one days on the train, and the days nobody has entered since the twenty-fourth of November stand at a hundred and forty-five, and a man walked up a road at about six in the morning and came down it at about ten and nobody sent for him and nobody has asked him why.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man of about thirty-four who digs loam went up the only road in this district that climbs on his own account, and that the fourth holding on the delivery sheet at the second reservoir is the top field of a farm four miles out on that road, **and that the fourth holding has not taken a drop since the first of the second month, which is seventy-six days, and that a channel at the head of that field has been dry since the same day, and that a man who digs loam opened a sluice for that holding on the night of the thirteenth of the month before last, which is thirty-six days ago, and that nobody has asked him to do it again and that he has not done it again.**

---

He said it himself at about ten and nobody asked him and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody sent for him and that he was not asked a first thing.

"**I went up a road with a climb on it and I stood at the bottom of two hundred yards of soft ground and I looked at a field and I came back down, and I have not got a finding, and a road is a place and a place is not an instrument, and I am not going to go up it again on a Friday either.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man said a day of the week and that no weekday is attached to any date in this district, and that she is not going to correct him because he is not wrong about the road.

A man of fifty-six said that a man who walks up a road and comes back down with nothing is doing the only thing anybody can do with a place nobody can measure, **and that the fifth of the five things this district does not have is a way to pay a person who is not in a household, and that a man who spent four hours of a morning looking at a field has done four hours of unpaid work for a figure he does not have, and that the sixth of the six instruments this district built and not named is not a man on a road and is not a seventh.**

Then a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said the other half of it and nobody picked it up.

"**A man who goes up a road and comes back with nothing has told this yard that the fourth holding has not taken a drop and the channel at the head of it is dry, and a stranger went up the same road on the eleventh with a yoke on a cart and came down on the fourteenth, and a road somebody opened in the night nine miles off on the twenty-second of the second month is on that road's own list and not on ours. I am refusing the join a third time and it is the same join and it does not make a fourteenth anything.**"

A man of fifty-six said that a third refusal of the same join is the same refusal and not a new one, **and that the refusals to join two things are thirteen and the registrar's own count is twelve and neither of them moved at about half past ten in the morning on the eighteenth of the fourth month, and that the man who digs loam has now refused the same join in three yards in twelve days and that a man who says the same true thing three times is not a man who has run out of things to say, and that this time the people he has run out of are the only ones.**

---

The man of about thirty-one was in that yard and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked him, in the six things, in front of about nineteen people, where the road went, and he gave the honest answer and a man of about nineteen counted it and got seventy, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**I am not going to say where the road goes. I said on the seventh of this month that I did not know how to find a place I could not name, and I have not found out, and a man who is asked a thing he cannot answer is a man who is not going to make one up at ten in the morning in front of nineteen people.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that he gave a reason and that a refusal with a reason is not one of the refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given, **and that the count of eight did not move this morning, and that a man who mends a dray is not a clerk of a house and is not a bearer of a road and is not one of the five things this district does not have and was refused entry into all three of those lists in about four seconds by a clerk who was not asked to refuse it.**

She was in the yard at about half past ten and had come on her own account, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that and entered that she was not asked a first thing, and that at about twenty to twelve she asked him for one thing in a lane behind the forge end off Salt Row where there was no clerk within nine feet of either of them and nobody counted either of what was said.

**She said she wanted the page of the ward market's own book where the figure of twenty-one years came off on the thirty-first of the month before last, in his own hand, to put in her own book, and that she was asking him for it and was not telling him why.**

**He gave her the other side of the same leaf and said that the figure is not on it and the date is on it and his own hand is on it and there is nothing written under it and he has not written anything under it, and that it is a page and it is not the one she asked for.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered afterwards that two people said two things in a lane on the eighteenth of this month and that nobody counted them and that the not-counting is entered, **and that a man of fifty-six said that a man who gives a woman a different page from the one she asked for has told her what a page says and has not told her what she asked about, and that those are two different things and the registrar's own count of refusals to join two things is twelve and did not move, and that the two of them have been in the same yard at the same hour on purpose nine times since the eighteenth of December and this was not one of the nine and is not going to be entered as one.**

A man of fifty-six said afterwards that he had been in this district for a long time and that a woman who asks for a page and gets a different page has not been refused and has not been given what she asked for, **and that a district which spends a month finding out that a yard is a door has not worked out what an office is yet, and that this office has been a room since the fourth of December and nobody in this yard has been inside it.**

---

The man of about thirty-six who keeps a scale was in that yard and had not been sent for, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked him, in the six things, in front of about nineteen people, what eleven houses of water weighs.

He gave the honest answer and a man of about nineteen counted it and got fifty-two, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**I can weigh the water in both buckets and I have weighed water since I was twenty and I cannot weigh the walk up the bank and the walk down it eleven times, and those are two different things and a man who sells the first one is selling the wrong one.**"

A man of fifty-six said that a man who keeps a scale has now told this yard three times in fourteen days that his instrument does not measure the thing he is being asked about, **and that the six instruments this district has built and not named are a rehearsal, a platform, a rail, a correction, a question and a security, and that a scale is not a seventh of them, and that a man who is corrected by his own instrument is a man and not a finding and the six did not move at about half past one in the afternoon on the eighteenth of the fourth month.**

A man of about thirty-four who digs loam said that the man who keeps a scale is the only person in this district who has ever been corrected by his own question and that he said so out loud on the fifth of this month, **and that a man who sells the wrong one on purpose is doing the district a kindness and is not a party to anything, and that the fifth of the five things this district does not have is a way to pay a person who is not in a household and a man who cannot weigh a day is the reason it is on the list.**

The woman of about thirty-four who keeps a goat at the third house up the lane from the scale was in that yard at about two in the afternoon and was not sent for and was not asked anything, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she was there and that she is in a pool of tolls with her name not written on anything and has been since the thirtieth of the month before last, **and that nobody has asked her about the pool and that the not-asking is entered and that it is not one of the four columns and is not a fifth and she did not rule one this afternoon and is not going to.**

A man of fifty-six said that a woman who watched a dray go up a road in the dark on the eleventh of this month and has not been asked one thing since is a woman this district has been getting for nothing for a month, **and that she is not owed eleven coppers a week because she is not copying anything and she is not owed nothing because she stood in the cold, and that those are the two halves of the fifth of the five and that a district which cannot tell them apart does not have a way to pay a person who is not in a household and never has.**

She said one thing to nobody in particular and nobody counted it because nobody was asked to, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody counted it and that she was not asked to.

"**I stood in the cold because I was awake. That is all it is and I am not going to have it made into anything in a yard.**"

A man of fifty-six said that a woman who has just told a yard that it must not make a thing of her standing in a doorway has done the same thing the man who digs loam does every morning of his life, **and that a clerk of nineteen years entered that and entered that it is the third time in this district a person has said out loud that they would rather not be made into a finding, and that the first two were a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing about two buckets and a man of about thirty-six who keeps a scale about nine sacks, and that three is not a column.**

---

The man of about thirty-one is in that yard and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked him, in the six things, in front of about nineteen people, whether the man who gave him the sheet said anything to him about a man, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that it was a new question and not a second asking of the sixth of the six, which was who he had come for.

He said the honest answer and a man of about nineteen counted it and got sixty-one, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**He did not say a word to me about a man and he did not say a word to me about water and he put a folded sheet under a yoke and pointed at it and went back up the hill, and I am not going to make a man out of a man who pointed at a piece of paper.**"

A man of fifty-six said that a man who was handed a document on a hill and not asked a question about it has been handed a document and not a job, and that a job taken is still a job and the person it was taken from is still the person who does it, **and that the person it was taken from this time is not a woman on a bank but anybody who has to answer a body by the last day of this month, and that the two of those are not joined and the refusals to join two things are thirteen and the registrar's own count is twelve and neither of them moved at about three in the afternoon on the eighteenth of the fourth month.**

The day went. A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing read the standing orders out again at about four in the afternoon for four people who had not been in the yard this morning, and got nothing wrong, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nine printed nights have now been read out in this yard twice and that the count of lines full on a board of eleven in this district is six and the seventh line at the ninth place is ruled and has nothing on it, and that a night a body prints is a day and a day is not a finding.**

**AND ON THE EIGHTEENTH OF THE FOURTH MONTH A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FOUR WHO DIGS LOAM WALKED UP THE ONLY ROAD IN THIS DISTRICT THAT CLIMBS AND CAME BACK DOWN IT WITH NO FINDING AND REFUSED THE SAME JOIN FOR THE THIRD TIME IN TWELVE DAYS, A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE REFUSED TO SAY WHERE THE ROAD GOES AND GAVE A REASON, AND A WOMAN ASKED FOR A PAGE AND WAS GIVEN A DIFFERENT ONE.**
