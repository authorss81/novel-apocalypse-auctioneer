# Chapter 216: Eleven Houses

---

The seventeenth of April was a day with nothing on the printed sheet and nothing in the road house's book, and about nineteen people were in the yard of Lot Seventeen at about ten in the morning, and the woman of fifty-eight was in it before the yard was asked for anything.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing gave the figures out loud and a man of fifty-six read the board out afterwards and got both of them right.

"**A hundred and fourteen days on the board and four hundred and thirty days on the train, and the days nobody has entered since the twenty-fourth of November stand at a hundred and forty-four, and a woman came into this yard at about half past nine and nobody sent for her and nobody has said a word to her yet.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the round of about ninety houses ended on the twenty-fourth of last month because a water came down a channel, and that this district took it off her and did not ask her and has had nothing to give her in place of it since, **and that a man of about thirty-six who keeps a scale offered her four houses of it on the last day of last month and has not repeated the offer and says four houses out of about ninety is not a round and is not going to become one.**

---

The man of about thirty-four who digs loam said it at about ten and he said it before anybody had asked him for it, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she was not asked and that she is not going to put a number on it.

"**This yard is going to give the woman of fifty-eight eleven houses of the round this morning and it is going to ask her first, and I want the asking written down as the whole of the reason, and not the eleven.**"

A man of fifty-six said that this yard took a job off a woman in the third month without asking her and spent the twenty-four days afterwards calling the not-asking a column, **and that a yard that about nineteen people can stand in and decide something about a woman who is not in it is a door, and that the door which got used last month was not the one anybody was proud of, and that this morning the same yard is deciding something about the same woman in the same room and the whole of the difference is that she is standing in it.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that this is the first thing this district has done in four months that it got right by accident and by a man saying it out loud, **and that a yard is not an instrument and is not one of the six and that the six are a rehearsal, a platform, a rail, a correction, a question and a security, and that the fact that a yard is the right shape for a thing is not the same as a yard being the right instrument, and she did not rule a seventh and is not going to.**

Then a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked her, in the six things, in front of about nineteen people, whether she would take eleven houses of her round, and she said yes in four seconds and gave the reason, and a man of about nineteen counted the reason and got sixty, and it went in the minute in her own words.

"**Because I said on the first of this month that I cannot say what a day of it is worth and I have not said it once this morning, and eleven of the ninety still have a door on them, and I am not going to be thanked by a yard in front of all of you for being sensible.**"

Nobody picked it up. A man of fifty-six said that a woman who has refused to price a thing since the first of this month and then takes it in four seconds without pricing it has not changed her mind and has not stopped asking, **and that the fifth of the five things this district does not have is a way to pay a person who is not in a household, and that nobody has moved that today and nobody is going to, and that eleven houses is a job and a job is not a figure and cannot be put on a page and is not going to be.**

---

The panel came at about eleven and nobody had asked for it, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody in that yard asked for it and that it appeared in the same place the others have appeared and that she wrote down what it said and not what anybody thought of it.

> **A ROUND IS A JOB AND NOT A FIGURE. ELEVEN IS NOT A ROUND. A ROUND THAT IS SMALLER IS NOT A ROUND.**
>
> **A FIGURE THAT PRICES ONE DAY OF A PERSON WHO IS NOT IN A HOUSEHOLD IS A WAGE, AND A WAGE IS A FIGURE, AND A DISTRICT WITH NO FIGURE HAS NO WAGE.**
>
> **A PAGE THAT NAMES A HOUSE NAMES A THING THAT CAN BE ASKED. A PAGE THAT NAMES A PERSON NAMES A THING THAT CAN BE FOUND. A PAGE THAT NAMES NEITHER NAMES A THING THAT WAS NOT WRITTEN.**

The woman of fifty-eight disagreed with the first line of it, and she disagreed out loud, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got fifty-one, and it went in the minute in her own words.

"**I am not a round and I never said I was, and you cannot make a smaller one of me and call it the same thing, and if you want to call it eleven houses then call it eleven houses and stop pretending you have given me back what I had.**"

Nothing was said for about nine seconds. A clerk of nineteen years entered that this is the third time in this volume that a person has disagreed with one of these out loud and in front of people, **and that the first was a man of about thirty-four who digs loam on the fifth of this month and the second was a man of about thirty-one on the seventh, and that one of these came on the tenth of this month and nobody in this yard disagreed with it, and that she is not going to count that as a third, and that the three of them are not the same disagreement and she is not going to make them one.**

A man of fifty-six said that a rule a person the rule is about disagrees with is not thereby true and is not thereby false, **and that the count of documents nobody owns in this district is three and did not move at about a quarter past eleven in the morning on the seventeenth of the fourth month, and that a panel is a rule and a rule is not a document and is not a fourth and is not one of the six instruments this district built and not named.**

The man of about thirty-four who digs loam did not disagree with it, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he was in the yard and that he did not and that she was not asked whether he was going to.

---

The man of about thirty-four who digs loam said the plainest thing in the yard at about half past eleven and he said it flat and in the ordinary voice, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got ninety-two, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**Nobody in this basin can say what one day of eleven houses is worth, and that is not a hole in this district's book, that is the fifth of the five things it does not have, and it has not moved in here since the twenty-ninth of last month, and a district that hands a woman a job on the understanding that nobody knows what the job costs has not handed her a job, it has handed her a second thing she cannot refuse without standing in a yard and saying so.**"

Nobody contradicted him. A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the man of about thirty-six who keeps a scale offered four houses on the last day of last month and has not repeated it, **and that eleven is not four and four is not eleven and nobody in this yard is going to add them together and call it fifteen, and that the four are still standing and the man said once and said he would not say it twice and this district is not going to make him a third time.**

A man of about thirty-six who keeps a scale was in that yard and had not been sent for and was not asked anything, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he was there and that the record says not asked, **and that the four columns of not-askings in this district are four and none of them has been ruled since the second of January and she did not rule one this morning and is not going to.**

He was asked, in the six things, in front of about nineteen people, what four houses of a round is worth a day, and he said the same thing he said on the fourth of this month and a man of about nineteen counted it and got forty-three, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**I have weighed nine sacks a day for nine years and I have never weighed a day, and four is not eleven and eleven is not ninety and nobody in this yard is going to make me weigh one of them this afternoon.**"

A man of about thirty-four who digs loam said that a man who keeps a scale has now been asked the same question twice in thirteen days and has given the same answer twice, **and that the six instruments this district has built and not named are a rehearsal, a platform, a rail, a correction, a question and a security, and that a man who weighs sacks for a living is a man and not a seventh of them, and that the six did not move at about ten past twelve in the afternoon on the seventeenth of the fourth month, and that a scale is a place and a place is not an instrument and that has been said twice this month now and I am counting it because nobody else is.**

Then a clerk of nineteen years did the arithmetic out loud and read it back, and a man of about fifty-six said she had never done a sum in a yard before today and asked her why, and she said that a clerk who reads words all day ought to be able to do a sum out loud.

"**ABOUT NINETY, OF WHICH ELEVEN, WHICH LEAVES ABOUT SEVENTY-NINE. AND ABOUT NINETY IS NOT A FIGURE, SO THAT IS NOT A SUBTRACTION, AND THE ROUND IS NOT COMING BACK.**"

A man of fifty-six said that a figure with a word in front of it is a figure this district has had for four months and has never once been able to hand to anybody, **and that the count of things this district does not have is five and did not move at about twenty past twelve in the afternoon on the seventeenth of the fourth month, and that a round of about seventy-nine houses that this district has taken off a woman and is not giving back is not a round and is not a wage and is not one of the four conditions with no end on it, and those are four and she did not rule a fifth.**

---

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked her, in the six things, in front of about nineteen people, whether the eleven are the eleven doors on this side of the channel or all of them, and she said the honest answer and a man of about nineteen counted it and got fifty-three, and it went in the minute in her own words.

"**Six of them are above the channel and five are below it and the water came down about a foot and a half and took the five and I have not carried for the five since the twenty-fourth of last month and I am not going to start on it on Friday either.**"

A man of fifty-six said that a woman has just split eleven into six and five in a yard in front of nineteen people and that a clerk of nineteen years wrote both of those figures down, **and that the two of them are the only figures in this district's business that came out of a woman's mouth in a month and that neither of them is a price and neither of them is a wage, and that the count of things this district does not have is five and it did not move at about half past one in the afternoon on the seventeenth of the fourth month.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a woman said a day of the week and that no weekday is attached to any date in this district, and that she is not going to correct her because she is not wrong about the five.

The man of about thirty-four who digs loam said that six and five is not a round either and that eleven is not a round and that about seventy-nine is not a round, **and that this district has been asking a woman of fifty-eight for a round since the twenty-third of last month and has not once asked her whether she wants one, and that not asking is the whole of what went wrong the first two times, and that a yard is a room and a room is not an office and this district has not got an office, and that the second of the five is a column that is a place, and that I have said the thing about a room and a place twice today and that is a habit and I am naming it.**

**AND ON THE SEVENTEENTH OF THE FOURTH MONTH THIS YARD GAVE A WOMAN OF FIFTY-EIGHT ELEVEN HOUSES OF A ROUND OF ABOUT NINETY AND ASKED HER FIRST, AND SHE TOOK THEM, AND NOBODY IN THIS DISTRICT COULD SAY WHAT ONE DAY OF THEM WAS WORTH, AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS READ OUT THAT ABOUT SEVENTY-NINE IS NOT A SUBTRACTION.**
