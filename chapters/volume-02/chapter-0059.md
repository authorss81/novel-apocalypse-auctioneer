# Chapter 59: A Destination Can Be a Victim

There were about five hundred people in the yard of Lot 17 on the morning of the eighteenth of this month, and on the wall behind the crate there were three things nailed up that had not all been on the same wall a fortnight ago, and any stranger who came cold into that yard that morning could have read the whole of this district's position off three sheets of paper and a slate, which was, and everybody in the yard knew it, a considerable achievement and also a fairly complete description of the problem.

On the left, the fourth page of the standing order of the ninth, as it had gone to the ward market's counter and to eleven boards and to a review table's file: a closure without a ground is a safety measure, and a safety measure is not subject to appeal. **Underneath it, in a different hand and smaller, a fitter's paragraph written on a staircase landing in about fifty seconds while holding a toolbox.**

In the middle, the ward market's rate at fifty coppers a quarter, in five columns, the fifth of them being a signal hut nine miles west, one lamp and one bushel of chalk, one quarter, and under the columns a line that says *a rate buys a service and does not buy a seat.*

On the right, nothing at all, which was the correct state of the wall.

And on the crate, a slate, in Nessa Pell's hand, with a count on it: **THE NINTH PLACE. THIRTY-ONE PEOPLE. FOUR HOUSES. A CHAPEL. ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-ONE MOVEMENTS. ONE DEPARTURE. AND THE REASON THE COUNT IS FOUR HOUSES IS THAT THE FORM ASKS FOR A HOUSEHOLD.**

---

**The question came at about ten in the morning and it came from a trader, and it was not cruel, and Adrian Vale could not answer it, and he stood in front of five hundred people for about four minutes not answering it on purpose.**

"Mr. Vale. There is a train on a village's ground nine miles west. **You read the thing that turns trains round on the seventh of last month, no — on the eleventh.** The reading's on your wall in a sleeve. And the book says the toll is the origin, and the origin is a place, and there's a village.

"So what happens today. Not in a month. **Today.** Does the train move, or does it not move, and who is the man who says, and what is the toll going to be, and can you tell me now or do I have to be told a date?"

"No," said Adrian Vale.

"You can tell me a date?"

"**I cannot tell you a date and I have not read anything since the eighteenth of last month, and I am not going to read anything today.** And I would like the yard to hear the reason, and the reason is that I have been not-reading for twenty-five days because every time I put my hand on that piece of iron I get four lines that say the toll is the origin and not a word about whose, and **if I read it again I will get a better four lines, and a better four lines will make me feel as though I know something, and I do not know something, and I have been selling the feeling for five weeks and I am not going to do it in front of a village.**"

"**Nineteen minutes**," said Nessa Pell, from the table, and put the two figures in the ledger, and did not say anything else about it, and the entry reads: *THE READER'S LIMIT, ENTERED AS THE EIGHTEENTH. HE DID NOT READ. NO COST. NO TIME. THE ENTRY IS OF A NON-EVENT AND IS ENTERED AS ONE.*

---

**And then he did the thing he has done twice in five weeks, which is put every honest option on the crate and refuse to choose between them, and this time there were three of them, and every one of them is an instrument this district spent five weeks building.**

"**The first thing is the proposal schedule.** Seven items, entered in the book in advance, and the board refuses to record a closing that is not in the book, and I have said in public about nine times that a document made at three in the morning is not a proposal.

"And here is what is wrong with it. **It is a schedule for a use somebody is proposing.** It has a route request, a toll in the words the travellers will hear, a named bearer, a term, a remedy, two fresh witnesses, and a way to refuse in public. Every one of those is about a thing that is about to happen.

"**Nothing at the ninth place is about to happen. It has already happened nineteen times.** The siding was signed for in the fourth year. The train went in on the eleventh of last month. And the toll has been taken out of that village — whatever 'out of' means, and I do not know yet, and I am not going to guess — **nineteen times since the water came.** There is no use left for me to schedule. The schedule has nothing to point at."

"Could you schedule turning the train round?" said Sabin Tull.

"**I could write nine lines that said a train is turned round, and if I did it, it would be the sixth time in five weeks that I have written a document about a thing that has already been done, and this board has found every one of those the hard way, and I am not going to be the first man who finds it the easy way.**"

"**The second thing is the no-name remedy.**" He put his hand flat on the crate. "Called twice, not discharged twice, known to fail on the day it is called, the whole district knows it is a machine that fails on a schedule and it fails on a schedule and it is right to have it.

"And it is built for a protected category. **A protected category is people who refuse to be named.** And Sarrow Water has not refused anything. Nobody has asked them anything, ever, in nine years, and a person who was never asked has not refused, and **a remedy for people who say no has nothing whatever to say about a place nobody offered to say yes to.**"

Somebody said, "Then ask them."

"**Then the remedy works, and that is the frightening part, and it is also why it cannot be called.**" He said it as though it cost him something, and it did, and the yard could hear it. "The no-name remedy is a remedy for a **breach**. It works when a charter has been broken and the affected people cannot be found, and it says so in a room and the record says it was not discharged. That is the whole machine.

"And this is not a breach of anything. **Nobody broke anything. There is no charter. There is no contract, there is no bearer, there is no term, there is no witness, and there is no sale.** There is a piece of iron in a hut that works, and a place that the line's own book uses as an origin a hundred and forty-one times, and a toll that has been taken nineteen times, and **every single one of those nineteen times was the correct operation of an object that nobody has ever bought.**

"I have spent five weeks saying that a thing you did not consent to is a breach. **It turns out that is a rule about things somebody put up for sale. This was never put up for sale. It was taken out of a flooded building by a man with a pump, and it was given to a hut, and it has been working ever since.**"

---

**And the third, and it is the one that cost him the most, and it is the sentence the volume is named for, and it came at about eleven o'clock in the morning in a yard of about five hundred people in Alder Reach, and it is in the book in his own hand and it is the only entry in the volume anybody has copied out.**

"The third thing is ours," he said. "And it is the best thing anybody in this district has written since the flood year, and it is four lines long, and a woman on a river stair fought for it against a room of six hundred, and I was in the room and I voted for it.

"**A person is accounted for by a count and a destination and a remedy, and is not accounted for by a household.**

"And look at the slate."

He turned round and put his hand on it without touching it.

"**Four houses. Thirty-one people. A chapel. And the reason the count says four is that our form asks for a household, and thirty-one people on a bank filled it in like a household, because that is what the form is.** A village entered as a household is precisely the thing that rule was written to stop being true of people. A household can be counted, and closed, and asked for its consent by one person, and the one person is a man with a pen.

"**So the district's central institutional gain is the reason a village is invisible, and I want it entered in those words, and I want the woman who found it entered as the one who found it, and she found it on the fifteenth of this month on a bank nine miles west standing in the rain with her arms folded.**"

The yard was very quiet for about nine seconds.

"Adrian," said Mara Quill.

"I have got to the part where I say it and I would rather you did not stop me, because I have never said it and I have had it since the twelfth."

"Say it."

"**A destination can be a victim too.**" He said it to about five hundred people and it did not go up the way a sentence goes up in a yard. "Every instrument this district has built points at a person or a lot. The charter points at a person and a lot. The count points at a person. The remedy points at a person. And a toll on that iron is charged to **a place**, and a place is not a person and a place is not a lot, and there is no column for it anywhere in eleven boards and nine copies.

"**And I have been standing in this yard for five weeks saying that the charge is the person, and I said it, and I was right about a bus and I was the man who made the line that says it, and it turns out that is a rule about roads I built and not a law. I did not know what else to charge. That is the whole of it. I did not know what else to charge and I did not ask.**"

---

**And then he made a payment, and it is in the book, and it is the third column of this kind of entry Lot 17 has ever held, and the middle column is the finding.**

> **A PAYMENT, ENTERED AS A PAYMENT AND NOT AS A SECURITY, ON AN UNSTATED CLAIM, ON THE EIGHTEENTH OF THIS MONTH, BY A READER, WHO CANNOT POST A SECURITY AND SAYS SO IN THE SAME LINE.**
>
> **ONE THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY, on his own figure. NO SHEET, on the ward market's rate sheet, and the reason is two reasons and both are entered: the ninth place is not in any ward, and the rate sheet for the two blocks west of the depot, which the market voted on the thirteenth, has not been made and will take a fortnight. ONE THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED AND THIRTY, in the room.**
>
> **AND THE SHORTFALL IS ENTERED AS UNCOMPUTED AND NOT AS NIL, BECAUSE THE CLAIM HAS NEVER BEEN STATED BY ANYBODY, AND A NIL WOULD BE A NUMBER THE PAYER CHOSE.**

The yard read the three figures out and one woman at the back said the only thing anybody said about it, which was that a thousand coppers is eleven weeks of the ward market's levy off every household in a quarter, and Ivo Stenn, who was in the room, said yes, and that it was a quarter of the new rate's whole take, and that the market would have to be told and would be told, and Adrian said that was the correct order of the two things and that the market must be told before the money moved and not after.

---

**And at about twenty past ten in the morning somebody in that yard said the word on behalf of Sarrow Water, and the district discovered that the only instrument it is proud of is the thing that would do the harm.**

It was a trader of about forty, off the top terrace, who had come in for nothing in particular, and he stood up in the fourth row and said it in a voice that shook and then steadied, and he said it four times, and the four times are in the book.

"No," he said. "**No. I have been sitting in this yard for an hour waiting for somebody to stand up and say that word on behalf of that village, and I am going to say it, and I am sorry, and I am saying it anyway.**

"No to the siding. No to the train. No to the column. They did not ask for it and they have not been asked and I will not sit in a yard and hear five weeks of paper about a thing that is being taken off a village of thirty-one people."

And about four hundred people in that yard agreed with him out loud, and Nessa Pell's hand went to the ledger, and Adrian Vale stood in front of the crate and said one sentence, and then four, and then stopped, and the four are the finding of the morning:

"**Stop there. Do not say the word twice. I am not stopping you because you are wrong. I am stopping you because I know what happens next and I have been awake since six and I have been thinking about it and nobody else in this yard has.**

"If Sarrow Water refuses the siding, the trains have nowhere to stand. The Alder Reach yards have been full since the flood year. The west end is under water. **And the only other place on that line a train can stand is the embankment, which is above the bank, and the bank is theirs, and there is a drain off the top of that bank at the sixth place that has been going straight onto the ballast since before the Fracture.**

"So the one act everybody in this yard has spent five weeks making holy — a place saying the word — **is the act that puts nine vehicles on top of the woman of seventy's wall.** And that is not a flaw in the village and it is not a flaw in her and it is not a flaw in the word. **It is a flaw in every instrument in this district, and all of them were built by people who assumed that saying no would be free, and saying no has never once been free, and it has never been free because nobody ever costed where the thing goes next.**"

"**Then cost it**," said the trader.

"Then it goes in the book as uncomputed and not nil," said Adrian, "and it goes in on a page nobody reads, and the next time a place in this basin wants to say the word, which it should, and which I will fight for — **they will not know what is upstairs.** And I have spent five weeks teaching this district that a toll has to land on a person, and the reason I did it is that I could not think of a second thing to charge, and **nobody in this yard has ever costed a refusal.**"

And Bett Ruhl, from about fifteen feet back, in the voice of a woman who has chalked four lines on her own kitchen wall and a fifth line in chalk above them and has never been thanked:

"**Then that is the fifth thing and it goes under the other four**, and somebody with a bit of chalk is going to have to write it on my wall, and it is going to be the longest one, and I am going to make them write it in a hand big enough to be read from a doorway, and I will not be thanked and I would like everybody here to know I would be insulted if anybody tried."

---

**And Selik Marne had been at the rail since half past eight and had asked for nothing, and at about a quarter to twelve in the morning he said the only safe thing anybody could say, and it was safe, and it cost a village its ground.**

"The west end should be closed."

Nobody argued with him about whether it was safe. Everybody in that yard had spent five weeks learning that Selik Marne is right about what is safe.

"**The ninth place. The points locked, the ground signal out, the siding sealed, and nothing moved west of the fourth place until a replacement instrument is certified.** That is the action I would take this afternoon and I would take it without a hearing, and in nine years of doing it I have never once wanted it more than I want it now, and I have written *that is not enough* on my own hand for eight years and I am going to write it on this as well."

"And it is safe," said Adrian.

"**It is the safest thing available and it is permanent, and those are two different facts and this yard has spent a month learning to tell them apart.**" Selik Marne took his hat off and held it. "That siding is the only stabling on this line. Your Road House yard is full. There are nine vehicles in it and a bank behind them that a woman of seventy looks at every evening. **If I close the west end, the nine vehicles stay on the bank for ever, and the bank is the thing the woman is afraid of.**

"So I have come here this morning to tell this yard that the safest thing anybody in this basin can do is to leave nine wagons on a village's hard ground permanently, and that I am right about it, and that **being right is the thing I have never once in nine years been able to explain to anybody.**"

"And you still want it."

"**I still want it, and I will not be the man who does it, because it is a Warden's order and this is a public lot, and if it is done it will be done by a room of four seats with no owner and not by me. That is the whole of my objection to everything in this yard and it has been the whole of my objection for five weeks.**"

---

**And at about half past twelve the board sat, all four seats named aloud, and Adrian Vale put the unattempted remedy on the crate, and it was attempted for the first time in the history of the book, and the book has it in three lines.**

The route-keeper remedy was written in the third week of last month, entered as known to fail on the day it is called, and it was a machine for a person put down at the wrong end of anything: a keeper who goes, a count, a destination, a way to call them back, and a named party at each end. Nobody had ever called it. **It was written and paid for and never used, and Adrian put it on the crate on the eighteenth of this month because the only thing anybody can point at is a train.**

And it worked. It worked completely and in about eleven minutes, because the machine is a machine and a train is the easiest thing in the world to point at: a keeper named, a count of nine vehicles, a destination, a named party at the fourth place, an appeal path, and a line that says a person inside a vehicle is carried as a person. **The remedy for the train is discharged. The remedy for the village is not discharged, and it cannot be called, and it is entered as not discharged, and the reason entered is not the protected category.**

> **THE ROUTE-KEEPER REMEDY WAS CALLED ON THE EIGHTEENTH OF THIS MONTH AND WAS DISCHARGED IN ELEVEN MINUTES FOR THE NINE VEHICLES, AND WAS NOT DISCHARGED FOR THIRTY-ONE PEOPLE, AND THE REASON IT COULD NOT BE ENTERED IS THE REASON THIS ENTRY IS THE FINDING: THERE IS NO CHARTER, AND THEREFORE NO BREACH, AND THEREFORE NOTHING TO REMEDY, AND THE ONLY THING THAT HAS HAPPENED AT THE NINTH PLACE IN NINE YEARS IS AN OBJECT WORKING AS IT WORKS.**

**And the board then did the fifth of the five things it can do, which is publish a result, and it published one in about four minutes, and it is the shortest entry in the book and the heaviest.**

> **PUBLISHED THE EIGHTEENTH OF THIS MONTH, ALL FOUR SEATS NAMED ALOUD, BY THE BOARD, WHICH CANNOT GRANT A USE:**
>
> **NO CHARTER, NO TERMS-CLOSING, NO PAYMENT, AND NO READING MAY BE ATTACHED TO THE HARROW TONGUE UNTIL THE PLACE ITS TOLL NAMES HAS BEEN ASKED. AND THE TOLL IS THE ORIGIN, AND THE ORIGIN IS A PLACE, AND THE READER CANNOT NAME IT, AND THE BOARD HAS NO POWER TO MAKE A READER NAME IT, AND THEREFORE THE LOT IS STOPPED BY A ROOM OF FOUR SEATS WITH NO OWNER, AND NOT BY THE READER, AND THE READER ASKED FOR IT TO BE STOPPED BY THE BOARD AND WAS REFUSED THE FIRST TIME AND ACCEPTED THE SECOND TIME IN THE SAME MINUTE.**

And the yard read that out twice, and about nine hundred people in this district heard it by the fourteenth, and the last line of the day is the one the tenth hand wrote in the margin of his page, and it is not a number and it is not an instrument:

> *He did not read. Twenty-five days. And I have been keeping a column for things I could not put anywhere, and there are two in it now, and this one is not a number either, and it is a man not doing the thing he is good at in front of five hundred people, and putting that in a column is the only thing I have got.*

---

**And at about ten past two in the afternoon the yard had gone and the crate was still there and the slate was still on it, and two people were left, and neither of them said anything about the third thing, which is that there were only two people left.**

Mara Quill put the payment in front of the ward market before the money moved, as it had to be, and she did it with three figures and the middle one empty, and Ivo Stenn read the middle one and looked at her for about three seconds and said, "**That is twice in nine days.**"

"It is three times in nine days."

"**And in nine weeks it will be four times, and the reason it is a column is that a market is a column with three figures in it, and you have made it one.**" He wrote the three figures on his own paper in his own hand and signed the paper, and then he said the thing he is going to keep saying about this entry for the next four years, and it is in the minute:

"I am the freight clerk of a market and I have just been handed the business of a village nine miles west that is not in any of my books, and I am not going to be the man who loses it, so I am going to do the only thing I know how to do, which is make a rate sheet for a place, and a rate sheet starts with somebody walking onto the ground and counting, and **there is no rate on any sheet in this city for a household I have not met.**"

And Adrian Vale stood in an empty yard in the afternoon and did the thing he has been able to do since the twenty-sixth of last month, which is nothing, and it took him about a quarter of an hour and nobody saw it and there is no instrument for it in this book and this paragraph is the only record of it.

At about half past two the tenth hand of the signal crew came back through the gate with his page, having been out to the fourth place on his own, four miles, on a Tuesday afternoon, for no reason anybody could give him, and he had one more number and it went into the column of things he cannot put anywhere:

> **THE STALL AT THE FOURTH PLACE MEASURED THREE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOUR YARDS ON THE NINTH. IT MEASURED THREE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-ONE YARDS ON THE EIGHTEENTH OF THIS MONTH, ON AN AFTERNOON WHEN NOTHING WENT THROUGH IT, AND I WENT AND LOOKED AT IT TWICE AND ASKED A MAN AT THE EIGHTH PLACE, WHO SAID HE HAD NOT SEEN IT MOVE, AND I BELIEVE HIM, AND I HAVE ALSO PUT THE TWO FIGURES ON THIS PAGE BESIDE EACH OTHER, AND NOBODY CAN TELL ME WHICH OF THEM IS THE LIE.**

And the yard was empty, and the wall had three sheets on it and a hole where a fourth would go, and the eleventh stall in a hut nine miles west is a floor a woman stands on every day of her life, and the twelfth was measured on the seventh of this month and has not been measured since, and there is a date in this book on which somebody has to go and measure it, and that date is the twenty-ninth, and it is announced in a yard on the twenty-first by a registrar with her hand shaking slightly and about four hundred people in it, **and it is the first date this district has ever given anybody for anything, and the sheet it is on says on its face that it is a date a person could be wrong about, and that phrase is on the sheet and not in a footnote.**
