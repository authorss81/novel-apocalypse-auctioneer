# Chapter 99: A Term of Labour and Stock

The month turned on the first of this month and nobody in Alder Reach put a line in a book about it, because the printed timetable prints days of the month and not days of the week and gives the reason on its face, which is that this district does not have a calendar and a day of the week is a thing nobody here can check against anybody else.

The first thing done in the new month was a page going into a book in the hours a counter is shut, done by a clerk of the ward market who is not the market. **The finding of the first is that a document written on a platform in the rain at about four in the morning can be in a public book in Alder Reach in about nine hours if two people are awake when it comes down the bank.**

She wrote the page and the man who had written the answer on the platform read it over her shoulder and did not touch it, and the page carries two hands and the entry says so, and the finding of the first of this month is that **two hands on one page in this district has meant two offices disagreeing for nine weeks and has meant one page for the first time**, and that nobody in the room has said anything about that and it is in the book anyway.

The reader at the counter read the whole of it out on the second at about thirteen in the morning in the ordinary voice, and about thirty people stood there, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she had read the thirtieth of last month's asking out three times and this one out twice and that the difference was that nobody asked her to read the first one and everybody asked her to read this one.

---

**And on the second of this month the fourth toll board at the ninth place stopped being the only one of the four with an empty column, and what went into it is three tolls, and none of the three is a figure, and that is the first time in this district that three amounts have been entered as things that cannot be stated.**

> **A TOLL THAT HAS A NAME. THE STANDING OFFICE OF CROSS-DISTRICT PASSAGE, ESTABLISHED, AND NOT AT THIS PLACE. NOBODY IN THIS BASIN CAN FIND THAT OFFICE IN ANY REGISTER THIS BASIN CAN REACH, AND IT IS SPELLED OUT ON THREE BOARDS AND FIVE HUNDRED SHEETS.**
>
> **A TOLL THAT HAS A DATE AND NO PLACE. THE TWENTY-NINTH OF JUNE. ONE MOVEMENT. THE NINTH PLACE. THE FIGURE IS ONE DEPARTURE OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-TWO, AND THE FIGURE IS NOT IN COPPER.**
>
> **A TOLL THAT HAS A PLACE AND NO NAME. ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-TWO, THE REST BEING WHAT THE NINTH PLACE CANNOT PRODUCE.**

The keeper of that board is a man of about fifty and he is not paid and he is not a bearer and he has said since the eighth of last month that keeping a board is not holding a pair. **He said one thing about the three and it is on the bottom line of the board in his own words, and the sentence is nineteen words long, and the finding of the second is that a man who has kept an empty column for a month has nothing to say about three things in it except that there are now three instead of none.**

"I have been wiping that column since the twenty-second of last month," he said, "**and I have been waiting to find out what the argument was for, and there was not one, and now there are three things in it and I still do not know what the argument is, and I would like that written down by a man who thinks he does.**"

He was asked whether he was paid and he said no and the answer went on the board, and it is the second line anybody has written under a heading that says anybody may write one sentence on it that anybody would be asked to explain.

---

The three stations went into the second printing on the second of this month as three places a thing stops at, and the fourth place is on the sheet, and the sheet says beside it that nobody is there. **That is the first time a printed sheet in this district has printed a thing that is not true next to a thing that is.**

The ninth place, the fourth place and the east end. Three toll boards, three keepers, none of them paid out of a rate, and a platform thirty feet long that did not exist in March.

A day and a date on the sheet, because a correction that has no date on it is a rumour with a press on it, and the day is the twenty-ninth of last month and the date is the twenty-ninth of this month, and **the finding of the second is that a printed sheet which promises one train a month and a correction which promises the same train on a named day are two different promises, and that the district owns one of them and has bought the other, and that the one it bought came from a man it has argued with in four public rooms.**

The fourth place is printed as a place a thing stops at and the line under it says NOBODY IS THERE, and the reason under that says that a keeper of a board is not a person anybody can be sent to, and the reason under that says that a man of nineteen is there and is nobody's keeper and has said so four times.

Three of the four people who got off that train in the dark on the night of the twenty-ninth wrote their names on the sheet afterwards. The fourth did not, and the finding is that a sheet a stranger can take cold is the only instrument this district has ever built that a person can decline to be on, and that this is either the best thing about it or the worst and that nobody in the room could say which.

**And on the second of this month the rotation turned for the first time under a closed charter, and it turned because the first week ran out, and nobody had to be given a day to make it turn, and the finding of the second is that a rotation which turns by itself is a rotation and that this district had never had one and had three samples of a subscription and a man who could tell the difference.**

The order of the weeks was settled in the middle of last month on the back of a mechanic's pencil on a trade's invoice and has not been touched since. The first week is the keeper's. The second is the man of sixty-eight at the pumping engine, who asked in a room for his week to be called the fourth place and not the Harrow end, and gave a reason that went in the minute, which is that a week is a place a person stands and not a place a person is, and it costs him two days a month in somebody else minding his engine and he did the arithmetic himself and put the two days in the minute in his own hand.

The third is the man of about thirty-one who is a signalman of the district's own crew, and who refused the week that ends on the twenty-ninth of this month in the middle of last month because the third asking was on the twenty-ninth and he was going to be two and a half miles up a bank reading somebody else's question back, and **the finding of the second is that the first reason anybody in this district has ever given for not holding a week is that the week was the wrong shape.**

Nobody extended the term and nobody shortened it. It ends on the twenty-ninth of this month and it is written in four places and three of them are nine hours away.

And on the third of this month, at a rail in Alder Reach, on a published day, a bridge administrator of nine years was asked what his office thought of a charter with four bearers and a toll and a term, and he answered in nine words and the nine words are in the minute.

"I am the office that has been paying a hundred and eighty coppers a quarter on the Harrow works for nine years," said Selik Marne, "**and I have not been asked whether that office would carry a week, and the reason I have not been asked is that an office is not a person, and that is my own man's sentence and he wrote it on a board in May and asked for it read out at a counter and not at a rail, and I would like it read out at a counter.**"

It was read out at a counter on the fourth of this month, in the ordinary voice, at about a dozen in the morning, and the entry is in the second clerk's hand and not in the ninth hand's.

And then he said the second thing, which is the one he has said every published day for nine weeks and which he says now with more facts in it and not fewer.

"That is not enough. **And I am going to keep saying it for as long as I hold this post, and nobody in this district believes I will stop and I am not going to stop.**"

---

And the finding of the fourth of this month is that the district has now declined, in public, in a figure, the exact thing that would have paid for the thing it bought instead. **The figure is eighty-eight coppers a quarter, and it is the reason a man at a rail is not a defeated friend and not a friend.**

The standing order of the ninth was lost five trades to five trades on the twenty-seventh of last month, on the market's own rule, which is by trade and not by household. Eight posts at eleven coppers a quarter is eighty-eight against a take of two thousand and fifty. The market could pay that office more than two thousand times over and it has never wanted it, and the reason it has given is that a seat which may be filled and cannot be emptied is a condition and not a service.

What the market bought instead was a timetable, five hundred sheets, nine hundred and six coppers, quoted on the fourteenth of March, less twelve coppers a quarter of printed matter, which left a gap of eight hundred and ninety-four coppers that it does not have, printed on the sheet in the same type as everything else.

**Eighty-eight a quarter against nine hundred and six, and the market could afford both, and it chose the sheet, and a man at a rail has said for nine weeks that a sheet is not a crossing, and the finding of the fourth is that the district did not decline a crossing by accident. It declined it five to five, on a count read out in full, in a room, and then bought a piece of paper with the money it had saved.**

And nobody wrote anything under that heading and the minute says nobody wrote anything under that heading, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the refusal of the office and the purchase of the sheet are two different votes taken on the same afternoon about two different things, and that the district has been treating them as one argument since March and that they are not one argument.

Then the man at the rail did the thing that makes him an ally, and he did it in public, and nobody thanked him for it and he did not ask to be thanked.

He asked the ward market, in writing, to pay the man in the hut at the ninth place, and he produced the two numbers in a public book that are about him. **Eleven coppers a quarter for the bench, the lamp, the chalk and the oil. Nothing for the man. And a rate sheet that has carried a post at thirty days since the twenty-fifth of April and has paid it twice without asking.**

"The second number is not a nil and I want that entered," he said, "**because it is not a nil. It is a person at a place that my office has said is unsafe, on a term it renews by itself, for nine days past the date, and nobody in this district has ever costed a signalman and it has costed a pump.**"

The market refused it on the fourth of this month in about four minutes and the reason is in the minute and the reason is that the office has not asked to be asked and a rate that is not asked for is a rate that has not been agreed to.

"He asked," said the secretary.

"He asked you to pay a man and he did not ask the man," said the tenth hand, "**and the difference between those two things is the whole of the last nine weeks and a man at a rail has just been the only person in this district to notice it in nine weeks, and I am not going to be the man who says he was right because he was.**"

---

And on the third of this month, in the hours the counter was shut, a printing came off the only press in this district that can do a run in a day, and it is a correction sheet, and it is one sheet, and it names three places, and it cost twelve coppers.

A correction is owed by a road. That is the man who printed it and it is his own argument and he has been right about it since April in public, in a yard, where he sold nothing.

Three places at four coppers a place. Twelve coppers. **The finding of the third is that this district has now got two twelves in one month, and the registrar entered that the twelve coppers of the correction and the twelve coppers a quarter of the printed-matter column are two different twelves, and that the sum of nine hundred and six less twelve is eight hundred and ninety-four and has not moved and is not going to.**

He was not thanked and he said he would not have been thanked, and he asked for it entered that he was paid, and it was entered, and the entry is the fourth time anybody has asked to be entered as paid in this district's history and three of the four times were his.

He came up the bank on the fourth of this month and stood in front of the board with the three tolls in it for about four minutes and said nothing, and then he said nine words.

"It costs four coppers to print a place you cannot name."

"And what does the correction cost," said the tenth hand, from about nine feet away, and he did not answer it, and nobody answered it for him, and the minute enters that nobody answered it and that the man who sells nothing and has a use said afterwards that he had wanted to answer it and had not, and that not answering was the correct thing to do, and that the district still does not have an instrument for saying what a toll lands on a place.

---

**And on the fourth of this month a page went into the public lot book in Alder Reach with two hands on it that are not his, and the second hand wrote the clause that makes his security inspectable, and the two of them have not said one word to each other about anything since the second of last month.**

A security is a thing somebody else can go and look at. She wrote that sentence in May and it is the only definition this district has, and on the fourth of this month she wrote under his name the words *inspectable by any person at any time without asking anybody's permission*, and the registrar is the registrar and not a vote and not independent, and she wrote the clause and she entered it in her own hand and she read it out in the ordinary voice at a counter at about eleven in the morning.

A slate went past in a kitchen under the Ward Nine tram line at a quarter to seven in the morning on the fourth of this month. Neither of them said a word. A reader at the counter wrote down on the fourth that six people have now seen that kitchen and that she is still not going to ask, because the answer would be in a room and not in a book, and **not asked is in the count.**

And the reading ledger was read out at the counter on the fourth of this month and it is one for April, none for May, and one for the twenty-ninth of last month, and the number of departures he has left is one, and the next permitted hour is not before the fifteenth of this month, and nobody wrote a reason on the page and Stenn wrote one on a separate sheet and read it out at the same counter in the same afternoon, and the separate sheet says in nine words: **the intervals go up on use and I will not defend it and I will not call it anything else.**

---

The standing instruction that somebody walks every yard of the line is not discharged, and on the third of this month a man and a boy walked it in two days and wrote down every place that reports itself and every place that does not. **The count is twelve places and eleven boards and one place that does not report itself.**

The one that does not report itself is the Harrow end. There is no ground signal there. The plinth is on the embankment thirty yards east of the engine house with the bolt-holes in it and a cap of lead over them, and it went out of the same frame in the fourth year when the iron was salvaged out of the flooded interchange.

A signalman offered to put one in a morning on the twenty-ninth of April and was refused in the fastest no of that day, on the ground that a place that reports every morning is a place nobody goes to. **Two men walked the whole line in two days in the first week of this month and the entry says the standing instruction is discharged and not satisfied, because a thing that reports itself and a thing that is walked are two different instruments and this district has now got the second one and still has not got the first.**

And the entry for the third also carries the one hundred and eighty-three coppers that went on nothing at the ninth place in the second week of last month, and nobody in this district can say who is owed it, and the finding is that **how a payment reaches a person who is not in a household is still the last open thing in this district that has no instrument, and that a market that has refused an office five to five and printed a gap of eight hundred and ninety-four on its own sheet has not yet found one, and that the entry of the third carries the figure with no answer against it and the answer is not to be struck.**

And on the third of this month the Harrow Tongue was entered as a lot with a condition and a keeper, in a remote custody that is not a case and not a counter and not a key, and the keeper is the woman who holds the only pair on that line, and the condition is published in full.

A custody with a keeper in it is a custody. A custody whose keeper is entered as nobody is a place where a thing is, and for nine weeks this one has been the second thing. **The boy of nineteen asked for his name on the sheet before the iron's and his name is on the sheet and it is not a keeper's name, and the entry says both of those, and the registrar was asked to keep them apart and agreed and gave the reason she gives every time.**

**And the fifth search for the office was run on the fourth of this month by four people in four rooms, and it is the first of the five that was not a search for a name, and the finding of the fourth is that this district can no longer state what the office was for and has not been able to for four months.**

The first four were a registrar through the municipal roll of public offices, a bridge clerk through his own office's standing instructions, a licensed valuer through the guild's register, and a sign-writer of sixty-one through thirty-one years of lettering jobs in which he found the phrase twice in his own hand and no office.

The fifth looked for the work. Eleven trades were asked to write down, in their own words, what a public office does that none of the eleven trades does, and the answers filled two pages and not one of the eleven trades wrote the same sentence twice, and the registrar entered that eleven trades and one office between them cannot agree on a function, and **that a name which four people have failed to find in five registers is a smaller problem than a function which eleven trades have failed to state, and that both of those are the same problem, and that the district has now written the second one down and has not joined it to the first.**

The name is on three boards, on five hundred sheets, on a security schedule, and in the water office's own book. It is in five registers this basin can reach and it is not in any of them, and **the finding of the fourth is that this district is getting better at writing a name down and no better at finding one, and that the count of places the name is written in has gone from three to five and the count of places it cannot be found in has gone from four to five, and that those two numbers are the only two numbers anybody in this room will be able to check in four years.**
