# Chapter 199: The Person At The Back Of The Book

---

The twenty-seventh of the third month was at the scale at about ten in the morning and about fourteen people were standing round it, and the man of about thirty at the counter came down the eleven mile road for the tenth time in eight weeks and was not sent for.

He laid a sheet face up on the trestle table in the yard of Lot Seventeen and a clerk of nineteen years read it, which is the whole of what a clerk does with a sheet, and nobody in that yard interrupted her.

> **THE HOUSE WILL NOT CALL IN THE CLAIM NAMED IN THE THIRD OF THE FOUR PRINTED THINGS WHILE THE WATER NAMED IN THE ROTA GOES WHERE THE ROTA NAMED IT. THE ROTA DOES NOT NAME A SLUICE.**
>
> **A WATER ROUTE AND THE CLAIM NAMED IN THE THIRD OF THE FOUR PRINTED THINGS CANNOT BOTH STAND. THE HOUSE IS NOT ASKING WHICH OF THE TWO THIS DISTRICT WOULD RATHER HAVE.**

Nothing was said to it for a moment. A man of fifty-six said that the third line is the first line a house has ever put on a sheet that is not a figure and not a day, **and that a house asking a district which of two things it would rather have is a house asking to be the thing that decides, and that the last time one of those came down an eleven mile road it was on the twelfth of this month with a price in the first line.**

The man of about thirty at the counter said the price out loud himself, and he said it before anybody asked him for it, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got thirty-four, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**Put the sluice back in the ground and the claim stands off until the last day of the month, and the tank at the top of your lane is at nothing inside a week.**"

It was not contradicted. A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked whether the claim was the claim on one place in a flood shelter and a boy of nine, and the man of about thirty said he was not going to talk about which place and gave no reason.

A man of about thirty-one who digs loam said the thing nobody had asked for, and he said it out loud in front of about fourteen people.

"**Then we open the sluice and the claim stands and the water comes, and the man of about fifty-five gets a tank and the boy of nine loses a place, and those two are not a choice anybody put to us, they are a choice the house put to us, and we are going to take the water.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a decision was made in a yard on the twenty-seventh of the third month and that the man whose tank it was was not in that yard and was not told, **and that the decision not to decide about a tank entered on the twenty-eighth of the second month has now been taken back and replaced by a decision to do something about one, and that going back on a decision is not the same as never having made it.**

Then the toll, and the woman of about thirty-six who keeps a scale at the sluice end of the loams and has stood at it nine years said it, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got forty-two, and it went in the minute in her own words.

"**A toll is a yoke-load on a day a route keeper names, and it is not a figure and it is not coppers, and I weigh about nine sacks a day and I have never once been asked what a yoke-load weighs.**"

Nobody picked it up. A man of fifty-six said that nine years standing at that scale has just brought the fifth of the five things this district does not have out of a woman who weighs sacks, **and that what she has named is a way of paying a person who is not in a household, and that a yoke-load is a job and a job is not a figure and cannot be put on a page and is not going to be.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked whether a mark could carry a yoke-load, and the man of about fifty-five said no in about two seconds and gave the reason in one sentence, **and that a mark is cut and a yoke-load is carried and a line of charcoal drawn on a stone is not a mark either, and that the rule is the rule and it was built around that and not in spite of it.**

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against said his part of it at about half past ten in the morning with his hand flat on the scale frame, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got fifty-seven.

"**I am going to stand at the back of a public book against the tolls of this water. I cannot tell you what a toll is in coppers because nobody has established one, and what I am offering is a day and my name, and the days are the twenty-eighth of the third month and the thirtieth.**"

The gate of that yard stayed shut for about nine seconds. A man of about thirty-one who digs loam said that the man the figure of twenty-one years is against had said on the nineteenth that a book is not a security because there is nothing at the back of it, and that he had said on the same day that there are two people in this district who can be held to a book, **and that a man who has done his own arithmetic for four months and got a different answer this morning is a man who found it and is not to be congratulated in a yard.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-SEVENTH OF THE THIRD MONTH A HOUSE NAMED A CHOICE AND A DISTRICT TOOK THE WATER, AND A TOLL WAS NAMED AS A YOKE-LOAD BY A WOMAN WHO KEEPS A SCALE, AND THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST SAID HE WOULD STAND AT THE BACK OF A PUBLIC BOOK AGAINST THE TOLLS.**

---

The twenty-eighth was a room with a door on it and a clerk of nineteen years was not in it and nothing was entered on the way and both of the two of them said so.

He asked her for one thing and he said it standing up, and a man of about nineteen was not there and the sentence was not counted by anybody, and it is in the minute in her own words because she put it there herself on the twenty-ninth.

"**Will you hold the book. The tolls of a water route go in it and I go at the back of it and I am not asking you for anything else.**"

The registrar of this district's records office said the other half of it, and she said it in the ordinary voice of a person who has said a version of it before, and there was nobody in that room to count it.

"**Say the rest of it. You have said three things and there is a fourth one and I am not going to hold a book with a hole in it.**"

He did not say it. He stood in that room for about a minute and a half and he said the reason instead, and the reason was short and no clerk was in the room and nobody counted it.

"**I know it. You said it out loud in a yard on the twelfth of the third month and you have not been asked about it since, and I am not going to be the man who asks.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered on the twenty-ninth that he was asked a question in a room and did not answer it, **and that she was not asked a second question and that the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was, and that a sentence a man says against himself in a room belongs to him and not to the room, and that he would not have it on a page.**

Nobody in that office contradicted her. A man of fifty-six said afterwards that the registrar has said her own instrument sentence four times in five months and said on the sixteenth of the third month that there was a reason for the fourth, **and that a fifth reason was given in a room with no clerk in it and is not in any book and cannot be put into one by anybody but her.**

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against wrote his undertaking out in his own hand on one sheet of paper on the evening of the twenty-eighth and it went into a drawer in a building with a door on it, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the paper is a page and not a book and is not a line and is not a board, **and that nothing which holds a sheet holds that sheet's claim on whoever wrote it, and that this district has a page in a drawer here and a receipt in a drawer thirty-four miles east and the two of them are not to be put in one sentence.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked, in the six things, at about five in the afternoon, who was going to be able to say the pool was worth something, and a man of fifty-six said that a pool is worth the whole of what is tied to each thing in it and nothing over that, **and that the list of the five was written on the back of a page in this district on the twenty-first of this month for the first time in four months and that a list on the back of a page has not changed what anything is.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF THE THIRD MONTH THE REGISTRAR OF THIS DISTRICT'S RECORDS OFFICE AGREED TO HOLD A BOOK, AND THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST DID NOT ANSWER A QUESTION SHE PUT TO HIM, AND THERE WAS NO CLERK IN THE ROOM AND THE SENTENCE WAS NOT COUNTED BY ANYBODY.**

---

The twenty-ninth was a day a route keeper named, and the water came at about six in the morning and went down at about two in the afternoon, and the tank at the top of the lane did not rise, and about nine people were in the yard of Lot Seventeen at about eleven in the morning because a man of about thirty-one had asked for something on the twenty-second of this month and had not been asked for it.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing read it out in one run of words with a stop at the end of each of them, and he read the five of them including the line with his own name on it, and a clerk of nineteen years read it back afterwards and got nothing wrong, which is the rule of the counter.

The man of about thirty-one stood at the back of that yard with his hands in his pockets and did not stand at anything, and he said the thing he had come for and a man of about nineteen counted it and got twenty-eight.

"**I asked for that on the twenty-second and I am not at the sluice and I never said I would be. Read it out and say it twice.**"

Nobody argued with that. A man of fifty-six said that the fifth of the five has stood among about nine people while his own line was read out twice and has not asked to move and has not asked why, **and that a person on a list is not a bearer of a list and the second reading is not a release and is not a consent and cannot be entered as either.**

The man of about thirty-one who digs loam said that the two of them being in the same yard at the same hour on purpose is the ninth time since the eighteenth of December and that a clerk of nineteen years has not been asked to write the arranging down as anything, **and that the man of about thirty-one was in that yard and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against was in it and neither of them spoke to the other, and that two men not speaking to each other in a yard is not a thing anybody has to be asked about.**

The woman of fifty-eight was at the top of the hill at about four in the afternoon and did not come down, and nobody sent for her and nobody asked her anything, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she was not asked about the pool and that the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was.

**AND ON THE TWENTY-NINTH OF THE THIRD MONTH THE LIST OF THE FIVE WAS READ OUT TWICE IN A YARD BY THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FOUR WHO MINDS FENCING, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE STOOD AT THE BACK WITH HIS HANDS IN HIS POCKETS, AND THE THIRTY-FIRST OF MARCH IS TWO DAYS OFF THIS DAY.**

---

The thirtieth was the sheet on the wall of the yard of Lot Seventeen and the man who prints on a tram arch put up the eighth of his sheets himself and was not sent for, and a clerk of nineteen years read it out and it took her about a minute and nobody interrupted her.

> **THE FIGURE CALLED IN ON THE THIRTY-FIRST OF THE THIRD MONTH IS THE FIGURE OF THE TOLLS OF A WATER ROUTE. THE TOLLS OF A WATER ROUTE ARE FASTENED TO A PERSON AT THE BACK OF A BOOK.**
>
> **THE BOOK IS A BOOK A STRANGER MAY ASK FOR. IT IS KEPT BY AN OFFICE AND NOT BY A PERSON. THE NAME AT THE BACK OF IT IS THE NAME OF THE PERSON WHO HOLDS THE BOOK.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked him, in the six things, in front of about nine people, where on the sheet the name was, and the man from the tram arch said that it was not on the sheet, and that the house has it and the house did not put it on a sheet.

It went no further for a moment. A man of about thirty-one who digs loam said that a house which has got a name out of a building by asking is doing the thing this district said a book a stranger can read is a place a house can look for a man for, **and that the man of about twenty-three has been saying that since the fifteenth of this month and the man who prints on a tram arch has printed it four times and did not write it down and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing has now said it in four yards.**

The registrar of this district's records office read the sheet twice and then said the other thing, and she said it to the man the figure of twenty-one years is against and not to the yard, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got forty-five.

"**I will write down that the claim was called in and I will not write down whose hand is on the page, and the reason is that a hand is not a person and this office has not printed a person since it was opened.**"

Nobody contradicted her. A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the house has joined a hand in a corner of a page to a person and has done it on its own sheet and not on anybody else's, and this district has refused that join in four yards in a fortnight and a man of about twenty-three has said out loud that a guess about a hand is worse than no hand at all, **and that the refusals to join two things are thirteen and the registrar's own count is twelve and neither of them changed on the thirtieth of this month.**

Then she said the rest of it, and it stood without anything said against it, and nobody who was standing there was going to interrupt her.

"**A book a stranger can walk up to is a place a house can look for a man, and I said that sentence in a yard on the twelfth of the third month, and you knew it when you asked me for the book, and you did not say it.**"

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against said the other half of it before anybody asked him for it, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he said it first and that she is not going to put a number on it, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got twenty-six.

"**I knew it. I did not say it. I am not going to stand in a room and tell you that makes it a different thing.**"

No one said the opposite. A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man and the registrar of this district's records office were in the same yard at the same hour on purpose for the ninth time since the eighteenth of December and that she was not asked to write the arranging down as anything, **and that what was said between them is in a minute in her own words and a minute in a person's own words is not a document this district can produce and was not produced on the thirtieth and is not going to be.**

A man of fifty-six said that the two of them have been in a room twice in a month and that one of those was a refusal and one of them was this, **and that a person who is refused and a person who is blamed are two different instruments and neither of them is a resolution, and that a slate went past a kitchen in Alder Reach on the fifteenth of this month and neither of them wrote on it and that is not to be asked about.**

**AND ON THE THIRTIETH OF THE THIRD MONTH A HOUSE'S EIGHTH SHEET SAID THAT THE TOLLS OF A WATER ROUTE ARE FASTENED TO A PERSON AT THE BACK OF A BOOK, AND THE REGISTRAR OF THIS DISTRICT'S RECORDS OFFICE REFUSED TO ENTER THE NAME AND GAVE THE REASON, AND SHE SAID IN FIFTY WORDS THAT HE KNEW IT AND DID NOT SAY IT.**

---

The rest of the thirtieth was at the scale and it was the tolls and it went on until the light went, and about fourteen people were there and a clerk of nineteen years wrote down what was said and did not write down three things that were said.

A woman of about thirty-four who keeps a goat at the third house up the lane from the scale said the first of the three, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got fifty-eight, and it went in the minute in her own words.

"**I said no to a sheet in the second month and I did not sign a sheet and I am in this water with my name not written on anything, so tell me what I owe and I will pay it, and if you cannot tell me then you have put me in a thing I cannot leave.**"

Nothing was said against it. A man of fifty-six said that a household which refused a sheet in the second month and is now in a pool of tolls has not broken a term and has not signed one, **and that a toll is not a contract and a refusal of a contract is not a refusal of a toll, and that the two of those are not joined and are not going to be joined by a yard.**

Then the woman of fifty-eight came down off the hill for the first time in two days and stood at the scale unasked, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody sent her and that nobody put a first thing to her.

She said the second of the three things, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got twenty-six.

"**Nine years on that hill for nothing and now you want one of them. Put it on the page and I will show anybody the page.**"

Nobody contradicted her. A man of fifty-six said that a woman who gave four yoke-loads a day for nine years for nothing and is asked for one of them as a toll has been asked for the same thing twice, **and that the two asks are not joined and are not added and that the second of them has a page and the first of them does not have one and that is the whole of the difference and it is not a small one.**

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against said the third thing, and he said it to the yard and not to her, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got forty-one.

"**It stops at me. That is what a person at the back of a book is for and I did not know that when I said the words on the twenty-seventh and I would not have said them if I had.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the man the figure of twenty-one years is against has undertaken to stand at the back of a public book against the tolls of a water route kept by a route keeper and worked at a scale by four things, and a person at the back of a book is not a bearer of a toll and is not a keeper of a route and is not a security and is not one of the five, **and that a house may now come at him for a toll, and that this is the change and the change is entered with what it takes.**

A man of fifty-six said the other half of it, and he said it in the voice he uses at a counter, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got forty-six.

"**He can pool them now. He could not pool them a fortnight ago and he could not be found either, and a man who cannot be found cannot be called in, and that was not a protection and it was the same thing as a protection.**"

**AND ON THE THIRTIETH OF THE THIRD MONTH A POOL OF TOLLS EXISTED IN THIS DISTRICT FOR THE FIRST TIME SINCE THE SECOND OF JANUARY, AND A HOUSE'S SHEET NAMED THE FIGURE AGAINST IT, AND A WOMAN WHO KEEPS A GOAT AND A WOMAN WHO CARRIES WATER WERE BOTH IN IT ON TERMS NEITHER OF THEM HAD AGREED TO, AND THE CALL ON THE FIGURE STOPS AT A MAN WHO IS NOT A BEARER OF IT.**
