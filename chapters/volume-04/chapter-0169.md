# Chapter 169: A Week Is a Week

---

The first week of the second month ended on the seventh and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said in the yard at about ten in the morning on the sixth that a week is a week and that the day the water came was a day inside it and that nobody has ever been able to say which one.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the nearest date this district had for nine days was a week and that the week is over, **and entered that a claim on a place in a building cannot be worked out in a week and cannot be worked out in a day either, and that the third of the four printed things says a claim passes if a family does not pay on the day and that no document in this district has ever named the day.**

Nobody argued. A man of fifty-six said that the bucket said the first of the second month and that the sheet on a nail in a shed said the first week of a named month, and that a woman carrying water and a house with a nail were two instruments and that the two have not agreed since the twelfth of January, **and that a woman with a bucket was right and a house with a nail was right too, and that a person cannot have two right instruments and get a day out of it.**

**AND ON THE SIXTH OF THE SECOND MONTH THE FIRST WEEK OF THE SECOND MONTH WAS ALMOST OVER AND NOBODY IN THIS DISTRICT COULD SAY WHICH DAY THE WATER CAME ON, AND A CLAIM ON A PLACE IN A BUILDING CANNOT BE WORKED OUT IN A WEEK AND CANNOT BE WORKED OUT IN A DAY EITHER.**

---

The man of about fifty-five who keeps the flood shelter at the sluice end of a lane off the lower terrace came up the bank on the sixth at about eleven in the morning and said that he had put a day in a space and that there was one name against it, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he came and that nobody sent for him.

He said the other half before he was asked for it and gave no reason, and it is in the minute in his own words and it is forty-one words, and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got forty-one and said it out loud.

"**One man came in on the fourth with a cart wheel under his arm and slept here two nights and went on the sixth, and that is the first day I have written since the fourth year I wrote the heading.**"

That went past unargued. A man of fifty-six said that one day is one and is not a rate and is not a column, **and that a page with one day on it and about forty names on it is a page that has started and cannot be unstarted, and that a man who has started a thing in his own book in the fourth month of a year he did not choose is a person and not a clerk of anything.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the figure of one was said by him and has not been checked and was not asked to be checked, **and that a man who has written one day and will write the next one is doing a thing this district has no name for and is not going to be given one this week.**

Nobody took that up. A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked, in the six things, whether anybody had come from this yard to read the page, and the man of about fifty-five said no, and said that a stranger came on the fifth and looked at it and did not ask him anything, and that the stranger wrote nothing down.

**AND ON THE SIXTH OF THE SECOND MONTH A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-FIVE WHO KEEPS THE FLOOD SHELTER AT THE SLUICE END OF A LANE OFF THE LOWER TERRACE HAD PUT ONE DAY IN ONE SPACE ON THE FOURTH OF THE SECOND MONTH AGAINST ONE NAME, AND A STRANGER CAME ON THE FIFTH AND LOOKED AT THE PAGE AND ASKED HIM NOTHING AND WROTE NOTHING DOWN.**

---

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said the rest of it and it is in the minute in his own words and it is forty words, and nobody counted them because a clerk of nineteen years said she would not count a sentence she had heard twice in four days.

"**We put five terms of a house in a public book and a man with a cart wheel wrote one day on a page in a lane, and neither of them has been read by anybody who was not sent.**"

Nobody in the yard said anything and a man of fifty-six said that a thing a stranger can go and look at that nobody goes and looks at is not a security and is not nothing, **and that the rule which came into the yard on the second of January says the only remedy for a thing that cannot be inspected is to take it down, and that a thing which can be inspected and is not inspected is a third thing, and this district has not got a word for the third thing.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a third thing is not a column and is not a finding and is not one of the six instruments built and not named, **and that a rule which arrived in a yard in January has now been asked a question in February that it does not answer, and that the rule is a rule and is not to be retired to make a question go away.**

A woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel said that the first of the four posted securities has been readable for nine years and nobody has ever read it, **and that a day a week and three bound copies of a book is a thing a stranger can go and look at, and that this district wrote that down about itself on the eighth of March and has been living inside it ever since and has not once gone and looked.**

Nobody took that up and a man of fifty-six said that the two are the same sentence said about a book in a market and about a page in a lane, and that he is not going to say they are the same thing, **and that a clerk of nineteen years was asked to say whether they are the same and said no in four seconds and entered the count of refusals to join two things as thirteen and her own as twelve and neither moved on the sixth of the second month.**

**AND ON THE SIXTH OF THE SECOND MONTH A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FOUR WHO MENDS FENCING SAID IN FORTY WORDS THAT THIS DISTRICT HAS PUT A HOUSE'S TERMS IN A PUBLIC BOOK AND A MAN HAS PUT ONE DAY ON A PAGE IN A LANE AND NEITHER HAS BEEN READ BY ANYBODY WHO WAS NOT SENT, AND THE REFUSALS TO JOIN TWO THINGS STAND AT THIRTEEN AND THE REGISTRAR'S OWN COUNT STANDS AT TWELVE.**

---

The slate went past the kitchen in Alder Reach for the tenth time on the evening of the sixth of February and the two of them stopped in the lane again and neither wrote on it, and the man of about nineteen who walked in from the road was not behind them this time and did not hear anything and was not asked.

The registrar of this district's records office said one thing in the lane and it is in the minute in her own words and it is twenty-seven words, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she said it in a lane and not in a room and that the two are two things.

"**I am going east on the ninth. I have not asked you and I am telling you, and you can do what you like with the day.**"

Nobody asked her why and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody asked her why and that the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was, **and that a person who says she is going somewhere in a lane and is not asked why is a person and not a question, and that a question which is not answered is not a question that has been put into a column and there is no column for it.**

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against said that he is going on the ninth as well, and that he did not ask her and that she did not ask him, and a clerk of nineteen years entered both of those, **and entered that two people going to the same place on the same day is not a conversation about the two of them and is not to be read as one, and that she is not going to be the person who reads it as one.**

**AND THE SLATE WENT PAST THE KITCHEN IN ALDER REACH FOR THE TENTH TIME ON THE SIXTH OF THE SECOND MONTH AND THE REGISTRAR OF THIS DISTRICT'S RECORDS OFFICE SAID IN TWENTY-SEVEN WORDS IN A LANE THAT SHE WAS GOING EAST ON THE NINTH AND HAD NOT ASKED HIM AND WAS TELLING HIM, AND HE SAID HE WAS GOING ON THE NINTH AS WELL, AND NEITHER OF THEM ASKED THE OTHER.**

---

The standing instruction, and it is the third time she has said it and a clerk of nineteen years entered that it is the third time and that the count of it is not a column, and she said it in the yard on the seventh of February without being asked and nobody asked her why.

"**A page a person keeps in a building is a record. My office has a standing instruction about those and nobody has ever told me what is in it, and I have now said so three times in a yard and the clerk is not asking me why.**"

Nobody took it up. A man of fifty-six said that a standing instruction nobody in a room has been told the contents of is not an instrument and is not a rule and is not one of the five things this district does not have, **and that a clerk of nineteen years is not going to ask her why in order to get an answer to a fifth thing, and that the answer to what a public office is for was not given on the seventh of the second month either and that nobody asked.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a man who sells on a tram arch has this district's figure on a board somewhere in his own hand with his own price against it and will not say where he got it, **and that a registrar who has a standing instruction and will not say what is in it is the same shape, and that the two are not joined, and that a clerk of nineteen years entered the refusal and did not add it to the thirteen.**

---

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing went down to the ward market counter on the seventh of February and asked for the bound copy of the public lot book that was bound in the ninth year, and a clerk of the ward market gave it to him and did not go and get the key, and he read it in the market for about two hours.

A man of fifty-six went with him and a clerk of nineteen years did not, and the two of them came back at about one in the afternoon and the copy went back on the shelf it came off, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a security nobody has ever read was read on the seventh of February and that a clerk of the ward market did not charge for it and was not asked to.

The man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the man the figure of twenty-one years is against is in that book four times, and that three of the four are closings and one of the four is a figure standing against a name, **and that the figure of twenty-one years is in a book in a market where a stranger can ask for it and has never been asked for it, and that the district spent the second of January deciding that a list of securities is itself a security and has had the list on a shelf for nine years.**

Nobody argued. A man of fifty-six said that a book on a shelf in a market is a security that has been looked at once in nine years and that the once was the seventh of February, **and that a man who reads his own name in a book and finds four of them has not been found out by anything, because the book was never hiding, and a thing that is not hiding is not hiding and that is the finding.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the copy is bound in the ninth year and that the oldest of the three is bound in the fourth year, **and that a man asked for a copy of a book in the ninth year and was not told what was in it, and that the not-telling is not a refusal and is not entered as one, and that the two occasions are nine years apart and are not joined.**

A man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper on a lane off the lower terrace said that a posted thing nobody has ever come to look at is worth as much as a thing nobody posted, and that this district said that on the second of January, **and that the difference between the two is now one day, because somebody did come and look, and one day is not a rate.**

---

A man of about thirty-four who digs loam and does nine days of it in ten said that the fifth of the five is a man who is not on the page and that the first of the five has been on a shelf in a market for nine years, **and that between them they are the whole of what the man the figure of twenty-one years is against cannot say out loud, and that he read the first of them on the seventh and read the fifth of them into a room on the ninth of January, and that the two are not the same kind of thing and are not joined.**

A man of fifty-six said that a man can go and look at four of his five and cannot go and look at the fifth without standing at a counter thirty-four miles east, **and that the distance is the whole of what is wrong with the list and that a list is a thing you can carry and the four of the five are a thing you can carry and the fifth is a thing thirty-four miles away.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a distance is not a reason and is not a finding and that the reason the list is not written is entered and is not a delay, **and that the reason has changed its shape three times since the second of January and has not moved, and that a shape is not a figure and three is not a column and the two are not added to each other.**

A man of about thirty-four who digs loam and does nine days of it in ten was not in the yard on the seventh and had sent no word and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he was not in it and that she was not asked about him and that the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was, and that a man who digs loam nine days in ten has been in this yard on ten of the last twenty days and that today is not one of them and the figure was not counted by anybody.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked a question in the yard on the seventh that nobody answered and it went into the minute with nobody's name against it, and it is in his own words and it is twenty words.

"**Who is the man who would write the fifth of the five down, if there was somewhere to write it.**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds and a man of fifty-six said that the question was not answered and that a question which is not answered is not a question that has been put into a column, **and that there is no column for it and none was made, and that the man who posted a man is the only man in this district who can take him off, and that this is not going to be a column either.**

The eighth of February was the day a woman of about forty-four who keeps a Road House on the tide did the thing she had not done in a month, and she did it in the yard at about four in the afternoon and about nine people were there and she was not asked to do it.

She took the sheet out of her pocket and put it on the trestle table and said that the twenty-ninth of December had been forty-one days ago, **and that forty-one is a figure and that a house which has not written to anybody can write tomorrow, and that tomorrow has come forty-one times and that she is not going to say the number out loud twice in a yard.**

She was asked, in the six things, by a man of fifty-six, what she wanted done about it, and she said that she wanted the day named, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she was asked and answered and was not asked why she wanted a day named.

A man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper said that naming the day does not stop the day, **and that a house which has not written for forty-one days can write on any of them, and that a district cannot say the day is the twenty-ninth of December to a house thirty-four miles east without also saying that the district knows the day, and that a house that learns a district knows the day has learned something.**

The room let that stand and a man of fifty-six said that is the best argument anybody has made in this yard for doing less, and that it is also wrong, **and that a district which says nothing protects nobody and that a district which says everything hands a house a date it did not have.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the day is the twenty-ninth of December and that it is forty-one days before the eighth of February, and that the claim did not pass and that the only reason it did not pass is that nobody did anything, **and that a sheet in a pocket is not a book and a man of about twenty-three who keeps the copy is not a record, and the count of documents nobody owns is three and did not move.**

A woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel said that a sheet which has been in a pocket for forty-one days is not a document and is not a security and is not nothing, **and that the only page in this district that knows on what day a family's claim passes is a copy on one page in the coat of a man of about twenty-three who is not paid and does not own it, and that the count of documents nobody owns is three and the copy is not a fourth.**

Nobody argued with it and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked the man of about twenty-three who copies for nothing whether he would let the sheet be read out, and the man of about twenty-three said no in four seconds and gave the reason in a sentence that a clerk of nineteen years entered in his own words and it is thirty-two words.

"**A copy of this read out in a market is a copy with a house's name on it. I will not be the man whose copy says the name of a house.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that he said the second half of that before he was asked for it and that she is not going to put a number on it, **and that a man of about twenty-three who will not have a house named in his copy has now refused the same thing the woman of about forty-four refused in December, and that the two are two people and are not joined and neither is a column.**

Nobody took that up. A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a district which has three pages and not one of them about water had a fourth kind of thing this month in a lane off the lower terrace, **and that a page with a day in it is a page, and a page is a thing a stranger can go and look at, and that the man who mends fencing read a bound copy of the lot book on the seventh and the man of about fifty-five wrote a day on the fourth, and that the two are a reading and a writing and are not the same act and are not joined.**

A man of fifty-six said that the boy is nine years older in forty-one days and not one day older, **and that the second of the four printed things says the named person shall not be below fifteen and that a boy of nine is two thousand one hundred and ninety days from that, and that a clerk of nineteen years is not going to work that sum out in a yard.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the second of the four printed things is on a counter thirty-four miles east in different words and that the district has had both of them in a book in its own market since the twentieth of January, **and that a family that has a sheet in a pocket and a house that has the same term in a book two days away are two families in one sheet and this district has not asked the house what it is doing with the second one.**

**AND ON THE EIGHTH OF THE SECOND MONTH A WOMAN OF ABOUT FORTY-FOUR WHO KEEPS A ROAD HOUSE ON THE TIDE PUT A SHEET ON A TABLE IN A YARD AND SAID THAT THE TWENTY-NINTH OF DECEMBER HAD BEEN FORTY DAYS AGO, AND A HOUSE THAT HAS NOT WRITTEN TO ANYBODY CAN WRITE TOMORROW, AND TOMORROW HAS COME FORTY TIMES.**
