# Chapter 158: The Second Reservoir

---

They went out of the yard of Lot Seventeen at about ten in the morning on the sixth of January, and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against walked, and Tarin Kest had a horse that was not his and said the same thing

And there were no two of them on the road who had been asked to go and the reason was given in the yard and not on the road, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the two of them were on the road for three days and that nothing on the road was entered on the day it happened, and that this is the first gap in this district's record since the eighth of March and that nobody has made a rule about it.**

Tarin Kest said the reason before he was asked for it. He said that a road keeper who is asked to take a man to a place he keeps for a living is a man who has been given a job, and that the district has spent eleven weeks refusing a mark for a job, **and that he was not asked and that he is not going to be paid and that the not-asking is a fact and carries no number on it, because a number on it would have been a column and a column is a thing a person is paid for.**

There was no clerk of nineteen years on the road and there was no minute, and a man of fifty-six said that in the yard before they got to the gate, and that what comes back from a road is a page in a man's own hand and that a page in a man's own hand is worth less than a minute and is not the same thing.

The room let it stand and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against said that he had been told the word worthless in October by a man who sold on a tram arch and had not been able to answer it then and could answer it now, **and that eleven weeks of minutes and one page in a man's own hand is a better rate than a district with no minutes at all and no page, and that a page is not a minute and is worth less and has a man's memory in it, and that a man's memory is the one instrument in this district that nobody has ever written a rule about.**

The road up is about eleven miles and it climbs, and Tarin Kest did not talk for the first four of them, and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against thought about the thirty-first of March for two of those four miles and about the eighty-six days that run to it from the fourth of January, and about the fact that a figure he had said out loud in a yard four days before was a number and not a thing he could spend.

**AND ON THE SIXTH OF JANUARY THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST AND TARIN KEST WENT OUT OF THE YARD OF LOT SEVENTEEN AT ABOUT TEN IN THE MORNING AND NEITHER OF THEM WAS ASKED TO GO AND THE REASON WAS GIVEN IN THE YARD AND NOT ON THE ROAD, AND THERE WAS NO CLERK ON THE ROAD AND NO MINUTE, AND WHAT CAME BACK WAS A PAGE IN A MAN'S OWN HAND.**

---

The second reservoir is about nine miles further and it is up where the road stops being a road, and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against had not seen that much standing water in one place in the eighteen years after the Long Fracture, and he stood on the bank for longer than he meant to and did not say anything at all for about a minute.

It is a mile and a bit long and it is full to a mark painted on a stone at the near end, and the mark is a foot below the top of the stone, and the stone has a second mark nine inches above the first, and Tarin Kest said the upper one is from the flood year and that nobody has cut it off, **and that a mark nine inches above the mark the water is standing at is the only account of the flood this basin has that is not in a book.**

Nobody asked him why not and he said it before he was asked: a man who cuts a mark off a stone is a man who says the water will not come that high again, and that he is not the man who will say it.

On the far end there is a shed with a door and a bolt and a window, and inside the shed there is a book on a shelf and a nail in the wall, and the nail is not a board and is not one of the five, **and a clerk was not there to say so and the man who can be found said it out loud on the bank afterwards and a man of about thirty at a counter heard him and said that a nail is not a board in this basin either.**

The man of about thirty at the counter is a clerk of the Underwriters of the Upland and has been there four years, and he came out into the yard because two men had come up the road, and he was not asked anything and asked the man the figure of twenty-one years is against what he wanted, and the man who can be found said he wanted to see the deliveries, **and the man of about thirty said that nobody had come up that road to see a delivery in four years and that he was not asked whether he minded and that he did not mind and that the two are two things.**

> **DELIVERIES FROM THE SECOND RESERVOIR. THE COLUMN HEADED HOLDING IS THE PLACE THE WATER GOES TO. THE COLUMN HEADED HAND IS THE PERSON WHO SIGNS FOR IT. THE PERSON WHO SIGNS FOR IT IS NOT ASKED WHAT IT IS FOR. THE DELIVERY IS MADE IN THE FIRST WEEK OF A NAMED MONTH AND NOT ON A NAMED DAY.**

Nobody argued about it and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against read it out twice because the rule of the counter is that a document goes out twice, and a clerk of nineteen years was not there and that rule is not a law and he said so out loud before the second reading.

The man of about thirty at the counter then took the sheet off the nail and put it in a book, and the book is a delivery book and it is not the sheet, and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against asked whether he might copy the four sentences out and take the copy down the road, and the man of about thirty at the counter said no in about four seconds and gave the reason in a sentence.

"**The book is the house's and the sheet is the house's and there is no copy of either, because a copy of this is a piece of paper with no house on it.**"

Nobody took it up and Tarin Kest said, standing in the shed doorway, that a man of about twenty-three in this district has a rule that a copy is the document and the original is a room, and that the man at the counter has the other half of it, and that the two halves are not joined and that the district's half is the one that lets a person who cannot read stand in front of a thing.

The man of about thirty at the counter said yes in four seconds when it was put to him that way, and then said the second half before anybody asked him for it, which is a thing about four people in this district do and which a clerk of nineteen years is not there to count.

Then the man the figure of twenty-one years is against asked to look at the book itself and was allowed, and the book is ruled to a year and the columns are ruled in ink and the rule is straight, and the hand column is filled in a hand that is not the holding column's hand, and he said so out loud and the man of about thirty at the counter said it was so and had not noticed and has four years of it in front of him.

**AND ON THE SEVENTH OF JANUARY THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY AT THE UPLAND REFUSED IN FOUR SECONDS TO LET THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST COPY THE FOUR SENTENCES AND GAVE AS THE REASON THAT A COPY OF THAT IS A PIECE OF PAPER WITH NO HOUSE ON IT, AND THE HAND COLUMN IN THE DELIVERY BOOK IS FILLED IN A DIFFERENT HAND FROM THE HOLDING COLUMN AND HE HAD NOT NOTICED.**

---

They came to the half-way house at about two in the afternoon and there was a sheet in a window frame with the four lines on it and the frame was painted round the sheet and not the sheet, and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against stood in the road and read it through the glass and could not read the third one because of a crack and did not ask anybody to move the frame.

The man of about thirty at the counter had come down with them and said that every house keeps it in the window and that the frame is painted round the sheet so that the sheet cannot be taken out without it being noticed, and that this is not a law and is done because a man who has been given a thing by a house in bad weather is not going to hand it back.

Nobody took it up and Tarin Kest said that a sheet nailed up in a window frame four miles from a counter is the only version of the four lines in this basin that a person can walk up and look at without asking anybody anything, **and that the district has spent eleven weeks insisting that a document a person can go and look at is the whole of the method, and that this sheet is on a frame in a window and the frame is the only security anybody in this basin has ever seen on one.**

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against said that he had looked at about nine of these in eleven weeks and had not seen one on a wall, and that a wall is a thing a district has and a window frame is a thing a house has, and that the difference between the two is the whole of what he came up the road to find out.

**AND ON THE SEVENTH OF JANUARY A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY WHO KEEPS THE DELIVERY BOOK OF THE UNDERWRITERS OF THE UPLAND READ OUT A SHEET FROM A NAIL IN A SHED AT THE SECOND RESERVOIR, AND THE THIRD OF ITS FOUR SENTENCES SAYS THAT THE PERSON WHO SIGNS FOR THE WATER IS NOT ASKED WHAT IT IS FOR, AND A NAIL IS NOT A BOARD AND THE COUNT OF BOARDS IN THIS DISTRICT IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE.**

---

The sheet has nine holdings on it and the fourth of the nine is a farm four miles out past the loams with a house and four fields and a sluice of its own, and the man who can be found read the fourth line twice and Tarin Kest read it over his shoulder and neither of them said anything for about nine seconds, **and the man of about thirty at the counter said that the fourth has been on the sheet every year he has kept the book and that nobody has ever asked him why the fourth is the fourth and that he does not know.**

The sheet has a tenth line under the ninth and the tenth line has no holding on it and has a figure against it, and the man of about thirty at the counter said that the tenth line is the house's own and that a house waters its own house, **and that there is no column on the sheet for what a holding is and that a column that is a place is one of the five things this district does not have and did not move.**

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against asked him what happens to the tenth line in a month when the house does not want the water, and the man of about thirty at the counter said that the house does not have months, and then said the second half before he was asked for it, and gave no reason.

Nobody argued with it. Tarin Kest said out loud on the bank afterwards that a row with nothing in it and a figure against it is a third thing and that the third thing has no word in any book anybody in this district has read, and that there is a ledger of nineteen rows and four columns in a house that guarantees things and that there are sixteen of those rows, and that he was not going to say the two are the same and would not have said it in the shed.

And the man who can be found said no in four seconds, and gave the reason in a sentence, and the reason is in his own hand on a page and not in a minute.

"**A rota is a list of places and a ledger of a house is a list of people, and joining them would be me deciding a thing I am the wrong person to decide.**"

The man of about thirty at the counter asked him what the district wanted, and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against said he wanted to know whether the water was real, and the man said it was standing in front of him and had been since the fourth year, and he said he knew,

And the man said it in a way that was not unkind and was worse than unkind, **and that was the moment of the two days for him, and he wrote the whole of it on the page in the shed and then wrote over the top of it four words and left the four words, and nobody in this district knows what the four words are and he is not going to be asked.**

**AND THE NINTH OF THE FOUR LINES ON THE SHEET IS A HOLDING AND THE TENTH IS NOT A HOLDING AND HAS A FIGURE AGAINST IT, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY AT THE COUNTER SAID THAT THE HOUSE DOES NOT HAVE MONTHS, AND A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY WHO COPIES FOR NOTHING'S RULE THAT A COPY IS THE DOCUMENT AND THE ORIGINAL IS A ROOM WAS SAID OUT LOUD ON A BANK BY A MAN WHO WAS NOT ASKED.**

---

The second thing the man of about thirty at the counter said was the reason he had come out into the yard, and he said it in a room with two strangers in it and did not lower his voice, and Tarin Kest entered in his own hand afterwards that the room was a shed and that a room with a stranger in it is a different instrument from a room with the district's people in it, and that the man of thirty knew that and said it anyway.

He said there are about four houses in this basin that put a sheet in front of a family before a season, and that his house writes the four lines on the face of every one of them, and that the other three write between two and five and do not write a third of it, and that the people who come to his counter with a hurt are nearly always holding one of the other three.

Nobody took it up on the road. The man the figure of twenty-one years is against asked him one question and it is on the page in his own hand and it is fifteen words, and he asked it because it was the only one he had wanted since the twenty-fifth of December.

"**Why does your house write them on the face if it costs you the family?**"

The man of about thirty at the counter said that it does not cost him the family and that the family that reads four lines on the face of a sheet is a family that has somewhere to take it, **and that there are four houses and the one that writes plainly is the one everybody in this basin tells the others about, and that the district has spent eleven weeks learning from the wrong one and that he is not saying that as a kindness.**

He said the last part before he was asked and gave no reason, and the man who can be found wrote it down and did not ask him why he had said it, and wrote that down too.

Tarin Kest asked whether anybody had ever refused the sheet and the man of about thirty at the counter said yes and that the refusals are about nine a year and that a refusal is a thing the house writes down and that the house has four hundred years of those, **and that the four hundred years are not in this basin and that the two are two things and that he was not going to be asked where they are.**

**AND A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY WHO KEEPS THE DELIVERY BOOK OF THE UNDERWRITERS OF THE UPLAND SAID THAT THE HOUSE HE KEEPS THE BOOK FOR WRITES THE FOUR LINES ON THE FACE OF EVERY SHEET AND THAT THERE ARE ABOUT FOUR HOUSES IN THIS BASIN THAT PUT A SHEET IN FRONT OF A FAMILY AND THAT THE DISTRICT HAD BEEN LEARNING FROM THE ONE THAT TELLS ON YOU.**

---

They came back into the yard of Lot Seventeen at about four in the afternoon on the eighth of January and it had been three hundred and thirty-one days since the eleventh of February, and a man of seventeen wrote the figure in an index in a hand that is not a clerk's hand, and beside the index there are six dates on one line and a rule under the sixth and no seventh date.

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against put the page on the trestle table and a clerk of nineteen years read it out in the yard because a page is read out, and she read it out twice, and the rule of the counter is that a document goes out twice, and she entered that it was a man's account of three days and not a minute and that the difference between the two is whose memory it is, **and that a page in a man's own hand that has been read out twice in a yard is a document and that is the one instrument this district built on a road and has not named, and the count of six did not move today.**

The room let it stand and a man of fifty-six said that a minute can be argued with and a page in a man's hand can be argued with the man, and that the district has spent eleven weeks building the first and has been given the second by two men who were not asked to go, and that the two are two things and that the second one is thinner.

The finding of the yard that afternoon was the fourth of the nine holdings, and a man of fifty-six read it out and a clerk of nineteen years entered it, and Tarin Kest said the other half of it on his own account and was not asked, and it is thirty-two words.

"**Your shelter at the sluice is fed off the fourth holding, and the fourth holding is a farm with a sluice, and when the fourth holding stops taking, your tank stops filling.**"

Nobody took it up for about nine seconds. A man of fifty-six said that a shelter which is fed by somebody else's column is a shelter with a bearer and that the district has a page of six columns in a yard and no page of six columns for a building, **and that nobody in this district has ever written down who bears a shelter, and that about nine hundred households in this district are in no row of anything and the two are not the same and are not joined.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a man of forty-three who buys standing seed-crop offered two thousand four hundred coppers and a cart of meal for four fields and a sluice and a house on the twenty-ninth of December and that he went west and said he would come back when somebody could tell him who owns it, and that he has not come back and that nobody has sent for him.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody sent for him and that the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was, **and entered that Selik Marne was not asked to go and was not on the sheet and did not meet the woman whose name is in no register in the west bank, and that the fourth line of an offer that has been on a table three times has not been read out loud in this district and was not read out loud on the seventh of January either.**

The registrar of this district's records office was in the yard when they came in and asked him what he had brought back, and he said a page in his own hand, and she said that a page in a man's own hand is a page and not a minute and that she knows what to do with a page, and then said the other half before he finished asking what that was.

"**And what I can do with it is put it in a book where a person comes and looks, and it will be there on the twelfth of January and nobody will be harmed by it.**"

Nobody took it up and a man of fifty-six said that the harm was going to be done in the second month and not in January, and that the fourth of the nine holdings is the only piece of the water in this district that can be moved a mile, and that he is the man who is on the page of six columns and is not on the page of the delivery book and that he knows which of the two he would rather be on.

The man of about thirty at the counter was asked, in the five things, by a woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel, whether the house has ever stopped a delivery, and he said yes, and that he does not know what happens when it does because it has not happened, **and that the four hundred years of refusals in another basin are not in this one and that he was not going to be asked where they are and that he was asked that once and refused once and the two occasions are not joined.**

Then the last of the day, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the count of unentered days since the twenty-fourth of November is forty-five and is a count of days and not a column, **and that three of the forty-five are days this district spent on a road, and that a day spent on a road is a day a clerk of nineteen years had to be told about afterwards, and that she was, and that telling is not the same as being there**

And the nearest delivery on a nail in a shed at the second reservoir is in the first week of the second month, **AND THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST CAME BACK FROM THE UPLAND WITH A PAGE IN HIS OWN HAND THAT IS THINNER THAN A MINUTE, AND THE FOURTH OF NINE HOLDINGS IS A FARM WHOSE OWNER IS ABSENT AND WHOSE SLUICE IS THE ONLY PIECE OF THE WATER THAT CAN BE MOVED A MILE, AND A SHELTER AT THE SLUICE IS FED OFF IT.**
