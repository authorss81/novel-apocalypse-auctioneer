# Chapter 154: The Owner Is Absent

---

The man who came to the yard of Lot Seventeen about the farm on the twenty-ninth of December had been in the yard before ten in the morning, and he had a fold of paper in his coat and he took it out before he sat down, which a man of fifty-six said afterwards is the mark of a man who has been told to produce a thing and not a man who has come to ask one.

He is about forty-three and he buys standing seed-crop and has bought it for eleven years out of a yard on the Verge, and he said his own name and the name of the farm in the same breath and a clerk of nineteen years entered both. **The farm has a house, a yard and four fields, and a sluice of its own, and it lies four miles out past the loams where the lane stops being metalled and the water runs to it for about a mile and a half.**

The fold of paper is a copy of an entry in a register thirty-four miles away and the entry names a man who is the legal owner, and a man of fifty-six read the entry out and a clerk of nineteen years read it back and got nothing wrong, and the rule of the counter is that a document goes out twice.

> **THE FARM NAMED IS HELD OF THE REGISTRY OF THE WESTERN TIDE. THE PERSON NAMED IN THE ENTRY IS THE OWNER. NO ENTRY HAS BEEN MADE SINCE THE ENTRY OF THE FOURTH YEAR. THE ADDRESS OF THE PERSON NAMED IS GIVEN AS ONE HOUSE AT A CROSSING.**

Nobody argued with it and a man of fifty-six asked the man of about forty-three how long ago the person named had been at the house at the crossing, and he said nobody at the yard he buys in has seen anybody at that house in about four years, **and that he did not come to ask whether the man is alive, and that a register is a register and not a mystery, and that a man who buys standing crop does not need the owner to be alive and only needs to know who can be called on.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that he said the last part before he was asked for it and that the count of that is six and is not a column, **and that the six are not joined to each other and that a man who says a thing before he is asked for it has not thereby been asked a question and that the two are two things.**

A man of fifty-six said that this is the first document in eleven weeks that this district cannot argue with and did not make, and that the charter of four sheets is a document it cannot argue with and did make, and that the difference between the two is not the paper. **A charter may be closed by a person who is not the person who built it and may not be closed by a person who is not a party to it, and this register has no party to it in this district at all, and that is the difference, and it is not a paper difference.**

Nobody took that up and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a register is a thing a person goes and reads and not a thing a person goes and argues with, **and that the district's own strongest instrument is a page of six columns on a board of eleven in this yard, and that a page a person can write on is a page a person can argue with, and that this register cannot be written on from here and that is the whole of the finding and it took a man from the Verge to bring it.**

Then the man the figure of twenty-one years is against asked what the buyer wanted, and the man of about forty-three who buys standing seed-crop said he wanted to sow the four fields in the last week of the first month of the year, and that his buyer is two days' walk away and does not care who owns it, and that he will pay two thousand four hundred coppers and a cart of meal against the standing crop and the sluice.

A clerk of nineteen years entered the sum and entered that it is not added to the two thousand coppers or the four hundred coppers or the one thousand six hundred, **and that the two thousand four hundred is a new figure and is in one book and is not in any other, and that there is no rate anywhere in this basin that turns coppers into a year and there never has been.**

A man of fifty-six said that the buyer is offering a sum and that a sum is the easiest kind of security to look at, and that the district has five posted securities and not one of the five is a sum, and that a man who is proud that his public lot has no hidden terms is being handed the only instrument he has ever understood in the wrong direction.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the trouble is not the sum, **and that a lot has seven parts and the seventh is a person, and that a lot with no bearer is not a lot, and that a thing that is not a lot cannot be sold, and that the man who said that in a field in September has not been asked to say it again and does not need to be.**

Nobody asked the man the figure of twenty-one years is against what he intended to do, and he said that he intended to make a lot of the four fields and a sluice and a house and a standing crop, and that he sat down at about half past ten in the morning and wrote the first four parts out on the back of a page and could not write the fifth, and that the fifth is the bearer.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a man is not a field, and a woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel said that a house is not a field, and a clerk of nineteen years entered both in one line and entered that the two are two things and are not joined to each other, **and that a bearer is not one of the five things this district does not have.**

The five are a way of saying what a toll lands on a place, a column that is a place, an office, a station with nobody at it, and a way to pay a person who is not in a household, and a clerk of nineteen years entered the five out loud in the yard in the same order as in July because a clerk reads a list the way the list is written, **and that the count of the five is five and did not move on the twenty-ninth of December.**

**AND THE COUNT OF THINGS THIS DISTRICT DOES NOT HAVE IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE ON THE TWENTY-NINTH OF DECEMBER, AND THE FIVE ARE A WAY OF SAYING WHAT A TOLL LANDS ON A PLACE, A COLUMN THAT IS A PLACE, AN OFFICE, A STATION WITH NOBODY AT IT, AND A WAY TO PAY A PERSON WHO IS NOT IN A HOUSEHOLD, AND A BEARER IS NOT ONE OF THE FIVE.**

And on the twenty-ninth of december **A MAN OF ABOUT FORTY-THREE WHO BUYS STANDING SEED-CROP OFFERED TWO THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED COPPERS AND A CART OF MEAL FOR FOUR FIELDS AND A SLUICE AND A HOUSE, AND THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST WROTE OUT FOUR PARTS OF A LOT ON THE BACK OF A PAGE AND COULD NOT WRITE THE FIFTH, AND THE FIFTH IS THE BEARER, AND A THING THAT IS NOT A LOT CANNOT BE SOLD.**

---

He wrote them out anyway, and a man of fifty-six asked him to turn the page round, and he turned it round, and the four parts were on it in his own hand and were correct, and a clerk of nineteen years read them back and got nothing wrong and the rule of the counter is that a document goes out twice.

A lot is a thing, a use, a bearer, a toll, a term, a release and a remedy, and the man of about forty-three who buys standing seed-crop asked him which of the seven was the fifth, and he said that the fifth is the bearer and that the four he had written were the thing, the use, the toll and the term.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked whether the term was a year and the man said that the term runs to the end of the standing crop and that he had put the end of the standing crop in it and that the standing crop is the fourth of the fields.

The room then tried four ways of naming a bearer in about half an hour, and a clerk of nineteen years entered all four in the minute in the order they were tried, and none of the four is the answer and the four are not joined to each other.

The first was the owner in the register, and a man of fifty-six said that the owner has not been at the house at the crossing in about four years and that a person who is not at a house is not a bearer of four fields, and that the register does not say the man is dead and that nobody in this room is going to say it for him.

The second was the three households who work the farm, and a woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel said that a household is a count and not a person and that three of them is a figure and that a figure cannot hold a toll, and that the district has been told this before about a page and did not hear it.

The third was a mark, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said no in four seconds and gave the reason in a sentence, and the reason is in the minute in his own words and it is twenty-two words, **and that a clerk of nineteen years entered that the boundary was built around that rule and not in defiance of it and that the count of refusals of a mark for a job is one and is not a column.**

"**A mark may not carry a job and a field is a job, and the line of stones is not a mark.**"

The fourth was the buyer, and a man of about forty-three who buys standing seed-crop said no himself before anybody asked him, and said that a man who is going to be given a thing is not the man the thing is given to, and that if he were the bearer he would be paying himself.

**AND THE FOURTH OF A LOT IS THE THING, THE USE, THE TOLL AND THE TERM, AND THE FIFTH IS THE BEARER, AND THE OWNER IN THE REGISTER HAS NOT BEEN AT HIS HOUSE IN ABOUT FOUR YEARS, AND THREE HOUSEHOLDS ARE THREE COUNTS AND NOT A PERSON, AND A MARK MAY NOT CARRY A JOB, AND THE BUYER REFUSED BEFORE HE WAS ASKED, AND A LOT WITH NO BEARER IS NOT A LOT.**

Nobody argued and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against folded the page and put it inside his coat, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the page is not in any book in this district and that the four parts are correct and that the fifth is absent, **and that a lot with no bearer is not a lot and a thing that is not a lot cannot be sold and cannot be given away and cannot be left to a family, and that the three of those are the same absence seen three ways.**

Nobody took that up and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the page is the largest wrong figure this district has produced since the eighth of March, **and that a figure a man wrote down wrongly is a figure that can be argued with in about four years, and that a Binder who writes four parts of seven on a page and folds it into his coat has bought himself four years of a stranger asking him what the fifth was.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-NINTH OF DECEMBER FOUR WAYS OF NAMING A BEARER WERE TRIED IN ABOUT HALF AN HOUR AND NONE OF THE FOUR IS THE ANSWER, AND A FIGURE A MAN WROTE DOWN WRONGLY IS A FIGURE THAT CAN BE ARGUED WITH IN ABOUT FOUR YEARS, AND A BINDER WHO WRITES FOUR PARTS OF SEVEN ON A PAGE AND FOLDS IT INTO HIS COAT HAS BOUGHT HIMSELF FOUR YEARS OF A STRANGER ASKING HIM WHAT THE FIFTH WAS.**

---

Three households work the farm and none of them owns it, and a man of fifty-six asked them, in the five things, at about one in the afternoon, whether they had been told by anybody that it was for sale, and one of them said no and one of them said that a man had been through the yard on the Verge and asked what the rent was and that he was not a household and sells salt by the load.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the two answers are two answers and not one household, **and that a man of about thirty-seven who sells salt by the load out of a yard on the Verge has now been in this story twice without being in a room, and that a person who is not a household and is not in a room is a third thing with the sixteen rows and the two are not joined.**

A woman of about forty-four who has kept a lane house at the far end of the four miles came up on the second day with a bundle on her back and said the one thing she had come to say, and a clerk of nineteen years entered it in her own words, and it is thirty-one words, and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got thirty-one and said it out loud.

"**Nobody has told us anything and I am not going to say that is wrong, because I have had eleven months of somebody telling me things and then not doing them.**"

Nobody argued with it and a man of fifty-six said that the district has a name for a promise that is not kept and it is not a good name, and that a man who does not know whether he is a bearer of four fields is not a man who has been given a thing and is a man who has been left out of a thing, **and that a person left out of a thing is not the same as a person who refused it and that the two are two things.**

Tarin Kest came into the yard at about half past two in the afternoon on the second day and had come west on a road that is not his and had not been asked, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he was not asked and that he was in the yard and that the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was.

He is a route keeper and he keeps the road, and a man of fifty-six asked him, in the five things, what the water at the farm does in the fourth month, and he said it comes up to the second field and stays there about eleven days and that the lane is under it at the crossing, and that he has put a plank down at that crossing four times in four years and taken it up three.

A clerk of nineteen years entered the eleven days and the four times and the three times, **and entered that a figure a route keeper gives about water in a yard is not a figure a clerk enters as a finding, and that the eleven days is not added to the about nine days a bed of brine takes to go back into the water, and that the two are two things and are not joined.**

A man of fifty-six asked him why the buyer wanted a farm that stands in water in the fourth month, and Tarin Kest said that a buyer two days' walk away does not want the farm and wants the sluice, **and that a sluice is the only piece of the water in this district that can be moved a mile, and that a man who buys a farm for a sluice is a man who will sell the farm without the sluice and keep the sluice, and that the two are not two things and are one thing with a price on it.**

Nobody took it up and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a man who buys a farm for a sluice is a man who intends to become an underwriter without knowing the word, and that a word a man does not know is not an instrument and that the district has six instruments it has built and not named, **and that a sluice bought with a farm in it is a seventh and that the count of six did not move today and that nobody is entering a seventh.**

He said that in a yard and a clerk of nineteen years entered it and a man of fifty-six said he was not going to have the word underwriter put in a minute in this district before the twenty-fourth of December is out of the book, and that a word put in a minute becomes a category and a category becomes a list and a list becomes something people are counted in.

**AND ON THE THIRTIETH OF DECEMBER TARIN KEST SAID IN A YARD THAT A BUYER TWO DAYS' WALK AWAY DOES NOT WANT A FARM AND WANTS A SLUICE, AND A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FOUR WHO MENDS FENCING SAID THAT A MAN WHO BUYS A FARM FOR A SLUICE IS A MAN WHO INTENDS TO BECOME AN UNDERWRITER WITHOUT KNOWING THE WORD, AND A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX REFUSED THE WORD IN A MINUTE AND SAID THE COUNT OF INSTRUMENTS THE DISTRICT HAS BUILT AND NOT NAMED IS SIX AND DID NOT MOVE.**

---

The buyer went west on the second day and said he would come back when somebody could tell him who owns it, and a man of fifty-six said that the answer is not a delay and that the answer is that a clerk of nineteen years is right and that the finding is on the page and the page does not need the finding to be comfortable.

A man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper was in the yard on the second day and was asked, in the five things, whether the fold of paper was a forgery, and he said it was not, and he gave the reason in a sentence, and the sentence is in the minute in his own words and it is twenty-nine words, and a clerk of nineteen years counted them once and got twenty-nine and did not count them again.

"**A forgery is a thing made to be wrong. That is a true copy of a real book and it is worse and there is nobody to catch it.**"

Nobody took it up and a man of fifty-six said that a true copy of a real book is a document that cannot be argued with because the book cannot be argued with and the copy cannot either, and that this is the second time in two days that a document this district cannot argue with has come from outside it, **and that the count of instruments it has built and not named is six and did not move and that a fold of paper is not an instrument and is not to be entered as one.**

A clerk of the ward market was asked, in the five things, at about four in the afternoon, how long a letter to the registry of the western tide takes, and said four months, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that and entered that a letter has gone twice on this and that the two answers arrived in about four months both times and that neither of the two answers was the same as the entry and that the two are not joined.

A man of fifty-six said that the district's own instrument is a book that works if a person comes to the yard and that this entry is in a drawer four months away, **and that a person two days' walk away is never going to come to this yard, and that being the only person who can say no to a thing is not the same as being able to stop it, and that the registrar said that in September and the two occasions are not joined.**

Nobody asked the registrar why she holds two lines apart and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody asked her and that she was not going to enter a number on it, **and that the refusals to join two things stand at thirteen and her own count stands at twelve and that neither moved on the thirtieth of December, and that the question of what a public office is for was not answered on the thirtieth of December either, and that the name of it is in five places and in no register and that eleven trades could not state what it did in September and could not state it on the thirtieth of December.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the register's standing question is the one the district has been carrying since the eighteenth of December, and said it out loud in the yard, and it is in the minute in his own words, and it is twenty-eight words, and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got twenty-eight both times and did not say so.

"**What does a district do with a document it cannot certify and cannot argue with, and the answer is that it has to be able to do both.**"

Nobody took it up and a man of fifty-six said that the charter of four sheets cannot be certified and cannot be argued with and that the register of the western tide cannot be certified and cannot be argued with, **and that the difference between them is that this district can close one of them in a room with nineteen people in it and cannot do that to the other in four months, and that a document a district can close is not the same as a document a district can argue with, and that the two are two things.**

The train on the siding had stood three hundred and twenty-two days on the thirtieth of December and three hundred and twenty-one on the twenty-ninth, and a man of seventeen wrote both figures on one line in an index in a hand that is not a clerk's hand, **and a clerk of nineteen years read Ivo Stenn's separate sheet out because a minute is read out, and got twenty as printed and nineteen as called, and entered both figures and entered that the count of refusals to read is nine and that the departure is unspent.**

Nobody said anything about the farm after about five in the afternoon and a clerk of nineteen years entered that, and entered that the last entry of the thirtieth of December is nine lines and that it names a farm with a house and four fields and a sluice and one figure and no name of an owner, **and that the ninth line says what a district does with a document it cannot certify and cannot argue with and that the ninth line is a question and the minute has no answer under it.**

And the farm was not sold in December, **AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FORTY-THREE WHO BUYS STANDING SEED-CROP WENT WEST AND SAID HE WOULD COME BACK WHEN SOMEBODY COULD TELL HIM WHO OWNS IT, AND A LETTER TO THE REGISTRY OF THE WESTERN TIDE TAKES FOUR MONTHS AND HAS GONE TWICE AND THE TWO ANSWERS WERE NOT THE SAME AS THE ENTRY, AND THE ANSWER IS NOT A DELAY, AND THE TOMORROW IS THE THIRTY-FIRST OF DECEMBER.**
