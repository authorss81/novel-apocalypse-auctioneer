# Chapter 165: Who Bears a Building

---

The lane off the lower terrace is not metalled for about sixty yards and then stops being a lane, and they went down it again on the twenty-fourth of January, the man the figure of twenty-one years is against and the man of about fifty-five who keeps the flood shelter at the sluice end, and there was no clerk down there and nothing was entered on the way and both of them said so.

The building is one room and a stove and four shelves, and a woman of fifty-eight who carries water and does not read figures was at the stove with a kettle and was not asked for anything and said that she comes there to sit and that sitting is not the same as being in a shelter.

Nobody took it up and a clerk of nineteen years was not there and the only record of the twenty-fourth of January is the page the man of about fifty-five keeps, and a man of fifty-six said afterwards in the yard that a page in a man's own book is thinner than a minute and is not the same thing.

---

He had asked for a day to be put against a name, and the man of about fifty-five said no in four seconds, and then said the other half before anybody asked him for it, and it is on a page in the man the figure of twenty-one years is against's own hand because there was nobody else there, and it is forty-four words.

"**That space is for a mark and a mark is a thing a house puts beside a name, and if I put a day in it a man will come and tell me a day is not a mark and he will be right.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that afterwards, in the yard, in her own words, and that she was not asked about the four seconds, **and that the heading on the page says that no line on it is for a mark beside a name and that he wrote the heading himself in the fourth year, and that a heading a man has written himself is the one instrument in this district nobody is allowed to argue with and that this district has not been arguing with it for four years.**

Then he went back to the shelf and took the page out from under the blanket and put it on the table face up, and the fourth page of four is the one with the column on it, and beside about forty names there are about forty spaces the width of a finger and nothing is in any of them.

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against counted them twice and got about forty both times and did not say so, and a clerk of nineteen years entered afterwards that the figure of about forty was said by him and has not been checked and was not asked to be checked, **and that a figure a person says about another person's page is a figure and not a count and is not entered in a column of any kind.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF JANUARY THE FOURTH PAGE OF THE BOOK IN THE FLOOD SHELTER AT THE SLUICE END HAD A COLUMN HEADED WITH THE NAME OF A PERSON WHO IS IN THE BUILDING AND ABOUT FORTY NAMES IN IT, AND BESIDE EVERY ONE OF THE NAMES THERE IS A SPACE ABOUT THE WIDTH OF A FINGER AND NOTHING IS IN ANY OF THEM.**

---

The question was asked in the lane on the way back up and the man of about fifty-five asked it of himself out loud, and it is in no minute anywhere and a man of fifty-six entered it in the yard afterwards in his own words and said that he was not asked for it and did not ask.

"**A lot is a thing and a use and a bearer and a toll and a term and a release and a remedy, and a building is not any of those, and nobody bears this building, and the fourth column on a sheet eleven miles up a road can be signed by anybody at all.**"

Nobody took it up. A man of fifty-six said that a lot with no bearer is not a lot and a thing that is not a lot cannot be sold or given away or left to a family, and that this district found that in December about a farm, **and that a building was never in the argument because a building is not a thing anybody in this district thought to sell, and that a building is a building and a lot is a lot and the two have never met in any book this district has read.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that about nine hundred households in this district are in no row of anything, and that about nine of them stand in this lane in a cold month, and that a household is a count and a person is a count, and that a person who is in a building is a count that stops being a count the day they leave and does not stop on the page.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the page says a person is entered on the day they arrive and not on the day they leave, and that this is a third thing and is not a column, **and that a building which cannot record a leaving has about nine hundred households' worth of people in its own book and about nine of them standing in a lane, and that the two figures are in two places and are not added.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF JANUARY THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-FIVE WHO KEEPS THE FLOOD SHELTER SAID OUT LOUD IN A LANE THAT NOBODY BEARS THAT BUILDING, AND THAT A LOT IS A THING AND A USE AND A BEARER AND A TOLL AND A TERM AND A RELEASE AND A REMEDY, AND THAT A BUILDING IS NOT ONE OF THE SEVEN, AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS ENTERED IT AFTERWARDS IN A YARD AND WAS NOT ASKED FOR IT.**

---

The tank is at the back of the building and it is stone and it is about a person deep, and the woman of fifty-eight who carries water put her hand flat on the stone and said that it is down to about two days and that she has been filling it since the ninth year with a bucket and a yoke.

Nobody argued with that. A man of about thirty-four who digs loam and does nine days of it in ten was in the yard in the afternoon and said that a tank is fed off the fourth holding on a sheet nailed in a shed eleven miles up a road, and that the fourth holding is a farm, and that the farm's owner has not been at his house in about four years.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the fourth of the nine holdings is a farm four miles out past the loams with a house and four fields and a sluice of its own, and that the person named in the entry of the registry of the western tide is the owner, **and that a register is a register and not a mystery, and that nobody in this district is going to say for the man at the crossing that he is dead, and that the not-saying is not entered as a claim and is not a column.**

A man of about fifty-six said that when the fourth holding stops taking the tank stops filling, and that a man of about thirty who keeps the delivery book of the Underwriters of the Upland said that in a shed on the seventh of January, and that the two occasions are not joined because one of them was said in a shed and one was said in a yard and a page and a minute are not the same instrument.

Nobody argued **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the fourth holding has not been the fourth for any reason anybody has given, and that the man of about thirty said he does not know why the fourth is the fourth, and that the two of those are not a finding and are not entered as one.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF JANUARY THE TANK AT THE BACK OF THE FLOOD SHELTER AT THE SLUICE END WAS DOWN TO ABOUT TWO DAYS BY THE HAND OF A WOMAN OF FIFTY-EIGHT WHO CARRIES WATER, AND IT IS FED OFF THE FOURTH HOLDING ON A SHEET NAILED IN A SHED ELEVEN MILES UP A ROAD, AND THE FOURTH HOLDING IS A FARM WHOSE OWNER HAS NOT BEEN SEEN IN ABOUT FOUR YEARS.**

---

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked a question in the yard at about eleven in the morning on the twenty-fifth and nobody answered it and it went into the minute with nobody's name against it, and it is in his own words and it is twenty-seven words.

"**If nobody bears the building, what has a house got in its hand on the day it says it holds a claim on a place in it?**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds. A man of fifty-six said that a claim on a place in a building is a claim on a room in a room, and that a house that holds it holds a piece of paper about about sixty people and that the piece of paper is not a room, **and that a man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper said the whole of the law about it in January and that the whole of the law is that the building does not sell places, and that a house holding a claim on a place in a building is holding a claim on nothing that anybody can be shown.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the question was not answered and that a question which is not answered is not a question that has been put into a column, **and that there is no column for it and none was made, and that the man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper was entered before he spoke as not speaking for the room and that this is a count of not-speaking and not of not-askings and the two are not added to one another.**

A woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel said the other half of it, and she said it before anybody asked her, and it is in the minute in her own words and it is thirty-eight words, and the man of about nineteen counted them and got thirty-eight and said it out loud.

"**A house with a claim and no room is a house with a key to a room it has never been inside, and it will give that key to somebody one day when it wants the sheet back.**"

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six said that a printed gap is the cheapest thing a house can build, and that this house has built the opposite of a gap and that the opposite of a gap is not more expensive, **and that a sheet with no gap in it is a sheet a person cannot get anything out of, and that this district spent the twenty-fifth of December learning that and has not spent a day since putting it to work.**

---

The registrar of this district's records office came down the lane on the twenty-fifth at about one in the afternoon and looked at the fourth page for a quarter of an hour and did not touch it, and she was not asked why she came and she was not asked what she thought.

She said one thing about it when she came back up the bank and it is in the minute in her own words and it is forty-three words, and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got forty-three and said it out loud.

"**A record kept by a person in a building is a record, and my office has a standing instruction about records kept by persons, and I have never once been told what it is, and I have now been down that lane twice.**"

Nobody took it up and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she said it and that nobody asked her why and that the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was, **and that a standing instruction nobody in a room has ever been told the contents of is not an instrument and is not a rule and is not one of the five things this district does not have, and that the count of the five did not move on the twenty-fifth of January.**

A man of fifty-six said that she has said that sentence before and that he is not going to say where, and that the two occasions are not a column and that he is not going to be asked about either of them.

---

The rest of it was the page and a man of fifty-five who could not be asked about it, and a woman of about thirty-six who keeps a scale at the sluice end of the loams came up the lane and looked at the column for a while and did not touch it.

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against asked him what he would do if the fourth holding stopped taking, and he said he would fill the tank with a bucket and a yoke like the woman of fifty-eight does, and that he has one yoke and the woman has one and there are about nine households in this lane, and that a bucket is not a route and a yoke is not a road.

A man of fifty-six said that a man who has one yoke and a woman who has one is a district with two yokes and about nine hundred households, and that a district is a figure and a figure is not a plan, **and that the twenty-first year after the Long Fracture is not the year this is going to be worked out in and that he is not going to say what year it will be.**

Nobody took that up and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he said a year out loud in a yard and that a year is not a thing a room decides, and that the twelfth hour is a clock and this district has never agreed whose, **and that the eighteenth year is the one this district is in and that the flood year is the ninth and that a year counted from the flood and a year counted from the day a book was closed are two years and are not joined.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the man of about fifty-five has answered the question nobody in this district has answered, and that his answer is a yoke, **and that a man who has answered a question with a yoke has answered it better than a man who has answered it with a clause, and that a clerk of nineteen years was asked to write the yoke down as a remedy and said no in four seconds and gave no reason.**

She said that she has stood at a scale nine years and that a column of names is the only kind of page she has ever been asked to keep anything on, **and that a scale has a pan and a weight and a man and the four of them are four and a column has a name and a space and a rule and the three of them are three, and that a page is not a scale and a scale is not a page.**

A man of fifty-six said that she said the second half of that before she was asked for it, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she was entered before she spoke as not speaking for the yard, and that this is a count of not-speaking and is not a count of not-askings, and that the two are not added.**

A woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel said that the page has no name against the heading of the page, and that the heading is the name of a person who is in the building, and that a heading which is also a person is a fourth thing in this district, **and that a page in the ward market's own book has a heading and nothing against it, and that the two are two pages and are not a column and are not a pattern.**

The room let it stand **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that neither of the two pages moved and that the figure of a hundred and eighty-three coppers is on one of them and that the count of about forty names is on the other, and that the two figures are not added and that there is no rate in this basin that turns one into the other.**

**AND THERE ARE TWO PAGES IN THIS DISTRICT WITH A HEADING AND A COLUMN AND NOTHING IN THE COLUMN AGAINST ANYTHING, AND ONE OF THEM HAS A FIGURE ON IT AND THE OTHER HAS ABOUT FORTY NAMES, AND THE TWO FIGURES ARE NOT ADDED AND THERE IS NO RATE IN THIS BASIN THAT TURNS ONE INTO THE OTHER.**

---

A man of about thirty-four who digs loam and does nine days of it in ten was in the yard on the twenty-fifth and said the other half of the sluice, and the other half is the half nobody has said out loud in eleven weeks, and it is in the minute in his own words and it is fifty-four words.

"**A sluice is the only piece of the water in this district that can be moved a mile, and it is four miles out past the loams on a farm nobody owns, and nobody has ever come and asked me to open it and I would not know whose it was to answer for.**"

Nobody took it up and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the farm is still unsold, and that a man of about forty-three who buys standing seed-crop offered two thousand four hundred coppers and a cart of meal for the standing crop and the sluice on the twenty-ninth of December, and went west on the thirtieth, **and that he has not come back and that nobody has sent for him and that the not-sending is entered and the count is where it was.**

A man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper said that a sluice is worth more than four fields and that a man who buys a farm for a sluice will sell the farm without it, **and that the figure of two thousand four hundred coppers is in one book and is in no other and is not added to the two thousand coppers or the four hundred coppers or the one thousand six hundred, and that there is no rate in this basin that turns a copper into a year and there never has been.**

A man of fifty-six said that nobody is going to open a sluice on a farm with no bearer and that a sluice is a thing and not a lot, **and that a thing with no bearer cannot be sold, cannot be given away and cannot be left to a family, and that the fourth column of a sheet in a shed is signed by a house's own clerk and by nobody else.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the count of things this district does not have is five and that a bearer of a sluice is not one of the five, and that a column that is a place is one of the five and did not move on the twenty-fifth of January.

---

On the twenty-fifth of January the yard did the thing it does, which is stand about a dozen people in it until late, and a man of fifty-six read out at about eleven in the morning that the count of unentered days since the twenty-fourth of November is sixty-two and is a count of days and not a column, and that the days are not added to the askings and the askings are not added to the days.

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against said one thing about the tank and it is in the minute in his own words and it is forty-four words, and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got forty-four and said it out loud.

"**I cannot find out who bears a building and I cannot post against a tank and I cannot ask the man at a counter to write a name in a column, and the three of those are the whole of what I can do.**"

Nobody argued. A man of fifty-six said that a man who lists three things he cannot do on the twenty-fifth of January has done more in eleven weeks than he did in eleven days in the autumn, **and that a list of things a man cannot do is not a lot and is not a security and is not a page and is not entered in any of this district's books, and that a man who keeps one in his own head keeps it for nothing.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the Underwriter's power is not begun in this district and that the reason is entered and the reason is not a delay, **and that a man who cannot list what he has posted cannot pool a single toll, and that the fifth of his five posted securities is a person who walked east on the nineteenth of January to stand at a counter, and that a person is not a list.**

**AND THE UNDERWRITER'S POWER IS NOT BEGUN IN THIS DISTRICT AND THE REASON IS ENTERED AND THE REASON IS NOT A DELAY, AND THE FIFTH OF THE FIVE POSTED SECURITIES WALKED EAST ON THE NINETEENTH OF JANUARY TO STAND AT A COUNTER, AND A PERSON IS NOT A LIST, AND NOBODY IN THIS DISTRICT HAS EVER WRITTEN DOWN WHO BEARS A SHELTER.**
