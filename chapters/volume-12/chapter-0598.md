# Chapter 598: A Woman Was Asked To Hold One Of The Sheets In Her Own Shop In Front Of Customers And Said Again That She Cannot Tell Which Of Them Is This District's And Her Shop Was Open

"**I have been asked four times in a month to tell a person which of two sheets is this district's, and I have said four times that I cannot tell, and about nine people in my shop know that, and I am going to say the same thing this morning in this yard with my door open, and then I am going to carry a page up that bank and put it on my own counter and I am not going to shut my shop to do it and I am not going to be thanked for it.**"
