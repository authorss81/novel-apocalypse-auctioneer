# Chapter 631: A Clerk Of Nineteen Years Said Out Loud On The Seventh Of The Sixth Month That She Was Going To Count A Month Off A Board In Front Of Everybody And The Count Of That Lane Is Five And Did Not Move

Entered that the count of counted months is four and did not move, and entered that she said out loud this morning what she is going to do on a morning two days off, and entered the reason, which is that the reason comes before the hand, and the record about the not asking says not asked.

About four of them have said that a clerk of nineteen years has entered a thing on that table every morning for nineteen years and has never once said before she did it what she was going to do.

"**The board carries five hundred and twenty-nine, the train on that siding has stood eight hundred and forty-five days, nobody has entered anything for five hundred and fifty-nine, and five hundred and twenty days separate the second of January and this morning.**"

"**That leg is loose and it has been loose since before the frost, and I have been waiting for a morning when about nineteen people were standing about in here, and I have one question and I have been carrying it up and down that bank for a week, and I am going to put it tomorrow morning and not this morning, and about four of you have worked out that I have put tables up for nineteen years and have never asked one question out loud in front of a crowd.**"
