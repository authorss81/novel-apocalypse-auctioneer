# Chapter 277: In A Room With A Shut Door

---

The twenty-seventh of the sixth month was clear and still and about nineteen people were in the yard of Lot Seventeen at about ten in the morning, and the chair at the end of the trestle table had been standing there sixty-five days and nobody had sat in it on any of the sixty-five.

The man of about thirty-four who mends fencing gave the three figures and the man of fifty-six read the board out over his shoulder and got the other two right.

"**A hundred and eighty-five days on the board and five hundred and one days on the train, and the days nobody has entered since the twenty-fourth of November stand at two hundred and fifteen, and a hundred and seventy-six days separate the second of January and this morning, and the first day of the month after next is thirty-five days off counted off this morning, and I have asked this yard to go and tell about nine people that there is a room with a shut door in it four hundred yards from that gate at about four o'clock this afternoon, and I have not told this yard yet what is going to be said in that room, and I am going to leave at twelve and not say.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man who has said out loud in a yard that he is not going to say what he is going to say has not thereby asked anybody to come and hear it, and that about nine people were asked in a yard by a man in about half an hour and that nobody refused and that the record about the nine not refusing says not asked, and that a person who has been asked to come to a room and has come has not consented to what happens in the room.

---

About nine people were in the room where the books are at about four in the afternoon, which is about nine feet by eleven with one table, one shelf, one chair, a door, and a fanlight over the door that lights nothing and has not lit anything in four months, and two books on the shelf, one of which was not there on the sixth of this month.

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against shut the door and said out loud, before he said anything else, that the woman of this district's records office said on the eighteenth of this month in about nine feet of lane outside that door that there is no room in this district that nobody can walk into, and that he was going to use this one, and that the only thing shut in this room this afternoon is the door, and that he wanted that said out loud before anybody in the room could say afterwards that they did not know which room it was.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that about nine people were in that room, and that a clerk of nineteen years was four hundred yards away in a yard and was not told about it until about seven, and that the record came from the man the figure of twenty-one years is against and not from the woman who keeps that room, and that a conversation is not a record and is not a document and did not become a fourth of the three documents this district does not own.

Then he said the reason out loud, and it was counted and came to three hundred and ninety-two, and a clerk of nineteen years was not in that room and entered it afterwards out of his hand.

"**I am choosing the second of the two shapes. I am choosing the lot about the room and not the lot about the night, and I am going to say why in a room with a shut door in it before anything is signed anywhere, and I am not doing that because a shut door makes a thing safe, because a shut door does not make a thing safe, it only makes it repeatable, and what I want is for it to be repeatable, because I have found out this month that a thing said in a yard is a thing about nineteen people and I would like this one to be a thing about the nine of you and about me. Here it is. The first shape is a lot about a night. A lot about a night names everybody who heard that bell. To put a child of about eight into a lot like that in this basin you write his name, or you write where he sleeps, and where he sleeps is the same thing with more words on it. To write his name you have to ask him. Nobody in this district has ever asked that child a single question in his life and he is about eight years old and he is not in a book. The second he is asked he stops being a person nobody has asked and he starts being a person who was asked, and this district found out on the seventeenth of this month, in a yard, at about ten in the morning, in front of about nineteen people, what asking a person does to a person, and I was the man who asked. And his name goes into a book in this basin that a stranger may walk up to and read, and a name put on a figure cannot be taken off it by the person it is put on, and he will be eight for another year and forty when it could be taken off him. There is no version of an accurate lot that does not cost that child the rest of his life, and I have looked for one for two days and I have not found it, and I am not going to be the man who decides that on a child's behalf in a yard.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man who has said out loud that he is choosing between two shapes of a thing has not thereby made a finding and has not thereby bound anybody, and that a lot is an instrument and a document and that neither creates a consent and neither makes a person a bearer and neither turns a right to give up into a refusal, and that a person who has not been asked has not refused and cannot be counted either way and the record about the child says not asked, and that a man who convenes nine settlements is not a representative of a settlement that has not asked to be represented.

Then the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps the room with nothing over the door said one thing, and it was counted and came to a hundred and sixty-three, and a clerk entered that she was asked one question in that room and that she answered it and that nobody asked her a second one.

"**Ask me the one question and I will answer it and then it is asked and it cannot be unasked, and that is the deal and I am not able to improve on it. No. He is not to be named and he is not to be described either, and the reason is that a description of where a child of about eight sleeps in this district is two miles long and about nine houses and one lane with nothing over the door on it, and any of you could read it and know where he is, and I have kept that room for four years on the understanding that nobody in this basin could find it, and a man with a case found it in nine minutes on the fourteenth of this month because a woman at a house on that lane told him where it was, and that was not my fault and it was the end of the four years.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a woman who keeps a room is not a clerk of a house, that a refusal with a reason is not one of the refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given, and that the eight did not move at about half past four in the afternoon on the twenty-seventh of this month, and that a room with nothing over the door is not an address and a wall is not an address and a lane with nothing over the door on it is not an address, and that the record about the four years says not asked, and that a person who has said out loud that a thing has been found who had been keeping it is not a consent and is not a refusal and cannot be counted either way.

The man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said one thing, and it was counted and came to a hundred and fifty-four, and a clerk entered that nobody in that room argued with it and that the record about their not arguing says not asked.

"**I argued in that yard yesterday for the other shape and I have not changed my mind and I am not going to pretend I have, and I am going to lose this and I want it entered that I said the true thing about the third column of that covenant and the true thing about the man of fifty-six and the man with a case, and that I would like somebody to notice that the child of about eight is the reason the other shape is not the right one and that the child of about eight did not have anything to do with it. I do not know what I would have done. I would have written it. I would have been right about the hole and I would have been wrong about him, and I have not got an answer to that and I am not going to be given one.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man who mends fencing is not a clerk of a house and is not a bearer of a lot and is not a party of the first of the two shapes or the second of the two shapes, and that a man who says out loud that he would have done a thing has not done it and has not refused it, and that the record about the answer he is not going to be given says not asked.

Then a man of fifty-six said the last thing in that room, and it was counted and came to a hundred and seventy-two, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that it was the only thing said in that room that was not about the child.

"**On the night of the twenty-fourth I was not in that building with two doors. I walked four miles out past the loams in the dark to be standing under that frame when it went, and I got there about two minutes after it stopped, and a man of about thirty-four was standing there with a rope in his hand about a foot and a half long, and neither of us said anything for a bit. I am fifty-six. I have not walked four miles in the dark since I was a different age and I got back up that road in about an hour and ten minutes with a hand that has not been right since and I have not said so, and I am saying so in a room with a shut door in it because I would like one thing said out loud in this district this month to have been said by a man who was not in a building when it happened and did not know either.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the man of fifty-six walked four miles out past the loams and four miles back in the dark on the night of the twenty-fourth, being eight miles on his own two feet, and that nobody sent him and that the record about the sending says not asked, and that a man who was not in a building when a thing happened has not been in a building and is not a keeper of one and is not a bearer of one, and that a body is not a figure about a man and that his hand is not a price of anything.

The day came down about half past seven. The sixth month was twenty-seven days old, the first day of the month after next was thirty-five days off, and nothing had been signed anywhere, and a sheet of paper with nine names on it in nine hands was still face up on the end of a trestle table in a yard four hundred yards from a room with a shut door in it, and the record about the not signing says not asked.

**AND ON THE TWENTY-SEVENTH OF THE SIXTH MONTH A MAN CHOSE THE PARTIAL LOT IN A ROOM WITH A SHUT DOOR IN IT AND SAID THE REASON OUT LOUD BEFORE ANYTHING WAS SIGNED, AND THE REASON WAS A CHILD OF ABOUT EIGHT WHO HAS NEVER BEEN ASKED ANYTHING BY ANYBODY, AND A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX SAID IN THAT ROOM THAT HE HAD WALKED EIGHT MILES IN THE DARK ON THE NIGHT OF THE TWENTY-FOURTH AND HAD NOT BEEN IN THE BUILDING WHEN IT WENT.**
