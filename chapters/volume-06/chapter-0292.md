# Chapter 292: The Second Line

---

The twelfth of the seventh month was close and hot and about nineteen people were in the yard of Lot Seventeen by ten, and the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing gave the three figures and the man of fifty-six read the board over his shoulder and got the other two right.

"**Two hundred days on the board and five hundred and sixteen days on the train, and the days nobody has entered since the twenty-fourth of November stand at two hundred and thirty, and a hundred and ninety-one days separate the second of January and this morning, and the first day of the eighth month is twenty days off counted off this morning, and the ninth of the nine printed nights is eighty-three days back, and there is a table against a wall eleven miles off this morning that has stood up on six mornings and this is the sixth.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that twenty is nineteen days to the end of the seventh month plus the first of the eighth, and that the length of the eighth month is not stated and was not used this morning, and that the sixth of those mornings is the figure a man of about thirty-four who keeps a road said in that yard yesterday is a figure nobody has asked for, **and that nobody asked for it yesterday and nobody has asked for it this morning and the record about the not asking says not asked.**

At about half past ten the hand-cart came through the gate that takes two goes with a sheet of print rolled on the back of it, and there was no name on the sheet and no seal on it and no body named on it, and the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked for it to be read out in that yard, and a clerk entered that a yard reads a document when somebody asks it to read one and that nobody was asked on his behalf.

He read it out in the ordinary voice, in one run of words with a stop at the end of each of them, and there were five of them, and it was five on the fourth of this month.

> **AN OFFER TO PAY A PERSON WHO IS NOT ENTERED IN ANY HOUSEHOLD IS A FIGURE ABOUT PEOPLE WHO ARE NOT ENTERED IN ANY HOUSEHOLD.**
>
> **A PLACE WHERE A SHEET HAS BEEN READ OUT IS A PLACE WHERE AN OFFER IS MADE.**
>
> **THE PLACE AN OFFER IS MADE IN IS ENTERED IN THE RECORD OF THAT PLACE.**
>
> **A PERSON WHO IS PAID WEEKLY BY ANYBODY IS A PERSON WHO CAN BE FOUND.**
>
> **THE FIGURE OF THE PEOPLE IN THE GROUND IN THE SHADOW OF A BUILDING ELEVEN MILES OFF THIS DISTRICT IS TO BE COMPLETED BEFORE THE FIRST DAY OF THE EIGHTH MONTH.**

The man of about twenty-nine who drives a cart, who is not the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a room with nothing over the door and is not the man of about nineteen who counts, read it back and he got nothing wrong, which is the rule of the counter, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he was asked to read it back and was not asked whether he agreed with it and that reading a thing back is not agreeing to it.

Then the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said what the five lines are, out loud, in the ordinary voice, and it was counted and came to two hundred and eighty-three, and a clerk entered that nobody in that yard was going to be able to do anything with it before the end of the day.

"**I want the second of those five lines read out again and I want this yard to hear that it was not on that sheet eight days ago. The second of the four that came in here on the fourth of this month is the third on the sheet as it stands, and nobody moved a line. Nobody in this district wrote on that sheet. Somebody four hundred miles off printed one more line and put it in the middle, and the four of them that were here are the four of them still, and the one they called the second is the third now, and if a man in this basin has been reading a sheet and taking a number off a line he is holding the wrong number. Now the part I want said out loud. The second of the four says the place an offer is made in is entered in the record of that place. The new one says a place where a sheet has been read out is a place where an offer is made. On the second of this month I read five lines out in this yard at the request of a man with a cart and about nineteen people stood here and heard them, and nobody in this district asked anybody four hundred miles off to print that line, and nobody has asked for it since it came, and I would like that entered. A place a stranger can walk into has become a place on paper because it did the one thing this place has ever done, and it did it because a man with two hands that do not work asked it to.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man who mends fencing is not a clerk of a house and is not a bearer of a printed rule, and that the eight refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given did not move at about eleven in the morning on the twelfth of the seventh month, and that **a document that prints a consequence nobody asked for is not a consent and does not make a person a bearer and cannot be refused by anybody who has not been asked about it**, and that the second of the original four is the third of the sheet as it stands and the four lines of print and the five are not added together, and that the record about the not asking says not asked.

---

The woman who keeps the books in the room four hundred yards from that gate came up the bank at about twenty past eleven because about nine people had come up the bank out of that yard and were standing in the lane outside her door and a clerk of nineteen years was four hundred yards off in a yard and did not know she was at the door of her own room until about seven that evening.

She read the five lines from about nine feet away without being handed them and said one thing about them, and it was not counted, and a clerk entered that it was not counted and that nobody asked her for it and that the record about the not asking says not asked.

"**I have kept a book in a room nine years and in nine years not one line has come into that book because of something I did. There is going to be one now, or there is going to be one about this place, and I know what it says and I know where it is aimed and I am not going to be asked about it by anybody today, and I would like it entered that I said all of that before anybody asked me for any of it. I have not read the offer. I have not read any of it. That is not a refusal. I do not know what it is.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a registrar who keeps a book in one room by one person is not a clerk of a house and is not a bearer and is not a holder and is not a steward and is not a party of anything, and that a keeper of a room is not a thing she is and has never been asked to be, and that a woman who has said out loud that she has not read a document has not refused it and cannot be counted either way, and that the second book on that shelf has been open since the sixth of last month and was not read this morning and nothing was entered in it, and that a room with a shut door in it is not a counter and about nine people standing in a lane outside a door is not a market.

Then the man of about thirty-two who puts tables up for anybody who will use them said one thing back, and it was not counted, and about four people in that yard heard it and a clerk entered that it was not counted and that the record about the not counting says not asked.

"**I am not going to take that table down. I want that entered before anybody works out what it means, because it is the worst thing I have got in my hands this month and I have got two hands that do not work. If I take that table down then a body four hundred miles off has printed that this yard is a place where an offer is made and there is nothing standing at it, and the next man who comes down eleven miles of road with a hand-cart will not have a wall to put a table against in a place that is on a piece of paper, and I do not know how many of those men there are and I do not know what they are selling. I have got a fifth line on my own sheet that a man of about thirty-four told me in this yard on the second of this month is not true, and I wrote it there on purpose. I have got a second line on this one now that I did not write and that nobody asked for and that is aimed at a yard I am standing in. Both of those are mine and neither of those is a person.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man who says out loud that he is not going to take a table down is not a consent and is not a refusal and cannot be counted either way, and that a document does not become a person because its writer is frightened of it, and that the instruments this district has built and not named are six and that a table on a cart and a sheet of five lines of print and a figure of mornings are three of the things a clerk has been asked about this month and that none of them is a seventh of the six, and that the six did not move on the twelfth of the seventh month.

By about six the yard had the lengthening shadow of the west wall across it and about nine people were still on the low wall along the lane, and the sheet of five lines of print was still on the end of the trestle table with the second of the five turned over at one corner by somebody who had been reading it and holding it.

By about six the shadow of the west wall had come across that yard and about nine people were still sitting on the low wall along the lane, and the sheet of five lines of print was still on the end of the trestle table with one corner of the second line turned over by somebody who had been holding it down with a thumb.

She entered the remainder of what had not moved, in the same hand and not as a paragraph. Nobody put a line on a board on any of the hundred and three days since the first of the fourth month, counting the first day in. The chair at the end of that trestle table has stood eighty days and nobody has sat in it on any of the eighty. The six households of the man of about forty-eight who keeps a tally have not moved on any of the eighty-two days since the twenty-first of the fourth month, and she did not ask him. Three documents this district does not own, five protected things, four conditions with no end on it, thirteen refusals to join two things and the registrar's own count of twelve — and not one of them moved today, and a document that came into that yard this morning did not become a fourth of anything, and a place is not a sixth protected thing and a document is not a sixth and a figure is not a sixth.

The man of about thirty-four who keeps a road came through the gate at about half past four in the afternoon and said one thing about the new line, out loud, in the ordinary voice, and a man of about nineteen counted it and got two hundred and sixty-five, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**I have kept four miles of that road eleven years and I walked out to that wall on the fifth of this month behind his cart and back on the sixth, and there is a turning off the eleven mile road about a mile before that wall which is not on any sheet in this district and which nobody has marked and which is not going to be marked. On the twelfth of this month a body four hundred miles off has printed a line that says a place where a sheet has been read out is a place where an offer is made, and I have been standing in a yard listening to about nineteen people be pleased about that, and I would like to say one thing about it and then I am going to go home. A line of print has found a place. A turning is still not on anything. Those are two different things and only one of them has been written down this month, and the one that has been written down is the one nobody can use to get there, and the one that could get somebody there is the one that is not on a sheet. I am not being clever. I am a man who walks a road. Somebody in this basin is going to have to write that turning down at some point, and I would like this yard to have noticed today that the only new fact in this district arrived on a cart and is not a fact about how to get anywhere.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man who keeps a road is not a bearer of the road and has never been asked to be one, and that a turning that is not on a sheet is a turning and not an address, and that a printed line of print is not a turning and is not a map, and that a document that names a job that does not exist has found nobody and this one names none, and that the eight refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given did not move at about half past four in the afternoon on the twelfth of the seventh month.

The day came down about half past eight and the seventh month was twelve days old and the first day of the eighth month was twenty days off, and about nine people stood in a lane four hundred yards from a gate reading a printed line that nobody had asked for.

**AND ON THE TWELFTH OF THE SEVENTH MONTH A SHEET CAME INTO THE YARD OF LOT SEVENTEEN WITH FIVE LINES OF PRINT ON IT INSTEAD OF FOUR, AND THE SECOND OF THE FIVE SAID THAT A PLACE WHERE A SHEET HAS BEEN READ OUT IS A PLACE WHERE AN OFFER IS MADE, AND NOBODY IN THIS DISTRICT HAD ASKED FOR IT, AND THE SECOND OF THE ORIGINAL FOUR WAS THE THIRD OF THE SHEET AS IT STOOD AND NOBODY HAD MOVED A LINE.**
