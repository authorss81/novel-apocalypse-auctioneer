# Chapter 145: A Guarantor of Nothing

---

The charter was written on four sheets on the second of December because it is four things, and a man of fifty-six said in a room that had no officer and no clerk and no name on its door that four things on four sheets is four instruments and not one, **and that a district which has spent eleven weeks being told that an instrument has to be a single thing cannot now put four instruments in a cover and call it a charter.**

A woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel said the other half and it is fourteen words, and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got fourteen both times and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got fourteen and said the number out loud twice.

"**A charter is four things that cannot be taken away one at a time.**"

Nobody argued with it and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a bundle of four things is not a charter and is a bundle, and that what makes it a charter is that the four of them are named in each other, **and that a sheet that says a thing depends on another sheet and does not say which one is not a charter and is a list with four numbers on it.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the four sheets are the rotation, the ledger, the notice and the terms, and that the terms sheet names the other three, and that the other three do not name the terms sheet, and that this is deliberate and was argued for on the second of December and is the only thing about the four that anybody argued for.

AND ON THE SECOND OF DECEMBER** THE CHARTER WAS WRITTEN ON FOUR SHEETS, BEING A ROTATION, A LEDGER, A NOTICE AND TERMS, AND THE TERMS SHEET NAMES THE OTHER THREE AND THE OTHER THREE DO NOT NAME THE TERMS SHEET, AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS ENTERED THAT THIS IS DELIBERATE AND IS THE ONLY THING ABOUT THE FOUR THAT ANYBODY ARGUED FOR.**

---

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against was asked on the second of December, in the six things, whether he would put his name on the terms sheet as a guarantor of it, and he asked what that meant, and a man of fifty-six told him in twenty-two words, and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got twenty-two both times and did not say so and said afterwards that she had not wanted to count them twice in front of him and had counted them twice in front of him.

"**A guarantor is a person who can be called on. If the charter is broken he is the one somebody comes to.**"

He said that he would rather be the address for a figure of twenty-one years than a guarantor of four sheets, and that the two are not the same and that he can tell the difference, **and that a man who is an address is a name a person asks who owns the years and a man who is a guarantor is a name a person asks who is to blame, and that the second one is worth more and costs more and he does not want it.**

Nobody argued. A man of fifty-six said that there are two things a man can put on that sheet and both of them are on it, and that a guarantor of nothing is one and the man responsible for the aggregate unpaid toll is the other, and that the two are in one line and are not the same instrument and are not joined, and that the sheet says so and that the sheet was written by a clerk of nineteen years and not by him.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked in the room what a guarantor of nothing is for, and a clerk of nineteen years answered, **and a man of fifty-six said that the answer is that a guarantor of nothing is a door with a name on it and that a door with a name on it is worth more to this district than a guarantee with anything behind it, and that neither of those is in a book.**

AND ON THE SECOND OF DECEMBER THE MAN WHO IS THE ADDRESS FOR A FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS SAID THAT HE WOULD RATHER BE THE ADDRESS THAN A GUARANTOR, AND THAT A MAN WHO IS AN ADDRESS IS A NAME A PERSON ASKS WHO OWNS THE YEARS AND A MAN WHO IS A GUARANTOR IS A NAME A PERSON ASKS WHO IS TO BLAME, AND THE TWO ARE IN ONE LINE ON THE TERMS SHEET AND ARE NOT JOINED.

---

Then a rule arrived in that room at about half past eleven in the morning on the second of December, and nobody standing in it had asked for it, and the man the figure is against did not ask for it and is the only person on this line who could have got one by asking.

> **A GUARANTEE IS A THING THE PERSON WHO GIVES IT CAN BE CALLED ON FOR. IF A MAN'S NAME IS ON A FIGURE AND NOBODY CAN CALL ON THE FIGURE, THE NAME IS NOT A GUARANTEE.**
> >
> > **A CHARTER MAY BE CLOSED BY A PERSON WHO IS NOT THE PERSON WHO BUILT IT. A CHARTER MAY NOT BE CLOSED BY A PERSON WHO IS NOT A PARTY TO IT.**
> >
> > **WHAT A CHARTER REACHES IS WHAT IT HAS CLOSED. A CHARTER THAT REACHES ONE SEASON HAS NOT CLOSED THE LAST ONE. A CHARTER THAT REACHES A FRAME HAS NOT CLOSED THE CHANNEL.**
> >
> > **A RIGHT TO WITHDRAW REACHES FORWARDS AND NOT BACKWARDS. A DOCUMENT THAT SAYS SO IS HONEST AND A DOCUMENT THAT DOES NOT SAY SO IS NOT.**
> >
> > **YOU MAY REFUSE TO PUT YOUR NAME DOWN. A REFUSAL TO SIGN IS NOT A REFUSAL TO READ AND IS NOT ENTERED IN THE LEDGER OF READINGS. IT IS ENTERED WHERE A REFUSAL IS ENTERED.**

The room did not argue with it. A man of fifty-six said that the last of the five is the one he needed, **and that a man who has refused to read nine times has spent something on every one of them and did not know it until the third of November, and that a refusal to sign costs nothing and is entered in a different column and that the two columns are not joined.**

A clerk of nineteen years asked whether a refusal to sign was a reading and the rule did not answer her, and a man of fifty-six said that the rule did not answer her because it is not hers to answer and that the count of refusals to read is nine and did not move on the second of December and that nobody has entered a tenth.

Nobody argued and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against said that he was not going to refuse, and said the second half before anybody asked him for it, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he said the second half before he was asked for it and that this is the second time in eleven weeks a person has done that and that the first was a man of about twenty-six in a yard of brine in the fourth month of this year.**

"**I am not refusing, and if I did refuse it would be in a column and not on that sheet, and you would not have it on a sheet either.**"

**AND ON THE SECOND OF DECEMBER A RULE ARRIVED IN THAT ROOM AND NOBODY ASKED FOR IT, AND THE LAST OF ITS FIVE LINES WAS THAT A REFUSAL TO SIGN IS NOT A REFUSAL TO READ AND IS NOT ENTERED IN THE LEDGER OF READINGS, AND THE COUNT OF REFUSALS TO READ IS NINE AND DID NOT MOVE, AND THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST SAID HE WAS NOT REFUSING AND SAID THE SECOND HALF BEFORE ANYBODY ASKED HIM FOR IT.**

---

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said on the second of December that a rotation of four people on a lot is a lot with four bearers, and a man of fifty-six said that a lot with four bearers is a lot with four bearers and that this is the first thing in eleven weeks this district has got right by accident, and that a bearer is a person the toll falls on and that the four people in the rotation are not bearers and may not be entered as bearers and are entered as NOT A BEARER in the second line of the first sheet.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the second line of the first sheet says NOT A BEARER four times, **and that it is the first line in any document in this district that says what a person in it is not, and that a man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper said in a room that a form that says what it is not is the first one he has ever been handed and that he said the same about the third form of a notice in the same month and that the two are not joined.**

Nobody argued and the man of fifty-six said that a person bound to a channel is not a bearer and that the distinction is in the second line, and that the four in the rotation are named as people who stand in a frame and that the channel under the frame is where the year comes out of and that the two are not joined and that no document in this district has ever held both in one line.

A woman of thirty-eight who has been in the second channel four years was asked in the five things on the second of December whether she would be one of the four, and said she would be asked about it in December and not on the second of December, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she was asked and answered and that she was not asked about the four seasons and was not asked about the eleventh year and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move.**

**AND ON THE SECOND OF DECEMBER THE SECOND LINE OF THE FIRST SHEET SAYS NOT A BEARER FOUR TIMES, AND A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FOUR WHO MENDS FENCING SAID THAT A ROTATION OF FOUR PEOPLE ON A LOT IS A LOT WITH FOUR BEARERS, AND A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX SAID THAT A BEARER IS A PERSON THE TOLL FALLS ON AND THAT THE FOUR IN THE ROTATION ARE NOT BEARERS AND MAY NOT BE ENTERED AS BEARERS.**

---

The four sheets went to a guild inspector of forty-four thirty-four miles away on the second of December and came back on the fourth, and he did not come and was not asked to and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he was sent four sheets and returned four sheets and that he has now been asked eleven times in eleven weeks for a form and every one of the eleven wanted a column in for one person.

What he wrote back is on the back of the second sheet in his own hand **and it is the first thing anybody outside this district has put into one of these four sheets, and a man of fifty-six read it out and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he read it out and was not asked to and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move.**

"**A certificate says four people were asked. I can certify that four people were asked. I cannot certify what any of the four said to the other three and neither can anybody, and if anybody in this district prints the word certified on any of these four sheets I will come thirty-four miles and take it off.**"

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six said that the finding is that this district has built a document that cannot be certified, and that a document that cannot be certified is not weaker than a document that can, and that two certificates in a drawer thirty-four miles away saying opposite things about the same sheet was the argument against a form in the middle of September and that this is the same argument in a different instrument and that the two are not joined.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a guild inspector of forty-four declined a fifth time in eleven weeks, on the fourth of December, to say what a public office was for, and that he did it in writing, **and that the refusals to join two things stand at thirteen and the registrar's own count stands at twelve and that neither moved on the fourth of December and that a letter is not a question in a room.**

**AND ON THE FOURTH OF DECEMBER A GUILD INSPECTOR OF FORTY-FOUR SENT BACK THE FOUR SHEETS THIRTY-FOUR MILES WITH NINE LINES IN HIS OWN HAND ON THE BACK OF THE SECOND SHEET, AND THE FINDING IS THAT A CERTIFICATE SAYS FOUR PEOPLE WERE ASKED AND CANNOT CERTIFY WHAT ANY OF THE FOUR SAID TO THE OTHER THREE, AND THAT A DOCUMENT THAT CANNOT BE CERTIFIED IS NOT WEAKER THAN A DOCUMENT THAT CAN.**

---

A man who prints was asked on the second of December to print the four sheets and he printed one of them, and the one he printed is the first sheet and it is the rotation and it is about one thing and it cost four coppers, and he declined the other three and gave the reason in a sentence and the sentence is in the minute in his own words.

He said that a rotation can be copied by nine hands and that a copy of a rotation is a rotation, **and that the ledger cannot be copied by nine hands and that the notice cannot be printed at all because the form has a hole in it and a printed hole is a hole in a shop, and that the terms sheet has a name on it and a name is not a thing a man prints for four coppers.**

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six entered that the charter is therefore four sheets of which one is printed and one is on a board of eleven in a yard and two are hand copies, and that this is the first instrument this district has built in eleven weeks that is going to exist in more than one form in four different states, and that a man who has declined three corrections since the middle of September has declined a fourth and that nobody has asked him to explain it.

He took the four coppers and did not say thank you and did not ask to be thanked, and a clerk of nineteen years entered all three, **and that the count of sheets he has printed in two months and a half is six, and that the six is about one thing each and that the four hundred printed sheets of the office's name are not in that count because they were printed in June and under a different arrangement.**

AND ON THE SECOND OF DECEMBER A MAN WHO PRINTS PRINTED ONE OF THE FOUR SHEETS, BEING THE ROTATION, FOR FOUR COPPERS, AND DECLINED THE OTHER THREE, AND THE CHARTER IS THEREFORE FOUR SHEETS OF WHICH ONE IS PRINTED AND ONE IS ON A BOARD OF ELEVEN IN A YARD AND TWO ARE HAND COPIES, AND HE HAS DECLINED FOUR CORRECTIONS SINCE THE MIDDLE OF SEPTEMBER.

---

He put his name down at about one in the afternoon on the second of December, and a clerk of nineteen years wrote it and did not read it back, **and a man of fifty-six asked her why and she said that a name is not a figure and is not read back and that she has read back every figure this district has entered since the eighth of March and has never read back a name.**

Nobody argued with that and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a clerk who will read back a figure and will not read back a name has worked out something in one sentence that a boundary took two women and eleven weeks to work out, and that the two are not joined and that a name is not a mark and a mark may not carry a job and this is a name and the job is his.

A man of fifty-six asked him whether he wanted to be a party to the charter, and he refused in four seconds, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he had been asked in the six things and had refused and that not asked is not the same as asked and declined and that the count of things this district does not have is five and did not move today.**

He gave the reason in six words and nobody argued and a man of fifty-six entered that a party to a charter is a person the charter can be ended against, and that the man is the address and not a party and that the two are different instruments, and that the address is the door and the party is the wall, and that a district with a door and no wall is where this district has been since the eighth of March.

AND ON THE SECOND OF DECEMBER** THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST PUT HIS NAME DOWN AT ABOUT ONE IN THE AFTERNOON AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS WROTE IT AND DID NOT READ IT BACK, AND HE WAS ASKED WHETHER HE WANTED TO BE A PARTY TO THE CHARTER AND REFUSED IN FOUR SECONDS, AND THE SIX WORDS WERE THAT THE ADDRESS IS NOT A PARTY.**

---

The terms sheet carries the figure of twenty-one years in the same line as his name and the entry runs to the thirty-first of March, and it carries a year and a half on a line of its own that is not added, and two people in a column as not known and not asked, and one frame that took nothing, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the three are not added and that the two are not asked and that the frame that took nothing is not a fault and is not a mercy and is a frame.

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six said that the aggregate unpaid toll is a floor and not a total, **and that four plus two plus eleven and four is twenty-one and that the year and a half is on a line of its own and that there is no rate in this basin that turns a year into coppers and that the sheet does not have a figure in coppers on it anywhere.**

A stranger asked what a year was worth at the ward market counter on the second of December and was told the same thing he was told on the sixteenth of November, and the reader who is about fifty read the sixth column out first, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a person who cannot read a figure can read that, and that this district has now said the same sentence about a column in three rooms and a lane and the four are not joined.

The word steward is in the same book and on the same run and ends on the thirty-first of March, and the second half of it is not finished, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered on the second of December that the reason has not changed and that the reason is nineteen words and that this is not a delay and that a clerk of nineteen years entered the words this is not a delay of her own accord and was not asked to and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move.**

And the thirty-first of March is the day THE NAME COMES OFF THE FIGURE AND THE DAY THE STEWARD'S ADDRESS ENDS AND THE DAY THE SEASON RENEWAL FALLS, AND THE ENTRY OF THE SECOND OF DECEMBER SAYS THAT THE THREE ARE ON ONE LINE AND ARE NOT JOINED, AND THE FIRST OF APRIL IS NOT A DATE THIS DISTRICT HAS REACHED AND IS A DATE A ROOM IS BUILDING TOWARDS.

And the charter is four sheets AND THE FOURTH OF THE FOUR IS THE TERMS SHEET AND THE FIRST OF THE FOUR IS A ROTATION OF FOUR PEOPLE WHO ARE NONE OF THEM THE MAN WHO CAN BE FOUND, AND THE SECOND OF THE FOUR IS THE LEDGER AS IT STANDS ON A BOARD OF ELEVEN IN A YARD OF LOT SEVENTEEN, AND THE THIRD OF THE FOUR IS THE THIRD FORM OF A NOTICE, WHICH HAS FOUR LINES UNDER THREE THINGS AND NINE WORDS AT THE TOP THAT NAME A SEASON THAT HAS NOT STARTED.

---

The last entry of the fifth of December is eleven lines and it is in the clerk of nineteen years' own hand, **and it names the four sheets and the four days the room has left and it does not say the word charter in it, and a man of fifty-six asked her why and she gave the reason in a sentence and the sentence is in the minute in her own words and it is thirty-nine words.**

"**A charter is a word people use after it is closed. On the fifth of December there are four sheets and a hole in one of them and I am not going to give the hole a word yet.**"

Nobody argued and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that this is the third time in eleven weeks she has refused to name a thing and that the count of it is three, and that the three are the office, the board, and this, and that none of the three is a refusal and all three are a decision and that the two are not joined.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that she had entered the count of three herself and that she had wanted to enter the word refusals and had not, **and that a refusal is a thing a person says to a room and a decision is a thing a clerk writes down and that she has done two of each in eleven weeks and that the two are in two columns.**

**AND ON THE FIFTH OF DECEMBER A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS ENTERED THAT THE LAST ENTRY OF THE DAY NAMES FOUR SHEETS AND FOUR DAYS AND DOES NOT SAY THE WORD CHARTER IN IT, AND THE REASON IS THAT A CHARTER IS A WORD PEOPLE USE AFTER IT IS CLOSED, AND ON THE FIFTH OF DECEMBER THERE ARE FOUR SHEETS AND A HOLE IN ONE OF THEM.**
