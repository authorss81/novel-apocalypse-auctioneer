# Chapter 126: One Sentence Across a Room

---

He went west on the fourth of October with a cart and a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households, and it was three days, and the man of fifty-six said the whole way that thirty-four miles was thirty miles on a road and about thirty-four over the loams and that the four extra were a rise of about ninety feet that a cart does not enjoy, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that twice and that she had asked him to say it only once.

They came off the raised plank road at the ninth mile on the sixth and the water was not on the planks, and the plank nearest the far end is limewood and about nineteen years old, and the man of fifty-six looked at it and said nothing, and a man of about forty who had put in the road in the third month of this year was not there and was not expected and the clerk entered that nobody had expected him and that she did not know whether that was a good thing or not.

The cart had the figure of twenty-one years on a sheet in an oilcloth against the rain, and the sheet went to the ward market counter on the seventh and then to the board of eleven at eleven in the morning, and about nineteen people were in the yard and a stranger who has been to this district four times in nine weeks was in the yard and took a copy of it cold without stopping anybody.

---

A reader at the counter, who is about fifty and was a signal clerk before her shoulder went and is on eleven coppers a quarter for four days a week, read it out in the ordinary voice and it took nine minutes, and **the rule of the counter is that a document goes out twice and it went out twice**, and a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households said the arithmetic out loud while she was reading it, and the two figures are in the minute in two different hands and are not added anywhere.

"**Twenty-one years. One hundred and eighty-three coppers. A year and a half on a line of its own. Two people in a column as not known and not asked. I have read the sheet twice and I have four numbers on it and not one of them is the same kind of thing as any of the other three, and this district has spent ten weeks learning that a household is a count and a person is a count and the two are not added, and I would like somebody to write the same rule about a year.**"

The reader at the counter said that she had read the sheet and that she could not do the arithmetic on it and would not do it in front of forty people, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that nobody had asked her to and that this was the fourth time in ten weeks she had said a thing like it and that the other three had been said by a man at a counter who does not read figures, and that the reader entered the two in one line and did not join them.

And then a man of about thirty-one who runs a cart of meal out of a lane in Alder Reach asked the reader a question in eleven words, and she answered it, and the answer is in the minute in her own words.

"**Is the twenty-one coppers.**"

"**No.**"

"Then what is it."

"**It is twenty-one. It has not got a unit in this basin. I have been at this counter for six years and I have read out the price of a bushel of white paint and a rate that is nine years old and a hundred and eighty-three coppers that nobody is owed, and this is the first number that has come up this counter that I cannot finish.**"

---

**AND ON THE SEVENTH OF OCTOBER A FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS WENT ONTO A BOARD OF ELEVEN IN A YARD IN ALDER REACH AND A STRANGER TOOK A COPY OF IT COLD, AND THE FINDING IS THAT THIS DISTRICT CAN NOW WRITE DOWN WHAT A PLACE HAS PAID IN A YEAR OF SOMEBODY'S MEMORY AND CANNOT WRITE DOWN WHAT IT OWES IN COPPERS, AND THAT A SHEET WITH FOUR KINDS OF NUMBER ON IT IS A SHEET NOBODY IN THIS BASIN HAS A COLUMN FOR, AND THAT A READER AT A COUNTER WHO HAS READ OUT A PRICE AND A RATE AND AN UNPAID FIGURE FOR SIX YEARS SAID SHE COULD NOT FINISH THE FOURTH.**

---

The registrar asked him to come up to a counting room over a chandler's shop in the afternoon, and nineteen people were in it, and she did not ask him anything at all for the first nineteen minutes.

She was working on the entry that the address would go into, and the entry is the market's own book and not the public lot book, and the difference is that the market's own book has a column for a name and the public lot book has a column for a figure and a line for a name underneath it, and neither of them has a column for what a person is to the figure.

Four people in that room had asked him the same question in three days and the four questions were all a version of the same thing, and the clerk of nineteen years had written all four of them in a column of her own and had entered that she had done so because she was the only person in the room who could see the shape of them.

A man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households asked what a person is to a figure, and was told that there is no column for it in this basin and that there is no rate and that a year does not go twice.

Then she said that she had come down here to say one thing and then she was going to stop, and the room went quiet in the ordinary way.

"**You have said that figure out loud in three rooms since the second of October and you have not said it to one person, and I am not going to be the fourth room, and I would like the entry to say that I decided not to ask and not that I did not think of it.**"

And the clerk of nineteen years, who was at the back of the room with a book, wrote both halves of it down and underlined nothing, and the minute says she underlined nothing, and says that a reader at a counter has asked twice which line she underlined the last time and has not been told, and that the count of not-askings is a column and not a bucket and it did not move, because the sentence was said in a room and not in a kitchen.

He answered it in one sentence and it took four seconds.

"**Enter it as decided.**"

A man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households looked at the registrar for nine seconds and then asked her whether the man had answered her, and she said that he had, and the man of fifty-six said that he had not heard a sentence, and the registrar said that it was a sentence and that it was four words of it and that the man of fifty-six was not the person it was for, and nobody in that room said anything else about it and the clerk of nineteen years entered all of it and entered that the sentence was four words long and that she had counted them.

---

**AND THE SENTENCE CROSSED THE ROOM ON THE SEVENTH OF OCTOBER AND WAS ANSWERED IN ONE SENTENCE AND FOUR SECONDS, AND ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE HEARD BOTH AND NOT ONE OF THEM ENTERED EITHER AS ANYTHING BUT A SENTENCE AND A SENTENCE, AND THE REGISTRAR DID NOT SAY IT WAS ANYTHING ELSE, AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS UNDERLINED NOTHING FOR THE FIRST TIME IN TEN WEEKS AND A READER AT A COUNTER HAS ASKED TWICE WHICH LINE SHE UNDERLINED THE LAST TIME AND HAS NOT BEEN TOLD.**

---

Then the room did the thing it does, which is go on, and about nine people argued for eleven minutes about whether the entry in the market's own book should carry a date or a term, and the registrar said that a figure of twenty-one years is not a figure that comes round again by itself and that a term is not a quarter, and a man of fifty-six said that the season runs to the thirty-first of March, and nobody wrote anything down for four minutes.

Then a woman of about thirty-six who keeps a stall of chalk and oil on a cart wrote it down, and she had not been asked, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that she had not been asked and that she had asked to write the date down and that nobody had given her a date.

"It is a term and it ends on the thirty-first of March, and the thirty-first of March is not a quarter and it does not come round by itself, and if nobody writes the day on it then somebody in four years will read it as a standing thing, and a standing thing is a thing nobody can stop, and that is the thing we have spent ten weeks learning about."

"And who ends it on the thirty-first of March."

"**That is the question that is going to be asked on the thirtieth of March and not today, and I am not a keeper of this book and I keep a stall, and I have written the day down because nobody else would and that is all.**"

---

And then the two lost days were put to the room, because the clerk of nineteen years had carried the figure from the flat in a book and had not put it anywhere, and the man of fifty-six did the arithmetic in four minutes, and the minute has it in his own words, and it came to fifty-one words and he stopped in the middle of the last one to make sure, and then went on.

"**The season was to start on the first of October and it did not, and the loss is two days of six months, which is a figure I could put in a book and which would be a lie. What the two days did was move a fortnight, because nine households had built a fortnight around a date that was written on a sheet in a folder with no name on it, and there is no column in any book in this basin for a plan, and a plan is not a person and a plan is not a household and a plan is not a year.**"

"What is it."

"**It is two days and a fortnight, and the fortnight is the part that is going to cost, and I have said that in ten weeks of a man saying things in a room and this is the first time I have said it as the man who is going to be standing in it.**"

And a woman of about forty-four who keeps a Road House on the tide heard the word fortnight and asked one question, and the clerk entered the two descriptions of the two women of forty-four in one line and did not join them, and the question was whether the five tolls at the west end were going to be asked again, and the answer was that they had been asked again on the twenty-ninth of September from two rooms by two people who are not on either bank.

She said the other half and it is in the minute in her own words and it took nineteen words, and it is about her cellar and it is not about the boundary.

"**I have eleven pounds of salt in that cellar and I counted them on the second of October and I have eleven pounds on the seventh, and nobody has taken any and nobody has put any in, and that is what two days is.**"

---

**AND THE FINDING OF THE SEVENTH OF OCTOBER IS THAT TWO DAYS OF A SALT SEASON IS ELEVEN POUNDS OF SALT IN A CELLAR AND A FORTNIGHT IN NINE HOUSES' PLANS, AND THAT A PLAN IS NOT A PERSON AND A PLAN IS NOT A HOUSEHOLD AND A PLAN IS NOT A YEAR, AND THAT THERE IS NO COLUMN IN ANY BOOK IN THIS BASIN FOR A PLAN, AND THAT A WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-SIX WHO KEEPS A STALL OF CHALK AND OIL ON A CART WROTE THE THIRTY-FIRST OF MARCH ON A PAGE BECAUSE NOBODY ELSE WOULD AND SAID THAT SHE KEEPS A STALL AND IS NOT A KEEPER OF ANYTHING.**

---

On the road the two of them did the thing people do on a road, which is to talk about nothing for two days and then to say the thing on the third morning.

The man of fifty-six asked about the private channel and the man of about forty-four who has it, and the man of thirty-eight said that he had not stopped thinking about the four hundred and that he had got to the end of what he knew about it.

He said that the end of it was that a man who charges four hundred and takes his own year is not charging a price and is not avoiding a book and is running a channel, and that the only thing wrong with him is that nobody can check.

The man of fifty-six said that a district that has spent ten weeks learning that a person is the seventh part of a lot has found the cheapest possible way to make the seventh part invisible, and that it did not need a criminal and it did not need a bank, it needed a sheet with no column on it and a man who did not want to be in a book, and that the second of those two is the commonest kind of man there is.

They came down off the loams on the sixth about four in the afternoon and the man of fifty-six pointed at the ninth mile and said that the figure of twenty-one years was on a cart behind them and that in four weeks it would be in a book with a name on it, and that in about four years a stranger would take a copy of it cold and would not know that four hundred coppers had gone into a channel thirty-four miles away, and that the two facts would be in the same district and would not be joined because joining them is a job and the job does not exist.

And then the yard did the other thing it does, which is that about four people who had not spoken in nineteen minutes asked a man of thirty-four who mends fencing what a man of thirty-eight is going to do about a figure of twenty-one years, and he said four things and the clerk of nineteen years wrote all four down and entered that they were asked by four different people and that he had given the same answer to all four.

"**He is not going to do anything about it. That is the finding and it is not a failure, and this district has spent ten weeks asking a man to price a thing and the thing has turned out not to have a price, and a man who cannot price a thing is not a man who has stopped working, he is a man who has found the edge of his own trade.**"

A man of about thirty-one who runs a cart of meal out of a lane in Alder Reach said that the edge of a man's trade is the last place anybody in this district has looked, and that four thousand and one households have been looking for nine weeks for somebody who can do the thing nobody can do, and that the edge of a trade is not a place anybody sends anybody.

Nobody argued with that, and the clerk entered it, and entered that it is the first time in ten weeks that a person in this district has said out loud that the answer to a question might be that there is nobody to give it to.

The stranger who had taken a copy of the sheet cold in the yard came back into the room at five in the afternoon and asked for the minute of the twenty-eighth of September, and the clerk of nineteen years said that the minute is on a flat thirty-four miles east and that a copy of it had not been made, and the stranger said that he had understood and that a copy would be welcome in four weeks if anybody was making one.

The clerk of nineteen years entered that a stranger had asked for a copy of a document that exists once, and that she had said the word welcome in a room about nineteen people had heard, and that she had not said the word on purpose and did not know where it had come from, and that a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that was the first time in ten weeks that anybody in this district had said welcome to a stranger in a book.

And then a woman of fifty-eight who carries water and does not read figures was asked whether the twenty-one was a figure she could carry, and she said no in four seconds and then said the rest of it, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that she had been asked a question she could not answer and had answered it anyway and that this is the fourth time in ten weeks.

"**I carry water to about ninety houses and I can tell you what a house owes me in a fortnight, because a house does not pay in coppers, it pays in having the water come when you knock. And twenty-one years is not that. You cannot knock.**"

"And what happens on the last day of a season."

"**I carry water to ninety houses and every one of them will be short on the first of April and not one of them will be short because of me, and that is the whole of what a service is, and if you want to know what happens on the last day of a season then ask the person who is in the frame, and she has been asked in six things, and the six things have taken a week, and the week is the thing and not the day.**"

A man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households wrote that down in his own hand and read it back to her and she told him that he had got the last bit wrong, and he had got the word *knock* wrong, and she told him what it should have been, and he wrote it again, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that four people corrected a document in ten weeks by saying what they needed in it and that three of the four have been women and that nobody has asked why.

The last thing of the seventh of October was said by the registrar to nineteen people, and it was nineteen words and she did not take longer over it than over a figure, and it is in the minute in her own hand, and she has refused twelve times in nine weeks and this is not one of the twelve times.

"**I have twelve refusals in this book and all twelve of them are mine, and I have never once been asked which one of the twelve I would decide differently, and I am not going to be asked now, and that is a decision and not a wall.**"

And at six in the evening a clerk of nineteen years wrote the day's entry in the public lot book, and it is nineteen lines long, and it went out twice off the board of eleven, and this is the whole of it.

> **THE SEVENTH OF OCTOBER. A FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS, A YEAR AND A HALF ON A LINE OF ITS OWN, AND TWO PEOPLE IN A COLUMN AS NOT KNOWN AND NOT ASKED, HAVE COME TO THIS BOARD FROM A FLAT THIRTY-FOUR MILES EAST. THE FIGURE IS AGAINST NOBODY'S NAME YET. THE ADDRESS IS TO BE ENTERED IN THE MARKET'S OWN BOOK AND NOT ON THIS BOARD AND THE ENTRY IS THE REGISTRAR'S AND NOT A CLERK'S. THE FIGURE OF ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-THREE COPPERS IS A PAGE IN THAT SAME BOOK WITH A HEADING AND NO NAME AGAINST IT AND WAS NOT READ OUT ON THE THIRTIETH OF SEPTEMBER BY ANYBODY. THE TWO FIGURES ARE IN ONE BOOK FROM THE TENTH OF OCTOBER AND ARE NOT ADDED AND THERE IS NO RATE.**

A man who does not read figures read that out at the counter and got through it in four minutes, and the rule of the counter is that a document goes out twice and it went out twice, and forty people were at the counter and a stranger was at the counter and took a copy of this one too, and he now had two sheets, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he had two and that she did not know whether he knew they were about the same thing and that he had not been asked and that not asked is in the count.

**AND THE FINDING OF THE SEVENTH OF OCTOBER IS ALSO THAT A DISTRICT WHICH HAS SPENT TEN WEEKS LEARNING THAT A PERSON IS THE SEVENTH PART OF A LOT HAS NOW FOUND THE CHEAPEST WAY OF MAKING THAT SEVENTH PART INVISIBLE, AND IT DID NOT NEED A CRIMINAL AND IT DID NOT NEED A BANK, IT NEEDED A SHEET WITH NO COLUMN ON IT AND A MAN WHO DID NOT WANT TO BE IN A BOOK, AND THE SECOND OF THOSE TWO IS THE COMMONEST KIND OF MAN THERE IS, AND THE ONLY THING WRONG WITH HIM IS THAT NOBODY CAN CHECK.**

**AND THE FINDING OF THE SEVENTH OF OCTOBER IS THAT A PERSON WHO IS TO BECOME THE ADDRESS FOR A FIGURE CANNOT BE UNNAMED BY HIMSELF, AND THAT THE ONLY INSTRUMENT IN THIS DISTRICT THAT CANNOT BE UNDONE IS A BOOK ON ELEVEN BOARDS THAT WORKS IF A PERSON COMES TO THE YARD, AND THAT THE REGISTRAR SAID IN A ROOM THAT SHE HAS NEVER BEEN ASKED WHICH OF HER TWELVE REFUSALS SHE WOULD DECIDE DIFFERENTLY, AND THAT NOBODY ASKED HER, AND THAT NOT ASKED IS IN THE COUNT, AND THE COUNT IS A COLUMN AND NOT A BUCKET.**
