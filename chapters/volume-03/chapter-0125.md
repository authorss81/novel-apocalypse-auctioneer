# Chapter 125: A Figure Against a Name

---

The room where the four things are read out had about nineteen people in it on the morning of the second of October and the sun came in at one side about eleven in the morning, and the woman of forty-four who has done it eleven years was in it, and she is not its clerk and will not be, and the only thing she did in it on the twenty-eighth of September was say no in four words when she was asked whether she would be its clerk, and that was the whole of her office and it is the only office there is.

A man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households was asked to state the job in the five things and he did it in nine seconds and he is a person who does not count for a living.

"What is the job."

"**Put a figure on what the looms have taken off this flat since the flood year, in a form that a person who cannot read figures can still check by asking somebody.**"

"How long is it."

"**I do not know and I am not going to guess, and I have been counting things for nineteen years and I have never once counted the same thing twice and got the same number, and a figure that cannot be got twice is not a figure.**"

"What does it cost."

"**A sheet of paper and a clerk who is nineteen and four hours, and I have not got four hours of anybody's this week and I am going to take them anyway, and I would like that entered before I take them and not after.**"

And the clerk of nineteen years entered it before he took them, and the minute says she entered it in four seconds and that this is the first time in ten weeks that a person has asked for a thing to be entered before it happened.

---

The count was done in four hours on the second of October by four people and the clerk of nineteen years, and it is in the party's minute in four hands, and **the entry says the four wrote the same four lines and the clerk wrote them in a fifth order and the two orders were read out against each other and matched, and that a man of thirty-four who mends fencing read both and said he could not tell which was his and which was not.**

The four people who counted were a woman of thirty-eight who has been in the second channel four years, a man of twenty-six who has been in a bed of brine for a year and a half, a woman of thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel, and a man of about nineteen who does not live on the flat and had walked in from the road and said he could count and had been asked to and had said yes.

The man of nineteen was not the boy of the fourth place.

He is a different man of about nineteen, and the clerk entered the two in one line and did not join them, and the minute says that this is the sixth time she has done that in ten weeks and that nobody has asked her about any of the six.

> **A WOMAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT IN THE SECOND CHANNEL, FOUR SEASONS: FOUR.**
>
> **A WOMAN OF THIRTY-ONE IN THE THIRD CHANNEL SINCE THE FIRST OF APRIL, THIS IS HER SECOND: TWO.**
>
> **A WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR OUT OF IT ELEVEN YEARS AND THERE WAS NO SIXTH YEAR: ELEVEN.**
>
> **A MAN OF FIFTY-TWO OUT OF THE FOURTH CHANNEL IN THE FOURTH MONTH OF THIS YEAR AFTER FOUR YEARS: FOUR.**
>
> **A MAN OF TWENTY-SIX IN A BED OF BRINE FOR A YEAR AND A HALF: A YEAR AND A HALF, AND THIS LINE IS NOT ADDED TO ANYTHING, AND THE REASON IS THAT A YEAR DOES NOT GO TWICE AND A HALF OF A YEAR IS NOT A THING A PERSON CAN BE OWED.**
>
> **TWO PEOPLE WERE IN OR BESIDE TWO OF THE SIX FRAMES ON THE SECOND OF OCTOBER AND NEITHER WAS DESCRIBED TO ANYBODY AND NEITHER WAS ASKED: NOT KNOWN AND NOT ASKED.**
>
> **THE FRAME THAT FAILED TOOK NOTHING OFF ANYBODY BECAUSE THERE WAS NOBODY IN IT: NOTHING.**

The clerk of nineteen years wrote the woman's age as thirty-eight twice and struck it once and entered the strike, and the second of the two was wrong and was struck, and the minute says the strike is in it and that the clerk said the strike was the finding of the four hours and that the four counters had all written thirty-eight and that a figure of four years in a woman's memory had nearly been entered against a man of thirty-eight because a clerk was tired by the fourth hour.

The four whole numbers are four, two, eleven and four, and the sum of them is twenty-one, and the clerk of nineteen years added it in front of nineteen people and read it out twice, and the finding of the second of October is that **a figure of twenty-one years of a working person's memory has been standing in a room on a flat where ninety pans have been counted twice and it took four hours to make and nobody had ever made it before.**

---

And then a rule arrived in that room at four in the afternoon on the second of October, and nobody in the room had asked for it, and the man of thirty-eight did not ask for it either, and he is the only person on either bank who could have got it by asking and did not.

> **A TOLL THAT IS TAKEN AND NOT WRITTEN DOWN IS STILL TAKEN. WRITING IT DOWN DOES NOT GIVE IT BACK AND DOES NOT MAKE IT SMALLER.**
>
> **A FIGURE OF YEARS IS A FIGURE AND NOT A REMEDY. IT IS NOT A SUM OF MONEY AND IT IS NOT COPPERS AND THERE IS NO RATE IN THIS BASIN THAT TURNS IT INTO COPPERS. IT CANNOT BE CLOSED BY A TRANSFER, BECAUSE A TRANSFER NEEDS A HOLDER TO TRANSFER FROM AND THESE YEARS HAVE NO HOLDER.**
>
> **AN ADDRESS IS NOT A BEARER. A PERSON NAMED AS AN ADDRESS MAY NOT BE ENTERED AS A HOLDER, A WITNESS, A RESPONSIBLE PARTY, OR A PERSON WHO WAS ASKED.**
>
> **A NAME PUT ON A FIGURE CANNOT BE TAKEN OFF IT BY THE PERSON IT IS PUT ON. YOU MAY REFUSE THE NAME BEFORE IT IS READ OUT. YOU CANNOT REFUSE IT AFTER.**

Then a clerk of nineteen years asked what an address was, and the rule did not say, and a man of thirty-eight said it in nineteen words and the minute has it in his own words.

"**An address is a name on a figure so that a person asking who owes it is answered. It is not a person who has the thing. It is not a person who has to pay it. And it is the only name in this district that nobody can hand back.**"

And the man of twenty-six asked, from the second channel, in nine seconds, whether the address would have to be in the frame, and the man of thirty-eight said no in four seconds and the clerk entered that a man bound to a channel is not a bearer and that an address is not a bearer either and that the two of those are two different rules that arrive at the same place and that he would like that entered.

---

**AND ON THE SECOND OF OCTOBER A FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS STOOD AGAINST NOBODY'S NAME IN A ROOM ON A FLAT THIRTY-FOUR MILES EAST OF A RATE SHEET NINE YEARS OLD, AND A YEAR AND A HALF STOOD ON A LINE OF ITS OWN AND WAS NOT ADDED, AND TWO PEOPLE STOOD IN A COLUMN AS NOT KNOWN AND NOT ASKED, AND THE FINDING IS THAT THE FIGURE IS NOT A COST, IS NOT A CLAIM, IS NOT A PRICE, AND IS NOT A REMEDY, AND THAT A FIGURE WITH NO NAME ON IT HAD BEEN READ OUT AT A COUNTER ONCE A QUARTER FOR TEN WEEKS AND A FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS HAD NEVER BEEN READ OUT AT ALL.**

---

The two people in the column as not known and not asked were the subject of nineteen minutes, and the man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households said both halves of it and the clerk wrote both down.

"**I know who they are. I know their names and I have known for eleven years and I am not going to write them on that sheet, and the reason is that the four people who counted are the four people who have been asked, and the two who were not asked are the two who were not in the room, and if I put their names down then a stranger reads the sheet and sees five names and thinks five people were asked, and four were.**"

"So it is a floor."

"**It is a floor and it is not a cost and it is not the total, and there are about eighty people in this flat and eleven years, and if a person who has been in a frame for a season is a year, then somebody in this district has been counting in a room and not on a flat, and the answer is written on the sheet and it is two.**"

---

The man of thirty-eight was asked to put his name against the twenty-one in the same room and the asking was made in the five things, and he was asked properly and he answered, and the answer is in the minute and the clerk of nineteen years read it back and got nothing wrong.

"**I did not take them. I have never stood in a channel and I do not know what a year of a bed of brine takes out of a person and I am not going to find out by reading one. I am the only name on a page in a folder with nine other sheets in it, and the frame is held by nine households and not by a person, and the bed is a hole, and the year is in neither of them, and if a figure of twenty-one years has no name against it then there is nobody in this basin to ask, and I will not have it that way, and you can have my name and I will not be your bearer and I will not be your witness and I will not be a person who was asked.**"

"And when it ends."

"**On the thirty-first of March. I am not agreeing to it past the thirty-first of March and I want that in the same line and not in a second one.**"

And a woman of about forty-four who has done it eleven years asked him one question and it is in the minute and it is the only question anybody asked him in nine minutes, and the clerk entered that the clerk was not in the room and was told about it afterwards and wrote it down from the mouth of a man of thirty-four who mends fencing and that she asked for it to be read back and that it was.

"**What happens to the twenty-one on the first of April.**"

"**Nothing. That is the honest answer and I have got it in the room and I do not like it. If the season starts then two more go on the sheet and the sheet is a sheet and it gets full, and if the season does not start then the twenty-one stands on its own for another year, and there is nothing in this district that can make it not stand, and I would like the finding of this afternoon to be that and not a promise.**"

---

The four hours were not four hours of counting.

They were four hours of asking, and about nineteen people on that flat had to be asked the same question in four different orders because four people said the same thing in four different words.

The clerk of nineteen years wrote down every version and then wrote down the four lines, and the minute says the eleven versions are in the book as well as the four lines and that nobody has ever asked to read the eleven.

The man of twenty-six answered in the fewest words of the four.

He said he had been in a bed of brine for a year and a half and that he could not tell anybody how he knew that, and that a year and a half is two things and not one.

He said that in the fourth month of this year he had been asked whether the bed had been in the wet or the dry and he had said the wet, and that a man of fifty-six who does count for a living said the bed had been in the dry in the fourth month, and that the two of them had gone and looked at the weather and the weather had not settled it.

He said that a man of twenty-six has been the better judge of a bed of brine in this basin for a year and a half and did not know it.

The clerk entered that, and entered that a man who cannot be entered as a bearer may still be asked a question about the wet and the dry, and that being asked is not being used.

The woman of thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel was the fourth counter and she counted the two people she could not name, and she did it in four minutes and the minute says she did not describe either of them and said why in nineteen words.

"**I know the two of them by their boots at the sluice. I could tell you the two of them out of nine people in the dark. And I am not going to write down a thing I know about a person who is not in this room, because the four things on the four sheets are about people being asked, and the whole of that is that they were in a room, and if I put two names on that line then a stranger reads five and thinks five and there were four.**"

And a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that this is the closest anybody has come in ten weeks to saying what a person is in a column, and that she had said it without being asked and about a thing that is not hers, and that a clerk of nineteen years entered the whole of it and entered that the column for things that are not findings did not get it, and that the clerk asked whether it should, and the man of thirty-four said no, and gave the reason in nine words.

"**It is a finding and it is about boots, and a column for things that are not findings is for things a person got wrong.**"

**AND ON THE SECOND OF OCTOBER A FIGURE OF FOUR HANDS AND ELEVEN VERSIONS CAME OUT OF FOUR HOURS OF ASKING, AND A MAN OF TWENTY-SIX COULD NOT TELL ANYBODY HOW HE KNEW THAT HE HAD BEEN IN A BED OF BRINE FOR A YEAR AND A HALF, AND A MAN WHO COUNTS FOR A LIVING SAID HE HAD THE FOURTH MONTH WRONG, AND A PERSON BOUND TO A CHANNEL IS NOT A BEARER AND IS NOT ENTERED AS ONE, AND A YEAR DOES NOT GO TWICE.**

**AND THE FINDING OF THE SECOND OF OCTOBER IS THAT THE NUMBER IS NOT HIS FAULT AND IT IS HIS RESPONSIBILITY, AND THE ENTRY SAYS BOTH IN ONE LINE, AND THE ENTRY ALSO SAYS THAT HE DID NOT TAKE IT AND THAT A PERSON WHO DID NOT TAKE A THING CAN STILL BE THE ONE A ROOM ASKS, AND THAT BINDING CANNOT CREATE A PERSON AND THAT A FIGURE CANNOT ASK.**

---

And a sheet went west on a cart on the third of October with the count on one side and the request on the other, and the request is nineteen words long and the man of thirty-four who mends fencing wrote it and the clerk of nineteen years read it back.

> **THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST THE NAME OF ONE MAN AND THE NAME IS HIS AND HE DID NOT TAKE IT. A COPY OF THIS SHEET IS FOR THE PUBLIC LOT BOOK AT LOT SEVENTEEN AND A COPY IS FOR THE WARD MARKET COUNTER AND NEITHER COPY IS THE ORIGINAL. THE ORIGINAL IS IN THE PARTY'S MINUTE ON THIS FLAT AND A SHEET IS NOT A LEDGER AND A LEDGER IS A DIFFERENT THING AND IS NOT HERE.**

The man who drives salt was not on that cart and was not asked to be, and his two pans are under four feet of river.

A man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households said that the man who had walked eleven miles twice in two years to ask a question was thirty-four miles away in a lane with a cart that was not free until the twelfth of October.

He said that this district has now sent two parties east in a month and the man who started the first one was not on the second one, and that nobody had told him that, and that it was going to be told to him on the twelfth of October by a person who was not him.

The man of thirty-eight said the last thing of the second of October to nineteen people and it is in the minute in his own words and it is forty-nine words long, and he said it before he knew there was going to be a rule in the room, and the clerk entered the order in which the two happened because the clerk is the only person in that room who was keeping order.

"**A figure of one hundred and eighty-three coppers is a page in the market's own book with no name against it and a figure of twenty-one years is a line in a minute with my name against it, and the two are in two books and neither is in the other's and I am not going to stand here and add them, because one of them is money and the other one is not and there is no rate.**"

---

The six frames were walked on the morning of the second of October and there are six of them and none of them is the one that failed, and the fourth hour of the counting was spent walking from one to the other with a woman of thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel and a man of twenty-six who has been in a bed of brine for a year and a half, and neither of them could get out of a bed without a hand on the frame and the hand on the frame is the whole of how the count was done.

A man of fifty-six who does count for a living carried a length of rope and used it to measure the distance between the frames and said the number out loud twice and got the same number twice, and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing wrote it down and the clerk of nineteen years wrote it down and the two figures are in the minute and they are the same figure and one of them is in coppers and one of them is in yards and they are not added.

A woman of thirty-eight who has been in the second channel four years was asked whether the frames would still be there in a year, and she said that a frame is oak and that the frame nearest the sluice is about forty-one years old and that the frame over the second channel was put up in the fourth year by four men who are all dead, and that a man who puts a frame up does not write his name on it and that this district has five boards and none of them says who laid anything.

She was asked what happens to a frame when there is nobody in it, and she said that a frame does not care and that a frame is a frame, and then she said the other half and the clerk of nineteen years entered it in her own words because the clerk was standing next to her.

"**The channel cares. The channel is a hole with a lip and the lip is what holds it exactly, and the man who lays the lip lays a foot of loam because that is what he has got, and the lip is not on any of the five boards and the lip is not a channel and the lip is not a frame and I have never seen one written down anywhere in my life.**"

A man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that this is the fourth time in ten weeks that a person has described a part of a thing in this district and not the thing, and that the four are the sill of a channel, a plinth with bolt-holes in it, a column of sixty-one, and a sheet with a heading and no name on it, and that nobody has joined the four and that the clerk had entered all four in one line and had not joined them.

**AND ON THE THIRD OF OCTOBER THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS WENT WEST ON A CART PAST THE NINTH MILE OF A RAISED PLANK ROAD THAT A HAULIER OF ABOUT FORTY SAID WAS UNDER WATER OR NOT IN AUGUST, AND THE WATER WAS NOT ON THE PLANKS, AND NOBODY HAS ASKED THE MAN WHO PUT IN THE ROAD WHETHER FOUR CROSSINGS A MONTH IS WHAT HE MEANT FOR IT, AND HE IS NOT ASKED HERE AND IS NOT TO BE ASKED IN THE NEXT TEN CHAPTERS.**

**AND ON THE SECOND AND THE THIRD OF OCTOBER THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS WENT WEST ON A CART AND THE SEASON HAD STILL NOT STARTED, AND WHEN IT STARTS TWO MORE YEARS GO ON THE SAME SHEET, AND A SHEET IS A SHEET AND A SHEET GETS FULL, AND THE FINDING IS THAT THE ONLY THING THIS DISTRICT HAS BUILT IN TEN WEEKS THAT IS ABOUT A PERSON'S MEMORY IS A SHEET WITH FOUR HANDS ON IT AND NO LEDGER, AND THE PERSON WHO ASKED FOR THE FIGURE IS A PERSON WHO TAKES IN WASHING AT THE SECOND CHANNEL AND WHO HAS A CHILD OF NINE, AND SHE IS NOT THE CLERK OF ANYTHING.**
