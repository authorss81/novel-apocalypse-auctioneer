# Chapter 129: Nobody Knows What It Is Called

---

He went west on the fifteenth of October with a cart and it was two days and a bit, and the sheet from the guarantee house went west on the same road on the same day by a different cart, and neither of them knew about the other and the clerk of nineteen years entered that on the sixteenth of October two sheets left that flat for the same place and were not on the same cart, and that this is the fifth time in ten weeks that two things have been in one line in a minute and not joined.

He came off the raised plank road at the ninth mile on the seventeenth and read the sheet in a room over a shop at the fourth place with about nine people in it, and he read all three things on it and the third one twice.

**The four words he wrote under it are in his own hand in the party's minute and are not in any book, and they are *we already have it*, and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing read them and said nothing about them and wrote nothing under them.**

**There are still five boards.** The number of instruments this district has built and not named is still six, and the number of things it does not have is still five, and neither of those two numbers has changed in three days, because **a board is a place a line of chalk goes and not an instrument**, and a line of chalk is not a way of saying that a toll lands on a place even when it is one.

---

**The board at the fourth place had two more lines on it than it had when he left, and the two lines had been ruled and empty since the twentieth of August, and a boy of nineteen who is paid nothing had filled them and had not been asked and had not said he would not**, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he is not a keeper and that the count of conditions with no end on it is four and that he is in none of them and that the count of protected things is five and that he is in none of those either.

**The four lines on all five boards are the same four, and the four are the four tolls**, and a reader at the counter, who is about fifty and was a signal clerk before her shoulder went and is on eleven coppers a quarter for four days a week, read them out at the fourth place on the seventeenth in four minutes with about nineteen people in the room, and the boy of nineteen did not read them and would not have.

> **A TOLL IS A PLACE, A DAY, AND EITHER AN AMOUNT OR THE FACT THAT THE PLACE CANNOT SAY ONE.**
>
> **THE FIFTH LINE AND THE SIXTH LINE WERE RULED AND EMPTY ON THE TWENTIETH OF AUGUST AND ON THE SEVENTEENTH OF OCTOBER THEY ARE NOT EMPTY AND THE FIFTH AND THE SIXTH ARE THE TWO OF A BOY OF NINETEEN AND THE ENTRY SAYS HE IS NOT A KEEPER.**

**Nobody in that room could say what a board was for**, and about four people said so out loud, and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing asked three people what the thing was called and got three answers and entered all three and chose none.

A man of about fifty-one who keeps a stall on the lower terrace and who supplies the Harrow engine's oil out of her own till said it was a place where people put things they could not say out loud. A woman of about fifty who is the water at the ninth place said it was a notice. A haulier of about forty said it was a **bill**, and then said that a bill is a thing a person posts and a notice is a thing a person reads and a place where people put things they cannot say out loud is a **confessional**, and that he had said the word once in his life and had not known it was a word.

The man of sixty-one with a frame stayed for nineteen minutes after he had written his line and did not sit down, and a clerk of nineteen years stood near him and did not ask him what the line was and entered twice that she had not asked him.

A man of thirty-four who mends fencing came in at about eleven and was going to ask him and did not.

The minute says that he had the question in his mouth and that the man of sixty-one had not come to be asked, and that a man of sixty-one who has come two miles twice a month for nineteen years and stands in a frame at the end of a season is not a person who is in a room to be asked, and that the man of thirty-four said that out loud and the clerk entered it.

The line itself is a place and a day and an amount, and the amount is eleven, and the clerk entered that the amount is eleven and that nobody in the room knows what it is eleven of, and that a haulier of about forty said that if it is eleven coppers it is a figure and if it is eleven of something else then it is not a figure and it is a fifth line on a board, and that the man of sixty-one is not in the room to say which.

**The boy of nineteen was asked by four people in the room over two days why he had put his nine words on a board, and he gave the same answer four times** and it is nineteen words long, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that he was asked four times and gave the same answer four times and that a person who is asked the same question four times and gives the same answer four times has either one answer or is saying a thing he has been asked to say, and that she does not know which and did not ask.

"**The sheet at the fourth place says a train comes once a month in the dark and it does not, and I have said that three times and everybody wrote it down and nobody put it on a wall, and a wall is where people look, and I am not paid and I am not a keeper and I am standing here.**"

Nobody argued with that and a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households said that it was the fourth time in ten weeks that a person in this district had said the thing about literacy out loud, and that the other three had been said by people who could not read, and that this one had been said by a person who can and that makes it four and not three, and that the clerk entered the four and did not join them to the four lines on a board.

**AND THE FINDING OF THE SEVENTEENTH OF OCTOBER IS THAT FIVE BOARDS CARRY SIX LINES AND NOBODY CAN SAY WHAT THE THING IS CALLED, AND THAT THREE PEOPLE WERE ASKED ON ONE AFTERNOON AND GAVE THREE ANSWERS AND THE CLERK ENTERED ALL THREE AND CHOSE NONE, AND THAT A HAULIER OF ABOUT FORTY SAID THE WORD CONFESSIONAL AND THEN SAID HE HAD SAID IT ONCE IN HIS LIFE AND DID NOT KNOW IT WAS A WORD.**

---

**The six lines are full at the fourth place on the eighteenth of October and none of the six was entered by a keeper**, and the minute says that and says it twice, and the six hands are these.

**The first is the man of thirty-one who walks every yard of the railway line, and he is not paid and has not been asked to be paid**, and his line is the ninth place and the twenty-ninth of August and a figure nobody can say.

The second is a woman of fifty-eight who carries water and does not read figures, and she asked a man of thirty-four who mends fencing to write it for her, and he did, and **the minute says that he wrote it and that she did not give it to him and that she watched him write it, and that the two of those are not the same thing and that the board is the only one of the six that has both.**

**The third is a man of sixty-one with a frame who walks two miles twice a month and who has never been in that room**, and he came on the eighteenth and wrote a line in a hand that shakes and did not say what it was, and the clerk entered that he did not say and that nobody asked him.

The fourth is a woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel, and the clerk of nineteen years read her line out and it is nineteen words long and it is about the first channel and about the twelfth of September and about the day not being known.

The fifth is the boy of nineteen, and it is the same nine words he has said three times about a train, and the minute says he said them and that the clerk entered that he is not a keeper and is not paid and was not asked and did not stop, and that **the count of conditions with no end on it is four and the count of protected things is five and neither moved and the boy is in neither.**

**The sixth is a woman of about thirty-six who keeps a stall of chalk and oil on a cart and who wrote the four-line minute about the thirtieth of September**, and her line is about a figure that was not read out.

A man of about fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households said that **four rooms in a district is not a search**, and that nobody in this district has ever called a thing a search before the ninth of March, and that a clerk of nineteen years has entered four rooms in a minute and that a clerk is not a search either.

The three answers about what a board is were written out and read out in four minutes, and all three are in the minute in the three people's own words, and a man of about fifty-six who does count for a living was asked which of the three was right and said in nine words that he had been asked a fourth question and that the answer to the fourth question is that the other three are the three answers and that the three answers are the finding.

A reader at the counter said that a bill is a thing a person posts and a notice is a thing a person reads and a confessional is a thing a person says, and that about four people in this district have said something in a room in ten weeks that they could not say anywhere else, and that there is no column for that and that the column for things that are not findings is not it either because none of the four has been wrong.

A man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households said that the answer to the fourth question is the answer and that the three answers are the finding, and that if anybody in this district could say what the board was for then there would be a heading on it, and that the reason there is no heading is the finding and not an oversight, and that a man who does not read figures said in nine words that the reason there is no heading is that the man who prints charges four coppers for a sheet about one thing and nobody has ever paid four coppers for a sheet with a heading on it.

Nobody argued with that and the clerk of nineteen years entered the whole of it and entered that the figure of four coppers for a sheet about one thing has been said out loud three times in ten weeks by three people who are not the man who prints, and that the man who prints has been asked about it once and said that he could not tell anybody which of the five sheets he had printed was worth the money and would not be asked.

**AND ON THE EIGHTEENTH OF OCTOBER SIX LINES WERE FULL ON ONE BOARD IN A ROOM IN ALDER REACH AND NOT ONE OF THE SIX WAS ENTERED BY A KEEPER, AND THREE OF THE SIX ARE PEOPLE WHO HAVE SAID NO IN A ROOM IN TEN WEEKS, AND ONE OF THE SIX IS A BOY OF NINETEEN WHO IS NOT A KEEPER AND WAS NOT ASKED, AND ONE OF THE SIX IS A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE WITH A FRAME WHO CAME TWO MILES AND DID NOT SAY WHAT HIS LINE WAS, AND NOBODY ASKED HIM.**

---

And then a man of about thirty-one who runs a cart of meal out of a lane in Alder Reach was stopped by the board, on the eighteenth of October, in the street, in four minutes, and the whole of it is in the minute and the clerk of nineteen years read it back to both of them.

He came to the ninth place to take a load of meal to a household that had asked for it and there was a line in the fourth position on the board at the ninth place that said a toll had landed on this place on a day, and the line did not say what the toll was for and did not say who had paid it, and the man of the meal said four words.

"**What does that stop me doing.**"

Nobody answered him for nine seconds, and a woman of about fifty who is the water at the ninth place said the answer and it is in the minute in her own words and it is nineteen words long.

"**It does not stop you doing anything. It is a notice. I wrote two of the six and I am telling you that it does not stop you and I am telling you in a street and not in a room.**"

And the man of the meal took his load and went, and the minute says he went, and says that four people watched him go and that nobody stopped him and that the woman of the column said the other half afterwards in a room and it is in the minute in her own words.

"**It has been used four times this month to stop somebody and every one of the four was a person who had not been asked anything. That is not a rule and I am not going to make it into one, and I am going to keep writing my line.**"

And a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households was told about the four in a room on the seventeenth and asked whether the four had been counted by anybody, and said that they had not, and that a woman of about fifty who is the water at the ninth place had said four and that she might be wrong, and that nobody had asked her to be right, and that the count of four is a count made by a person in a room and is not a column and that a column is a thing a person is paid for and she is not paid.

And on the eighteenth of October, at half past four, a woman of about forty-four who keeps a Road House on the tide asked the man of thirty-eight about the three things on a guarantee house's sheet, because the sheet had come west on the fifteenth and she had read it at a counter and had not understood it and had not asked anybody at the counter, and he read all three of them out to her in four minutes and she listened and did not interrupt him once.

"**Say the second one again.**"

"**First refusal on the person who stands in the frame. My house names the person before the room does and nobody may ask why.**"

"And you did not say no to that in a room on a flat."

"**I was thirty-four miles east and I have not been in that room and I am not going to pretend I have. I have read a sheet and I have read it twice and I have written four words under it and the four words are we already have it, and those four words are mine and they are not an answer and they are not a no, and if you want an answer from me then you have to ask me and I have not been asked.**"

And a clerk of nineteen years entered that, and entered that the man of thirty-eight had been asked a question by a woman of about forty-four who keeps a Road House on the tide and that he had answered it in four minutes and that he had said he had not been asked, and that a woman of about forty-four who has done it eleven years had said the same two sentences on the twenty-eighth of September about four sheets, and that the clerk had put the two in one line and had not joined them, and that this is the sixth time.

**AND THE FINDING OF THE EIGHTEENTH OF OCTOBER IS THAT THE FIFTH OF THE FIVE INSTRUMENTS THIS DISTRICT DOES NOT HAVE WAS USED FOUR TIMES IN ELEVEN DAYS TO STOP A PERSON WHO HAD NOT BEEN ASKED ANYTHING, AND THAT EIGHT HANDS HAVE PUT SOMETHING ON IT, AND THAT A BOARD IS NOT AN INSTRUMENT, AND THAT NOT ONE OF THE FOUR USES WAS RULED ON BY A KEEPER, AND THAT THE COUNT OF THE FOUR WAS MADE BY A PERSON WHO IS NOT PAID AND WAS NOT ASKED TO BE RIGHT.**

---

Then the man of thirty-eight was asked, on the eighteenth of October, in a room over a shop at the fourth place, by a person he had not met, whether the boards were a way of saying that a toll lands on a place, and he said no in four seconds and then said the other half in nine seconds and it is in the minute in his own words.

"**They are the fifth thing this district does not have and they are the only one of the five anybody has ever put a hand on. If they were the thing itself then one of us would be able to name them and nobody can and I have been asked by three people in ten weeks and I have said the same words to all three of them, and the words are that a board with four lines on it and no name on it is a notice until somebody in this district can say what a notice is for.**"

"And you could say what it is used for."

"**I could say what it is used for. Used for is not what it is for. This district has spent ten weeks finding that difference out the hard way and it is going to be handed down to somebody in four years and I would like it written that I said used for and not for.**"

And on the eighteenth of October, in the early evening, a man of about forty who had put in the road came into the room at the fourth place and was asked what the boards were, and he said that he had put in a road and that a road is a thing a cart goes on and that he had put in nine miles of it in the third month of this year and had been asked by nobody what it was for, and that a board is not a road and that he did not know what a board was and that he was not going to pretend.

A man of thirty-four who mends fencing asked him whether he had ever been asked whether a road was worth a toll, and he said that he had, once, by a woman of about fifty-eight who carries water and who had asked him in a lane whether a cart that pays four coppers at a stall in front of a counter of oil on Ash Row is paying for the road, and that he had said no at the time because he did not know, and that she had not asked him again, and that he had thought about it every day since the third month of this year.

Nobody asked him anything else and a clerk of nineteen years entered the whole of it and entered that a road and a board and a figure of twenty-one years and a figure of one hundred and eighty-three coppers are four things in this district and that no two of the four are joined, and that she had wanted to put them in one line and had not and that not doing a thing is also a thing she does.

**AND THE FINDING OF THE EIGHTEENTH OF OCTOBER IS ALSO THAT A MAN WHO PUT IN NINE MILES OF RAISED PLANK ROAD IN THE THIRD MONTH OF THIS YEAR WAS ASKED ONCE BY A WOMAN WHO CARRIES WATER WHETHER A CART THAT PAYS FOUR COPPERS AT A STALL IN FRONT OF A COUNTER OF OIL ON ASH ROW IS PAYING FOR THE ROAD, AND HE SAID NO BECAUSE HE DID NOT KNOW, AND SHE DID NOT ASK HIM AGAIN, AND A BOARD, A ROAD, A FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS AND A FIGURE OF ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-THREE COPPERS ARE FOUR THINGS IN THIS DISTRICT AND NO TWO OF THE FOUR ARE JOINED.**

And the finding of the fifteenth to the eighteenth of October is that **the fifth of the five things this district does not have is the only one of the five that anybody puts a hand on, and that it is not one of the six things the district has built and not named, and that it is not one of the four and not the one document nobody owns and not the nineteen lines, and that the only thing that is true about it is that it has no owner, and that nobody can say what it is called, and that nobody has asked.**
