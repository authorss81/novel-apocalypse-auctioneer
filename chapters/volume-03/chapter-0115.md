# Chapter 115: East of Four Thousand and One

The month turned on the first of September and nobody in Alder Reach put a line in a book about it for the third time in a year, and the reason is the same reason it was not written down at the first of July and was not written down at the first of August, which is that the printed timetable prints days of the month and not days of the week and gives the reason on its face.

Two carts went out of Alder Reach at about six in the morning on the first with eight people on the first and two on the second, and the man who drives salt had said that the two carts go together or not at all, and nineteen people were at the ninth mile of the raised plank road at about two in the afternoon to see them go over it, and the water was off the planks and nobody wrote that down in the right place and it was written down in a minute by a clerk of nineteen years four hours later.

The walk up the bank to the ninth place is five and a quarter hours in ordinary weather with four changeovers and nobody in this district has ever recomputed it from its halves since a paperer put her own figure on a sheet and the figure is five and a half going and six coming and eleven and a half in the day, and a rehearsal's total is not the district's total and there has been no rehearsal since the twenty-seventh of last month's line.

**AND ON THE FIRST OF SEPTEMBER TEN PEOPLE WENT EAST OF THE ABOUT FOUR THOUSAND AND ONE HOUSEHOLDS, AND NOBODY FROM THIS DISTRICT HAS EVER BEEN EAST OF THEM, AND THE FINDING OF THE FIRST OF SEPTEMBER IS THAT A DISTRICT OF FOUR THOUSAND AND ONE HOUSEHOLDS IS NOT A DISTRICT AND HAS NEVER BEEN ONE AND WAS NEVER MEANT TO BE.**

Thirty miles is four days with two carts and ten miles a day, and the ground between the ninth mile and the loams has not carried a wheel since the flood year came up, and a haulier of forty said in a yard in the third week of August that this is the whole of the risk and that nobody is going if the water is on the planks.

The river is nine feet above where it was and it is not a ford and the raised plank road is nine miles above the crossing and it is a causeway of oak plank on a bed of laid stone with four feet of freeboard on a normal day and a rail on one side put there in the fourth year and never taken off.

Nobody in this district knows who laid it. A man who drives salt has crossed it about nine times in two years and says the plank nearest the far end is not oak and never was, and that a man about forty who was in that lane on the second of September looked at it for four minutes and said that was limewood and would be a limewood tree about nineteen years, and that nothing in the basin grows limewood and has for a long time.

Nobody entered that. The minute says nobody entered it and the reason given in the minute is that a timber is a timber and a road is a road and this district has nine weeks of one kind of question in front of it and the clerk who wrote it down said she did not want to be the person who started a second kind.

---

The first night was on a rise about four miles short of the loams and ten people slept in two carts and nineteen of the household that owns that ground came out with mats and a kettle and were not thanked and were not asked, and a man of thirty-eight was given a folded blanket by a woman of sixty-one who holds the ground the third loom stands on, and she gave it to him and not to the party and she said why in four seconds when he asked her.

"**You are the only one of them who is going to be asked what it costs, and I have watched four people come to this bank in two years and two of them were asked what it costs and one of those two asked it as though the answer were a figure, and I am not saying that to be unkind, I am saying it because I am sixty-one and I have about eleven years of being right in.**"

The party did not reach the loams on the third and it reached them on the fourth, and four days is a figure two people gave and one of them was the man of fifty-six who came out to meet them on the second evening and walk back with them and give the other half of the number.

"**Thirty miles is thirty miles on a road. Over the loams it is about thirty-four, and the four extra are a rise of ninety feet that a cart does not enjoy, and I have watched a man from your bank come thirty miles in a day and I could not do it and I have been doing it for two years.**"

The party's own minute was written on the second of September at about seven in the evening and it is four lines long and it is the only document any of them made on the far side of the river, and the minute says it was composed in a cart by a clerk of nineteen years on her knee and read out twice, and the man who drove salt asked for one thing to be added to it.

> **TEN PEOPLE CAME. NINE WERE ASKED IN THE FIVE THINGS ON THE WEST BANK AND ONE WAS NOT ASKED AND IS ENTERED AS NOT ASKED. FOUR DAYS OUT. NOBODY WENT BACK WITH ANYTHING TO SELL. THE QUESTION WE CAME WITH IS STILL THE QUESTION WE CAME WITH AND IT IS STILL NOT ANSWERED, AND NOBODY OUT HERE HAS ANSWERED IT EITHER, AND THAT IS ENTERED TOO.**

**AND IT IS ENTERED THAT NOBODY OUT HERE HAD EVER BEEN ASKED IT BEFORE, AND THAT FOUR THOUSAND AND ONE HOUSEHOLDS AND ABOUT NINE HUNDRED HOUSEHOLDS IN NO ROW AND NINETY PANS ARE TWO SIDES OF A RIVER AND NOT ONE PERSON HAS STOOD ON BOTH.**

And a paperer of thirty-eight who comes up that bank once a month for four houses had brought a copy of the printed timetable in her bag because she is the kind of person who brings a sheet, and she took it out on the second evening and read it aloud to about nineteen people in a cart by a lamp, and it says in its own printed type that a train stops at a place called the fourth place once a month in the dark.

"And where is the fourth place."

"**Two miles west of the ninth place, and thirty-four miles from here by the road, and it has not been true since the twenty-ninth of June.**"

Nobody in that cart argued with her and four people did not know what she was talking about and one of them was a signalman of thirty-one from the west bank who said so in nine words and then said the rest of it.

"**I have been on a line for nine years and I have never once had to think about a sheet being wrong, and I have just found out in a cart that a thing this district prints is a thing that can be wrong and cannot be fixed, and I do not know what to do with that and I am not going to pretend I do.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered on the second of September that a printed sheet has now been found to be wrong in a place where nobody in that place had ever seen it, and that nobody at the counter of that counter had told anybody, and that a document which has become wrong for a reason nobody caused is a document nobody owns, and that the man who prints it is thirty-four miles away and would not print a correction.

The second of September was the crossing, and the crossing took four hours and nineteen people were on the near bank and ten on the far one, and nothing happened on it and the finding of the second of September is that the most dangerous thing ten people did in three days was a thing nobody wrote about.

The pan ground is not pans. It is loam, laid out in rectangles, with a channel cut at the head of each rectangle and a bed at the low end of it, and the water comes in at the top and is walked down by a person's judgement and not by a fall, and the salt comes out of the water whole in the bed and nowhere else.

About sixty-one of the ninety are under water. Nine of the sixty-one have water standing in them and no loam in them at all, and the party counted them on the fourth from a rise with a field book and got sixty-one twice and the man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households watched them do it and said the number was right.

"**And you could have told us before we came.**"

"**I could have. I did not, because a man who has been asked a question by a stranger four times in two years has learned to write down what he is told and not what he knows, and I would like that entered, because it is about me and not about you.**"

And then the thing the man who drives salt had actually come for, which he said out loud for the first time on the fourth of September in nine seconds to a man of thirty-eight who had not asked him.

"**Two years. Every time I came west somebody told me about a number. Nine hundred and six. Four thousand and one. Fifty coppers a quarter. And every one of those numbers is about how much of a thing there is, and not one of them is about how much of me there is going to be in November, and that is the number I want and it is not a copper.**"

The man of thirty-eight did not answer him and the minute says he did not answer him, and says that a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said afterwards that a man who drives salt had spent two years carrying a number that is not money between two banks of a river, and that the number is the same shape as a year of somebody's memory, and that neither of them is a price and both of them are a thing somebody has to pay.

**AND THE FINDING OF THE FOURTH OF SEPTEMBER IS THAT A LOOM IS DESCRIBED THE WAY A PERSON DESCRIBES A JOB AND NOT THE WAY A PERSON DESCRIBES A THING, AND THAT A RATE SHEET IS DESCRIBED THE WAY A PERSON DESCRIBES A THING, AND THAT THE TWO DESCRIPTIONS CAME FROM OPPOSITE BANKS OF THE SAME RIVER AND NEITHER OF THEM HAS MET THE OTHER.**

The third of September the loams came in sight at about eleven in the morning and the party stopped on a rise about two hundred yards short of them because the man of thirty-four who mends fencing would not go on until somebody asked him to, and four hours were lost and nobody regretted them afterwards, and the finding of the third of September is that **a man who has been asked in the five things for a job can stop a whole party, and that is not a power and it is the only thing that has worked like a power in nine weeks.**

There are about ninety pans on the Verge and the party counted about sixty-one standing in river water, and nine of the sixty-one have water standing in them and not a loam, and four of the ninety are being worked by anybody, and the man who drives salt had said that in August in a lane in Alder Reach and had been believed and had not been checked and was right.

Two of the ninety have gone to salt on their own since the flood year and one of those two is a pan a mile and a half from the loams and the salt off it came out of the ground in nine weeks. **A third has been doing it since before the man who drives salt came west, and there is a wind frame standing over that third one and there is nobody in it, and the finding of the fourth of September is that this district has been arguing for nine weeks about a piece of iron in a bracket four hundred yards from a water office and the thing thirty miles east of it has been standing there with nobody in it since before any of them arrived.**

Halla Dree is thirty-eight and she has charge of the second channel and has stood in it four years, and she was the first person the party spoke to and she was in it when they came over the rise, and she did not get out of it, and four people asked her to come out and she said no in nine seconds and then said why in nine more.

"**Because if I come out of it the water goes where it wants and I have to be the one who tells it where to go, and I can tell it from in there and not from out here, and that is the whole of what I am and I am not going to be a woman standing beside a channel showing a foreign district what a channel is.**"

Then a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households on this bank said the thing that is now the entry of the fourth of September, and he said it to about ten people who had come thirty miles and had been in this district's books for nine weeks.

"**You have a rate sheet and a timetable and four toll boards and five boards and a hole in the ground and six things you have built and cannot name, and we have a channel and a wind frame and a bed of brine and a person in it, and I have been trying for two years to work out how to write the second list down and I have never once been able to, and I would like it entered that I have never been able to.**"

Nobody argued with him and nineteen people in that party had said the same sentence in Alder Reach in a different month about a different thing, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that she knew it and that she had said the sentence herself, and that a person who has said a thing in one place and hears it in another place is not a person who has found a thing.

---

**AND ON THE FOURTH OF SEPTEMBER A LOOM WAS DESCRIBED FOR THE FIRST TIME BY A PERSON WHO IS STANDING IN IT, IN HER OWN WORDS, IN ABOUT NINE SECONDS, AND IT WAS DESCRIBED THE WAY A PERSON DESCRIBES A JOB AND NOT THE WAY A PERSON DESCRIBES A THING, AND NOBODY IN THE PARTY WROTE IT DOWN AT THE TIME AND ALL TEN OF THEM WROTE IT DOWN AFTERWARDS IN FOUR PLACES.**

What Halla Dree said was this, and she said it without getting out of the frame and without stopping, and the man who drives salt heard it first and repeated it to the rest of the party and it is his repetition that is in the minute, and the minute says so.

"**It is a job. You are on your feet from before it is light and the frame turns when the wind is right and you stop it when the wind is wrong, and your hands are cold for nine hours and your back is not yours after the fourth day. You do not watch a loom. A loom is the only thing in this basin that does the work and it will not do it for you and it will not stop for you. The bed fills and you empty it or the salt goes back into the water. You eat standing up. You sleep four hours. And when the season turns you are not asked whether you will do it again and you are told, and the telling comes the same way the wind comes.**"

The man of thirty-eight was in the party and did not say anything for eleven minutes, and then he said the only question he asked in three days, and it is in the minute in his own words and it is four words long.

"**What do you lose.**"

Nobody in that frame answered him for four seconds and a woman of thirty-one who had been in a channel about two hundred yards off and who had come over to stand nine feet away said it, and her name is Ressa Karrow and she is in her second year and this is the first time anybody from the west bank has asked her anything at all.

"**A year. Not a thing that happened. A year. I can tell you what the water did last week and I cannot tell you what I did last spring, and I know the channel and I know the bed and I know the wind, and there is a gap in me the shape of twelve months and I did not put it there and nobody told me it was going there.**"

The man of thirty-eight did not ask a second question and the minute says he did not ask a second question, and says that a man of thirty-four who mends fencing asked him afterwards in four seconds whether he wanted to be asked again and he said no, and that he said it in four seconds and not eleven.


---

And on the fourth, after she had said the thing about a year, Ressa Karrow was asked one question by a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households, and it was the only question anybody asked her that day, and the party heard the answer and did not understand it for two days.

"Are you paid."

"**No.**"

"Then who is."

"**Nobody, and that is not the interesting part. The interesting part is that I am asked this every spring by about nineteen people and I say yes every spring, and there is a word for it out here and the word is standing, and standing is not a wage and it is not a wage with a bad name, it is a different shape of thing, and I have been in it two years and I would not know how to be paid and I am not saying I would not like to be.**"

"You would."

"**I would like the year back. That is not the same as being paid and I want the two of them kept apart in whatever you write down, because a person who says she would like a year back has said something about a year and a person who says she would like money has said something about a market, and this bank is not a market and the year does not come back whether anybody is paying or not.**"

The clerk of nineteen years wrote that down and read it back and got one word wrong and was corrected in four seconds by the man of fifty-six, and the clerk entered that this is the sixth time in nine weeks that a person has corrected a document by saying what they needed in it, and that all six were said by people who were not clerks and none of the six were paid for saying it.

A licensed valuer of twelve years was not in the party and nobody explained why, and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing was asked it on the second evening and gave a reason that is fifty-five words long and is the whole of what the valuer is for in this district's books.

"**A lot is a thing somebody can be shown and a loom cannot be shown and it can only be stood in, and a man with a certificate has to be able to write a column, and there is no column, and he would have had to stand in a channel and get a year.**"

"And you did not ask him."

"**I did not ask him because I did not want to be the man who asked a man of twelve years and a great many certifications to stand in a channel for a year so that ten of us could have a document, and I have been in nine rooms in nine weeks and I know what that looks like before it happens and I stopped it.**"

Nobody argued and the minute says nobody argued, and says that a man of thirty-eight who has four powers and has used none of them in nine weeks was in a cart and listened to that and wrote nothing down, and that the clerk of nineteen years saw him not writing it down and entered that he had chosen not to, and that she entered it because it is the fourth time in nine weeks he has been asked for a limit of his and has given a different one.

---

And the last thing of the fourth of September was not a loom and not a pan and not a channel, and it is in the minute at the end of the day, and it is a question asked by a man of thirty-one who is a signalman of the west bank's own crew and who is not on the west bank's list of nine and who came on the second cart.

He had been in nine rooms in nine weeks and had watched a man refuse a thing in a yard in front of two hundred people, and he had not understood it and had said so afterwards, and he asked the question on the fourth of September at about seven in the evening with about nineteen people sitting on a bank because there was nowhere else to sit.

"**If it is a year, and nobody pays for it, and it goes in a hole in the person, then what happens to the person on the first of October, and who writes that down, and is it written down on this bank or on yours.**"

Nobody on either bank answered him and a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households answered him at half past seven in the evening in nine seconds, and then said the other half, and the other half is the entry of the fourth of September.

"**It is written down on this bank. Not one line of it is on yours. And I have been a secretary for two years and I have nine households and nineteen names and I have never once been asked by anybody on that bank to write down what a year costs that is not a copper, and I have not written one, and I am telling you that on the fourth of September and not after, because you are the first person from that side of the water who asked a question instead of a price.**"

Then the man who drives salt said the thing that ends the fourth of September, and it is twelve words long and it is a question and not an answer, and it is in the minute in his own words.

"**Then who do I write this on, when it is a place.**"


On the first of September the train on the siding stood two hundred and two days, and on the fourth it stood two hundred and five, and a boy of seventeen wrote both figures in his own hand in an index at Alder Reach and struck none of the old ones, and about nine people at the ninth mile of the raised plank road had said in a yard that a district of four thousand and one households is not a district and has never been one, and the boy wrote the two figures under that sentence because it was the only thing in nine weeks he had wanted to write down and nobody had asked him to.

A clerk of nineteen years was in the party and she entered on the fifth of September, in a cart thirty-four miles from a counter, that the fifth of the five dates in the index is the twenty-ninth of last month, which was the twenty-ninth of August, and that the party was thirty-four miles from the only person who writes those figures down, and that she entered it because a line with no name against it is a column and not a payment, and that a figure in a book can be read by a stranger and carried to a door, and that the stranger in this case is thirty-four miles away and does not know it yet.

And the finding of the fourth of September is that a person who stands in a thing all day and is not paid to be anywhere else and writes nothing down has now been described four times in nine weeks by four people who have never met, and **the district has joined none of the four, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a fourth description of one thing is a coincidence and is not a finding until somebody puts a thing on the paper that joins them, and that the fourth of the four is about two hundred yards from a channel and there is no boy in this party.**
