# Chapter 119: Four Names and One Room

A guild inspector of forty-four certified a consent on the fifteenth of September in nine minutes on the back of a page in a room over a shop on the second channel, with about nineteen people in it, and the certificate is valid, and the finding of the fifteenth of September is that **a certificate is valid and truth is a different thing, and this district has spent nine weeks learning to tell those two apart in every room it has been in except this one, and the exception is a room with a form on the table.**

> **CONSENT OF THE THIRD CHANNEL TO BE RUN FOR TWO DAYS FROM THE SIXTEENTH OF SEPTEMBER TO THE EIGHTEENTH OF SEPTEMBER, FOR THE PURPOSE OF FILLING TWO PANS HELD BY A MAN WHO DRIVES SALT. FOUR PERSONS HAVE CONSENTED. CERTIFIED BY A GUILD INSPECTOR OF SIXTEEN YEARS ON THE FIFTEENTH OF SEPTEMBER. THE FOUR ARE A WOMAN OF THIRTY-ONE, A MAN OF TWENTY-SIX, A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-SIX, AND A WOMAN OF ABOUT SIXTY-ONE. THIS CERTIFICATE IS VALID.**

He read it out twice before he signed it and the clerk of nineteen years read it back and got one word wrong, and the word was *consented* and she had written *agreed*, and he corrected her and said the reason in nine words.

"**A person can agree to a price. A person cannot consent to a thing that is not offered to them. I have certified four hundred lots and the word on all four hundred is consented, and I am not going to change it at nine minutes to nine on the far bank of a river.**"

Nobody in that room argued with him and nineteen people were in it and a man of thirty-eight who is a Binder was in it and said nothing at all, and the minute says he said nothing at all and that the clerk of nineteen years entered that she noticed.

**AND THE FINDING OF THE FIFTEENTH OF SEPTEMBER IS THAT A FORM WITH TWENTY-NINE COLUMNS HAS FOUR COLUMNS FOR TWO PEOPLE, AND THAT A CONSENT SHEET WITH FIVE NAMES ON IT HAS ONE COLUMN FOR CONSENT SET WIDE ENOUGH FOR FIVE, AND THAT NEITHER OF THOSE IS A FAULT IN ANYBODY, AND THAT THE ONLY THING MISSING FROM BOTH IS A COLUMN FOR WHETHER A PERSON WAS IN THE ROOM.**

The inspector was asked it by a man of thirty-four who mends fencing, in four minutes, and he gave the answer that is now the entry of the fifteenth of September and it took him nine seconds and it is nineteen words long.

"**A signature has always meant the person was in the room. That is what a signature is. And I have certified four hundred lots on it and it has been true every time, and nobody in sixteen years has ever needed the column because nobody has ever handed me a signature from two days' walk away.**"

"And this one."

"**This one is not a forgery. That is the thing I would like written first, before anything else, in four words, on the front. Nobody has forged anything. Four people were asked. Four people answered. One of the four was not in the room, and she told me on the fourteenth that she was not in the room and she told me she understood it in four minutes and she is not taking it back, and I certified it anyway because my form asks me whether they consented and they did.**"

Nobody in that room said anything about that and a woman of fifty-six who keeps the only pair on the west bank was not in it and has not been told and the count of not-askings did not move.

Then the man of thirty-eight said the thing he had come to say and he said it in eleven seconds and the clerk entered it in her own hand and the minute says it is the fifth thing he has said in nine weeks that is about a limit and it is different from the other four.

**A STRIKE IS A THING AN INSTRUMENT DOES, AND A BINDER HAS FIVE POWERS, AND A STRIKE OF ANOTHER BODY'S INSTRUMENT IS NOT ONE OF THEM, AND A BINDER MAY WRITE A BOUNDED CHARTER AND SPLIT A USE AND CLOSE A TRANSFER AND MAKE A LOT PORTABLE UNDER A STATED TERM AND POST A SECURITY, AND NOT ONE OF THE FIVE REACHES INTO A ROOM HE HAS NOT BEEN IN.**

"**I cannot strike it. I want that in the certificate's own file and not only in our minute. A strike is a thing an instrument does, and I have five powers and a strike of another body's instrument is not one of them, and there is nothing in any of the five that reaches into a guild room thirty-four miles from a counter, and a man who cannot strike a thing is not a man who has been refused.**"

"And can it be stopped."

"**No. And the second half of that is worse than the first. If it could not be done I would have said so and this would be a wall. It can be done, by about four people, and the four of them are not here and one of the four is a mark and the mark is a man of twenty-six who said yes in four seconds and whose yes is worth as much as anybody's, and there is no column for him.**"

---

And the registrar was told on the sixteenth of September, in a counting room over a chandler's shop, in nine minutes, by a clerk of nineteen years who had crossed the plank road and back in two days with a certified copy in her bag, and a chandler of thirty-four who keeps a counter of oil on Ash Row was in the room and heard all of it.

She read the certificate out twice. Then she read the sheet it had been made from, which had been copied into the minute of the party of ten, and she compared the two for eleven minutes and four people were in the room and nobody interrupted her.

Then she refused, and it is the twelfth refusal, and the reason she gave is the reason she gives every time and the minute says it is the reason she gives every time and that nobody in that room expected anything else.

"**I will not enter four consents on that page in nine copies on eleven boards, and here is the whole of it in three parts. A mark is not a person and a mark may not be counted as a person who is asked. A woman of sixty-one signed a sheet on the thirteenth of September and was two days' walk away at a market and has said so herself and has not taken it back, and this basin has a word for a person who was never asked and no word at all for a person who was asked from two days' walk away. And a certificate is not a record in this book, and a record in this book cannot make a thing in a guild room untrue, and I have been keeping two lines apart for nine weeks and this is the first time in nine weeks that keeping them apart has cost anybody anything.**"

Nobody in that room argued with her and the clerk of nineteen years wrote all three parts down and the clerk did not ask why, and the minute says she did not ask why, and says that it is the fourth time in nine weeks that a clerk in this district has had a reason in front of her and has not asked for it.

"You could enter it with a line under it."

"**I could, and I did not, and here is the fourth reason and it is the one I would like anybody to argue with. If I enter a consent and then put a line under it, then in four years a stranger will take that page cold and see a consent and a line, and he will read the consent, because people read the top of a page, and the line will be underneath it where I put it because I wanted it to be a good place for it.**"

A man of thirty-four who mends fencing was in that room and he said the other half, and it is in the minute in his own words and it is nine words long.

"**Then the page goes in and the line goes on.**"

"**Then the page goes in and the line goes on, and I will put the line in the column for things that are not findings and not in the book, and the column has seven things in it in nine weeks and this is not one of them, because I am not wrong out loud about this one. I am right and I am the only one in this room who can prove it, and being right about a thing and being able to stop the thing are two different things and this district has been building the first one for nine weeks and it is the second one that has never once worked.**"

Nobody in that room argued and the entry of the sixteenth of September says that the registrar entered nothing, and that the certificate stands, and that the finding of the sixteenth of September is that **a book that a stranger can go and read is the only instrument this district owns and it has never once been able to reach a thing outside the room it is read in, and that this is the first time that has cost a person a year.**

---


---

The clerk of nineteen years was gone for two days and the party of nine was down to nine, and the finding of the eighteenth of September is that a district with four thousand and one households can lose a member of its own party to a document and cannot be told about it for two days, and that nobody on the far bank knew she had gone and nobody on this bank knew when she was coming back.

She went over the raised plank road on the morning of the sixteenth with a certified copy in a bag and a sheet of her own in a second bag, and a man of thirty-one who is a signalman of the west bank's own crew walked as far as the ninth mile with her and came back at about four in the afternoon, and the minute says he walked her there and came back and did not go further, and that he had said on the tenth of September that he would walk one person and had said it in four words.

"Will you cross."

"I walked to the ninth mile and back. That is nine miles of a road and a foot of plank and I have a road I have to know about and a rail I have to know about, and there is one pair of me."

"And if somebody needed walking on the far bank."

"Then somebody who has a pair walks them, and it is not going to be me, and I have said that in four words and the four words are in a minute and I am not going to say them a fifth time."

She was two days on the road both ways and the ground between the ninth mile and the loams had not carried a wheel since the flood year and had now carried two carts and a person with a bag, and the haulier of forty had said in the third week of August that this is the whole of the risk and he had been right twice.

A paperer of thirty-eight read out the certificate to about nineteen people on the seventeenth, in a room on the second channel, in the ordinary voice, and read it out twice because the rule of a counter is that a document goes out twice and there is no counter on this bank and there has never been one, and a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households said afterwards that the rule travelled thirty-four miles without anybody deciding that it should and that this is the only administrative instrument anybody on either bank has ever exported by accident.

---

And on the eighteenth of September, in the evening, the man of thirty-eight and the guild inspector of forty-four sat on a bank about four hundred yards from the third channel and worked out what would have to exist, and it took an hour and a half and the clerk of nineteen years was not back yet and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing wrote it down because nobody else was writing and it is nineteen lines in the party's minute.

"It has to be a column."

"It has to be more than a column. A column can be ruled on any form in this basin in four minutes and the man who prints has a press and a tram arch. What it needs is a form that everybody who signs has to use, and a person who keeps the copies, and a place the copies are kept in, and four people whose job it is to be wrong in public once a season."

"That is a public office."

The man of thirty-eight said nothing for eleven seconds and the minute says he said nothing for eleven seconds and that the inspector said the next thing.

"**I did not say that. You said that. I have been sixteen years in a guild and I have certified four hundred lots and I know what I need and it is a column and a keeper and a place, and none of those is a thing anybody has to ask a person about, and I have watched you cross a river four days ago to find out that the thing you are for cannot be done and I am not going to help you build a name for it in a field.**"

"I did not say anything."

"**You did not say anything. You went quiet for eleven seconds and I have been in a room with nine hundred people in sixteen years and eleven seconds of a man of thirty-eight going quiet is a sentence, and I am a man who writes down other people's sentences for a living and I am telling you that you said it.**"

Nobody in that bank said anything about it and the clerk of nineteen years came back on the evening of the eighteenth and read the nineteen lines out in the ordinary voice when she had read everything else, and the man of thirty-four who mends fencing asked her whether she was going to enter them, and she said no, and gave the reason in nine words.

"**They are nineteen lines about a thing that does not exist yet. If I enter them, then in four years a stranger will read nineteen lines about a thing that does not exist yet and he will think it does.**"

And the registrar read the nineteen lines on the twentieth of September, in a counting room over a chandler's shop, in front of nineteen people, and entered them, and the entry says that the nineteen lines are in the public lot book in nine copies on eleven boards and that they are not a finding and that the district has four things it does not have and six instruments it has built and not named and one document that has become wrong for a reason nobody caused, and that the nineteen lines are the seventh of the four and are not to be added to the six.

---

And on the eighteenth the man of thirty-one walked the man of thirty-four who mends fencing back to the carts because he had been in a room for two days and had not eaten, and the minute says the walk took four minutes and that neither of them said anything about the thirtieth of last month and that neither of them said anything at all, and that the man of thirty-one has not written a word in a book since the sixteenth of July and has been asked it four times in nine weeks and has said four times that a man is not a column.

And the line book at the east end has nine hundred and six movements in it and one more, and the one more is the twenty-ninth of June, and there is nothing in it for the twenty-ninth of August because nothing moved, and the boy of seventeen measured the stall for the sixth time in the fourth month of this year and it has not moved and the party of nine was nine hundred miles from the tape when he did it.


---

And on the eighteenth of September a man of fifty-two who came out of a channel in the fourth month of this year, after four years, and who has been trying to sell a loam for two years without telling anybody, sat in the room when the second certificate was read out and then said one thing about it, and the clerk of nineteen years was in the room by then and wrote it down and the minute says she asked him whether he wanted it in his own hand and he said yes and gave the reason in nine words.

"**Because it is the first thing I have said out loud about selling and it is going to be in a book on the other side of a river and I would like it in a hand that a woman of thirty-one can recognise.**"

What he said was this, and it is nineteen words long.

"**I have two valid certificates in a drawer and neither is wrong, and I have no way to sell the frame and no way to fill my own pans, and the difference is me.**"

Nobody argued and the man of thirty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households said afterwards that the sentence had been in that bank for two years in nine houses and that a man had finally said it in a room with a form on the table, and that the form was not the reason and the room was.

**AND THE FINDING OF THE EIGHTEENTH OF SEPTEMBER IS THAT THE THING WHICH STOPS A SALE OUT HERE IS NOT THE PRICE AND NOT THE CERTIFICATE AND NOT A MARK, AND THAT IT IS A PERSON WHO SAYS OUT LOUD THAT THE THING IS HIM, AND THAT FOUR PEOPLE HAVE NOW SAID IT IN FOUR WEEKS AND NONE OF THE FOUR WERE ASKED.**

And then the man of thirty-eight did the last thing he did on the far bank of the river, and it was in nine minutes, and the clerk of nineteen years entered it and the minute says it is the last entry of the party's time there and that it is nineteen lines and that the tenth hand read it out twice before it was agreed to.

> **A LOOM IS NOT A THING THIS DISTRICT CAN BUY, AND THE REASON IS NOT THE PRICE. A LOOM IS A THING, A USE, A BEARER, A TOLL, A TERM, A RELEASE AND A REMEDY, AND SIX OF THE SEVEN CAN BE WRITTEN DOWN AND THE SEVENTH IS A PERSON. THE PRICE WAS TWO THOUSAND COPPERS AND IT WAS A FIGURE DONE IN ELEVEN SECONDS OFF THE PRICE OF SALT, AND THE PERSON SAID NO FOUR TIMES IN FOUR SENTENCES, AND THE MAN KNEW ON THE EIGHTH OF SEPTEMBER AND WROTE THE TERMS ANYWAY, AND THE TERMS ARE IN A FOLDER WITH NO NAME ON IT.**

And then a man of thirty-four who mends fencing added one line to it in his own hand and the line is nineteen words long and the clerk of nineteen years did not take it out and the registrar read it on the twentieth and entered it under the nineteen and not above them.

"**AND A DISTRICT THAT BUILT SIX INSTRUMENTS IT CANNOT NAME IN NINE WEEKS HAS NOW MET ONE IT CANNOT PRICE, AND NEITHER OF THOSE IS THE OFFICE, AND I AM NOT ALLOWED TO WRITE THE WORD AND I AM NOT GOING TO.**"

And on the sixteenth and the seventeenth the third channel was run for two days, and the man of twenty-six and the woman of thirty-one were in it and nineteen people from households on this bank stood about two hundred yards off and watched, and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing wrote down what he saw in nineteen lines and it is in the party of ten's minute and the minute is the only document any of them made about it.

The finding of the seventeenth of September is that **a year does not go twice.** The toll is a year and it has gone once, in her first April, and two days of running a channel in her second September cost her nothing anybody on either bank could see, and four people on the west bank said afterwards that this was the first thing about a loom that they had believed and it was not a thing anybody had told them.

The two pans of the man who drives salt were under four feet of river and are not now, and the salt off them has not been touched and will not be until the water has gone down, and he said that in nine seconds to a man of thirty-eight and the minute has it.

"**That is two pans of ninety and I have had four of ninety about two years and I have been walking eleven miles twice a year to ask a question that four people in a yard cannot answer, and the answer was a woman of thirty-one and a certificate.**"

"And is that the answer you wanted."

"**It is not the answer I asked for and it is the answer I got, and I have been asking the wrong question for two years and I worked that out on the thirteenth of September in a room and I would like it entered that the wrong question was mine.**"

And the guild inspector of forty-four wrote a second certificate on the seventeenth of September, in nine minutes, on the same form, and the clerk of nineteen years copied it and read it back and it is in the party's minute and it is the second of the two and neither of the two can be struck.

> **A DEFECT UPON THE CERTIFICATE OF THE FIFTEENTH OF SEPTEMBER. ONE OF THE FOUR PERSONS NAMED WAS NOT IN THE ROOM WHEN THE CONSENT WAS GIVEN, ON THE ADMISSION OF THE PERSON HERSELF, AND THE FORM HAS NO COLUMN FOR IT. THIS CERTIFICATE IS ALSO VALID.**

**AND TWO VALID CERTIFICATES IN ONE DRAWER IN A GUILD ROOM SAY OPPOSITE THINGS ABOUT THE SAME SHEET, AND NEITHER OF THEM CAN BE STRUCK, AND THE INSPECTOR OF SIXTEEN YEARS WROTE BOTH OF THEM AND SAID SO IN THE SAME ROOM, AND HE IS NOT WRONG IN EITHER, AND THE FINDING OF THE SEVENTEENTH OF SEPTEMBER IS THAT THE ONLY WAY TO BE WRONG ABOUT A CONSENT IN THIS BASIN IS TO BE RIGHT TWICE.**
