# Chapter 127: The Eighth Refusal

---

He went east on the eighth of October with a cart and it was two days and a bit, and the man of fifty-six travelled with him, and on the ninth of October in the middle of the afternoon the party of nine read him the entry that had come west and come back east on the same sheet, and the entry is nineteen lines long and it is in the party's own minute and it was written by four people and a clerk of nineteen years.

> **THE NAME OF ONE MAN IS AGAINST THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS UNTIL THE THIRTY-FIRST OF MARCH AND HE DID NOT TAKE IT AND HE IS NOT A BEARER AND HE IS NOT A WITNESS AND HE WAS NOT ASKED WHETHER HE WOULD STAND IN A CHANNEL. A YEAR AND A HALF IS ON A LINE OF ITS OWN AND IS NOT ADDED. TWO PEOPLE ARE IN A COLUMN AS NOT KNOWN AND NOT ASKED. THIS IS A SHEET AND NOT A LEDGER. WHEN THE SEASON STARTS TWO MORE GO ON IT.**

The room over the shop has been the room over the shop for ten weeks and it is the only room on that flat with two benches that face each other, and about nineteen people were in it and nine of them were standing, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that the nine who stood were the nine who worked in a frame that morning and had come out of it to be in the room, and that one of the nine was a woman of thirty-one who had sat down on the bank at about one o'clock on the first of October and was on her feet on the ninth.

The man of thirty-four who mends fencing had put nineteen lines on the board at the door before the reading was asked, and the board outside the room has the days on it in a hand that cannot be read, and the days are the twenty-eighth of September and the twenty-sixth of October and the twenty-fourth of November, and about four people had come in on the strength of the board and had not been told what they were coming to and had stayed.

He read it out in a room over a shop with nineteen people in it and did not say one word about it, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that he read it out and that he said nothing and that she had expected him to say something and had not been asked to expect anything.

The sheet came back east in an oilcloth and the oilcloth was wet on one corner and the corner was the corner with the two people in it who are not known and not asked, and nobody in that room asked which of the four people had carried it.

---

The reading was asked on the ninth of October at ten in the morning, in the five things, standing up, in front of about nineteen people, of whom nine were from households on this side and ten were from the loams, and the asker was the man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households and who had been told that a man from the west bank could read a thing and say what it was for.

He had also been told four other things, and the clerk entered all four, and the four are that the man is thirty-eight, that he can write the terms of a thing two people have agreed to, that he cannot decide anything, and that he has one thing left that he can spend and does not talk about.

"What is the job."

"**Read what a year off a person is, so that nine households can read it and not be lied to about it.**"

"How long is it."

"**About an hour, and I have said that figure out loud four times now and I have never once been in a channel, and there is a woman of thirty-one standing nine minutes' walk from this room and she is not in this room and that is the whole of what I know about the arithmetic.**"

"What does it cost."

A man of thirty-four who mends fencing said it, and he said it in the ordinary voice, and the reason he said it and not the man is that the man had not asked to have the answer read out for him and nobody reads a price for a man who has not asked.

"**A part of the toll. A year of a bed of brine, and a frame with a woman in it, and the frame is thirty-four miles from a table, and the year is not smaller for the walking.**"

---

He said no, and it took him four seconds, and the reason took half an hour, and the clerk of nineteen years read it back twice and corrected one word and the word was *spend* and she wanted it to be *use*.

"**I am not going to do it, and I am not going to give a reason about courage, and I have said that sentence seven times in ten weeks and I would like it struck out of my mouth and it cannot be. The cost of a reading is a part of whatever the lot's toll is, and I have got a figure of twenty-one years in a book with my name against it and I am not going to open a second book to find out what one of them is worth, and the twenty-one is not mine either, and I have written that in the entry and I meant it.**"

"And what is the good of reading it."

"**There is none. That is the whole of my refusal and it took four seconds, and I want it in those words: I am refusing because the price is not mine and the thing is not mine and the twenty-one years are not mine, and I have been the only person in this district who could spend the last of one thing for five months and I am not going to spend a piece of twenty-one years on a question I can ask with my mouth.**"

The man of fifty-six asked him whether a question asked with a mouth was going to be any use, and the man of thirty-eight said that it was going to be an answer in four days, and that the answer was going to be the four things a woman of forty-four wrote down and would not sign, and that he was going to ask it anyway, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that this was the first time in ten weeks that a refusal had been given with the answer attached and that the answer had not been given by the person refusing.

**A REFUSAL TO READ IS A READING AND IS ENTERED, AND IT IS NEITHER COWARDICE NOR DISCIPLINE, AND IT IS THE EIGHTH, AND THE COST OF IT WAS NOTHING AT ALL.**

---

And Ivo Stenn read out the ledger and then his own sheet on the ninth of October, in a cart, in the morning, in the ordinary voice, and the ledger is one for April, none for May, one for the twenty-ninth of June, one for the sixth of September and one for the ninth of October, and the number of departures left is one, and **there is still no date in the ledger for the next reading**, and the intervals have gone up on use.

"The intervals go up on use and I will not defend it and I will not call it anything else."

Then a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households asked him what the intervals were, and he read the ledger out again, and said that was the rule of the counter and not his own, and the man said he knew it was the rule of the counter and had wanted to hear it twice, and a clerk of nineteen years wrote that down in her own hand and entered that she had heard a number she had been reading for ten weeks and had never once been told what it was about.

A woman of about fifty-eight who keeps the ninth place was in the room and had not heard the sheet read out before, and she said that she had heard a man say nine words in a lane in the last week of July and had not known then what it was about either, and the minute says that Ivo Stenn said nothing at all when she said that and that not saying anything is not the same as not hearing and that the clerk entered both.

---

**AND ON THE NINTH OF OCTOBER A READER SPENT NOTHING AND WAS ASKED TO SPEND THE LAST OF ONE THING HE HAD, AND HE SAID NO IN FOUR SECONDS, AND THE REASON WAS THAT A FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS NOT HIS AND HE IS NOT GOING TO OPEN A SECOND BOOK TO FIND OUT WHAT ONE OF THEM IS WORTH, AND THE COST OF THE REFUSAL WAS NOTHING AT ALL, AND THE INTERVALS WENT UP ON USE FOR THE THIRD TIME IN TEN WEEKS AND HE WOULD NOT CALL IT ANYTHING ELSE.**

---

Then, at four in the afternoon of the ninth of October, a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households asked the man of thirty-eight whether he would still say yes to the name, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that the question was asked twice, once on the second of October and once today, and that a person asked twice is not a person asked once and not a person asked three times, and that the two answers were the same two words and that the two words are in the minute and that the minute does not say the two answers were the same.

He said yes, and then he said the other half, and the other half is the finding of the day and it is in the minute in his own words and it is the longest thing the man of thirty-eight has said in ten weeks.

"**I want it entered that a man of fifty-six has read me a rule that says a name put on a figure cannot be taken off it by the person it is put on, and that I have read it, and that it is the only rule anybody has ever read out to me that was about me, and I am saying yes to it in front of nineteen people, and the reason I am saying yes is that a figure with no name on it is a figure nobody can ask, and I would rather be a name on a figure than be a reason nobody asks.**"

A man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households was asked afterwards, by the clerk of nineteen years, whether he had expected the answer, and he said that he had, and that he had been going to be angry if the answer had been no.

He said that a man of fifty-six who does count for a living has been wrong about a bed of brine in the fourth month of this year and did not know it until the second of October, and that he was not going to be the second man in nine weeks to be wrong about a person in this district.

The clerk entered that, and entered that this was the first time in ten weeks that a person had said out loud that he had been wrong about a person and not about a figure.

---

And a woman of about thirty-eight who has been in the second channel four years asked the man of thirty-eight, in nine seconds, whether his name against the twenty-one meant that somebody would come and ask her the same six things again, and he said that he did not know and that the honest answer was that nobody had said.

She said that she had asked him because in the fourth year of this district she was asked a thing in a room and it was a job and she said yes and she has been in the frame since, and that the only thing anybody has ever asked her about since is what the water is doing, and that she would like it entered that a woman of thirty-eight asked a man with five powers a question about what a power is for and he did not know.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that and entered that it is the fourth time in ten weeks that a person has asked a man with powers what a power is for, and that the other three were all in rooms of nineteen people, and that the man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that the answer is always the same and gave it in nine words.

"**A power is a thing you can do to a document and not to a person, and the day somebody asks me to do one of mine to a person is the day I hand the five of them in and go and stand in a frame myself.**"

And the clerk entered the nine words and did not join them to the four things and did not join them to the nineteen lines and entered that she had wanted to and had not.

And the clerk of nineteen years then did the thing that ended the day, and she did it in nine seconds and the minute has it in her own words.

"**I am not entering the refusal to read in the same column as the name, because the refusal is a reading and the name is not a reading, and a reader who refuses and an address who says yes are two different people in one room and if anybody puts them in one line then the line is a sentence about a man and not about two things.**"

And on the tenth of October, at eleven in the morning, a woman of about forty-four who has done it eleven years was asked by a man of twenty-six who has been in a bed of brine for a year and a half whether the four things would be on the board at the door of the council, and she said no in four seconds, and then she said the other half, and the clerk entered that the two of them had been in the same room on the twenty-eighth of September and that this was the first time they had been asked about it by each other.

"**The four things get said in a room and a room has a door and a door is shut, and if the four things are on a board outside then a person who cannot come into a room can read what a person said in one, and that is a different thing and I have not agreed to it and I am not going to.**"

And the man of twenty-six said that he was in a bed of brine and that a bed of brine is not a room and that he had understood that in the fourth year and had not said so, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that this is the second time in ten weeks that a man of twenty-six has told somebody about himself that nobody asked him for, and that the first was on the seventh of September in a cart, and that neither of the two had been put in a column and that the clerk had thought about it and had not and that not doing a thing is also a thing she does.

**AND ON THE NINTH AND THE TENTH OF OCTOBER A FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS WAS ENTERED IN THE MARKET'S OWN BOOK AGAINST ONE NAME BY A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS WHO PUT THE ENTRY UNDER THE SENTENCE SHE WAS ENTITLED TO WRITE AND NOT ABOVE IT, AND THE FINDING IS THAT THE ONLY INSTRUMENT IN THIS DISTRICT THAT CANNOT BE TAKEN BACK IS A BOOK ON ELEVEN BOARDS AND A PERSON HAS TO COME AND STAND IN FRONT OF IT, AND THAT THE STRONGEST THING THIS DISTRICT OWNS AND THE ONE THING IT CANNOT UNDO ARE THE SAME OBJECT, AND THAT IT WORKS.**

---

Then on the morning of the tenth of October a man of forty-four who is a guild inspector and who came thirty-four miles on a letter from a licensed valuer of twelve years who would not travel, said the thing about the two certificates, and it is in the minute in his own words and it is the fourth thing he has said in this district and the last one of the four.

"**I have the two certificates in a drawer thirty-four miles from this room. I put the second one in the drawer myself on the seventeenth of September and I put it in the drawer on top of the first one, and both of them are valid, and neither of them can be struck, and a man came to this desk on the first of October and asked me whether a certificate can be got at and I said that it can be got at by anybody who comes to the desk, and he said that is not what he meant, and I said that I know that it is not what he meant and that it is what I have.**"

"And the consent sheet."

"**The sheet has four names and one of them is a mark and one of them was signed two days' walk away, and the form has no column for whether a person was in the room, and I have said all of that twice on the same form and both of my certificates are good, and I would rather have one bad piece of paper in the world than three good ones in a drawer, and that is in your minute from the seventeenth of September and I am not saying it again.**"

And a clerk of nineteen years entered that the two certificates are in a drawer and are both valid and that neither can be struck, and that the inspector had been asked a question and had given an answer that was not the answer and had said so himself, and that the drawer is thirty-four miles east and that the strongest instrument this district owns and a drawer thirty-four miles away are the same instrument pointing in opposite directions, and that a stranger took one of each cold on the same afternoon in September and did not know they were about the same thing.

---

Then on the tenth of October, in the evening, in a room over a shop, about nineteen people heard the four things read out for the second time, and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing read them and the woman of forty-four who wrote them did not read them and the minute says she was asked and said no in four words and that the words were the same four words as on the twenty-eighth of September.

And the minute for the tenth of October also carries the standing line about the echo of an origin toll, which is a loss of spatial sense and not a pain and not a forgetting, and a person with it cannot tell which way a river is going and has to be walked to a door by somebody who can. **It is the fourth time in ten weeks that the line has been copied and the second time in a minute, and nobody has added to it and nobody has joined it to the four things, and the clerk entered the fact that she had put it in the same minute as the four things and had not joined them.**

And on the tenth of October, in the middle of the afternoon, a woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel was asked whether the figure of twenty-one years was going to be in a book that her child could read, and she said that she could read and that the child of nine could not read yet, and that the child had stopped asking her what she had done the day before in the fourth month of this year and had asked somebody else, and that the two of those things had nothing to do with each other and she had not been able to say so in a room until the twenty-fourth of September.

She was asked whether she wanted the four things changed, and she said no in four seconds, and then she said the rest of it and the clerk of nineteen years wrote it down and read it back and got nothing wrong.

"**I wrote the fourth one on the twenty-fourth of September and I have had twelve days to think about changing it and I am not going to, and if a book comes with my name against it in it then my child will be told that the woman who took the years was somebody else, and that is the sentence I have been trying to say since the fourth month of this year and I have not got a better one.**"

Nobody in that room argued with her and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that the four things are read out and not answered and that a woman in the room had just answered one of them anyway, and that the rule said the four things are not answered and did not say that a person in the room could not say something, and that the clerk of nineteen years entered the difference and that the difference is about nine words long and is in the minute.

**AND ON THE TENTH OF OCTOBER A WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-THREE WHO IS THE FOURTH OF THE FOUR THINGS SAID IN A ROOM THAT A BOOK WITH HER YEAR IN IT WOULD TELL HER CHILD THAT SOMEBODY ELSE TOOK IT, AND THE RULE OF THE ROOM SAYS THE FOUR THINGS ARE NOT ANSWERED AND DOES NOT SAY A PERSON IN THE ROOM CANNOT SAY SOMETHING, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS NINE WORDS LONG.**

**AND THE FINDING OF THE TENTH OF OCTOBER IS THAT A COUNCIL OF FOUR THINGS WITH NO OFFICER AND NO CLERK MET TWICE IN TWELVE DAYS, AND THAT THE SECOND TIME WAS READ BY A DIFFERENT MAN, AND THAT A THING READ BY WHATEVER PERSON COMES IS NOT A DOCUMENT AND IS NOT A LEDGER AND IS NOT A WAY OF SAYING THAT A TOLL LANDS ON A PLACE, AND THAT A PERSON WHO WROTE FOUR THINGS AND WOULD NOT SIGN THEM HAS NOW SAID NO TWICE IN TWELVE DAYS AND BOTH NOS HAVE BEEN ENTERED UNDER HER OWN LINE AND NOT ABOVE IT, AND THAT TWO VALID CERTIFICATES ARE IN A DRAWER THIRTY-FOUR MILES AWAY AND AN INSPECTOR HAS SAID IN A ROOM THAT HE KNOWS THAT IS NOT THE ANSWER.**
