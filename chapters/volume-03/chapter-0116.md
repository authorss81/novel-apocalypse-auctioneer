# Chapter 116: How a Job Is Described

Nobody on the west bank read anything on the fifth, sixth or seventh of September, and the reason is not that there was nothing to read and it is not that a man would not, and the finding of the seventh of September is that **this is the first three days in nine weeks in which nothing in this district was read, and it happened thirty miles east of the district, and it happened because four people told a party of ten what a loom is in their own words and not one of the four of them could be asked a question about it.**

The party of ten was on the loams for three days and on the second of the three there were seven of the ninety pans with a frame over them and a bed of brine in them and a person in or beside six of the seven.

Halla Dree, who is thirty-eight and has been in the second channel four years, described her job on the fourth and the minute has it and the party had it written down in four places by the fifth morning, and nobody repeated it in front of her, and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said afterwards that not repeating it was the hardest thing he did in three days.

"A man who is not a clerk said to me on the fifth that we should ask her the same question again in a week so that we had it twice."

"Why."

"**Because a thing said once in this district in nine weeks has turned out to be a rule about nine times, and the only reason the sentence about a loom is not a rule is that a woman said it once and would say it differently tomorrow, and I have worked that out about four days too late to do anything about it and I am writing it down so that whoever reads this in four years knows the sentence was only held together by a person.**"

---

The second of them was a man of twenty-six who has been in a bed of brine for a year and a half and who introduced himself, which nobody on the west bank expected, and the introduction is in the minute because the clerk of nineteen years thought at the time that it was a form of words.

"**I am the loom. That is what a man out here says when he has been in one long enough. I am the loom and the loom is me, and if I am not in it in the morning the salt goes back into the water, and by the afternoon there is no loom and no salt and no me.**"

The man of thirty-eight asked him what happened to the words, and the man of twenty-six said he had not lost the words, and the clerk of nineteen years wrote both halves down in four minutes and the minute says that she asked him to slow down and he did not.

"I have the words. **I had a bed of brine and I had a frame and I had the wind and I had a woman at the second channel who is a better judge of it than I am. What I do not have is the year before. I can tell you what this bed did in the wet and I can tell you what it did in the dry and I can tell you about the ninth of April, and the ninth of April is the first thing I know and there is nothing in front of it.**"

"And is that a small thing."

"**It is the only thing about me that is a loss and I have never once been asked it by anybody, and I have been asked the water nine hundred times.**"

The third of them was a woman of forty-four who has done it eleven years, and she was the one who said the sentence that went into the party of ten's own minute and into four books on the west bank when they got back, and it is eleven words long.

"**Eleven. I know this channel the way I know my kitchen.**"

"And the sixth year."

"**There was no sixth year. There was a season and I was good at it and I was cold in it, and that is the whole of what is in the sixth year, and nothing in the sixth year that anybody misses, and I have looked.**"

Nobody in that bed said anything and the man of thirty-four who mends fencing said afterwards that the finding of the sixth of September is that **a person who has looked for the sixth year and not found it has not lost less than a person who has not looked, and this district has spent nine weeks arguing about what a toll is on the evidence of nineteen people and none of the nine had looked.**

The fourth of them was a man of fifty-two who came out of a channel in the fourth month of this year after four years and who came over to the party on the sixth because he had heard there was a man from the west bank who could read things.

He is the reason the party has anything at all to write down, and he is fifty-two and he is a hauler of loam and he has not been in a channel since the fourth month and he describes what is left of himself in four sentences.

"**I can tell you the height of the water on the second channel in any month of the year and I could not tell you what I had for supper on the eleventh of June of the second year, and it was not because I was hungry and it was not because I was hurt, and I went looking for the eleventh of June twice and it is not there and I have stopped going.**"

"And you are all right."

"**I am all right for the thing I do now. I am not all right for the thing I did, and I do not know how to say that in nine words, and this district has a great many nine words in it.**"

**AND ON THE SIXTH OF SEPTEMBER A PARTY OF TEN HAD FOUR DESCRIPTIONS OF A LOOM FROM FOUR PEOPLE WHO HAD NEVER MET EACH OTHER AND HAD NEVER MET ANYBODY FROM THE WEST BANK, AND NONE OF THE FOUR DESCRIBED A THING, AND ALL FOUR DESCRIBED A JOB, AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS ENTERED THAT THIS IS THE FIFTH TIME IN NINE WEEKS THAT SOMEBODY HAS DESCRIBED SOMETHING IN THEIR OWN WORDS AND IT HAS NOT BEEN JOINED TO ANYTHING, AND THAT A FIFTH IS STILL NOT A FINDING.**

---


---

And then on the sixth and the seventh he did the other thing, which is the thing he said he was going to do, and he asked four people four questions with his mouth in nine minutes each, and nobody on either bank thought of stopping him and a clerk of nineteen years wrote all four down.

He asked the woman of thirty-eight what the wind frame is made of and she said wood and did not say what wood, and he asked her whether a frame can be taken down and moved and she said a frame is a frame and it goes where the water is and the water is the only thing that decides it, and he wrote both of those down and the minute says that neither of them is a figure.

He asked the man of twenty-six what happens to the salt in the bed if the person comes out, and the man said it goes back into the water in nine days and that it has happened to two beds since the flood year and that the district on this bank has not written either of them down because the district on this bank has no book for it.

He asked the woman of forty-four what the eleventh year was like compared with the first year, and she said the first year was the hardest and that she was better at it in the fourth than the first and worse in the ninth than the fourth, and that the whole of the difference between the three of them is in her body and none of it is in her head, and that a person who has given up nine years can still be the best judge of a bed of brine in this basin.

He asked the man of fifty-two, who came out in the fourth month, the only question that the party had gone thirty miles to ask, and he asked it in nine words, and the man of fifty-two took nine seconds to answer and the answer is in the minute in his own words and it is the reason this district could not buy a loom and does not yet know that it cannot.

"**You cannot buy one, and I have been trying to sell one for two years and I have not told anybody I am trying, and I have not told anybody because I do not know what I would be selling.**"

The man of thirty-eight did not answer him and the man of fifty-two went on, and the minute has both of the halves.

"**It is not the frame. The frame is forty-one years old and it is oak and it will outlive me. It is not the bed. The bed is dug and it is not dug by me and I cannot sell a hole. And it is not the salt, because the salt goes into a cart and a cart goes to a market and I have done that nine times. What I cannot sell is the year, and the year is not mine, and the person who has it does not know they are holding it for me.**"

Nobody in that field said anything, and the man of thirty-four who mends fencing wrote down four words and underlined them, and the clerk of nineteen years did not know what they were and asked, and he told her, and she wrote them out.

**THE YEAR IS NOT THE SELLER'S, AND HE SAID IT ON THE SEVENTH OF SEPTEMBER, AND HE IS FIFTY-TWO AND HE HAS BEEN TRYING TO SELL A LOAM SINCE THE FOURTH MONTH OF THIS YEAR AND HAS TOLD NOBODY, AND A MAN WHO CANNOT SELL A THING HE IS STANDING IN IS NOT A MAN REFUSING A PRICE.**

"**THE YEAR IS NOT THE SELLER'S.**"

---

**AND ON THE SEVENTH OF SEPTEMBER A DISTRICT ON ONE BANK OF A RIVER ARITHMETICED ITSELF FOR THE FIRST TIME IN NINE WEEKS, AND THE ARITHMETIC WAS DONE OUT LOUD IN A CART BY A PERSON WHO IS NOT A CLERK, AND THE NUMBER IS IN THE PARTY'S MINUTE AND NOT IN ANY BOOK ON THE WEST BANK.**

The salt man was asked on the seventh, in four minutes, about his own two pans, and he answered in nine seconds and the answer is nine words long and it is not what anybody expected.

"**They are under four feet of river and they have been since the flood year and I have not tried to get them and I am not going to.**"

"You have four pans and the water came up."

"**I have ninety pans and four that a person is standing in. That is the whole of what I have and it took four years of being wrong about it and two years of walking eleven miles to say so in front of people.**"

And then a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households did the arithmetic that the party had not done, and he did it out loud on the seventh of September in a cart, and the clerk of nineteen years copied it and it is in the minute and it is the only arithmetic in nine weeks that anybody outside the ward market has ever written down.

"**Ninety pans. Sixty-one under water. Nine with water standing in them and no loam. Seven with a frame over them. Six with a person in or beside them. One with a frame and nobody in it, and that one has been making salt on its own since before any of you came, and the reason it is the one that matters is that it is the only one of the seven that is not costing anybody a year, and the only reason that is, is that there is nobody on it.**"

"That is a good pan."

"**That is not a good pan. That is a pan that will be the first of the seven to fail and nobody will know it has failed, because a pan with nobody on it cannot report itself, and this district has a place at the end of a line that cannot report itself and I have never met it and I have never been west of here, and I am telling you that I have read about it and that I do not believe it and I would like to see it.**"

And the minute says that a secretary of a cooperative on the far bank of a river that came up nine feet in the flood year said the words *cannot report itself* in the same week that a man who walks every yard of a railway line put his hand on a plinth thirty yards east of an engine house, and that the two of them had never met and had not been told about each other, and that a clerk of nineteen years entered the two in one line and did not join them and entered the fact that she had put them in one line and not joined them.

---

And on the evening of the seventh the party of ten sat on a bank and read out what they had, and it took an hour and a half, and the clerk of nineteen years read it out and nineteen people from the west bank read it out afterwards in four places, and the finding of the seventh of September is that **four people had described a loom in their own words and not one of them had said what it was for, and that the party of ten had gone thirty miles to find out and had come back with four descriptions of a job and a number of pans and a sentence about a year that is not a seller's.**

And the man of thirty-four who mends fencing said the last thing of the day and it is in the minute in his own words and it is nineteen words long, and it is the sentence the rest of this week turns on, and neither of the two men who has said it knows that the other one has said it.

"**A district that can price a thing and cannot name a person has just spent four days learning that the thing is the easy half, and four of us have been treating the other half as the problem since the eighth of March, and it is not the problem, it is the floor.**"

The reading was asked on the sixth of September at about nine in the morning, and it was asked in the five things, standing up, in front of nineteen people, of whom nine were from the west bank and ten were from households on this side, and the asker was the man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households and who had been told that a man on the west bank could read a thing and say what it was for.

He had also been told four other things, and the clerk of nineteen years entered all four, and the four are that the man is thirty-eight, that he can write the terms of a thing two people have agreed to, that he cannot decide anything, and that he has one thing left that he can spend and does not talk about.

"What is the job."

"**Read a loom and tell nine households and a district what it is for.**"

"How long is it."

"**About an hour, I am told, and I am told it by a man who was told it by a man who was told it, and neither of the two of them was in a channel.**"

"What does it cost."

The man of thirty-four who mends fencing said it, and he said it in the ordinary voice, and the reason he said it and not the man is that the man had not asked to have the answer read out for him and nobody reads a price for a man who has not asked.

"**A part of the toll. The cost of a reading is a part of whatever the lot's toll is, and the toll on this one is a year of a person's memory, and he would be taking a piece of twelve months out of a woman of thirty-one to find out what a loom is.**"

Nobody in that field said anything and a man of fifty-two who came out of a channel in the fourth month of this year said one word, and the minute says he said it once and did not say it again.

"**Yes.**"

"What happens to the person who does it."

"He said nothing at all for eleven seconds and then he said it, and it is in the minute in his own words and it is nineteen words long."

"**That I will not be able to say which way the river is going for an hour and will have to be walked to a cart, and that a man of thirty-nine did that for me in the rain on the thirtieth of last month and would not let go of my arm.**"

"Who is standing in the room."

He described the field and did it in nine sentences, and he was the second person in nine weeks to answer the fifth thing, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that and entered that the first had been a man of thirty-nine on the west bank who had said that a man who mends fencing was going to be the man who came back and write down what they all said and that he would like it entered as an answer and not a description.

And then a rule arrived in a field on the sixth of September in front of nineteen people, and nobody in that field had asked for it, and the registrar was not there and was told on the ninth.

> **A LOOM IS A THING AND A USE AND A BEARER AND A TOLL, AND ALL FOUR OF THOSE ARE TRUE OF IT, AND NOT ONE OF THE FOUR CAN BE FILLED IN WITH A PERSON WHO IS STANDING IN IT.**
>
> **YOU MAY NOT SPLIT A CHANNEL. YOU MAY NOT CLOSE A TRANSFER OF A YEAR. A YEAR IS NOT AN ASSET AND IT IS NOT A SECURITY AND NOBODY CAN POST ONE AGAINST IT.**
>
> **A PERSON BOUND TO A CHANNEL IS NOT A BEARER AND MAY NOT BE ENTERED AS ONE. A PERSON WHO HAS REFUSED MAY NOT BE COUNTED AS A WILLING WITNESS. A PERSON WHO WAS NOT ASKED WAS NOT ASKED AND MAY NOT BE COUNTED EITHER WAY.**
>
> **A CERTIFICATE THAT FOUR PEOPLE SIGNED AND ONE OF WHOM DID NOT ATTEND IS NOT FOUR CONSENTS. IT IS ONE CERTIFICATE AND ONE ABSENCE, AND THE ABSENCE IS NOT ON ANY FORM IN THIS BASIN, AND YOU WILL HAVE TO CARRY IT YOURSELF.**

Then he said no, and it took him about four seconds, and the reason took half an hour and it is in the minute in his own words and the clerk of nineteen years read it back twice.

"I am not going to do it, and I am not going to give a reason about courage, and I have said that sentence six times in nine weeks and I would like it struck out of my mouth and it cannot be. **The cost of a reading is a part of the lot's toll. I have known that since March. In March the toll was the origin and it took a direction off me and in April it took which gate I went out of my own city by and it has not come back, and I have one of those left and it is mine. And the toll on this one is a year out of a woman of thirty-one who has not been asked whether she will give it and who is standing in the frame while I stand here.**"

"And what is the good of reading it."

"**There is none. That is the whole of my refusal and it took four seconds, and I want it in those words, and I want the second half of it in the book too: I am refusing because the price is not mine and the thing is not mine and I have been the only person in this district who can spend the last of one for five months and I am not going to spend a piece of somebody else's on a question I can ask with my mouth.**"

The secretary of the cooperative of nine households asked him whether a question asked with a mouth was going to be any use, and the man of thirty-eight said that it was going to be an answer in four days and that the answer was going to be no, and that he was going to ask it anyway.

**A REFUSAL TO READ IS A READING AND IS ENTERED, AND IT IS NEITHER COWARDICE NOR DISCIPLINE, AND IT IS THE SEVENTH, AND THE COST OF IT WAS NOTHING AT ALL.**

**THE FINDING OF THE SIXTH OF SEPTEMBER IS THAT A READER WHO SPENT AN HOUR HERE WOULD HAVE SPENT A PIECE OF A PERSON HE HAS MET TWICE, AND THE REFUSAL WAS ENTERED BY A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS IN A CART ON THE SEVENTH AND READ OUT TWICE, AND THE LEDGER ON THE WEST BANK IS ONE FOR APRIL AND NONE FOR MAY AND ONE FOR THE TWENTY-NINTH OF JUNE AND THERE IS STILL NO DATE IN IT FOR THE NEXT ONE.**

And Ivo Stenn read out his own sheet on the seventh, in a cart, in the morning, in the ordinary voice, and it is the same nine words it has been after every one of the six and it is the seventh.

"The intervals go up on use and I will not defend it and I will not call it anything else."

And a woman of fifty-six who keeps the only pair on that line had not gone east and the sheet was read out in her absence and the minute says so and says that a sheet that is read out in a room where nobody is going to be in the room is a sheet and not a service.
