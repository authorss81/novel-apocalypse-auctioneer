# Chapter 148: The Rotation

---

The rotation is four people and the first sheet names them in a hand a clerk of nineteen years wrote out on the twelfth of December from a page a man of about thirty-four who digs loam and does nine days of it in ten had filled in, **and none of the four is Adrian, and the second line of the sheet says NOT A BEARER four times, and a man of fifty-six entered that the man the figure of twenty-one years is against was not asked to be one of the four and was not asked and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said on the twelfth of December that a rotation of four people with a term is the one thing this district has got right and that it was got right in the middle of the year by five other people, and that the four-bearer charter of the railway ran to the twenty-ninth of July and one of those weeks ran out with nobody in it, and that the two rotations are separate instruments and are not merged and are not to be merged.

Nobody argued and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the two rotations are in two books and were not on the same table, **and that one of them is a rotation of bearers and one of them is a rotation of people who stand in a frame, and that a person in the second is not a bearer and may not be entered as one, and that the two are not joined.**

A woman of thirty-eight who has been in the second channel four years said that the rotation turns when the season turns and not when a person wants it to, and that this is the whole of what a rotation is and that a person who has done it eleven times knows that the last day of a season is a day the person is standing in the thing, and that she was not asked about the eleven years and was not asked about the sixth year.

And on the twelfth of december **THE ROTATION OF FOUR PEOPLE WAS FILLED IN AND THE SECOND LINE OF THE FIRST SHEET SAYS NOT A BEARER FOUR TIMES, AND NONE OF THE FOUR IS THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST AND HE WAS NOT ASKED AND THE COUNT DID NOT MOVE.**

---

A man of fifty-six said on the twelfth of December that a charter with four things in it has no way to be argued with, and that this is a hole, and that the hole is not one of the five things this district does not have.

A woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel gave him the other half and it is twenty-six words, and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got twenty-six both times and did not say so and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got twenty-six and said the number out loud twice.

"**A thing you cannot argue with is not a hole in a charter. It is a thing the district has and not a thing it lacks.**"

Nobody argued with that and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the count of things this district does not have is five and did not move on the twelfth of December, **and that the count of instruments it has built and not named is six and did not move, and that the count of documents nobody owns is three and did not move, and that a charter four people can argue with is a fourth thing in a room and not in any of the three lists.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered all three counts and entered that she had wanted to make a fourth list and had not, and that a clerk who starts a fourth list in the last nine days of December is a clerk who is tidying up and tidying up is what she has been told not to do for eleven weeks.

And on the twelfth of december **A CHARTER WITH FOUR THINGS IN IT WAS FOUND TO HAVE NO WAY TO BE ARGUED WITH, AND THE FINDING IS THAT A THING YOU CANNOT ARGUE WITH IS A THING THE DISTRICT HAS AND NOT A THING IT LACKS, AND NONE OF THE THREE COUNTS MOVED.**

---

The four on the rotation were named in that room on the twelfth of December and the order was not the order of the six who hold the ledger, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the two orders are two instruments and that the six hold a page and the four stand in frames and that a person who holds a page and a person who stands in a frame are not the same job and that nobody has been asked to be in either.

The first of the four is the woman of thirty-eight who has been in the second channel four years. She was asked whether the rotation is a term and said it is a length of time, **and a man of fifty-six entered that she has said the same thing in December that she said about a notice in the first week of November, and that the two are not joined and that the word she uses is the same word twice and that the room has noticed.**

The second is a man of about thirty-eight who is at the second channel and has done three seasons. He was not asked anything and said one thing, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he said it in thirteen words and that it was thirteen words and that he was not asked what it meant and that a man of fifty-six said he was not going to ask and that the thirteen words are in the minute in his own words.

"**I will be in it. I am not in it for the salt.**"

Nobody argued with him and a man of fifty-six said that the finding of the whole of December is in thirteen words said by a man who was not asked, **and that the ninth household asked a different question on the fourteenth and that the two are not joined and that neither of them is a rule.**

The third is a man of about twenty-seven who came west in the first week of December and has never stood in a frame and was asked whether he would and said he would, and then said the second half, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he said yes and then said the half of it that made it true and that this is the third time in eleven weeks a person has done that and that he is not the second one and the first and the second are in October and November and are not joined to this one.

The fourth is the man of about thirty-four who digs loam and does nine days of it in ten, and he filled in his own name, and a man of fifty-six asked him why and he gave the reason in twenty-five words, **and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got twenty-five both times and did not say so and entered that he had not been asked to be on the rotation and had not offered and had written his name down and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move.**

"**I fill the page. If I stand in the channel I cannot fill the page and the page is the part somebody can ask about.**"

Nobody argued and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that this is the reason the district has a rotation at all, and that a district that has no rotation is a district where the person who keeps the book also stands in the thing, and that this district has been that for eleven weeks and that the twenty ruled and empty rows are what it looks like.

And on the twelfth of december **THE FOUR ON THE ROTATION WERE NAMED, AND THE SECOND SAID IN THIRTEEN WORDS THAT HE WILL BE IN IT AND IS NOT IN IT FOR THE SALT, AND THE FOURTH WROTE HIS OWN NAME DOWN BECAUSE HE FILLS THE PAGE, AND NEITHER OF THEM WAS ASKED FOR AN ANSWER AND BOTH WERE ENTERED.**

---

A man of about twenty-three who sells nothing and copies for nothing asked on the thirteenth of December whether a hand copy of a rotation is a rotation, and a man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper answered him in a sentence, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he was asked in the six things and answered and that he was not asked to copy anything and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move.

"**A hand copy of a rotation is a rotation. A hand copy of a page is a page, and a hand copy of a notice is a notice that somebody can hold.**"

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six said that the four sheets are now in four states, which is printed, on a board, and hand-copied twice, **and that this is the most beautiful instrument this district owns and that he said the word beautiful out loud and did not take it back and that a clerk of nineteen years entered that he said it and that she did not cross it out.**

A man of about thirty-eight said that the word beautiful is the first thing anybody has said about a document in this district that was not a complaint, and that a form that can be in four states at once is a form that cannot be lost and cannot be checked, and that those are the same thing and the same thing is not a virtue.

**AND ON THE THIRTEENTH OF DECEMBER A MAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-THREE WHO SELLS NOTHING AND COPIES FOR NOTHING ASKED WHETHER A HAND COPY OF A ROTATION IS A ROTATION AND WAS TOLD THAT A HAND COPY OF A NOTICE IS A NOTICE SOMEBODY CAN HOLD, AND A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX SAID THE WORD BEAUTIFUL OUT LOUD ABOUT THE FOUR SHEETS AND DID NOT TAKE IT BACK.**

---

The second of the four sheets in the charter is the ledger as it stands, six columns and a page ruled to thirty-one rows with eleven filled and twenty ruled and empty, **and a man of about thirty-four who digs loam said on the thirteenth of December that he is putting a page with twenty empty rows in it into a charter and that this is correct and that he is not going to rule the twenty rows out of it.**

A man of fifty-six asked him what happens to the twenty rows and he said a thing that is in the minute in his own words and it is thirty-nine words, and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got thirty-one both times and did not say so and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got thirty-one and said the number out loud twice.

"**A charter that says how many rows there are is a charter about a page. A charter that says there are rows and does not say how many is a charter about a page that has not been filled.**"

Nobody argued and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the second sheet of the charter does not carry the number thirty-one, **and that it carries the words A PAGE RULED TO THIRTY-ONE ROWS and that the words are the same as the number and are not the number, and that a stranger cannot check a page he has not stood in front of, and that this is the second time in eleven weeks a document has deliberately not carried a figure.**

**AND ON THE THIRTEENTH OF DECEMBER THE SECOND SHEET OF THE CHARTER NAMES THE LEDGER AND DOES NOT CARRY THE FIGURE THIRTY-ONE, AND THE MAN WHO FILLS THE PAGE SAID IN THIRTY-NINE WORDS THAT A CHARTER WHICH SAYS HOW MANY ROWS THERE ARE IS A CHARTER ABOUT A PAGE AND A CHARTER THAT SAYS THERE ARE ROWS AND DOES NOT SAY HOW MANY IS A CHARTER ABOUT A PAGE THAT HAS NOT BEEN FILLED.**

---

A woman of about twenty-six is at the fourth place and has been since the eleventh of December, and the fourth place has a toll board and a paperer once a month and a frame with no bracket in it and a platform thirty feet long, and it has not had a person in it for nine years and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the nine years is a figure and that the figure was not in any book before the eleventh of December and that she entered it out loud and not out of a book.

She was asked, in the five things, on the thirteenth of December, whether she would keep the board at the fourth place, and she said no in four seconds and gave the reason in a sentence, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she was asked and declined and that a person who declines a job is not a person who has refused a season and that the third kind still has no column and that the count of it did not move.**

The reason is that the platform is thirty feet long and it is the only place in this district with a roof a person can stand under, and that she has nowhere to be this winter, and that a man of fifty-six entered that she is at the fourth place because it is dry and not because anybody asked her to be, and that the entry is the first in this book that says a person is somewhere for a reason of their own.

The boy of nineteen at the fourth place is not her keeper and is nobody's keeper and is not paid, **and a man of fifty-six said in that room on the thirteenth of December that the fourth place now has a person in it and that the boy is not the person, and that a clerk of nineteen years entered the two in one line and did not join them and that the two are nineteen and about twenty-six and neither of them is a keeper.**

Nobody argued and the boy of seventeen was told about the woman and did not say anything and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road asked him why and he said it was a rule, and the man of nineteen entered that he had been told it was a rule on the third of November and on the fifteenth and on the twenty-second and now on the thirteenth of December, and that four is a count and that he had stopped asking after the third one and nobody told him to.

**AND ON THE THIRTEENTH OF DECEMBER A WOMAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-SIX IS AT THE FOURTH PLACE BECAUSE THE PLATFORM IS THIRTY FEET LONG AND IT IS DRY, AND SHE WAS ASKED WHETHER SHE WOULD KEEP THE BOARD AND SAID NO IN FOUR SECONDS, AND THE BOY OF NINETEEN AT THE FOURTH PLACE IS NOBODY'S KEEPER AND IS NOT HER KEEPER AND THE TWO ARE IN ONE LINE AND ARE NOT JOINED.**

---

The ninth household came into the yard of Lot 17 on the fourteenth of December at about ten in the morning on its own account and nobody asked it to, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody had asked it and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move.

It asked one question and it is the only question it has ever asked in a room, and it went into the minute in its own words and it is thirty-four words, **and a man of fifty-six counted them once and got thirty-four and a clerk of nineteen years did not count them and said so and he said count them anyway and she did and got thirty-four both times.**

"**We took the two thousand and we took the four hundred and nobody has told us what happens in March, and we are not asking for the salt. We are asking to be told.**"

Nobody answered her for about nine seconds and a woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel said the answer in a sentence, and a clerk of nineteen years entered the sentence and did not underline it and a man of fifty-six said that underlining is what a clerk does to a line she is not going to be asked about again.

"**You will be told in March. That is the first line of the terms sheet and it says a season starts on the first of April and not before.**"

Nobody argued and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the ninth household has now been told and is the only one of the nine that has been told, **and that the other eight were not told and were not asked and that the count of not-askings about the eight is eight and is a column of its own and is not the count about the woman of forty-five and did not move.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the figure of two thousand coppers and the figure of four hundred coppers and the difference of one thousand six hundred coppers are in one line on the fourteenth of December, and that the ninth household is the only one of the nine that has understood the finding about a private loom, and that it came on its own, and that nobody in this district had to bring it.

And on the fourteenth of december **THE NINTH HOUSEHOLD CAME INTO THE YARD OF LOT SEVENTEEN ON ITS OWN ACCOUNT AND ASKED TO BE TOLD WHAT HAPPENS IN MARCH, AND IT IS THE ONLY ONE OF THE NINE THAT HAS EVER ASKED ANYTHING IN A ROOM, AND THE OTHER EIGHT WERE NOT TOLD AND WERE NOT ASKED.**

---

A man of about thirty-seven who sells salt by the load out of a yard on the Verge wrote on a board on the fourteenth of December, in a hand that shook less than it did in November, that a load of salt that left a yard on the ninth of October came past a place on this flat and lost four days, and that the four days he wrote in November have been on the back of a board for four weeks and that nobody has written the four days in a book and that a man who loses four days wants them in a book and not on a board.

It worked and a man of fifty-six said that this is the third time in sixteen days that a thing written on a board has worked, **and that a man losing four days and a woman in a channel and a man whose salt is not on anybody's sheet are three different people and that the three are not joined and that the count of lines is six and did not move.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the seventh line at the ninth place is ruled and empty and was not written on, and that the count of uses of a board in sixteen days has been made five times and has given five numbers, and that a woman of fifty who is the water at the ninth place made the last one and she is not paid and was not asked to be right and is not going to be.

Nobody argued and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the instrument is still unnamed and unowned and that five boards, six lines full and a seventh ruled and empty is the count, **and that the count has not moved in sixteen days and that a person who writes on a board four times in sixteen days is not using an instrument and is using a person.**

**AND ON THE FOURTEENTH OF DECEMBER A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-SEVEN WHO SELLS SALT BY THE LOAD WROTE ON A BOARD THAT THE FOUR DAYS HE LOST IN NOVEMBER HAVE BEEN ON THE BACK OF A BOARD FOR FOUR WEEKS AND THAT NOBODY HAS PUT THEM IN A BOOK, AND IT WORKED, AND IT IS THE THIRD TIME IN SIXTEEN DAYS THAT A THING WRITTEN ON A BOARD HAS WORKED, AND THE COUNT OF LINES IS SIX AND DID NOT MOVE.**

---

The train on the siding had stood three hundred and six days on the fourteenth of December and a man of seventeen wrote the figure in an index in a hand that is not a clerk's hand, and the sum was put under the figure in a minute for the ninth time in four weeks and every figure is built from the eleventh of February and none is carried forward.

A man of about thirty-four who digs loam said again that a man who does a sum in front of strangers is not checking a number and is teaching it, **and that this district has been teaching numbers in lanes for eleven weeks and calling it counting, and that he is going to stop saying it in December and start again in January and that he has told nobody why and was not asked.**

Nobody asked him. A clerk of nineteen years entered that he had said he was going to start again in January and had not been asked why and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move, and that the count of conditions with no end on it is four and the count of protected things is five and he is in neither and neither moved.

AND THE CHARTER IS FOUR SHEETS **AND THE FIRST OF THEM IS A ROTATION OF FOUR PEOPLE WHO STAND IN FRAMES AND ARE NOT BEARERS, AND THE FOURTH IS A TERMS SHEET WITH A NAME ON IT AND A FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IN THE SAME LINE AS THE NAME, AND THE SECOND HALF OF THE WORD STEWARD IS A SEASON AND THE SEASON HAS NOT STARTED AND THE ENTRY SAYS THAT IS NOT A DELAY.**
