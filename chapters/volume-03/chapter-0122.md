# Chapter 122: Four Hundred and Two Thousand

---

Two men came over the raised plank road on the twenty-third of September and walked into the ward market counting room and sat down without being asked to sit down, and neither of them had been in that room before, and one of them was asked to state the job in the five things and one of them was not asked because he had come about his own business and had asked nobody's permission to be in the room.

He was a man of about forty-four and he had a channel and it was his, and he said the four things about it in nine minutes, and the clerk of nineteen years wrote all four down and the minute says she wrote all four down and that she did not enjoy it and entered that as her own opinion and not as a finding.

He had come in with mud to the knee on the second cart and he had not wiped his boots, and there were about forty people in the counting room at half past ten in the morning, and the counting room has one window and it is nine feet up, and nobody had asked for the window to be opened and the man of forty-four had asked for it and it was opened and then it was shut again after four minutes because the room was cold, and the minute enters that the window was open for four minutes and that he had asked for it and that the clerk had shut it and had entered why.

---

> **I HAVE HAD THE SECOND CUT SINCE THE FOURTH YEAR AND I HAVE WORKED IT MYSELF EVERY DAY SINCE THE TWELFTH OF AUGUST AND NOBODY IS IN IT BUT ME. I WILL PUT OUT A CART IN A DAY AND A HALF IF THE WIND HOLDS AND I WILL TAKE FOUR HUNDRED COPPERS FOR IT AND I WILL NOT WRITE ANYTHING ELSE DOWN.**

"Who buys it."

"**I do not care who buys it. That is the price and that is the whole of the paper. If you want my name in a book then I am not the man for your book and I will go home and there is a man on the far side of this water who will write his name in a book and charge you more and get it right.**"

Nobody in the room argued with him, and the clerk entered that the offer is four hundred coppers, that the toll is a year out of the man himself, that the man asked to be asked and was asked and answered, and that the four hundred is not a price for the salt and is a price for a year.

Then the other of the two men said the other thing, and he was the secretary of a cooperative of nine households and he had come four days over a road and he had his own offer and it was longer and it had a date in it.

---

> **THE COOPERATIVE OF NINE HOUSEHOLDS WILL RUN THE THIRD CHANNEL FOR THE SEASON AT TWO THOUSAND COPPERS, WHICH IS NOT A PRICE WE WORKED OUT BUT THE ONLY FIGURE ANYBODY ON EITHER BANK HAS FOR A LOOM. THE TOLL IS A YEAR OUT OF WHOEVER STANDS IN IT AND THE YEAR IS WRITTEN DOWN AGAINST A NAME IN A BOOK ANY PERSON CAN GO AND LOOK AT. FOUR PEOPLE HAVE ALREADY SIGNED A SHEET FOR TWO DAYS AND THE FOUR HAVE TO BE ASKED AGAIN IN SIX THINGS BEFORE A SEASON, AND SIX THINGS TAKE A WEEK, AND THE SEASON STARTS ON THE FIRST OF OCTOBER.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said the arithmetic out loud in nine seconds, and it is in the minute in his own words, and the two thousand is not amortized to the two hundred, and the clerk entered that nobody in the room had asked for it to be.

"**Two thousand against four hundred is one thousand six hundred, and the one thousand six hundred is the price of the six things, and the six things are not a price anybody can charge because they are a week and the week is not here.**"

---

The nine carts were named in the room over a shop on the afternoon of the twenty-fourth, and the clerk of nineteen years read the list out twice and forty people were at the counter, and **the list is nine carts of meal and seed against a quantity of salt that six frames and a season can fill, and the clerk entered that the figure and the figure are both in the minute and that neither of them is a column.**

Nine households is a count. Nine carts is a count. A household is a count and a person is a count and the two are not added anywhere in this district's books, and the clerk of nineteen years said that out loud in four seconds when a man of about thirty-one asked her which household had two carts in it, and the man of thirty-one said he had not meant anything by it and the clerk said she knew he had not and entered that she had said it anyway.

The choice was put to the room and it took four minutes to be framed and eleven minutes to be argued, and the argument is in the minute in four voices and all four of them are people who are not the man of thirty-eight.

A woman of about forty-four who keeps a Road House on the tide and who lends her yard once a month and is not thanked said the first of the four voices, and she is not the woman of forty-four who has done it eleven years, and the clerk entered the two in one line and did not join them.

"**I will take the four hundred and I will say why in nine words, and if you write that down you must write that I said it, and I have said it twice in my life and both times to men who were not asking me for it.**"

"**Say the nine words.**"

"**I have eleven pounds of salt in a cellar and I am going to feed a yard of about nine people and the six things cost a week and the week is the twelfth of October and by the twelfth of October I have no meal.**"

Then a haulier of about forty who had said the whole of the risk in four minutes in August and had been right said the second voice, and it took him nineteen words and he did not stop in the middle of it.

"**You are not choosing between two looms. You are choosing between a thing a man will do on his own and a thing four people have to be asked about, and the second one costs more and gives you the year.**"

The third voice was a woman of fifty-eight who carries water and does not read figures, and she said eleven words and the clerk entered them and did not correct them.

"**Give me the two thousand and I will write your name where I can find it.**"

And then a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing spoke fourth, and he was not a member of the cooperative and had not been asked for an opinion, and the clerk entered that he spoke fourth and that he was the only one of the four who was not on the list, and the minute says that he said so himself before he spoke.

"**The private loom is not cheaper. The private loom is the same salt out of a different man, and the only thing you are buying with the four hundred is a year that is not in a book, and that is a thing this district has never once been able to refuse and I am not going to be the reason it learns to.**"

---

Nobody argued with the man of thirty-four and the room took the two thousand in four minutes, and **the minute says that the vote was not taken by a show of hands and that nine households were asked in turn and all nine said yes, and that the ninth added that it would rather have the four hundred and was not argued with, and that a household is a count and a person is a count and the two are not added anywhere in this district's books.**

The woman of fifty-eight who carries water was asked afterwards whether she had been a household, and she said no in four seconds, and said that she carries water to about ninety houses and that a house is not a household and that she has never once been asked which of the two she is, and the clerk entered that and entered that the question had been asked badly and that the woman had answered it anyway, which is the third time in ten weeks a person has done that.

And then on the twenty-fifth of September, one day after that, four of the nine households hired the private channel anyway, and the clerk of nineteen years entered it as a line of its own and did not put it under the two thousand.

The line is nineteen words long and the man of about forty-four wrote it himself and gave it to the clerk and she read it back and got nothing wrong.

> **THE FOUR HUNDRED IS PAID AND THE CART GOES IN A DAY AND A HALF IF THE WIND HOLDS AND THE YEAR IS MINE AND I AM NOT ASKING ANYBODY TO WRITE IT DOWN AND NOBODY HAS ASKED ME AND I AM NOT COMPLAINING.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-FIFTH OF SEPTEMBER A DISTRICT OF FOUR THOUSAND AND ONE HOUSEHOLDS PAID A THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED COPPERS MORE FOR THE SAME SALT ON THE TWENTY-FOURTH AND HIRED THE OTHER ONE ON THE TWENTY-FIFTH, AND BOTH ARE RUNNING, AND THE FINDING IS THAT THE PRIVATE LOOM IS NOT CHEAPER, IT IS THE SAME SALT OUT OF A DIFFERENT MAN, AND THE PRICE OF THE PUBLIC ONE IS NOT ONE THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED COPPERS, IT IS FOUR PEOPLE BEING ASKED IN SIX THINGS, AND THIS DISTRICT HAS JUST PAID FOR THE ASKING AND WILL HAVE TO PAY IT AGAIN EVERY SEASON.**

---

Then the man of thirty-eight asked the man of about forty-four a question about the wind, and it was the only technical question anyone asked in that room all day, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that it was the only one and that the man of thirty-eight had come to a counting room to ask a man who works with water a question about water.

"**How long does a cart wait.**"

"**A day and a half is what I said and it is what I will hold it to, and the truth is that on this flat a cart waits three days one time in four and I said a day and a half because I have said it every year for eleven years and I have never once been wrong in a way I could not get out of.**"

"**Then you have said a thing you cannot hold to.**"

"**I have said a thing I can hold to in the direction I care about, and I care about the four hundred arriving and not about the salt, and I know which of those two is the one I am selling and I have known for two years and I have not told anybody, and I am telling it to four people in a room and not to nine because you did not ask me in front of nine.**"

And a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households was asked whether that was the reason the man had said a day and a half, and the man of about forty-four said yes in four seconds, and the clerk entered that the man of fifty-six had asked a question he already knew the answer to and that he had asked it because the room needed it said out loud and not because he did not know.

The man of about forty-four stayed in the counting room for the rest of the afternoon and did not leave and did not ask anybody anything, and about four people offered him a chair and he took one of them and gave the other three to a woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel and who had come in out of the rain and had not been to the room for anything.

A man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households asked him, in nine minutes and in front of about nineteen people, whether he had ever worked a channel for anybody else, and he said yes in four seconds and then said the rest of it, and it is in the minute in his own words.

"**Once. Eleven years ago, for nine households, and they paid me in meal and I was twenty-nine, and I did not know then that a year of it was a year and I know now, and the difference between then and now is that then nobody thought of the year as a thing and now there are nineteen people in this room who have written it down, and I would like it entered that I have gone from not thinking about it to not being able to stop it in about eleven years and that is the whole of what has happened to me.**"

"And if they pay you four hundred again in a year."

"**Then I will do it again and I will not have written a thing down, and the year will be mine, and I will be the same man and about two years shorter.**"

Nobody in that room said anything and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing wrote down four words and underlined them and the clerk did not know what they were and asked and he told her and she wrote them out, and the four words are in the minute and are not a finding.

**SHORTER BY TWO.**

---

Then the man of thirty-eight did the thing he was not asked to do, and it is in the minute in his own words, and the clerk of nineteen years entered the length of it beside it and got fifty-one and then got it again, and it was nineteen seconds after the two thousand had been agreed and before anybody had left the room.

"**I want to say the number I did in eleven seconds and I want it said in this room and not in a book. I set a price on a loom on the eighth of September off the price of salt at nine coppers a pound, and I did it in eleven seconds, and the clerk of nineteen years has entered that a man of fifty-six told me I had written a sale with no worker in it, and he was right, and the entry of that is under the terms and not above them, and I have not taken it out.**"

"And now."

"**And now nine households have put two thousand coppers behind the same figure, and the two thousand is still a number I did in eleven seconds off the price of a pound of salt, and I would like somebody in this room who is not me to say what it ought to be, and I have thought about it for four days and I do not know.**"

And a haulier of about forty answered that in nine words, and the minute says he was the second of the four voices and that he had been right about the water in August and that the clerk entered the two in one line and did not join them.

"**It ought to be four answers and a week, and you have got four answers, and the week is what you have not got.**"

---

**AND THE FINDING OF THE TWENTY-THIRD OF SEPTEMBER IS THAT A MAN WHO HAS WORKED A CHANNEL FOR ELEVEN YEARS CAME FOUR DAYS OVER A ROAD TO A COUNTING ROOM, ASKED FOR A WINDOW, GOT IT FOR FOUR MINUTES, AND TOLD FOUR PEOPLE IN ONE ROOM THAT HE HAS SAID A THING HE CANNOT HOLD TO IN THE DIRECTION HE CARES ABOUT, AND THAT THE DIRECTION HE CARES ABOUT IS THE FOUR HUNDRED ARRIVING AND NOT THE SALT, AND THAT HE SAID IT IN FRONT OF FOUR PEOPLE AND NOT NINE BECAUSE HE WAS NOT ASKED IN FRONT OF NINE, AND THAT A DISTRICT WHICH BUYS ITS SALT AT NINE COPPERS A POUND HAS SPENT TEN WEEKS FINDING OUT WHO SIGNS FOR A TON AND HAS NOT ONCE ASKED THE MAN WHOSE YEAR SIGNS FOR IT.**

**AND THE FINDING OF THE TWENTY-FOURTH AND THE TWENTY-FIFTH OF SEPTEMBER IS THAT A PRICE CAN BE TOO LOW AND STILL BE A PRICE, AND THAT A NUMBER A MAN DID IN ELEVEN SECONDS OFF THE PRICE OF SALT STAYS A NUMBER A MAN DID IN ELEVEN SECONDS OFF THE PRICE OF SALT WHENEVER NINE HOUSEHOLDS PUT IT BEHIND IT, AND THAT THE ONLY THING ANYBODY IN THE ROOM COULD STATE ABOUT WHAT A LOOM SHOULD COST WAS FOUR ANSWERS AND A WEEK, AND THAT A MAN OF ABOUT FORTY-FOUR WHO HAS WORKED A CHANNEL FOR ELEVEN YEARS SAID HE HAS GONE FROM NOT THINKING ABOUT THE YEAR TO NOT BEING ABLE TO STOP IT IN ABOUT ELEVEN YEARS, AND THAT HE IS GOING TO BE ABOUT TWO YEARS SHORTER AND NOBODY IN THE ROOM WROTE THAT DOWN BUT A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR WHO MENDS FENCING DID.**

---

And then a woman of fifty-eight who carries water and does not read figures did a thing nobody had asked her to do, and it took her nine minutes, and it is the only arithmetic in ten weeks that a person who cannot read figures has done in a room and had read out by somebody else.

She counted the nine households on her fingers and then on the edge of the counter, and she got a number, and she would not say it, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said it back to her from his own count, and the two numbers were the same.

"**Say it.**"

"**Nine. And I have been carrying water to about ninety houses for about eleven years and there are nine households in this room and I want somebody to write down that those are two different numbers and that both of them are true and that I have never once been asked which of them I am.**"

A man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households said that she had just answered the salt man's question of the eighteenth of August, and she said no in four seconds.

The clerk of nineteen years entered that the question on the eighteenth of August was *who do I write this on, when it is a place*, and that the woman of fifty-eight who carries water had said that the answer is that you write it on a person and a person is not a household.

She entered that the registrar had been told this and had entered it as a question with a heading and nothing under the heading, and that the two women had not been put in one line by anybody, and that the clerk had put them in one line and had not joined them, which is the fifth time she has done that in ten weeks.

On the evening of the twenty-fifth a man of thirty-four who mends fencing and the man of thirty-eight walked from the counting room to the ninth place and back, which is about two hundred yards each way, and they said almost nothing for the first hundred of them, and then the man of thirty-four asked what a week is, and the man of thirty-eight said that it is seven days, and the man of thirty-four said that he knew that and that he had meant the other one.

The other one is the delay between a question being asked in six things and a person being answered in six things, and the man of thirty-four said that in ten weeks he has watched four people be asked in five things and three of the four had said yes in under a minute, and that the three who said yes fast were saying yes to a job and that the fourth had been asked about a season and had said no, and that the difference between the two is a week and that nobody in this district can put a week on anything.

The man of thirty-eight said that he had known about the week since the fifteenth of September and had said it in a room over a shop on the far bank and had been told by a woman of thirty-one that she would rather be a bed with a man's name on it than a thing that was bought in a week, and the man of thirty-four said that he had been in that room and had heard it and had written it down, and that he had been carrying it for ten days and had not known what he was carrying it for until the twenty-fifth.

---

The last thing of the twenty-fifth was said by the man of about forty-four, who had not been asked anything on the twenty-fifth and had come back into the room to fetch a coat he had left on the back of a chair at half past ten on the twenty-third.

He was asked, by a man of thirty-four who mends fencing, whether he was going to be in a book at some point, and he said no in four seconds, and then he said the other half, and the minute has it in his own words and it is thirty-nine words long.

"**I am going to be in a book. I am going to be in one of yours, the day one of the four of you says out loud that a year came out of me, and not before, and you will not know it happened and neither will I.**"

Nobody in that room said anything, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that the man had come back for a coat that had been on the back of a chair for two days, and that the clerk had not thought to ask anybody to look after it, and that this is the first thing this district has lost in ten weeks and that it is a coat.
