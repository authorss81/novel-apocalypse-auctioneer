# Chapter 147: Nineteen Rows and Four Columns

---

The agent of a guarantee house came west on the ninth of December and was in a room for three days and Adrian was thirty-four miles east on the ninth and the tenth and the eleventh and did not see her, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that this is the second time in eleven weeks he has been thirty-four miles away for the whole of a visit and that the first was in October and that nobody has asked him about either and that not asked is in the count.**

She is an agent of a house that guarantees things and her name was said in that room on the ninth of December in her own words and a clerk of nineteen years wrote it on a slip, and the slip was not copied into the book and the slip was not kept and the minute of the ninth of December says that a name was said and does not have the name in it, and this is the first minute in this book that has been entered on purpose.

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six said that a minute with a thing left out of it on purpose is a document nobody owns, **and that the count of documents nobody owns is three and did not move and that a name said out loud in a room is not a document and is not one of the three and is not one of the five and is not one of the six.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a name not in a book is a name a person can be held to in about four years and not before, and that the agent of a guarantee house said the same thing about her own name in October and that the two are not joined and that the count of times a person in this district has said that a name in a room is a thing they can be held to is two and both are her and one of them is from a woman who deals in second-hand paper on a lane off the lower terrace.

And on the ninth of december **A NAME WAS SAID IN A ROOM AND WAS NOT ENTERED IN ANY REGISTER IN THE WEST BANK, AND THE MINUTE OF THE NINTH OF DECEMBER SAYS THAT A NAME WAS SAID AND DOES NOT HAVE THE NAME IN IT, AND THIS IS THE FIRST MINUTE IN THIS BOOK THAT HAS BEEN ENTERED ON PURPOSE.**

---

The ledger of the guarantee house is nineteen rows and four columns and it was on the table in that room for three days, and a man of fifty-six read it and a clerk of nineteen years read it and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing read it and none of the three wrote anything down on the first day.

Three of the nineteen rows have a name in the bearer column. Sixteen do not. A man of fifty-six said the three and the sixteen out loud and a clerk of nineteen years entered both and entered that they are not added and that a name in a bearer column and a space in a bearer column are two different things and that the sixteen is not a count of missing people and is a count of rows.

Nobody argued and the agent said what the sixteen are, and it is in the minute in her own words and it is thirty-four words, **and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got thirty-four and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got thirty-four both times and did not say so and said afterwards that she had wanted to say so.**

"**The sixteen are the rows my house took off a woman in a city I will not name, in one afternoon, in one sitting, from a book she did not know was a book.**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a book a person does not know is a book, and that this district has nine copies of one page in nine hands and does not know which is the first and that the two are not joined and that he is not going to join them in a room with her in it.

A man of fifty-six asked her the question that was asked in October, in the same words, and she answered it in the same words, **and this time a man of about nineteen wrote the answer down in the room as she said it, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody wrote either of the two answers down in October and that one of them was written down in December and that the two are not the same event and are not joined.**

The answer was that it is not a courtesy to withhold the name, and that a name in a room becomes a thing a person can be held to, and that she has held one.

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six said that a man who has held one and will not say where is not the same as an office that is in five places and no register, and that the two are not joined, and that the name of the city is not to be written on any document in this district by anybody, and that the ninth place is what the documents say and that a clerk of nineteen years entered both of those as a rule of this district and not as a finding.

And on the ninth of december **A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX ASKED HER THE SAME QUESTION HE ASKED IN OCTOBER AND SHE GAVE THE SAME ANSWER, AND THIS TIME A MAN OF ABOUT NINETEEN WROTE IT DOWN IN THE ROOM AS SHE SAID IT, AND THE CITY IS NOT TO BE WRITTEN ON ANY DOCUMENT IN THIS DISTRICT BY ANYBODY.**

---

A man of fifty-six asked her, on the tenth of December, in six things, whether the sixteen were asked, and she said no, and the room went quiet for about nine seconds and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the answer was no and that nobody said anything for nine seconds afterwards.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said the other half and it is in the minute in his own words and it is thirty-eight words, and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got thirty-eight both times and did not say so and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got thirty-eight and said the number out loud twice.

"**A row with no name in the bearer column is not a row about somebody who refused and it is not a row about somebody who was asked. It is a third thing and it has no word.**"

Nobody argued and a clerk of nineteen years said that her own page has a word for it, **and that the word is NOT ASKED and that it is in the sixth column of a page ruled to thirty-one rows in a yard of Lot 17 and that the ledger of a guarantee house has four columns and none of them is that one, and that the two are not joined.**

A man of fifty-six asked whether that was a fault in the guarantee house's ledger and the agent said no, and said why, and it is in the minute in her own words and it is twenty-eight words and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got twenty-eight both times and entered that she did not say so.

"**A book that had a column for it would have been used against the person in it. Mine has four and one of them is empty on purpose.**"

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six said that a column left empty on purpose is a column a person can be held to in about four years, **and that this district's sixth column is filled in by six people and a clerk and that the two are not joined and that a room of about nineteen people found a third thing on the tenth of December and that the third thing has no word in any book anybody in this room has read.**

**AND ON THE TENTH OF DECEMBER A ROW OF THAT LEDGER WITH NO NAME IN THE BEARER COLUMN WAS SAID TO BE A THIRD THING AND NOT A REFUSAL AND NOT AN ASKING, AND THE ROOM FOUND A THIRD THING IT HAD NO WORD FOR, AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS SAID THAT HER OWN PAGE HAS THE WORD AND THAT HER OWN PAGE IS ON A BOARD OF ELEVEN IN A YARD AND THE TWO ARE NOT JOINED.**

---

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against came back on the twelfth of December and read what the room did, and it is a copy in his own hand of four pages and he was given it and not asked for it and did not ask for it.

He read the two figures, which are the twenty-one years in this district and the four hundred years in hers, and he said the thing that is in the minute in his own words and it is forty-eight words, and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got forty-eight both times and did not say so and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got forty-eight and said the number out loud twice.

"**I am not going to put my twenty-one next to her four hundred. Mine is one figure against one name in a book in this district and hers is a figure against nobody and the two are not a rate and not a total and not a comparison.**"

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six said that this is the first time in eleven weeks a man has refused a figure he could have used, **and that a man who is the address for twenty-one years would have been stronger with four hundred years under his name and has declined it and that the reason he gave is that his is a floor and hers is not a floor and that two floors are not a sum.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the figure of twenty-one years is a floor and was entered as a floor in October and did not move, and that the four hundred years is not a floor and is not in a book in this district and is not to be copied into one, and that the two figures are in two rooms and were not on the same table and are not joined.

**AND ON THE TWELFTH OF DECEMBER THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST READ WHAT THE ROOM DID AND REFUSED IN FORTY-EIGHT WORDS TO PUT HIS FIGURE NEXT TO HERS, AND A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX SAID THAT TWO FLOORS ARE NOT A SUM, AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS ENTERED THAT THE FOUR HUNDRED YEARS IS NOT IN A BOOK IN THIS DISTRICT AND IS NOT TO BE COPIED INTO ONE.**

---

A man of about nineteen who walked in from the road was asked on the eleventh of December to read back the three wrong words he had written down, and he read them back, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he had been asked in the six things and had read three words back and that the three words are in the minute in her spelling and not in his and that a man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper said that a misspelt word in a minute is a word somebody will correct in four years.**

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six said that is the finding and that it is seventeen words, and a clerk of nineteen years counted them once and got seventeen and a man of about nineteen who wrote the three words down said count it again and she said no and entered that she said no and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move.

The seventeen words are that a word written down wrongly is a word that can be argued with in about four years, and that a word said out loud wrongly is a word that stays a word, **and that this district has said the same three things out loud for eleven weeks and has not written one of them down, and that the two are not joined and that the eleventh of December is the first day this district has written a wrong thing down on purpose.**

And on the eleventh of december A MAN OF ABOUT NINETEEN READ BACK THE THREE WRONG WORDS HE HAD WRITTEN DOWN AND A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX SAID IN SEVENTEEN WORDS THAT A WORD WRITTEN DOWN WRONGLY IS A WORD THAT CAN BE ARGUED WITH IN ABOUT FOUR YEARS, AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS SAID NO WHEN SHE WAS ASKED TO COUNT IT AGAIN AND ENTERED THAT SHE SAID NO.

---

The fourth column of that ledger is a year, and a man of fifty-six found it on the second day and said what it was, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he said what it was and that she did not correct him.

The column has no heading. It is a column of figures and it is in the fourth place and the fourth place is where a year goes in a document about a thing with a bearer, **and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a document with a year in it and no heading over the year is a document that has been in a room where somebody knew what the year was, and that the year in it is a year of a worker's memory and not a price and not a term and not a date.**

Nobody argued with that. The agent said the same thing in her own words and it is in the minute and it is twenty-five words, and a clerk of nineteen years counted them once and got twenty-five and a man of about nineteen said count it again and she counted it again and got twenty-five both times.

"**The same. It is taken and it is not asked and the body makes it work, and we have had four hundred years of it.**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds and a man of fifty-six said that the figure of four hundred years is the first figure anybody from outside this basin has put on a year, **and that there is no rate anywhere in this district that turns four hundred years into coppers and there never has been, and that the twenty-one years in a book in this district and the four hundred years in a book in hers are not added and that the two books are not in the same room and were not on the same table.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered the two figures in one line on purpose, and entered that a hundred and eighty-three coppers is a page in the ward market's own book with a heading and no name against it and was not read out on the ninth of December and nobody refused, and that the three figures are in three books and that she has entered them in one line and that this is the fourth time.

And on the tenth of december **THE FOURTH COLUMN OF THE LEDGER OF A GUARANTEE HOUSE WAS FOUND TO BE A YEAR WITH NO HEADING OVER IT, AND THE AGENT SAID THAT IT IS THE SAME AND THAT IT IS TAKEN AND NOT ASKED AND THAT THE BODY MAKES IT WORK, AND THAT HER HOUSE HAS HAD FOUR HUNDRED YEARS OF IT, AND NOBODY SAID ANYTHING FOR ABOUT NINE SECONDS.**

---

She has no word for what a loom takes and she uses three wrong words for it and has known that for eleven years, and on the eleventh of December she said all three out loud in a room and about nine people heard them and a man of about nineteen wrote all three down.

Nobody corrected her. A man of fifty-six asked whether the room was going to correct her and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said no in four seconds, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a woman from outside this basin used three wrong words for the thing this district spent four batches naming and that nobody in the room corrected her and that nobody was asked to and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move.

A man of fifty-six said that a word said out loud in a room cannot be counted and a word written down can, **and that in October two of the three were said out loud and nobody wrote either of them down, and that the day somebody writes a wrong word down is the day a district starts being able to disagree with a person about a word, and that this is not the same as the district knowing what the word is and it is not joined.**

Nobody argued and the agent said that she was not asking the district to adopt three words and that she has three because she has eleven years, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that eleven years is a length of time and not a figure and that the district's own arithmetic of years is a figure and that the two are not joined and were not joined to each other on the table.

**AND ON THE ELEVENTH OF DECEMBER THE AGENT OF A GUARANTEE HOUSE SAID THREE WRONG WORDS OUT LOUD FOR THE THING A LOOM TAKES AND A MAN OF ABOUT NINETEEN WROTE ALL THREE DOWN, AND NOBODY IN THE ROOM CORRECTED HER, AND A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX SAID THAT THE DAY SOMEBODY WRITES A WRONG WORD DOWN IS THE DAY A DISTRICT STARTS BEING ABLE TO DISAGREE WITH A PERSON ABOUT A WORD.**

---

The offer was put on the table again on the eleventh of December and it is four lines and it is the same four lines, and the first refusal on the person who stands in the frame is still the first line and is still named by her house before the room does.

Nothing was accepted and nothing was refused. A man of fifty-six asked the room whether it was going to decide and a woman of forty-four who has done it eleven years said that it is not their decision and gave the reason in a sentence, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she was entered before she spoke as not speaking for the room and that this is the third time she has been entered that way and that the count of it is three and is not a count of not-askings.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the four lines are the same four lines as in October and that the fourth line was put back on the table unasked then and has been put back on the table unasked again, and that a man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper said that a fourth line nobody asked for is a line the room has not looked at and that two rooms have now not looked at it twice.

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six said that the guarantee house is not an office and is not a guild and does not appear in any of the five places, **and that the answer to what a public office is for is not on the table in front of anybody and that the room was not going to be asked about it in order to get it, and that the refusals to join two things stand at thirteen and the registrar's own count stands at twelve and that neither moved on the eleventh of December.**

Selik Marne was in the yard on the tenth of December while the agent was in a room and they did not speak, and a man of fifty-six told her about him and she did not ask about him, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the agent of a guarantee house did not meet him and did not ask about him and that the span at the hut he works at is still shut and that the seventeen people on the far bank are still on the far bank and that the two are in one line and are not joined.

AND THE SAME MEMORY TOLLS ARE IN THAT LEDGER AND A YEAR IS IN THE FOURTH COLUMN WITH NO HEADING OVER IT AND FOUR HUNDRED YEARS OF IT ARE IN A HOUSE THAT GUARANTEES THINGS, AND THE CITY IS NOT NAMED AND IS NOT TO BE NAMED, AND THE AGENT IS NOT ENTERED IN ANY REGISTER IN THE WEST BANK, AND A MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST WAS THIRTY-FOUR MILES EAST FOR ALL THREE DAYS AND HAS NOT MET HER AND IS NOT TO BE WRITTEN AS HAVING MET HER.

---

The three days ended on the eleventh of December and the agent went east on the twelfth, and a man of fifty-six entered the whole of the three days in nine lines, **and a clerk of nineteen years read the nine lines back and got nothing wrong and the nine lines have a date on them and a place and a person and no figure in them at all.**

What is in the nine lines is this. A ledger of nineteen rows and four columns was on a table for three days. Three of the rows have a name in the bearer column and sixteen do not. The fourth column is a year and has no heading over it. The same toll is taken and is not asked and the body makes it work. A house that guarantees things has had four hundred years of it. Sixteen rows came out of a book a woman did not know was a book, in one afternoon, from a city that is not named. Three wrong words were said for the thing and were written down. A name was said and not entered. Nothing was accepted and nothing was refused.

**AND THE NINE LINES OF THE ELEVENTH OF DECEMBER HAVE A DATE ON THEM AND A PLACE AND A PERSON AND NO FIGURE IN THEM AT ALL, AND THE CITY IS NOT NAMED, AND THE NAME IS NOT ENTERED, AND THE FOUR HUNDRED YEARS IS IN THEM AS A FIGURE AND IS NOT ENTERED IN ANY BOOK IN THIS DISTRICT, AND THE COUNT OF REFUSALS TO JOIN TWO THINGS IS THIRTEEN AND THE REGISTRAR'S OWN COUNT IS TWELVE.**
