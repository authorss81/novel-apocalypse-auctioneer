# Chapter 139: What a Name Is Worth

---

A man came into the yard of Lot 17 on the thirteenth of November at about ten in the morning and sold on a tram arch and had sold on a tram arch for nine years and had not come into a yard in nine years, and about nineteen people were standing about and none of them stopped him, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a man who has been outside an instrument for nine years walked into one at ten in the morning and was let in by nobody.

Halloway Reed read the ledger from the board of eleven in eleven minutes and he did not ask for it and he did not pay for it **and he read the sixth column twice, and a reader at the counter said that a man who reads a column twice has understood which column the page is about, and that he has understood it in a yard in about nine years of never coming into one.**

He read the second form of the withdrawal notice after that and he read the line about the season not being named twice as well, and he said one word out loud in that yard and the word was *right*, and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing asked him which thing was right and he said the whole of it, and then he said the thing the yard had been waiting about all morning without knowing it.

"You have a name against a figure of twenty-one years and a date it comes off, and you have a notice that lets a person walk out of a frame, and you have neither of them on a form anybody outside this basin has to sign. In my trade that is not a market. In my trade that is a rumour with a good head on it."
---

He was let in because a yard is not a fort, and a man of fifty-six said that out loud in front of about nineteen people and the clerk of nineteen years wrote it down and entered that this is the first rule about a yard anybody in this district has said in a room, and that it took nine weeks and a man from a tram arch, and that a rule a man from outside your basin makes necessary is not a rule you chose and is not to be written down as one.

A reader at the counter asked him, in nine seconds, what happens if he takes the number, and he said that a man who asks a question like that is asking a bookseller a bookseller's question and he would answer it, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that he answered it and that she was not asked whether she wanted to hear it and was not asked.

"Then I write it on my board in my own hand with my own price against it, and I do not say where I got it, and I do not say it is checked, and in four years somebody comes and asks me where a fifth came from and I say a man in a yard said it."

A man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that the whole of this district's trouble in one sentence had just been said by a man who sells on a tram arch, and that nobody in the room had said it better in eleven weeks, and that a clerk of nineteen years entered it and did not join it to the four hundred printed sheets or to the copy a stranger took cold or to the three documents nobody owns.

**And on the thirteenth of November a yard let in a man who sells on a tram arch on the ground that a yard is not a fort, and he was told by a reader at a counter that he would write the figure on his own board in his own hand with his own price against it and would not say where he got it and would not say it was checked.** A man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that the whole of this district's trouble in one sentence had just been said by a man who sells on a tram arch.


---

Then he priced it, and he priced it in about nine minutes in that yard in front of about nineteen people, and the clerk of nineteen years wrote all nine minutes down and read it back to him and he said it was right and that he had not said it better the first time and that a man who can say a thing better the second time is a man who was not pricing, and the whole of it is in the minute in his own words.

"The difference between a flat that has a name against its figure and a flat that has not is about a fifth. I cannot tell you a fifth of what **and I have tried twice in my own head and both times it came out as coppers and coppers is not the thing."**

A man of fifty-six said the word fifth out loud in the room and then said the second thing, and a clerk of nineteen years entered both and entered that the word had been used in a room on the same day about a fraction of a price and about the fifth of five instruments this district does not have, and that the two are not joined.

"A fifth of a price is a fraction and a fifth of an instrument is a count. I have been in this district nine weeks and I have stopped being surprised and I have started being tired, and I will say the tired part once."

Then he said the rest of it and he said it to the man the figure is against, in front of nineteen people, and a clerk of nineteen years wrote it down and did not stop him and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said afterwards that she should have stopped him and that she did not and that this is the second time in eleven weeks she has not stopped a man who was not stopped.

"**I have been selling on a tram arch for nine years and I have never sold a year. I cannot post it, I cannot warrant it, and I cannot strike it out when the year turns up short, and it will turn up short, because nobody can tell me whose it is.**"

"And a figure with no name on the far end of it is a figure I have to take on somebody else's word, and my whole trade is taking things on people's words, and I have been doing it since I was nineteen and I have never once done it for a year."

**AND ON THE THIRTEENTH OF NOVEMBER A MAN WHO SELLS ON A TRAM ARCH AND HAS NOT COME INTO A YARD IN NINE YEARS WALKED INTO THE YARD OF LOT SEVENTEEN AND PRICED THE NAME ON THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS AT ABOUT A FIFTH, AND SAID HE COULD NOT TELL A FIFTH OF WHAT, AND THAT A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS ENTERED THAT THE WORD WAS ALSO IN A ROOM THAT DAY ABOUT THE FIFTH OF FIVE INSTRUMENTS THIS DISTRICT DOES NOT HAVE, AND THAT THE TWO ARE NOT JOINED.**

---

Adrian was asked to answer and he answered, and the answer took him nine seconds and it is in the minute in his own words, and a man of fifty-six said afterwards that nine seconds is four seconds longer than the four he has given to refusals in eleven weeks, and that a man who has a real answer takes longer than a man who has a ready one.

"**You are right about the fifth. I put a name on a figure on the nineteenth of October and I did not ask what the name was worth to a person who buys salt off that flat, and that is my trade's fault and not yours.**"

He said the second half after about nineteen seconds and the room was quiet for all of them, and a clerk of nineteen years wrote it down and this is the first time in eleven weeks she has written a thing down twice because she did not believe she had it the first time.

"**A security is what a bidder pays for twice. What a man is right about twice does not cost him a second time. I sold the district a name and I did not price it, and the price is a year, and I cannot give it back and I am not going to pretend I can.**"

Nobody in that yard said anything and about four people wrote it down at home and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road said that this is the first time in eleven weeks that a person in this district has said a sentence and then said the half of it that makes it true, and that the second half was longer.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that and entered that she had wanted to enter the word *fault* and had entered it, and that a man who says a fault is his and is a person who is not being careful, and that a man who says a fault is his and cannot name the fault is a person who is.

---

On the fourteenth of November the ninth household was asked one question and it was the first thing that household had been asked since the nineteenth of October, and the question was whether a name against a figure changes what that figure is worth to the people who are paying for it, and it answered in four words.

The ninth household is the one that said on the twenty-fourth of September that it would rather have the four hundred coppers **and it hired the four hundred on the twenty-fifth, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the two are the same household and that the figure of two thousand coppers and the figure of four hundred coppers and the difference of one thousand six hundred coppers are in this district's books in two places and that this is the second time she has put the three in one line and not joined them.**

A man of fifty-six said that the answer was the answer and that it had been the answer since the twenty-fifth of September, and that the ninth household had understood the finding about a private loom in ten words on the first day it was said, and that the finding was that a private loom is not cheaper because it is the same salt out of a different man, and that a household which has understood that is a household that does not need a name on anything.

A man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that eight of the nine households hired the asked loom and four of the nine then hired the unasked one a day later, and that the ninth household is the only one of the nine that has never once been asked a second time because it did not understand, and that a household which is only asked once is a household the district is not managing.

Nobody managed anything. **A clerk of nineteen years entered that the four hundred coppers is not in the market's own book and that the four hundred coppers is not in the ledger and that the two thousand coppers is in nine households' mouths and in nobody's book, and that the twenty-one years is in a book and is not coppers, and that there are three figures in this district that cannot be added to one another, and that she has entered all three in one line on purpose and that this is the twenty-first time.**

**And on the fourteenth of November the ninth household, which said on the twenty-fourth of September that it would rather have the four hundred coppers and hired them on the twenty-fifth, was asked whether a name against a figure changes what that figure is worth to the people paying for it, and answered in four words.** A man of fifty-six said that the household had understood the finding about a private loom in ten words on the first day it was said, and that two thousand coppers, four hundred coppers and one thousand six hundred coppers are in this district in two places and are not added, and that a household which is only asked once is a household the district is not managing.

---

**AND ON THE THIRTEENTH OF NOVEMBER THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST SAID IN A YARD THAT THE OTHER MAN WAS RIGHT ABOUT THE FIFTH, AND THAT HE PUT A NAME ON A FIGURE ON THE NINETEENTH OF OCTOBER AND DID NOT ASK WHAT THE NAME WAS WORTH TO A PERSON WHO BUYS SALT OFF THAT FLAT, AND THAT THIS IS HIS TRADE'S FAULT AND NOT THE OTHER MAN'S, AND THAT A SECURITY IS WHAT A BIDDER PAYS FOR TWICE, AND THAT THE PRICE OF HIS IS A YEAR, AND THAT HE CANNOT GIVE IT BACK AND IS NOT GOING TO PRETEND HE CAN.**

---

A man of thirty-four who mends fencing said afterwards in that yard, and the clerk of nineteen years wrote it down, that being wrong in your own trade in front of nineteen people is the cheapest price anybody in this district has ever paid for anything, and that the price is not cheap if you are the one paying it, and that the man who sold it to him does not have to live here and goes home at night.

"**He is right. That is the whole of it and I want it entered that a man from outside this district walked in here and was right on the first morning and went home, and that we have spent eleven weeks being right about things he has been right about since he was nineteen.**"

A man of fifty-six said that the fraction had not been entered anywhere, and that a clerk of nineteen years asked him in nine seconds whether it should be, and that he said no, and that the reason he gave is in the minute in his own words and it is forty-one words.

"**A fifth of a year that nobody can name is not a figure. You cannot put a fraction of a thing you cannot point at in a book, and if you do then in four years a clerk will total it.**"

A man of thirty-eight said the other half, and he is the man the figure is against, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he said it and that he is not a keeper of anything and was not asked to be one.

"**And there is no rate. A rate is a thing two people agree on and this has one name against it and a man from a tram arch and no second person anywhere.**"

Nobody argued and a man of about twenty-three who sells nothing and copies for nothing said in that yard that he had copied the notice nine times and that a figure nobody can point at is a figure that gets copied nine times with a different man in it, and that this is the first time he has been able to say why he would not put his name on nine copies, and that a man who will not put his name on nine copies of a number has understood something about numbers in one morning.

**AND ON THE THIRTEENTH OF NOVEMBER A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR WHO MENDS FENCING SAID IN A YARD THAT BEING WRONG IN YOUR OWN TRADE IN FRONT OF NINETEEN PEOPLE IS THE CHEAPEST PRICE ANYBODY IN THIS DISTRICT HAS EVER PAID FOR ANYTHING, AND THAT IT IS NOT CHEAP IF YOU ARE THE ONE PAYING IT, AND THAT THE MAN WHO SOLD IT TO HIM DOES NOT HAVE TO LIVE HERE AND GOES HOME AT NIGHT, AND A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX SAID THAT A FIFTH OF A YEAR THAT NOBODY CAN NAME IS NOT A FIGURE, AND YOU CANNOT PUT A FRACTION OF A THING YOU CANNOT POINT AT IN A BOOK.**

AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS ENTERED ON THE THIRTEENTH OF NOVEMBER THAT A FRACTION WAS PRICED OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND WAS NOT ENTERED **AND THAT NOBODY ASKED HER TO ENTER IT AND THAT SHE DID NOT ASK, AND THAT THE REASON A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX GAVE WAS THAT A FIGURE NOBODY CAN POINT AT IS A FIGURE THAT A CLERK WILL TOTAL IN FOUR YEARS, AND THAT A COLUMN FOR FRACTIONS DOES NOT EXIST IN THIS BASIN AND WAS NOT CREATED.**

---

And then the man from the tram arch did the thing nobody asked him to do, which was to read the twenty-one years out loud from the board of eleven, in his own voice, standing up, without being asked, and a clerk of nineteen years did not stop him and entered that she had been asked to and had not.

He read it and then he read the date it comes off, which is the thirty-first of March, and then he said that a man who sells on a tram arch can sell a thing that is going to happen in five months and cannot sell a thing that has no date on it. **The date is the only part of the whole arrangement he would put his name to, and he is not going to.**

Nobody asked him to and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that this is the sixth time in eleven weeks that a person has been said something in a room and has not been asked whether they wanted to be here **and that the count of that is not the count of not-askings about the woman of forty-five, and that a clerk of nineteen years entered the two counts in two lines and did not join them.**

He left at about eleven in the morning and took no copy and asked for no correction and had said on the eighteenth of October that a document does not owe anybody a correction, and a man of fifty-six said that he has declined three corrections since the middle of September and has printed five sheets in two months and has now walked into a yard and read somebody else's document out loud for free.

"He did not want anything. Put that in the board's book, if the board has a book. It has not got one and I am not going to make it."

---

The train on the siding had stood two hundred and seventy-seven days on the fifteenth of November and a man of seventeen wrote the figure in his index and there is no seventh date on the line under the rule, and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road asked him what the rule was for on the third of November and was told it was a rule, and asked him again on the fifteenth and was told the same thing in the same way, and entered that he had been told the same thing twice and had not asked why and that not asked is in the count.

---

A man of about thirty-eight was asked on the fifteenth of November, in a room in Alder Reach, whether he wanted the name taken off the figure, and a clerk of nineteen years was in the room and was not asked and entered that she had not been asked, and the question is in the minute and his answer is in the minute in his own words and it is fifty-one words.

"**I have said yes to this name twice and I am not going to say it a third time in a week, and if you want it off you have to tell me the four words for, and I do not think anybody in this district has fifty-one words for it.**"

Nobody had four words for it and a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that this is the fourth time in eleven weeks that a person in this district has been asked a question and has answered the question instead, and that the count of the four is four and that the column for things that are not findings is for things a person got wrong and that none of the four is in it, and that a man who answers a question instead of answering it has not got the thing wrong.

A man of fifty-six said that a name cannot be taken off a figure by the person it is put on, and that the entry of the nineteenth of October says so in writing and that the entry was written by a clerk of nineteen years and read out by a reader at a counter, and that a rule which only exists in a minute is a rule a person can forget and a rule in a market's own book is a rule a person has to argue with, and that this one is in the book.

**AND ON THE FIFTEENTH OF NOVEMBER THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST WAS ASKED WHETHER HE WANTED THE NAME TAKEN OFF IT, AND HE ANSWERED THE QUESTION INSTEAD, IN THIRTY-ONE WORDS, AND SAID THAT HE HAD SAID YES TO THE NAME TWICE AND WAS NOT GOING TO SAY IT A THIRD TIME IN A WEEK, AND THAT A NAME CANNOT BE TAKEN OFF A FIGURE BY THE PERSON IT IS PUT ON, AND THAT RULE IS IN THE WARD MARKET'S OWN BOOK AND HAS BEEN SINCE THE NINETEENTH OF OCTOBER.**

**AND ON THE THIRTEENTH OF NOVEMBER HALLOWAY REED READ THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS OUT LOUD FROM THE BOARD OF ELEVEN IN THE YARD OF LOT SEVENTEEN WITHOUT BEING ASKED, AND READ THE DATE IT COMES OFF, WHICH IS THE THIRTY-FIRST OF MARCH, AND SAID THAT A DATE IS THE ONLY PART OF THE ARRANGEMENT HE WOULD PUT HIS NAME TO AND THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO, AND HE TOOK NO COPY AND ASKED FOR NO CORRECTION AND LEFT AT ABOUT ELEVEN IN THE MORNING, AND HE HAS DECLINED THREE CORRECTIONS SINCE THE MIDDLE OF SEPTEMBER AND HAS PRINTED FIVE SHEETS IN TWO MONTHS.**

AND THE FIFTH IS NOT ENTERED **AND THE YEAR IS NOT ENTERED AS A PRICE, AND THE ENTRY OF THE THIRTEENTH OF NOVEMBER HAS A DATE AND A PLACE AND A PERSON AND A REASON AND NO FIGURE IN IT AT ALL.**
