# Chapter 142: The Fourth Day

---

The board outside the room had the twenty-fourth of November on it in a hand that cannot be read, and the room met at about ten in the morning on that day because the day was the last one on the board and not for any other reason, **and about nineteen people came and eleven of them had come to watch whether a fourth day was written, and the eleven did not say so and were not asked.**

A man of fifty-six put it in the minute in those words and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she had entered the word *watch* on the strength of nine pairs of eyes and not eleven, and that the difference of two is not a finding, and that a man who counts what people have come to watch has started a column this district does not have and will not have.

Nobody argued with her and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the two who were not watching were a man of about thirty-four who digs loam and does nine days of it in ten, and a woman of fifty-eight who carries water, **and that both of them had come to say something and that the room would find out what it was in about an hour and would not find out anything.**

It did not find out. A clerk of nineteen years entered at the end of the day that neither of them said anything, that she was asked in the six things at the end and both of them said no, and in the same words, and neither of them said what the words were, and that the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was, and that the man who digs loam is the fourth of the six who hold the ledger and is the one who will have to fill the page.

---

The fourth day was written at about eleven in the morning on the twenty-fourth of November and it is the twenty-fourth of December, **and it was written by a man of fifty-six who does count for a living, in a hand that anybody in that yard could read, and nobody asked him to write it and he was not asked and he said afterwards that he had not been asked and would not have written it if he had been.**

A woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel wrote the first three days and did not write the fourth, and a man of fifty-six did not ask her why and said out loud that he was not going to ask her why, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he had said that he was not going to ask and that nobody was going to ask him whether he had meant it, and that the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was.

Then the finding of the morning was said and it is in the minute in the words of a man of fifty-six and it is sixty-three words, **and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got sixty-three both times and did not say so and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got sixty-three and said it out loud and then said it again.**

"**The first three days are in a hand that cannot be read and the fourth is in a hand that can. A board a person can read is a board a person can argue with. A board in a hand that cannot be read is a board a person has to come and ask about, and this district has spent eleven weeks asking.**"

Nobody took it up. A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the two things are not joined, that the woman who wrote the three is not the man who wrote the fourth, and that a room which has run on a hand nobody can read for eleven weeks and has a hand anybody can read on its last day is a room that has changed and has not said why, and that nobody is going to make it say why.

**AND ON THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF NOVEMBER A FOURTH DAY WAS WRITTEN ON THE BOARD OUTSIDE THE ROOM, BEING THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF DECEMBER, IN A HAND ANYBODY COULD READ, BY A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX WHO DOES COUNT FOR A LIVING AND WAS NOT ASKED TO WRITE IT, AND THE FINDING OF THE MORNING IS THAT A BOARD A PERSON CAN READ IS A BOARD A PERSON CAN ARGUE WITH.**

---

A clerk of nineteen years said on the twenty-fourth of November that she was not the clerk of anything, **and a man of fifty-six counted it and said that it was the fourth time in eleven weeks she had said it and that the count of that is four, and that the count of a woman declining to be the clerk of a room is not the count of a registrar's refusals and is not added to it and is not the same instrument.**

She entered that herself and entered that she had wanted to join the two counts and had not, and that a man of fifty-six started a count in the middle of September and has now started five, and that a man who starts a count and cannot stop is the reason this column is called NOT A FINDING.

the man of fifty-six said that a room whose only instrument runs out on a date is a room a clerk can end, **and that this room had one day left on the twenty-third of November and one day left on the twenty-fourth and wrote a fourth day on the twenty-fourth, and that the two of those are the whole of the finding and are not joined.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked what happens if the fourth day comes and nobody writes a fifth, and the man of fifty-six said that it will be the twenty-fourth of December and that there is a charter to close before it and that if the charter is not closed by then the room will have a date again and nothing under it, and that a room with a date and nothing under it is a room that has told the truth about itself.

---

A man of fifty-six put three things on the table on the twenty-fourth of November and said that a charter that closes without the three of them is a charter that has been closed in order to be shut, **and the three are a person who rotates, a book anybody can go and look at, and a right a person can use.**

A woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel gave him a fourth and did not want to and gave it anyway, and a man of fifty-six wrote it down and read it back and she said it was right, and the fourth is that the document has to say what a withdrawal costs and that nobody in this district can say.

Nobody argued with the fourth. A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that a charter which says a person may withdraw and does not say what the withdrawal costs is the thirtieth of October again in a new shape, **and that the thirtieth of October was a notice that named a season that never started, and that a document that cannot be looked at in the dark is a document that a person finds out about from a stranger.**

And a man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper on a lane off the lower terrace said in nine words that he would not sign the fourth thing.

"**I will not sign it and not say why.**"

He had not been asked to say it out loud, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he had said nine words, that it was nine words, **and that he had not been asked to say them and that the nine words are entered as a shape and not as a sentence.**

---

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against was in the room on the twenty-fourth of November and said nothing for about two hours, **and a man of fifty-six asked him at the end whether he wanted to say something and he said no in three words, and the three words were on their own line, and he gave the reason in forty-three words, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he had been asked and had said no and that not asked is not the same as asked and declined, and that there is no column for the second one and none was made.**

"**No, not tonight.**"

"**The reason is that I am not one of the nine people keeping this and the room is not mine, and a man who is the address for a figure of twenty-one years does not stand in a room and is a person.**"

A man of fifty-six entered that the man was in the room for two hours and that the entry of the nineteenth of October says he is a guarantor of nothing, and that as he stands there he is not that either, and that the two are two things and that the word for it is not on any board in this district and there is no column for it and none was made.

Nobody took it up. **And on the twenty-fourth of November the man who is the address for a figure of twenty-one years was in the room for about two hours and said nothing, and said when he was asked at the end that he is not one of the nine people keeping this and that a man who is the address for a figure does not stand in a room and is a person.**

---

A man of about thirty-seven who sells salt by the load out of a yard on the Verge came west on the twenty-fourth of November on his own account and was not asked to come and was not paid for the day, and he had a complaint about a toll and it was not about a loom, and a man of fifty-six told him before he got to the yard that there is no column for a complaint and that he could put it on a board and that a board is a place a line of chalk goes and that the line would be read by strangers.

He put it on a board anyway. He wrote on the back of the fourth toll board, on a line that was already full, in a hand that shook, **that a load of salt that left a yard on the ninth of October came past a place on this flat and lost four days and nobody wrote the four days down, and a woman of fifty who is the water at the ninth place read it over his shoulder while he was writing and did not stop him.**

The four days are not in any book in this district and were not entered anywhere. A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man wrote them on the back of a board on the twenty-fourth of November and that a line which was already full was written on, and that the count of lines is six and did not move, and that the seventh line at the ninth place is ruled and empty and was not written on and nobody was asked why it is ruled.

It worked. That is the finding of the day and it is twelve words and the man of about thirty-seven who sells salt by the load said them before anybody else could and was not asked where he had heard them.

"**A board is not a complaint and a complaint is a demand.**"

Nobody said it was a rule and a man of fifty-six said that it is not a rule and is the first thing in eleven weeks that has worked on the first day, **and that a rule a man says out loud against his own advantage in front of nineteen people is not this, and that a sentence that stops a thing without costing anybody is not a rule and is not a finding and is a sentence.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked what stopped, and the man of about thirty-seven said in four words that somebody was going to be at the place on the flat, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the four days were on the back of a board and would be seen, and that nobody in this district has established that a board is read, and that the four days will be seen or they will not and that neither of those is a finding.

"**Somebody will be there.**"

---

The man of about thirty-seven who sells salt by the load asked, in a yard, for the four days to be entered in a book, and a man of fifty-six told him that there is no column for a day that a thing lost, and that a day a thing lost is not a toll and is not a figure and that a figure in this district is a thing somebody counts on purpose, **and that a board is a place a line of chalk goes and a book is a place a line is put in on purpose and that he has been offered the second and he chose the first in about four seconds.**

Nobody took that up and the man of about thirty-seven said that he chose the first because the second would take until December and his cart does not wait for December, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he had said the word cart twice in nine minutes and that a man who is losing days on a road does not want a book and wants a person, and that the two are not the same thing and that the district has been treating them as the same thing since the eighth of March.

He then asked the woman of fifty who is the water at the ninth place whether she is paid for the boards, and she said no, **and he asked her whether anybody had ever asked her that, and she said no, and a clerk of nineteen years entered both answers and entered that the second one is the seventh time in eleven weeks and that the count of it is seven and is not in a column.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF NOVEMBER A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-SEVEN WHO SELLS SALT BY THE LOAD ASKED FOR THE FOUR DAYS HE HAD WRITTEN ON A BOARD TO BE ENTERED IN A BOOK AND WAS TOLD THAT A DAY A THING LOST IS NOT A TOLL AND IS NOT A FIGURE, AND HE CHOSE A BOARD OVER A BOOK IN ABOUT FOUR SECONDS, AND HE ASKED THE WOMAN WHO IS THE WATER AT THE NINTH PLACE WHETHER SHE IS PAID AND SHE SAID NO AND HE ASKED HER WHETHER ANYBODY HAD EVER ASKED HER THAT AND SHE SAID NO.**

---

A man of fifty-six asked a question in a lane on the twenty-fourth of November about the five places the name of the office is written, and asked it in the form of whether anybody had been to the fifth, and a clerk of nineteen years said in ten words that she was not going to go and look.

"**She was not going to go and look for it.**"

A man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that the count of the refusals to join two things has not moved and stands at thirteen and that her own count has not moved and stands at twelve, and that neither moved today and that a question in a lane is not a question in a room.

A man of fifty-six said that is a new distinction and asked whether the room was going to make it, and a clerk of nineteen years said that the room was not, and that the room does not make distinctions, it records them, and that a man who asks a lane for a distinction and gets a room's answer has been told the same thing twice in two places and is still not an answer.

**AND A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX ASKED IN A LANE ON THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF NOVEMBER WHETHER ANYBODY HAD BEEN TO THE FIFTH OF THE FIVE PLACES THE NAME OF THE OFFICE IS WRITTEN, AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS SAID IN TEN WORDS THAT SHE WAS NOT GOING TO GO AND LOOK, AND THE COUNTS OF THIRTEEN AND TWELVE DID NOT MOVE AND THE QUESTION WAS NOT ANSWERED AND WAS NOT A REFUSAL AND WAS ASKED IN A LANE.**

---

The woman of fifty who is the water at the ninth place made the count of uses on the twenty-fourth of November and gave a number, **and a man of fifty-six made the same count in the same yard an hour later and gave a smaller number, and a clerk of nineteen years entered both and entered that she was not going to decide which one is right and that the difference is a count of what people think they saw and not a count of what was done.**

She said that the count of four in October was a count in eleven days and this one is a count in a fortnight, and that there is no column in this district for a rate of use and that a column is a thing a person is paid for, and that she is not paid, was not asked to be right, and is not going to be.

Nobody paid her and a man of fifty-six said that a woman of fifty who is the water at the ninth place has now been unpaid and unasked about the same instrument seven times in eleven weeks, **and that this is a fact about the instrument and is not a fact about her, and that the two are two things, and that the number of times is seven and is not in a column and is not a finding.**

The seventh time she said it was to a man of about thirty-seven who sells salt by the load, and he said he was sorry, and she said that being sorry is not a wage and that she has been sorry since the eighth of August, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a man said sorry to a person who carries water at the ninth place and that the entry does not say the man was right and does not say he was wrong.

---

A woman of forty-four who has done it eleven years came on the twenty-fourth of November and was not asked for anything and was not asked why she came, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she was not asked why she came and that the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was, and that she is not an officer of anything and was not asked to be one.**

She said one thing. It went into the minute in her own words and it is twenty-eight words, and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got twenty-eight and a clerk of nineteen years did not count them and said so and he said count them anyway and she did and got twenty-eight both times.

"**You are about to write a date on a board and the date is four weeks away. Ask me now and I will say it in four weeks.**"

Nobody asked her now and a man of fifty-six said that the room had been told something and that the something is that the date on the board is a question and not an answer, **and that a woman who has done it eleven years has said that the date a district asks on is the date it should have asked at, and that nobody asked her which date that was.**

She was not asked about the eleven years and was not asked about the sixth year and was not asked what she would say in four weeks, and a clerk of nineteen years entered all three and entered that the three are not the same column and that the count of them did not move and that the count of not-askings about the woman of forty-five is a different column about a different person.

---

The last entry of the twenty-fourth of November is seven lines and it is the shortest entry this book has carried since the thirtieth of August, **and it was read out twice at the ward market counter, and a reader who is about fifty read it out and then read out the day on the board again, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she did that and was not asked to and that the count of not-askings did not move.**

A man of fifty-six asked her why she read the day out again and she said it in a sentence and the sentence had no number in it, and he entered it in the minute in her own words and it is the first entry in eleven weeks with nothing in it that anybody has to count.

"**Because a person who comes here in December will not have heard it, and there is a day on a board and a person has to be told there is a day.**"

Nobody took it up and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that this is the second time in eleven weeks a person outside a room has given a better answer than a man who counts for a living, **and that the first was a woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel on the twenty-first of November, and that a clerk of nineteen years entered the two in one line and did not join them, and that this is the twenty-fourth time.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF NOVEMBER THE BOARD OUTSIDE THE ROOM CARRIED FOUR DAYS AND THE FOURTH IS THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF DECEMBER, AND THE LAST ENTRY OF THE DAY IS SEVEN LINES, AND A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR WHO MENDS FENCING DECLINED TO BE THE CLERK OF THE LEDGER FOR THE SECOND TIME IN NINE WEEKS AND SAID A NOTICE THAT DOES NOT SAY WHAT IT COSTS IS THE THIRTIETH OF OCTOBER AGAIN IN A NEW SHAPE.**

AND THE SEVENTH LINE AT THE NINTH PLACE IS STILL RULED AND EMPTY AND A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-SEVEN WHO SELLS SALT BY THE LOAD OUT OF A YARD ON THE VERGE WROTE FOUR LOST DAYS ON THE BACK OF THE FOURTH TOLL BOARD, AND THE FOUR DAYS ARE NOT IN ANY BOOK IN THIS DISTRICT, AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS ENTERED THAT THE COUNT OF LINES IS SIX AND DID NOT MOVE.
