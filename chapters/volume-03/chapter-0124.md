# Chapter 124: The Twenty-Ninth and the Thirtieth

---

The two rooms on the twenty-ninth of September were a counting room over a chandler's shop and a room at the ninth place with a lamp on a post cut out of a green pipe, and the registrar was in the first of them and the two named people who hold the boundary were in the second of them.

At the hour that minute was written, which was about eleven, the man of thirty-four who mends fencing and the clerk of nineteen years were thirty-four miles east on a flat between two rivers and were in neither, and the minute of the twenty-ninth says so and says it first, and it gives the hour and it does not give the hour for any other day.

The chandler whose shop the counting room is over is a man of about thirty-four called Pell Anselm and he keeps a counter of oil on Ash Row and a stall in front of it, and he is not one of the two named people and is not a keeper of anything, and on the twenty-ninth of September he let nine people who were not his customers into a room over his shop and did not charge them and did not stand in the doorway, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing was told this on the third of October and entered it and did not ask him about it.

The boundary at the west end was asked again in about forty-four minutes, of which about nine were the last question, and the two named people asked it in their own words and neither of them read anything out, and **the askings that carry a minute-figure are fifty-one, thirty-nine, forty-four, thirty-one, twenty-six and forty-four, which is a hundred and sixty-five minutes before this one and two hundred and thirty-five minutes in all, and a clerk entered all six and the total and did not call any of them the fifth.**

The term was stated and it is in the register in the registrar's own hand and it runs **from the twenty-ninth of September to the thirty-first of March**, and the reason was given in a mouth and not written down, and the minute has it.

---

"**The last term ran a month and the one before ran a month, and a month is not a thing two people can be asked about twice. This one runs to the end of the salt season, and the reason is that a boundary and a loom season that end on different days are two things that have to be asked about twice, and we have got the first asking free.**"

"And the hole in the ground."

"**The hole in the ground is not a mark and it is not a mark today either, and I would like the register to say that the two people who hold this boundary are two people and not a keeper, and that a mark is cut and that is laid, and that a hole is a hole in either month.**"

A woman of about fifty-one who keeps a stall on the lower terrace and who supplies the Harrow engine's oil out of her own till was in the second room and said one thing at about the eleven minute, and it is in the minute, and she is not the woman of forty-four who has done it eleven years, and she is not the woman of about forty-four who keeps a Road House on the tide, and all three descriptions are in one line and are not joined.

"**You have both been asked the same question for a month and neither of you has asked the other one anything, and I am not going to be the person who makes you, and I would like it entered that I noticed.**"

Then the registrar said the part that is hers, which is nine words, and then she did not say anything for eleven seconds, and a clerk of nineteen years was not in the room and read the entry back a week later and did not correct a word.

"**I am not entering a renewal. I am entering that it was asked from two rooms and what was asked in each, and the term is in the register and the term is theirs.**"

---

**AND ON THE TWENTY-NINTH OF SEPTEMBER A BOUNDARY AT THE WEST END WAS ASKED AGAIN FROM TWO ROOMS BY TWO NAMED PEOPLE AND ENTERED BY A REGISTRAR WHO WAS ASKED TO ENTER IT AS A FINDING AND DID NOT, AND THE MAN WHO HELD THE FIGURE WAS THIRTY-FOUR MILES EAST, AND NOBODY HAS ASKED THE REGISTRAR WHY SHE KEEPS TWO LINES APART FOR THE FOURTH TIME IN NINE WEEKS.**

---

The room at the ninth place has a lamp on a post cut out of a green pipe and about nine people in it on the evening of the twenty-ninth, and the two named people who hold the boundary are a woman of about fifty-eight who keeps the ninth place and a man of about forty-two who keeps nothing at all and has kept nothing for eleven years, and the minute gives both of them a trade and a doorway, and about four people have said since that a man who keeps nothing is a thing you could take a mark off.

Nobody took a mark off him, and he said in the room that he had been asked about a mark four times in a month and had said no four times and had not been argued with, and that a mark is a person's refusal of a name and a mark is not a person, and that he would rather be the second half of a boundary than a keeper, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing entered that the woman of fifty-eight who keeps the ninth place and the woman of about fifty who is the water at the ninth place are two people and the minute has both of them and does not join them.

The lamp was lit at about six and there is no clock in that room and the twenty-ninth of September has no weekday on it anywhere in this district, and the asking was timed by the man of about forty-two with a knuckle on the table and the count of forty-four is his and not a clerk's.

Then the thirtieth of September was a figure of one hundred and eighty-three coppers on a page in the market's own book with a heading and no name against it, and the rule of the counter is that it is read out once in every quarter until it moves, and it was not read out.

**Nobody read it out.** A man who does not read figures was at the counter and had been told in August that somebody else would do it and had asked for it in writing on the twenty-first of September, and on the thirtieth he stood at the counter and did not read it, and the entry says he did not read it and does not say he refused, because he was not asked to refuse and nothing was asked of him at all.

He stood there for nineteen minutes and read out the other four figures on the board, which were the four of a rate sheet nine years old, and he read those correctly, and four people in the room noticed that he had read four figures and not the fifth and nobody said anything about it at the time.

A woman of about forty-four who keeps a Road House on the tide was at that counter and had walked a mile and a half to be at it, and the clerk of nineteen years was not there because she was thirty-four miles east, and the minute of the thirtieth was written by a woman of about thirty-six who keeps a stall of chalk and oil on a cart and who had never written a minute in her life and wrote a four-line one.

> **THE THIRTIETH OF SEPTEMBER. THE FIGURE OF ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-THREE COPPERS WAS NOT READ OUT. NOBODY ASKED ANYBODY TO READ IT AND NOBODY REFUSED. THE TERM ENDED AT MIDNIGHT AND THE ENTRY IS THE ENTRY. THE ANSWER IS NOT TO BE STRUCK AND THE FIGURE IS NOT TO BE STRUCK WHETHER OR NOT.**

"**Is that a finding.**"

"**That is four lines and three of them are about a day and one of them is about a figure, and the clerk of nineteen years is not here and I have written what I know, which is that a man stood at a counter and did not read a number, and I have not written why, because I did not ask, and not asked is in the count.**"

The woman of about thirty-six who keeps a stall of chalk and oil on a cart read her own four lines out twice at the counter before she handed the paper to anybody, and got them the same both times, and the clerk who came back from the east on the third of October entered that this is the second time in ten weeks that a person has read a document out twice on their own initiative, and that the first was a paperer of thirty-eight on the twentieth of September, and that neither of the two was a clerk of anything.

---

And a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households was told all of this on the second of October, thirty-four miles away, by a woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel, and he wrote it out in his own hand that evening and sent it west on the third with the count, and the count and the four lines were on one sheet and the sheet had two things on it and the man who prints says a sheet about two things is twelve coppers and nobody printed it.

**AND THE TWO THINGS ARE ON ONE SHEET IN ONE HAND AND IT WAS CARRIED THIRTY-FOUR MILES BY A CART AND IT WAS NOT PRINTED, AND A SHEET ABOUT TWO THINGS COSTS TWELVE COPPERS TO PRINT AND THIS ONE COST NOTHING AND WENT TO A PLACE NOBODY CAN GO AND LOOK AT IT AFTERWARDS.**

And then a sheet went up on eleven walls and three toll boards, and it cost four coppers, and it is about one thing, and the man who printed it charged four coppers and said in nine seconds that a sheet about one thing is four coppers and a sheet about two things is twelve, and that he has now printed five sheets in two months and could not tell which of the five has been worth the money and would not be asked.

> **THE FIGURE OF ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-THREE COPPERS WAS NOT READ OUT ON THE THIRTIETH OF SEPTEMBER BY ANYBODY. IT WILL BE READ OUT ONCE IN EVERY QUARTER UNTIL IT MOVES. NOBODY HAS BEEN ASKED TO READ IT AND NOBODY HAS SAID NO. ANYBODY WHO WAS IN THE ROOM ON THE FOURTEENTH OF AUGUST MAY HAVE ENDED IT IN A SENTENCE AND NONE OF THEM HAS.**

And then a woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel, who was in Alder Reach on the twenty-ninth of September and who is not on this list and who was not asked to come, said one thing at the counter and it is in the minute in her own words and it is thirty-one words long.

"**I came in on the twenty-eighth of September to ask whether the second channel was going to be asked, and the man behind the counter said that the second channel is thirty-four miles east and there is no figure for it, and I asked him what a person is to have to walk thirty-four miles to find out whether a thing is going to be asked, and he said a person is nothing, and he was not being cruel, and I would like both of those things in the minute.**"

The clerk of nineteen years entered that the man behind the counter was not a clerk and had said a person is nothing without cruelty, and entered that the two of them had both been in the room and neither had been asked anything, and that the woman had come on her own and had not been paid for the day and had asked for nothing.

The clerk entered that this district has four things it does not have and one of them is a way of finding out whether a thing thirty-four miles away is going to be asked.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing put up four of the eleven himself and would not put up the fifth because it was on his own wall and he said that a man who puts a sheet on his own wall is a man who has made the wall into a document, and the clerk entered that and entered that he had offered to do the other seven and had not and that a man of fifty-six asked him why and he gave a reason in nine words and the reason was that the other seven were on other people's doors and he had not been asked about those doors.

**AND THE FINDING OF THE THIRTIETH OF SEPTEMBER IS THAT A DISTRICT WITH FOUR THOUSAND AND ONE HOUSEHOLDS AND FIVE TOLL BOARDS AND A RATE SHEET NINE YEARS OLD HAS ARRIVED AT THE STATE WHERE A FIGURE WITH NO NAME ON IT IS READ OUT BY A MAN WHO IS NOT THERE, AND THAT THE FIGURE IS NOT TO BE STRUCK WHETHER OR NOT, AND THAT THIS IS THE ONLY SENTENCE IN TEN WEEKS THAT NOBODY HAS ARGUED ABOUT.**

---

The counter on the thirtieth of September was a counter in a room over a chandler's shop and about forty people were in it, and the man who does not read figures stood behind the middle of it and not at the end of it, and a clerk who is not nineteen years old read the four figures of the rate sheet and stopped before the fifth and did not look up.

Nobody in that room said anything about the stopping and about four people looked at the fifth figure on the board and four people looked at the man, and the man looked at neither and read the sixth figure, which is the price of a bushel of white paint, and got it right, and a woman of about thirty-six who keeps a stall of chalk and oil on a cart was in the room and saw all of it and wrote the four lines that afternoon on the back of a chalk bill.

And then on the first of October came and the season did not start, and the reason was on a sheet in a folder with nine other sheets that were begun and not finished, and the folder has not a finding on it in the hand of the man of thirty-eight, and the clerk of nineteen years wrote not a finding on the folder in the fourth month of this year and nobody has asked her why a folder needs a name.

The season's sheet has three signatures on it and the guild form has twenty-nine columns and four of them for two people, and the inspector of forty-four is thirty-four miles away and cannot be fetched by the first, and the channel stood empty on the first of October with a full bed of brine in it and a frame over it and a woman of thirty-one standing beside it who had come to work and who was not in it.

The bed was full. That is the thing nobody expected, and a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households put his hand in it and it came up to his wrist and it was salt water and it was not cold, and the channel that was supposed to fill it was not running because the sheet that says it may run has three names on it and a fourth is required and the fourth said no on the twenty-seventh of September.

A woman of thirty-eight in the second channel was in the second channel and could not leave it, because leaving it is the whole of what she does and the man of fifty-six said so in a cart on the seventh of September. A woman of thirty-one stood beside the third frame for about an hour and then sat down on the bank, and the minute says she sat down at eleven in the morning and did not get up until about one.

She said one thing, and it is in the minute in her own words, and it is nineteen words, and she had it off her in one go and did not stop to be asked whether she wanted it taken down.

"**I have been here since the first of April and I am going to be paid in salt for a season that has not started, and nobody has told me not to come, and I came.**"

"**Then the loss is two days and it is entered as two days and it is not entered as a year, because a year is not what has been lost and I am the man who wrote the terms and I am the last person in this basin who gets to decide what a loss is.**"

"And if the fourth comes back."

"**Then the six things are asked again and the fourth is asked again, and if he says yes twice he says yes twice, and a person asked twice is not a person asked once and not a person asked three times, and this time somebody is going to write down that he was in the room.**"

---

And in the evening of the first of October the four things were read out for the first time on that flat, in a room over a shop, by a man of thirty-four who mends fencing, and the woman of forty-four who wrote them sat at the back and did not read them and was asked and said no in four words, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that this is the first time in ten weeks that a person has read a document out that a person in the same room wrote and would not sign.

**AND ON THE FIRST OF OCTOBER THE SALT SEASON DID NOT START, AND THE THIRD CHANNEL STOOD EMPTY WITH A FULL BED OF BRINE IN IT, AND A WOMAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT CAME TO WORK IN IT ANYWAY AND WAS NOT IN IT, AND THE FINDING IS THAT A SEASON IS A TERM AND A TERM IS THE LAST DAY SOMEBODY CAN DO SOMETHING, AND ON THE FIRST DAY OF A TERM THAT WAS NEVER ASKED FOR THE THING IS EXACTLY AS IT WAS, WHICH IS WHAT HAPPENED ON THE TWENTY-NINTH OF AUGUST, AND THIS DISTRICT NOW HAS TWO DATES AND NEITHER OF THEM DECIDED ANYTHING.**

---

The first of October was not resolved and the party wrote it down as unresolved, and the entry is nine lines long, and it is in the minute in the clerk's own hand, and the clerk is nineteen years old and thirty-four miles from a counter that is about two hundred yards from where she is standing.

> **THE FIRST OF OCTOBER. THE THIRD CHANNEL IS NOT RUNNING UNDER ANY SHEET THAT THIS PARTY HOLDS. THREE OF THE FOUR OF SEPTEMBER HAVE SIGNED FOR A SEASON AND THE FOURTH REFUSED ON THE TWENTY-SEVENTH OF SEPTEMBER AND WAS ASKED PROPERLY. NO CERTIFICATE HAS BEEN ISSUED FOR THE SEASON. THE TOLL OF THE THIRD CHANNEL IS A YEAR AND THE YEAR IS NOT OUT OF ANYBODY'S HEAD TODAY. THIS ENTRY IS NOT A FINDING AND IS NOT TO BE READ AS ONE AND IS NOT TO BE AMENDED BY ANYBODY WHO WANTS A SEASON.**

And a man of thirty-four who mends fencing read that out in a room over a shop at four in the afternoon and about nineteen people heard it and four people wrote it down and a stranger who has come to this district four times in nine weeks took a copy of it cold and asked one question and got one answer.

"What happens on the second."

"**I do not know and neither does he and I have written that down, and a document that does not know what happens on the second is worth four coppers and the same as any other.**"

The stranger wrote the four words *what happens on the second* on the back of his own copy in a hand that is not a clerk's hand, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that he did it and did not stop him, and that nobody in this district has ever written on a copy of one of its own documents, and that she had not known it was possible and that she was not going to say whether it was.

---

And the man who does not read figures was asked one question on the thirtieth by a woman of about fifty who is the water at the ninth place, and he answered it, and the answer is in the minute in his own words and the clerk entered the length of it, which was thirty-nine, and entered that she had counted it and then uncounted it and got thirty-eight and had left thirty-nine in.

"**You read four numbers and you did not read the fifth.**"

"**I read four numbers because I know the four numbers. I have read the same four numbers off that board every quarter for nine years and I read them without looking, and the fifth one is a hundred and eighty-three and I know that one as well, and I did not read it because I am not the man who agreed to read it and nobody has asked me to read it and nobody has said no to me, and there is a difference between those two things and I have known it since the fourteenth of August.**"

"And the difference."

"**The difference is that if I am not asked then I am not the man, and if I am the man then a stranger can ask me why I did not read it, and I have not got an answer for that, and I would rather be nobody than be a man with no answer.**"

Nobody argued with him and a clerk of nineteen years entered the whole of it in four seconds and entered that it was the fourth time in ten weeks that a person had described not being the man as a thing they would rather be, and that the other three had all been said by people who had been in a room, and that this one was said at a counter by a man who does not read figures and who was not in a room.

**AND THE FINDING OF THE TWENTY-NINTH, THE THIRTIETH AND THE FIRST OF OCTOBER IS THAT THE ONLY TWO DATES THIS DISTRICT OWNED BOTH CAME AND NEITHER OF THEM DECIDED ANYTHING, AND THAT THE FIRST OF THEM WAS ASKED BY TWO NAMED PEOPLE WHO DO NOT HOLD THE THING AS A JOB, AND THE SECOND OF THEM WAS NOT ASKED BY ANYBODY, AND THAT A DISTRICT WHICH HAS RUN OUT OF DATES IS IN THE SAME POSITION AS A DISTRICT THAT NEVER HAD ANY, AND IT NOW TAKES ABOUT TEN WEEKS TO TELL THE TWO APART.**
