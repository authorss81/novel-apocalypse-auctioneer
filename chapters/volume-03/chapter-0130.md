# Chapter 130: The Right to Be Neutral

---

The entry went into the market's own book at ten in the morning on the nineteenth of October and it was written by a clerk of nineteen years and read out by a reader at the counter who is about fifty and was a signal clerk before her shoulder went and is on eleven coppers a quarter for four days a week, and about nineteen people were at the counter and a stranger who has been to this district four times in ten weeks was at the counter and took a copy of the page cold.

The rule of the counter is that a document goes out twice, and it went out twice, and the second time the reader stopped in the middle of it and said a thing, and the thing is in the minute in her own words and it is nineteen words long.

"**I am going to say the word steward and I have never said it at this counter in nine years, and I am saying it because it is in the entry and not because anybody asked me to say it, and I would like it noticed that it is in the entry.**"

---

> **THE NINETEENTH OF OCTOBER. ONE MAN IS THE ADDRESS FOR THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS AND FOR A YEAR AND A HALF ON A LINE OF ITS OWN AND FOR TWO PEOPLE WHO ARE IN A COLUMN AS NOT KNOWN AND NOT ASKED, UNTIL THE THIRTY-FIRST OF MARCH. HE DID NOT TAKE ANY OF IT AND THE ENTRY SAYS BOTH IN ONE LINE: IT IS NOT HIS FAULT AND IT IS HIS RESPONSIBILITY. HE IS NOT A BEARER. HE IS NOT A HOLDER. HE IS NOT A WITNESS. HE WAS NOT ASKED WHETHER HE WOULD STAND IN A CHANNEL. HE HAS SAID YES TWICE AND THE TWO YESES ARE IN THE MINUTE AND THE MINUTE DOES NOT SAY THEY WERE THE SAME. THE WORD STEWARD IS IN THIS ENTRY AT THE REQUEST OF NINE HOUSEHOLDS AND NOT AT THE REQUEST OF THE MAN NAMED IN IT.**

I have written a steward into the entry at the request of nine households and not at the request of the man named in it, and the reason is in the minute in nine words and the nine words were said by a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households and who crossed a raised plank road four times this month to be in rooms.

"**It is a name for the job and the job is already being done by about nine people and one of them is not you.**"

---

**AND ON THE NINETEENTH OF OCTOBER A STEWARD ENTERED THE MARKET'S OWN BOOK AGAINST A FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS AND A YEAR AND A HALF, AND THE WORD WAS WRITTEN BY NINE HOUSEHOLDS AND NOT BY THE MAN IT IS ABOUT, AND THE ENTRY SAYS THAT IT IS NOT HIS FAULT AND THAT IT IS HIS RESPONSIBILITY IN ONE LINE, AND THAT HE IS NOT A BEARER AND NOT A HOLDER AND NOT A WITNESS.**

---

And then he said the sentence of the day, and he said it in a yard, in front of nineteen people, and the clerk of nineteen years wrote it down and the reader at the counter asked for it afterwards and it went into the market's own book on a line of its own.

"**I was neutral until the seventh of October and I am not neutral now, and what it has cost me is the only thing I had left to give away, which is the ability to stand in a yard and say of a thing that it is not mine.**"

Then a woman of about forty-four who keeps a Road House on the tide asked him what neutral had been worth, and he said that, and the minute has the whole of it.

"**It was worth the ability to be wrong about a thing without anybody noticing that I was wrong, and there are four people in this district who noticed inside nine minutes, and I want that entered as a credit to them and not as a fault of mine.**"

And a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that four people had noticed inside nine minutes on more than one occasion in ten weeks and that the reason was not that he was often wrong, and the clerk entered that the man of thirty-four said that and did not finish the sentence, and that the clerk of nineteen years had wanted to write the end of it and had not, and that not doing a thing is also a thing she does.

And on the nineteenth of October, in the middle of the afternoon, the nine households were asked one question each, in turn, and it is the same question, and the answer is the same one word, and the clerk of nineteen years entered all nine and the minute says she entered all nine in the same hand and the same word and that a column of nine identical words is a column and not a finding.

The question was whether the household understood that the address is a name and not a remedy, and eight of the nine said yes and one of the nine said that it did not understand the difference and was asked again in four minutes and said yes the second time, and the minute says that a household was asked twice about the same thing inside four minutes and that this is the first time in ten weeks that the second asking has changed an answer on the same side.

The one that had not understood was the ninth household, and the ninth household was the one that had said on the twenty-fourth of September that it would rather have the four hundred coppers, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that the two are the same household and entered that she had put them in one line and had not joined them, and that this is the seventh time.

And a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households said, in a room, that a figure of twenty-one years is a thing nine households cannot pay and cannot refuse and cannot check, and that the word steward does not change any of those three, and that he had wanted to say that on the second of October and had not and had said the price instead.

**AND ON THE NINETEENTH OF OCTOBER NINE HOUSEHOLDS WERE ASKED ONE QUESTION EACH IN TURN AND EIGHT SAID YES AND ONE SAID THAT IT DID NOT UNDERSTAND THE DIFFERENCE AND WAS ASKED AGAIN IN FOUR MINUTES AND SAID YES, AND THE FINDING IS THAT A FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS A THING NINE HOUSEHOLDS CANNOT PAY AND CANNOT REFUSE AND CANNOT CHECK, AND THAT THE WORD STEWARD DOES NOT CHANGE ANY OF THOSE THREE, AND THAT THE ONE THAT DID NOT UNDERSTAND WAS THE NINTH HOUSEHOLD, WHICH IS THE ONE THAT SAID IT WOULD RATHER HAVE THE FOUR HUNDRED COPPERS.**

**AND ON THE NINETEENTH OF OCTOBER THE RIGHT TO BE NEUTRAL WENT OUT OF A YARD IN ALDER REACH AND THE FINDING IS THAT IT WAS NOT WORTH MUCH AND THAT FOUR PEOPLE SAID SO INSIDE NINE MINUTES, AND THAT HE HAS ASKED FOR IT TO BE ENTERED AS A CREDIT TO THEM.**

---

And the man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households then did the thing that the minute of the nineteenth of October is actually about, and it took nine minutes and the clerk of nineteen years read it back and got nothing wrong.

> **NINE HOUSEHOLDS ASKED FOR THE NAME AND THE JOB IS ALREADY BEING DONE BY ABOUT NINE PEOPLE AND THE MAN NAMED IS ONE OF THEM AND THE OTHER EIGHT ARE: FOUR PEOPLE WHO STAND IN FRAMES, A WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR WHO WROTE FOUR THINGS AND WILL NOT SIGN THEM, A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR WHO MENDS FENCING, A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS, AND A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX. NONE OF THE OTHER EIGHT ASKED FOR THE NAME AND NONE OF THEM WAS ASKED AFTERWARDS AND NOT ASKED IS WRITTEN AGAINST ALL FIVE.**

And a woman of fifty-eight who carries water and does not read figures read that out in a room over a shop on the evening of the nineteenth and got one word wrong, and the word was *four*, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that the reader was wrong and that the four are four people who stand in frames and that a room is not a set of frames.

And a man of fifty-six who does count for a living was asked whether he was one of the eight and said that he was, and that he had said the nine words himself, and that a man who says the reason a word was used is not an officer of the word, and that he had been in four rooms in this district in a month and had been in none of them as a keeper of anything.

---

And then the last thing of the nineteenth of October was said by the woman of forty-four who has done it eleven years, and she said it to the room and not to him, and the clerk of nineteen years wrote it and underlined it and **the minute says this is the first time in twelve days that the clerk has underlined anything and that a reader at a counter asked which line and was not told.**

"**He is the address. He is not the man who took it, and he is not the man who stands in the frame, and there are about nine of us and he is the one we can all find. Nobody has said out loud what happens when one of the four of us stops, and I am not going to ask him and he is not going to tell me, and that is a thing for the thirty-first of March and not for tonight.**"

And a man of about forty-four who has a channel and took four hundred coppers from four of the nine households wrote nothing in the market's own book on the nineteenth of October and was not asked to, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the four hundred coppers is not in the market's book and that there are two sets of books in this district and that the four hundred is in neither of them, because a man who is not on a cart and is not on a flat wrote it on a piece of paper and gave it to a woman who read it and gave it back.

She read it out in a room over a shop on the evening of the nineteenth and gave it back to him afterwards and it is in the party's minute as a line of nineteen words, and the minute says that the four hundred coppers and the year that came with it are the first thing anybody in this district has been paid for out of a public book in ten weeks and that neither of the two is on the public book.

A woman of about forty-four who has done it eleven years said one sentence about it and the clerk entered it and did not join it to the four hundred and did not join it to the twenty-one.

"**He took the year and he took the money and he is the only man in this room nobody can do anything about, and that is because we asked him a question he said yes to and not a question he could say no to, and I would like somebody to write down that we got the whole of this because we asked him properly and not because we were careful.**"

**AND ON THE NINETEENTH OF OCTOBER A WOMAN WHO HAS DONE IT ELEVEN YEARS SAID IN A ROOM THAT THE NEW STEWARD IS THE ONE EVERYBODY CAN FIND AND THAT NOBODY HAS SAID WHAT HAPPENS WHEN ONE OF THE FOUR STOPS, AND SHE WAS NOT ASKED AND SHE WAS NOT AN OFFICER OF ANYTHING, AND NOT ASKED IS IN THE COUNT, AND THE COUNT IS A COLUMN AND NOT A BUCKET.**

---

And on the twentieth of October the word steward was read out at the ward market counter twice and at the ninth place once and it was written on no board anywhere because a board in this district is a place a line of chalk goes and not a place a word goes, and about four people said out loud that they did not know what a steward was and that they had never been told and that they were not going to be asked.

And a woman of fifty-eight who carries water and does not read figures said that nobody had asked her whether she wanted to be a steward, and that she was not going to be one, and that a person who says no on the twentieth of October and is asked again on the first of April is a person who has been asked twice, and that the second time is the one that costs.

And a reader at the counter said that a word a person has said no to is a word they can still hear other people use, and that this is not a reason to stop using it and it is not a reason to start.

She said that a woman of fifty-eight who carries water asked her whether she had ever been a steward and she said no in four seconds, and then said that the word had been in a book on a board in her hands on the nineteenth of October and that she had read it twice and had not known what it was and had read it anyway.

She said that this district has been doing that for nine weeks with a hundred and forty-two departures in it, and that nobody has asked a person to read the two together.

And on the twentieth of October, at eleven in the morning, the nine households were asked whether they wanted the word put on the five toll boards, and eight of the nine said no and one said that it did not know and was asked a second time and said no the second time, and the clerk of nineteen years entered that the one that did not know was the ninth household and that this is the second time in two days the ninth household has been the one that did not know, and that a household is a count and is not a person and that the two are not added anywhere in this district's books.

Nobody argued with the eight noes and a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households said that a word is not a toll and a toll board is not a notice and that the two are not the same instrument, and that he had wanted to put the word on the boards in the fourth month of this year and had been told that a man who prints a word on a board has made a document and there is no column for a document in this district.

And a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said that a document with no column in it is a thing a stranger can take cold, and that this district has spent ten weeks finding that out twice about certificates and once about a train that does not run, and that the fourth time is a word, and that a word is the cheapest thing anybody has ever put on a wall and that is exactly why nobody should.

**AND ON THE TWENTIETH OF OCTOBER A WORD WENT OUT ACROSS FOUR THOUSAND AND ONE HOUSEHOLDS WITHOUT BEING PUT ON A SINGLE ONE OF THE FIVE TOLL BOARDS, AND THE FINDING IS THAT A TOLL BOARD IN THIS DISTRICT IS A PLACE A LINE OF CHALK GOES AND NOT A PLACE A WORD GOES, AND THAT A WORD IS NOT A COLUMN AND A WORD IS NOT AN INSTRUMENT AND NOT ONE OF THE SIX THINGS THIS DISTRICT HAS BUILT AND NOT NAMED IS A WORD.**

---

And in the evening of the twentieth of October a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked him, in a yard, what a steward could actually do, and he answered in nine minutes, and the answer is in the minute in his own words, and the clerk of nineteen years wrote all nine minutes down and read it back and got nothing wrong.

"**A steward can be found. That is the whole of it and it is more than this district has had in ten weeks. I can be found at a counter and a stranger can ask me what a figure is and I cannot say it is not mine. I cannot call a hearing, I cannot decide anything, I cannot make four people sign, and I cannot stand in a frame, and if you want a man who can do those four things then he is not a steward and he is the ninth person and you have already got him and he crossed a plank road four times this month.**"

"Then what is it for."

"**It is for the thirty-first of March. On that day a figure of twenty-one years either has a name against it or it has not, and if it has, then whoever asks about it asks me, and if it has not then there is nobody, and a man who has said in advance that he will be the name if anybody wants one has done the only thing a person can do in advance, which is stand there.**"

A woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel asked whether the thirty-first of March was a date anybody could refuse, and he said that it was a date and that a date is the last day somebody can do something, and that a term is not a quarter because a quarter comes round again by itself and a term does not, and that this has been said in this district four times in ten weeks and that he was not going to say it a fifth time in a yard.

And on the twenty-first of October the last entry of the day was made and it is eleven lines long and it is in the clerk of nineteen years' own hand, and it is the second-shortest entry this book has carried since the eighth of March.

> **THE TWENTY-FIRST OF OCTOBER. THE FIGURE OF ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-THREE COPPERS WAS NOT READ OUT IN THIS QUARTER AND NOBODY HAS SAID NO. THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS AND A YEAR AND A HALF AND TWO PEOPLE AS NOT KNOWN AND NOT ASKED STANDS AGAINST ONE NAME UNTIL THE THIRTY-FIRST OF MARCH. FIVE BOARDS CARRY SIX LINES AND NOBODY CAN SAY WHAT THE THING IS CALLED. A ROOM THAT READS FOUR THINGS OUT MET ON THE NINTH OF OCTOBER AND WILL MEET ON A DAY WRITTEN ON A BOARD IN A HAND THAT CANNOT BE READ. THE SEASON HAS NOT STARTED. NO CERTIFICATE HAS BEEN ISSUED FOR THE SEASON. THIS ENTRY HAS NO FINDING IN IT.**


And a man of fifty-six who is a secretary of a cooperative of nine households was asked on the twenty-first of October, at four in the afternoon, what happens to the figure of twenty-one years on the first of April if the season has not started by then, and he did the arithmetic out loud in a yard in nine minutes and it is in the minute in his own words.

"**The season runs from the first of October to the thirty-first of March. The third channel has not run and three of the four of September have signed and the fourth said no. If the fourth says yes on the first of April then a year goes out of whoever is in the frame and it goes onto the sheet and the sheet says twenty-two and the sheet is full, and if he does not say yes then the twenty-one stands and the bed of brine goes back into the water in nine days and there is a second pan with a frame over it and nobody in it and we already know what happens to those.**"

"Nobody is joining that to the first channel."

"**Nobody is joining that to the first channel. The first channel is a sill of loam laid by a man who is dead and the reason is on a sheet at the ninth mile with a date on it, and the second one would be a bed of brine going back into the water because a person was not asked in six things, and those are two things and this district has a fifth thing it does not have and it is a way of telling the difference between a ground and a decision.**"

And a man of thirty-four who mends fencing entered that and entered that the secretary had said a way of telling the difference between a ground and a decision, and that a way of saying that a toll lands on a place is the fifth of the five instruments this district does not have, and that the two are not joined, and that the clerk entered the fact that she had put them in one line and had not joined them, and that this is the eighth time.

And the train on the siding had stood two hundred and fifty-two days on the twenty-first of October, and nobody said so, and the man of seventeen wrote the figure in an index in a hand that is not a clerk's hand, and beside it there are five dates on one line and a rule drawn under the fifth of them and no sixth date.

And a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said the last thing of the twenty-first of October, in a yard, to about nineteen people, and the clerk of nineteen years wrote it down and did not underline it.

"**We have built six instruments in ten weeks and we cannot name the one that everybody is using, and we have got a steward and he cannot be neutral, and we have got a sheet with twenty-one years on it and it is not a ledger, and the thirty-first of March is the day the word steward stops and nobody has said what a worker does on the last day of a season, and on the first of April a woman is going to stand in a frame and somebody is going to have to write her name down, and none of the four of us is going to be the clerk of that, and that is the job for the next ten weeks and I am not taking it.**"
