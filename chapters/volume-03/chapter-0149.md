# Chapter 149: The Charter Closes

---

The charter was closed at about four in the afternoon on the eighteenth of December in a room with no officer and no clerk and no name on its door, and it was closed by about nineteen people and a clerk of nineteen years and none of the nineteen is Adrian, **and a man of fifty-six entered that the man the figure of twenty-one years is against was in the room and did not speak and that a clerk of nineteen years entered that he was in the room for about an hour and a half and was not asked to speak and that the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was.**

The closing was done by the six who hold the ledger and by the four on the rotation and by six of the nine households that took the two thousand coppers on the twenty-fourth of September, and the ninth household is one of the six and it is the only one of the six that asked a question, and the clerk entered that the ninth household was the only one of the six that spoke and that a man of thirty-four who mends fencing said the count of that is one and is not a finding, **and that the other three households that took the two thousand coppers were not in the room and were not asked and that the three are not added to the six.**

It was not argued with and a man of fifty-six said that a charter closed by nineteen people and a clerk and entered by a clerk of the ward market is not a document about a man, **and that he has been in this room since the eighth of March and this is the first document in this district that has nothing in it about him except a name and a figure, and that the name and the figure are the two things he is and neither of them is him.**

**AND ON THE EIGHTEENTH OF DECEMBER THE CHARTER WAS CLOSED BY ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE AND A CLERK AND NONE OF THE NINETEEN IS ADRIAN, AND IT WAS ENTERED BY A CLERK OF THE WARD MARKET, AND IT IS THE FIRST DOCUMENT IN THIS DISTRICT THAT HAS NOTHING IN IT ABOUT HIM EXCEPT A NAME AND A FIGURE, AND THE NAME AND THE FIGURE ARE THE TWO THINGS HE IS AND NEITHER OF THEM IS HIM.**

---

A man of fifty-six read the four sheets out at about half past four in the afternoon on the eighteenth of December and a clerk of nineteen years read them back and got nothing wrong, and the rule of the counter is that a document goes out twice and it went out twice and not three times.

The first sheet is a rotation of four people with a term. The second sheet is a page ruled to thirty-one rows with eleven filled and twenty ruled and empty, and the number thirty-one is not on it, and the words A PAGE RULED TO THIRTY-ONE ROWS are, and a reader who is about fifty said aloud that the difference between the number and the words is the difference between a book a person can check and a book a person has to stand in front of.

The third sheet is a notice with three things in the middle of it and four lines under them, and the fourth line of the third sheet says that nobody has established what a withdrawal costs and nobody is going to establish it by asking the person, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the four lines are what a district gets when it admits what it does not know and that a form with no column on it was what it got before.

The fourth sheet is the terms, and it carries the season, and the first of those seasons is the first of April to the thirty-first of March and has not been certified, and it carries a right to withdraw on any day before the first of April, and it says on its face that the right does not reach backwards, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the right does not reach backwards and that a frame is oak and that the year came out of the channel and that the three are on the face of a charter and are not joined.

a man of about thirty-four who digs loam said that the hole is in the charter on purpose, **and that a charter which said it had closed the year would be a charter a person could be held to by, and that the twenty-one years and the four hundred years are both outside it and that being outside is the only honest place for them.**

And the terms sheet carries **the figure OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IN THE SAME LINE AS HIS NAME AND THE ENTRY RUNS TO THE THIRTY-FIRST OF MARCH, AND IT CARRIES A YEAR AND A HALF ON A LINE OF ITS OWN THAT IS NOT ADDED AND TWO PEOPLE IN A COLUMN AS NOT KNOWN AND NOT ASKED AND ONE FRAME THAT TOOK NOTHING, AND NONE OF THE THREE IS ADDED TO ANYTHING.**

---

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing asked, at about half past four on the eighteenth of December, what happens on the first of April if the six of them who hold the ledger all say no, and about nine people were asked it in a short question and nobody answered for about nineteen seconds.

A woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel said the answer, and it is thirty-five words, and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got thirty-five both times and did not say so and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got thirty-five and said it out loud and then said it again.

"**Then the season does not run and the beds of brine go back into the water in about nine days and there is a second pan with a frame over it and nobody in it.**"

It was not argued with and a man of fifty-six said the other half, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the two are two things and that nobody is joining them to the first channel and that the first channel is a sill of loam laid by a man who is dead and that this is a bed of brine going back into the water because six people said no, and that the two are two things.

Nobody is joining that to the first channel. A man of fifty-six said it in a yard in November and the clerk wrote it in a minute and it is in that minute, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered on the eighteenth of December that the sentence was said again in a room and that the clerk had wanted to join the two occasions and had not, and that this is the twenty-sixth time.**

The room let it stand and the man of fifty-six said that the answer to the question is that a charter has no clause for what happens when every person in it says no,** and that this is not one of the five things this district does not have, because the five are a way of saying what a toll lands on a place, a column that is a place, an office, a station with nobody at it, and a way to pay a person who is not in a household, and a clause for a refusal is a thing the district has and not a thing it lacks.**

**AND ON THE EIGHTEENTH OF DECEMBER A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FOUR WHO MENDS FENCING ASKED WHAT HAPPENS ON THE FIRST OF APRIL IF THE SIX OF THEM ALL SAY NO, AND A WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-THREE WHO TAKES IN WASHING SAID THAT THEN THE SEASON DOES NOT RUN AND THE BEDS OF BRINE GO BACK INTO THE WATER IN ABOUT NINE DAYS, AND NOBODY IS JOINING THAT TO THE FIRST CHANNEL.**

---

A man of about twenty-three who sells nothing and copies for nothing was asked on the eighteenth of December whether he would take a copy of the charter to keep, and he said yes in four seconds, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he said yes in four seconds and that this is the fourth time he has been asked and the first time he has said yes to being the man who holds something.

He said the second half before he was asked for it. "**I will keep it. I will not be the man whose copies are the record and put a name on the nine.**"

It was not argued with and a man of fifty-six said that a copy is a document and the original is a room, and that the man who wrote those ten words in November is in this room and has not said them again and does not need to, and that a clerk of nineteen years entered the ten words and the date and did not join the two occasions, and that this is the twenty-seventh time.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the copy goes to a man of about twenty-three in a stall and not to a board and not to a book, and that he is not a keeper of it, is not paid for it, was not asked whether he would like to be,** and that a copy in a stall is a copy a stranger can buy for nothing and that this is the second time a document in this district has been put somewhere a stranger can reach it without a person.**

And on the eighteenth of december A MAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-THREE WHO SELLS NOTHING AND COPIES FOR NOTHING SAID YES IN FOUR SECONDS TO KEEPING A COPY OF THE CHARTER AND SAID THE SECOND HALF BEFORE HE WAS ASKED FOR IT, AND HE WILL NOT BE THE MAN WHOSE COPIES ARE THE RECORD AND PUT A NAME ON THE NINE.

---

The nine lines a guild inspector of forty-four wrote on the back of the second sheet were read into the record at about twenty past five on the eighteenth of December, and a clerk of nineteen years read them and got nothing wrong, and a man of fifty-six asked that they be entered in full.

The nine lines are a certificate that says four people were asked, and a certificate that cannot certify what any of the four said to the other three, and a refusal to put the word certified on any of the four sheets, **and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the four sheets are therefore un-certified and that the word is not on any of them and that a man thirty-four miles east has said so in writing and that the two facts are in a book together and that the two are two things.**

Nobody took it up and a man of fifty-six said that a charter that cannot be certified is the first one this district has built that a stranger cannot be sold, and that a stranger can be sold a thing that has somebody's name on it and that the four sheets have a name on one of them and that the name is a figure and not a person who can be sold anything.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the four sheets are not in any guild drawer and are not in a bank and are not in the records office, **and that they are one printed, one on a board of eleven in a yard, and two hand copies, and that this is a worse position than a drawer and a better one, and that the entry says both halves and does not join them.**

And the four sheets are not in a guild drawer **and are not in a bank AND ARE NOT IN THE RECORDS OFFICE, AND A GUILD INSPECTOR OF FORTY-FOUR HAS SAID IN WRITING THAT HE CANNOT CERTIFY WHAT ANY OF FOUR PEOPLE SAID TO THE OTHER THREE, AND THE FOUR SHEETS HAVE THE WORD CERTIFIED ON NONE OF THEM, AND THE TWO FACTS ARE IN A BOOK TOGETHER AND ARE NOT JOINED.**

---

The rotation turned on the eighteenth of December at about five in the afternoon and it was the first turn of this rotation and the man of about thirty-eight who is at the second channel and has done three seasons took the first week, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he is the second of the four and that the turn took about nine minutes and that nothing was said about the turn by anybody except a man of fifty-six who said that a rotation that turns once is a rotation that has been shown it can.**

The four-bearer charter of the railway turned for the first time under a closed charter on the second of July and again when the term ran out on the twenty-ninth of July, and one of those weeks ran out with nobody in it, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the two rotations are two instruments and that the two are not merged and that the week that ran out with nobody in it is in a book a stranger can take cold.

a man of fifty-six said that a rotation with a hole in it is a rotation and not a failure, **and that the finding of the second turn of a rotation in this district was that a thing which turns at no cost is not a rotation, and that this one turned at the cost of a man saying no four times and a man not offering, and that the two are two things.**

**AND ON THE EIGHTEENTH OF DECEMBER THE ROTATION TURNED FOR THE FIRST TIME AND A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT WHO IS AT THE SECOND CHANNEL AND HAS DONE THREE SEASONS TOOK THE FIRST WEEK, AND THE TURN TOOK ABOUT NINE MINUTES AND NOTHING WAS SAID ABOUT IT BY ANYBODY EXCEPT A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX WHO SAID THAT A ROTATION THAT TURNS ONCE IS A ROTATION THAT HAS BEEN SHOWN IT CAN.**

---

He did not speak for the hour and a half. A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said afterwards in a yard that this was the fourth time in eleven weeks a person had been in a room and said nothing and had been let, and that three of the four had been a man who digs loam and a man who carries water and a man of twenty-six in a bed of brine,** and that the four are not joined and that the room has stopped noticing and that this is the eleventh week and the room should still be noticing.**

It was not argued with and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a man who is the address for a figure of twenty-one years was in a room for an hour and a half and was not asked to speak, and that a room which does not ask a man who can be found to speak is a room that has worked out something about him, and that she does not know what and did not ask and that the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was.

He was asked one question at about five in the afternoon and the question was whether the word steward went on the charter, and he said no in four seconds, and the reason is thirty words and a clerk of nineteen years counted them and got thirty, and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them separately and got thirty as well, and neither of them said so.

"**The word ends on the thirty-first of March and the charter does not know that date is coming. I will not put a word on a document that outlives it.**"

Nobody took that up and a man of fifty-six entered that a word which ends on a date and a charter which does not carry the date are two instruments, **and that the second half of the word is a season and the season has not started, and that the entry of the sixteenth of November stands, and that on the eighteenth of December a clerk of nineteen years entered that this is not a delay and entered it of her own accord and was not asked to.**

Nobody asked her, and nobody asked the clerk who entered it. And a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody asked her and that this is the tenth time in eleven weeks nobody has,** and that the count of a clerk of nineteen years being asked nothing is ten and is not the count of the woman of forty-five and is not the count of the registrar's refusals and is in no column and is not going to be.**

---

A clerk of nineteen years read the minute of the sixth of December out at about a quarter past five on the eighteenth of December, in that room, in the ordinary way, and it is the minute in which the registrar of this district's records office was asked to be one of the two witnesses to a food agreement and said no in four seconds.

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against was in the room and heard his own name read out in it, because the minute says the man who wrote it read it out, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he was not asked whether he wanted the minute read and that nobody was going to ask him because a minute is read out.**

He did not say anything. Then he said one thing, and it is eighteen words, and it is about a year and not about anything else, and a clerk of nineteen years counted them once and got eighteen and did not count them again and entered that she did not count them again because he had not asked her to and the not-asking is entered and the count is where it was.

"**I am glad she was not in that room. There is nobody else who would have said why.**"

It was not argued with and a man of fifty-six said that the two of them have now said one thing each about a year in two rooms four days apart, and that her sentence is in a minute of the sixth of December and his is in a minute of the eighteenth and that the two are in two minutes and are not joined and that a clerk of nineteen years entered them in one line on purpose and would not say why.

**AND ON THE EIGHTEENTH OF DECEMBER THE MAN THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-ONE YEARS IS AGAINST WAS ASKED WHETHER THE WORD STEWARD WENT ON THE CHARTER AND SAID NO IN FOUR SECONDS, AND HE SAID IN THIRTY WORDS THAT THE WORD ENDS ON THE THIRTY-FIRST OF MARCH AND THE CHARTER DOES NOT KNOW THAT DATE IS COMING, AND THE SECOND HALF OF THE WORD IS A SEASON AND THE SEASON HAS NOT STARTED AND THE ENTRY SAYS THAT IS NOT A DELAY.**

AND THE CROP IS IN ALDER REACH AND THE CROP SURVIVED THE THIRD MONTH OF A SEASON THAT HAS NOT STARTED ON A PAN THAT IS NOT A FRAME, AND THE SALT CAME WEST IN ONE CART ON THE TWENTY-SECOND OF NOVEMBER AND WAS WEIGHED BY A PERSON WHO WAS NOT ASKED TO COME, AND THE FIGURE OF FOUR HUNDRED AND FORTY POUNDS OF MEAL IS ON A SHEET THAT SAYS HOUSEHOLDS, AND THE CROP AND THE PRECEDENT ARE IN TWO BOOKS AND ARE NOT ADDED AND ARE NOT JOINED.

AND THE CHARTER IS CLOSED **AND IT IS FOUR SHEETS AND ONE OF THEM IS PRINTED FOR FOUR COPPERS AND ONE OF THEM IS ON A BOARD OF ELEVEN IN THE YARD OF LOT SEVENTEEN AND TWO OF THEM ARE HAND COPIES, AND THE SIXTH SHEET OF THE FOOD AGREEMENT SAYS ON ITS FACE THAT THE TWO WITNESSES WERE NOT IN THE ROOM WHEN THE REASON WAS READ OUT, AND A YEAR IS THE REASON AND A YEAR HAS NO NUMBER ON IT.**

AND THE ROOM HAS SIX DAYS AND THE BOARD OUTSIDE THE ROOM CARRIES THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF DECEMBER IN A HAND ANYBODY CAN READ, AND THE COUNT OF THINGS THIS DISTRICT DOES NOT HAVE IS FIVE AND THE COUNT OF INSTRUMENTS IT HAS BUILT AND NOT NAMED IS SIX AND THE COUNT OF DOCUMENTS NOBODY OWNS IS THREE, AND NONE OF THE THREE COUNTS MOVED IN DECEMBER.

---

The ninth household was in that room when the charter was closed and it did not ask anything, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the only one of the six that asked a question on the fourteenth asked nothing on the eighteenth, and that this is the first time in eleven weeks a household has been in two rooms and asked in one of them and not the other, and that the two rooms are not joined.**

A man of fifty-six said that a household which has understood a finding does not need to be asked about it twice, and that the ninth household is the only one of the nine that understood the finding about a private loom in September and is the only one that came west on its own in December,** and that a district which manages a household by asking it once has stopped managing it and has not noticed and that the noticing is the finding.**

Nobody took it up and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the count of not-askings about the nine households is eight, and that the eight is a column of its own and is not the count about the woman of forty-five, which stands at four with one standing refusal and did not move, and that the two counts are not added.

A woman of fifty-eight who carries water and does not read figures said one thing at the end of the eighteenth of December, and it went into the minute in her own words and it is twenty-four words, and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got twenty-four and a clerk of nineteen years did not count them and said so and he said count them anyway and she did and got twenty-four both times.

"**You have all got a page now. I have got a bucket and I am the only one who has ever said so.**"

The room let it stand and a man of fifty-six said that the count of things this district does not have is five and that a bucket is not one of the five, **and that she has been saying this since the thirty-first of August and that a clerk of nineteen years entered the first time that she said nothing has been entered about it and that nothing is not to be struck.**

A clerk of nineteen years entered that she had wanted to write the word bucket in a column and had not, and that a column is a thing a person is paid for, and that she is not paid,** and that the count of times she has said this in eleven weeks is five and that the count of five is not in a book and that she did not ask for it to be.**

And on the eighteenth of december **A WOMAN OF FIFTY-EIGHT WHO CARRIES WATER SAID THAT EVERYONE IN THAT ROOM HAS A PAGE AND THAT SHE HAS A BUCKET AND THAT SHE IS THE ONLY ONE WHO HAS EVER SAID SO, AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS ENTERED THAT SHE HAD WANTED TO WRITE THE WORD BUCKET IN A COLUMN AND HAD NOT BECAUSE A COLUMN IS A THING A PERSON IS PAID FOR.**

AND THE ROOM HAS SIX DAYS AND THE LAST ENTRY OF THE EIGHTEENTH OF DECEMBER IS NINE LINES AND IT NAMES FOUR SHEETS AND SIX PEOPLE AND FOUR PEOPLE AND ONE FIGURE AND ONE NAME AND ONE LINE RULED UNDER AN ELEVENTH ROW WITH NOTHING UNDER IT, AND IT HAS NO FINDING IN IT, AND THE COUNT OF ENTRIES WITH NO FINDING IN THEM SINCE THE EIGHTH OF SEPTEMBER IS FOUR AND THE COUNT OF ENTRIES WITH A FINDING IN THEM SINCE THE EIGHTEENTH OF DECEMBER IS NONE.
