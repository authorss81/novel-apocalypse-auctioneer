# Chapter 143: What a Withdrawal Costs

---

The third form of the notice was written on the twenty-eighth of November in about four hours by a man of about thirty-four who digs loam and does nine days of it in ten, and it is the last of the three and none of the three is on a guild form, **and he would not let anybody help him and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he is the fourth of the six who hold the ledger and that he is not a clerk of anything and was not asked to be one.**

The second form said a person, a frame and a date on one line in the middle of the sheet. **The third form says the same three things in the same place, and it has four lines under them that the second form did not have, and none of the four lines is a figure.**

Nobody read the four lines aloud in that room until a man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper on a lane off the lower terrace read them himself, standing up, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he had read them and that he had not been asked and that this is the second time in four weeks he has read a line of a form out loud without being asked.

> **A PERSON WHO HAS STOOD IN A FRAME IN THIS SEASON MAY GIVE NOTICE ON ANY DAY BEFORE THE FIRST OF APRIL THAT THEY WILL NOT STAND IN THAT FRAME AGAIN.**
> >
> > THE NOTICE IS GOOD FROM THE DAY IT IS GIVEN. IT IS NOT A RELEASE. IT IS NOT A CONSENT. IT DOES NOT GIVE BACK A YEAR. NOBODY MAY ASK THE PERSON WHY.
> >
> > **THE FRAME IS OAK. THE YEAR CAME OUT OF THE CHANNEL. NOBODY HAS ESTABLISHED WHAT A WITHDRAWAL COSTS AND NOBODY IS GOING TO ESTABLISH IT BY ASKING THE PERSON.**
> >
> > A PERSON WHO WITHDRAWS HAS NOT CONSENTED TO THE SEASON THEY WITHDRAWED FROM AND A PERSON WHO HAS NOT BEEN ASKED HAS NOT REFUSED.
> >
> > **PERSON: ______________**
> >
> > FRAME: ______________
> >
> > **DATE GIVEN: ______________**

---

The third of the four lines is the finding and it is twenty-nine words, and a man of fifty-six wrote it into the minute and did not read it out again, and a man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the line is the first line anybody has written on any document in this district in eleven weeks that says what a document does not know, **and that a form with no column on it is the opposite of a form with a column that says nothing, and that the two are not joined.**

Nobody argued with that and a woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel said that the first form failed because it named a season that never started and the second form failed because it was pointed at the frame, **and that the third form does not fix either of those and says so, and that a document which says what it does not know is the first one in this district that can be read out loud in a house.**

A man of fifty-six asked her whether it can be read out loud in a house, and she said no, and then said what she meant, and a clerk of nineteen years entered both and entered that the second half of what she said was that it can be read out loud to a person in a room and that a room is not a house and that the woman of fifty-eight who carries water said that in the first week of November and the two are not joined.

**AND ON THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF NOVEMBER A THIRD FORM OF THE NOTICE WAS WRITTEN IN FOUR HOURS BY THE MAN WHO DIGS LOAM AND IT HAS FOUR LINES UNDER THE THREE THINGS THAT THE SECOND FORM HAD, AND NONE OF THE FOUR LINES IS A FIGURE, AND THE THIRD OF THE FOUR SAYS THAT NOBODY HAS ESTABLISHED WHAT A WITHDRAWAL COSTS AND THAT NOBODY IS GOING TO ESTABLISH IT BY ASKING THE PERSON.**

---

A man of fifty-six read the first line of the third form aloud at about half past ten on the twenty-eighth of November and then read the first line of the first form, which is in a book, and put the two side by side, **and the two first lines begin with the same nine words and the season in them is the first of October to the thirty-first of March and that season has not started.**

Nobody argued with that. A man of about thirty-four who digs loam said that the first form failed because it named a season and the third form has the same words at the top of it, and that he wrote the third form and did not write the first, and that a man who has been wrong twice about a form does not get to be right a third time by writing it himself.

A man of fifty-six asked him what he was going to do about it and he gave the reason in thirty-one words, **and a clerk of nineteen years counted them twice and got thirty-one both times and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got thirty-one and said the number out loud twice.**

"**The season is in the sentence and the sentence is what a person reads first. I cannot take the season out of the sentence without taking the person out of it.**"

Nobody argued with that and a woman of about thirty-three who takes in washing at the second channel said that the third line under the three things is the reason the form is not a failure, **and that a form which says what it does not know is a form a person can hold, and that a form that fails at the top and says what it does not know at the bottom is still a form a person can hold, and that the two are not joined.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF NOVEMBER A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX READ THE FIRST LINE OF THE THIRD FORM AND THE FIRST LINE OF THE FIRST FORM SIDE BY SIDE AND THEY ARE THE SAME NINE WORDS AND THE SEASON IN THEM HAS NOT STARTED, AND THE MAN WHO WROTE THE THIRD FORM SAID IN THIRTY-ONE WORDS THAT HE CANNOT TAKE THE SEASON OUT OF THE SENTENCE WITHOUT TAKING THE PERSON OUT OF IT, AND HE WROTE THE THIRD FORM AND DID NOT WRITE THE FIRST.**

---

A woman of fifty-eight who carries water and does not read figures read the third form on the twenty-eighth of November and asked one question, and it is the only question she asked, **and it went into the minute in her own words and it is thirteen words.**

"**Who is it handed to, and what does the house think it is.**"

A man of fifty-six did not answer it and a man of about thirty-four who digs loam said that the form is handed to the person and that the house finds out in about nine days the way the Vanns found out, and that he wrote the line about the frame being oak on the back of the second form in his own hand and would not let it be printed, and that the woman carrying water is right and that the printing is the mistake.

Nobody argued and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a woman of fifty-eight who carries water was asked one question and it was answered and she was not asked about the ninety houses and was not asked about the third house up from the sluice, **and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move, and that the Vanns are the only family named in any book in this district and they were named by a person carrying water and not by a person who was in the room.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF NOVEMBER A WOMAN OF FIFTY-EIGHT WHO CARRIES WATER ASKED ONE QUESTION OF THE THIRD FORM, WHICH WAS WHO IS IT HANDED TO AND WHAT DOES THE HOUSE THINK IT IS, AND A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FOUR WHO DIGS LOAM SAID THE PRINTING IS THE MISTAKE, AND THE FORM IS TO BE READ OUT LOUD IN A ROOM AND A ROOM IS NOT A HOUSE.**

---

A man of fifty-six proposed a fifth line at about ten in the morning on the twenty-eighth of November and the room refused it in about four minutes, and he had not proposed it to win anything, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he had said so before he said the line, and that the fifth line was that a person who gives notice is still standing in the frame on the day the notice is good and for about nine seconds after it, **and that a person cannot be told that by a piece of paper.**

A woman of thirty-eight who has been in the second channel four years was asked about the fifth line and was not asked about her four seasons, **and she said that a person knows that and that everybody who has been in a frame knows it, and that a person who had been told it in a room would be told it by somebody who has not been in a frame, and that she would rather be standing in it than be told.**

Nobody argued with her and a man of fifty-six entered that she was not asked about the four seasons and not asked about the eleventh year and not asked about the four hundred coppers, and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move, and that the refusal of the fifth line is not a finding and is a thing a room did in the middle of a morning without putting a day on it and will not get one.

And on the twenty-eighth of November A MAN OF FIFTY-SIX PROPOSED A FIFTH LINE AND THE ROOM REFUSED IT IN ABOUT FOUR MINUTES, AND THE FIFTH LINE WAS THAT A PERSON WHO GIVES NOTICE IS STILL STANDING IN THE FRAME ON THE DAY THE NOTICE IS GOOD AND FOR ABOUT NINE SECONDS AFTER IT, AND A WOMAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT WHO HAS BEEN IN THE SECOND CHANNEL FOUR YEARS SAID SHE WOULD RATHER BE STANDING IN IT THAN BE TOLD.

---

A stranger who has been in that yard four times in ten weeks read the third form standing up on the twenty-eighth of November and asked one question, and it is the only question he asked, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he has asked one question in that yard four times and that two of them have been about a form **and that nobody has asked him what he intends to use four copies of a page for and that the not-asking is in the count and the count did not move.**

The question was what the sheet was worth, to a person who was not standing in anything and had a cart.

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing answered it in nine words and a man of about nineteen who walked in from the road counted them and got nine, and the stranger said the man was right and then said the second thing, and the second thing is the first time in six weeks a stranger has agreed with anybody twice in the same morning.

"**It is worth what it is worth to her.**"

He said that a man with a cart is not the person the form is about and that a man who takes a sheet away and reads the four lines and the three things in the middle has a sheet about a person he has never met, **and that in four years a stranger is going to walk into a yard and read a line of this out loud and that the line he will read is the one that says nobody is going to establish what a withdrawal costs by asking the person, and that he will say it out loud and mean it.**

Nobody said anything about that and a man of fifty-six said that a sentence on a form is read by strangers and that a sentence in a room is heard by about nineteen people, and that the form has four lines under it and three of them are for the room and the fourth is for the stranger, **and that the district has spent eleven weeks finding a thing that works on a stranger and has not noticed that it worked.**

And on the twenty-eighth of November **A STRANGER READ THE THIRD FORM STANDING UP AND ASKED ONE QUESTION, WHICH WAS WHAT THE SHEET WAS WORTH TO A PERSON WHO WAS NOT STANDING IN ANYTHING AND HAD A CART, AND A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR WHO MENDS FENCING ANSWERED THAT IT IS WORTH WHAT IT IS WORTH TO HER, AND THE STRANGER AGREED TWICE IN THE SAME MORNING, WHICH IS THE FIRST TIME IN SIX WEEKS HE HAS AGREED WITH ANYBODY TWICE.**

---

A man of about twenty-three who sells nothing and copies for nothing was asked on the twenty-eighth of November to copy the third form out nine times, and he said yes in four seconds, and then said the second half, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he had said yes in four seconds and had not said no in four seconds this time and that this is the third time he has been asked and the first time the two answers have not been the same shape.

The second half was that he would copy a thing nine times and would not be the man whose nine copies are the record, and that the last time he was asked about that he was asked about the ledger and this is a form and a form is a different instrument, **and that he wanted to know whether a form goes nine times as far as a page does and nobody could tell him and that not asked is in the count and the count did not move.**

A woman of about twenty-seven who keeps a kitchen garden behind the second channel was in that yard on the twenty-eighth of November and was not asked to take a copy and was given one anyway, and she cannot read it and put it on a board by her door in the same place as the copy of the ledger, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that there are now two things on that board and that the woman keeps neither of them because she cannot read and that she put them there anyway and was not asked why.**

Nobody asked her why and a man of fifty-six said that a woman who cannot read and has put two documents on a board by her door has done the thing this district's strongest instrument does, **and that the yard is where a person comes to stand in front of a thing and a door is a thing a person stands in front of every day of their life, and that the two are not joined and that he is not going to join them in a lane.**

And on the twenty-eighth of November A MAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-THREE WHO SELLS NOTHING AND COPIES FOR NOTHING SAID YES IN FOUR SECONDS TO COPYING THE THIRD FORM OUT NINE TIMES AND SAID THE SECOND HALF THAT HE WOULD NOT BE THE MAN WHOSE NINE COPIES ARE THE RECORD, AND A WOMAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-SEVEN WHO KEEPS A KITCHEN GARDEN BEHIND THE SECOND CHANNEL WAS GIVEN ONE AND CANNOT READ IT AND PUT IT ON A BOARD BY HER DOOR, AND SHE WAS NOT ASKED WHY.

---

A man of fifty-two who is not in a frame and who said no to a right in four words in the middle of October was asked on the twenty-eighth of November what the third form would do to a person who is not in a frame.

He answered in a sentence, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that she had wanted to enter the word *nothing* and had not.**

"**It would do nothing to me, and that is a third kind of thing, and the third kind still has no column.**"

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six said that the three kinds are a person who withdraws, a person who declines a right, and a person who was never asked, and that two of the three are on a form and the third is not and never can be, **and that a person who was never asked cannot be written on a form because there is nothing about them to write, and that this is the reason the sixth column of the ledger is the only column anybody in this district can go and check by asking somebody.**

A man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said that the three kinds are not joined and that a person who has declined a right has not refused a season and has not withdrawn, and that the clerk of nineteen years has been entering that in the same words since the middle of October and that he would like it entered once in a place a stranger can see and was not going to ask her to.

Nobody asked her. **A clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody asked her and that this is the eighth time in eleven weeks nobody has and that the count of it is eight.**

**AND ON THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF NOVEMBER A MAN OF FIFTY-TWO WHO IS NOT IN A FRAME AND WHO SAID NO TO A RIGHT IN FOUR WORDS IN THE MIDDLE OF OCTOBER SAID THAT THE THIRD FORM WOULD DO NOTHING TO HIM, AND THAT THIS IS A THIRD KIND OF THING, AND THAT THE THIRD KIND STILL HAS NO COLUMN, AND NOBODY ASKED THE CLERK TO PUT IT ANYWHERE A STRANGER CAN SEE.**

---

A man of fifty-six did the arithmetic on the twenty-eighth of November at about eleven in the morning in a lane and a clerk of nineteen years wrote it down and put the sum under it for the fifth time in four weeks, **and the figure is a hundred and twenty-three days and seventeen weeks and four days between the twenty-eighth of November and the first of April, and a woman of fifty-eight who carries water and does not read figures called the same interval four months and was not asked to check it.**

A woman of thirty-eight who has been in the second channel four years said on the thirtieth of October that the same interval was twenty-two weeks, and she was not asked on the twenty-eighth of November, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the twenty-two weeks is in a minute of the thirtieth of October and the hundred and twenty-three days is in a lane on the twenty-eighth of November and that the two are in two places and are not joined and that the third place is a house and the house has not been asked.**

A man of about thirty-four who digs loam said that a hundred and twenty-three days is the time it takes a person to be told by somebody she has not met, **and that a man who is a stranger to a house can turn up in about nine days and cannot turn up in a hundred and twenty-three, and that the district has spent eleven weeks finding out that it is slow.**

Nobody argued and the man of fifty-six said that the interval is the finding of the form and not of the arithmetic, and that a form which is good for a hundred and twenty-three days is a form about a season and not about a week, and that the first form was about a season that never started and the second form was about a frame and the third form is about a hundred and twenty-three days and says what that costs is not known.

And on the twenty-eighth of November **THE INTERVAL FROM THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF NOVEMBER TO THE FIRST OF APRIL WAS GIVEN IN A LANE AS A HUNDRED AND TWENTY-THREE DAYS AND SEVENTEEN WEEKS AND FOUR DAYS, AND A WOMAN OF FIFTY-EIGHT WHO CARRIES WATER AND DOES NOT READ FIGURES CALLED THE SAME INTERVAL FOUR MONTHS, AND THE FIGURE OF TWENTY-TWO WEEKS SAID BY A WOMAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT IN THE SECOND CHANNEL ON THE THIRTIETH OF OCTOBER IS IN ANOTHER MINUTE AND WAS NOT ASKED ABOUT AND THE TWO ARE NOT JOINED.**

---

A man of about thirty-eight who deals in second-hand paper on a lane off the lower terrace was asked on the twenty-eighth of November to sign the third form and said no in four seconds, and a man of fifty-six did not ask him why, **and a clerk of nineteen years entered that he had said no on the twenty-fourth of November to a different sheet and no on the twenty-eighth to this one, and that the two are two sheets and one man and that the count of them is not the count of not-askings.**

A man of about thirty-four who digs loam said that the man who will not sign a form is the only man in this district who has found out what a form is for, and that a signature on a notice means the person who wrote it agrees that the person signing it was in the room, **and that a person who was not in the room can still be handed the paper, and that the paper does not stop travelling when nobody signs it.**

Nobody argued and a man of fifty-six said that a form with no signature on it is a form that has been made by one person and can be made by anybody, and that this is the thirtieth of October again in a third shape, and that the first time it was a form about a season and the second time it was a form about a frame and the third time it is a form nobody will put their name to, and that the three are not joined and that he is not going to join them in a lane.

The train on the siding had stood two hundred and ninety days on the twenty-eighth of November, **and a man of seventeen wrote the figure in an index in a hand that is not a clerk's hand, and there are six dates on one line and a rule under the sixth and no seventh date and nobody said so.**

And on the twenty-eighth of November A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT WHO DEALS IN SECOND-HAND PAPER REFUSED TO SIGN THE THIRD FORM IN FOUR SECONDS AND WAS NOT ASKED WHY, AND A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FOUR WHO DIGS LOAM SAID THAT A SIGNATURE ON A NOTICE MEANS THE PERSON WHO WROTE IT AGREES THAT THE PERSON SIGNING IT WAS IN THE ROOM, AND THAT A PERSON WHO WAS NOT IN THE ROOM CAN STILL BE HANDED THE PAPER, AND THAT THE PAPER DOES NOT STOP TRAVELLING WHEN NOBODY SIGNS IT.

**AND THE THIRD FORM IS STILL NOT ONE OF THE FIVE THINGS THIS DISTRICT DOES NOT HAVE AND IS NOT ONE OF THE SIX IT HAS BUILT AND NOT NAMED AND IS NOT ONE OF THE THREE DOCUMENTS NOBODY OWNS, AND A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR WHO MENDS FENCING ENTERED THAT ON THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF NOVEMBER AND WOULD NOT HAVE IT ENTERED IN EITHER LIST.**
