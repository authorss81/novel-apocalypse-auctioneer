# Chapter 327: A Shelter Cannot Be Rehearsed

---

The sixteenth of the eighth month came in about the same, and the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing had the three figures on the end of the trestle table and the man of fifty-six read the board out over his shoulder and got the other two right, and there were about nineteen people in that yard by ten and the sheet of print was still on the top step of two stones outside the door of the room four hundred yards from that gate where a man of about sixty-four had put it on the ninth of the eighth month.

"**Two hundred and thirty-five days on the board and five hundred and fifty-one days on the train, and the days nobody has entered since the twenty-fourth of November stand at two hundred and sixty-five, and two hundred and twenty-six days separate the second of January and this morning, and the first day of the eighth month is fifteen days past this morning, and the ninth of the nine printed nights is a hundred and eighteen days back, and the pool of refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given last moved on the twenty-fourth of the third month, which is four months back and a hundred and forty-five days, and the table against that wall stood up for the forty-first time this morning, and the chair at the end of this table was sat in once, on the tenth of this month, and had stood a hundred and nine days before that, and one is not a figure and is not a habit.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that fifteen days past is fifteen days past and is not a term in a ladder, and that the length of the eighth month is not stated in this district and was not used this morning, and that a man of about sixty-four has been in this district twenty-six days and has slept on that low wall on twenty-six of the twenty-seven nights of that run, and that nobody asked him about the one he did not sleep on it and the record about the not asking says not asked. She entered that the sheet of print is on the top step of two stones outside that door on the sixteenth of the eighth month, and that where a sheet is is not an answer to anything, and that nobody in this district has been asked whether anybody will read it and the record about the not asking says not asked, and that a sheet on a step is a state and not a place.

---

He said the word out loud at about eleven in front of about nineteen people, and the word was practise, and he said it because he had been awake since about five working out what a person does with a roll and a term when there is nothing to do today but write them, and a man of about nineteen counted what he said and got a hundred and eight, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**I have been in that building three days with a roll and a table and a sheet of print and I have worked out that nobody in this yard knows how to do any of it. I know how to do none of it. I have convened nine settlements and I have not got one of them to a second morning. I think it has to be practised. I have said the word out loud in this yard this morning because I have been sitting on it since the sixteenth of this month and I am not going to be the man who says it in a lane.**"

The man of about thirty-four who digs loam said one thing back at about half past eleven, in the ordinary voice, to about nineteen people, and a man of about nineteen counted what he said and got a hundred and forty-two, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**You cannot practise a thing with people who have not been asked, because a practice of a thing is a thing done by people who agreed to do it, and there is nobody in this yard who has agreed to do it and there is nobody in this yard you are allowed to ask, and if you ask them then it is not a practice, it is an instruction, and an instruction is a thing that goes out to people who did not agree to it. That is the whole of it. I have an arm that has not gone above my shoulder since the first of the seventh month and I have been in nine things in this basin that I did not agree to, and I am not going to be in a tenth one dressed up as a rehearsal.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a practice of a thing is a thing done by people who agreed to do it, and that a rehearsal is not a rehearsal when the people in it did not agree to be in it, and that a man who has said out loud in a yard that a thing cannot be practised has not refused to practise and cannot be counted either way, and that the record about the not asking says not asked.

---

Nobody went to the bank on the sixteenth of the eighth month. The woman of about thirty-six who keeps a scale was at the bank from before eight until about five with about nine sacks weighed one after another and the blanket of the scale folded over her arm, which she does not do, and nobody in that yard went down that lane to ask her whether she would come into a building at two in the morning, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that nobody went and that she was not asked and that the record about the not asking says not asked, and that a woman who weighs about nine sacks a day and has four houses that stand empty is not a figure about the roll inside that building and is not a refusal of it, and that neither of them is the other.

A child of about eight was at the foot of that gatepost from about eleven until about one with a stick about four feet long, turning over the stones at the foot of that gatepost where the ground comes up to it, and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against stood about nine feet off the child for about twenty minutes looking at the two sheets on that post and did not ask him one question, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a child of about eight was in that yard and was not asked anything by anybody in it on the sixteenth of the eighth month, and that the record about the not asking says not asked, and that a child is not a figure about a roll and a child is not a refusal of anything and is neither of those two things.

The woman of fifty-eight came down that bank at about five with two buckets, the way she does, and filled the second of them and said one thing out loud to nobody while she was doing it, and it was not counted, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that it was not counted, and that what she said was that there is one of the eleven she carries to that she has not asked a question about in nineteen years and that she was not going to ask it this evening, and that the ninth of the eleven is one of the five different ninths in this district and that the five are not added together, and that the ninth of the eleven is not the ninth of the nine charters and is not the ninth of the nine printed nights and is not the ninth place on a page out of a cave, and that a sixth ninth was available to a clerk of nineteen years this morning and was not taken, and that a clerk who could have made a sixth and did not is not a clerk who has found nothing.

---

He went into the building with two doors at about half past four and could not get his right hand to hold the near corner of the sheet of print on that chair, and tried it twice and had to have the clerk of nineteen years lift it off the chair and set it on the bench against the end wall, and a clerk entered that the man who wrote eight lines on that sheet on the tenth of the eighth month could not move it on the sixteenth and that a document is not lighter for being written by hand, and that a hand which will not close is a figure about a hand and is not a figure about a document.

The rest of that evening went the way the evenings of this month have gone. The man of about thirty-four who keeps a road was at the foot of the low wall along that lane from about six until the light went and did not come into that yard at all, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the forty-first morning is a figure about a table eleven miles off against a wall about four feet high and is a figure nobody in this yard has asked for, and that a man who stays outside a gate on a day a word is said inside a gate has not agreed with it and has not disagreed with it and cannot be counted either way, and that a man of about thirty-four who keeps a road is not a bearer of that road and has never been asked to be one. The man of about twenty-nine who drives a cart read the eight lines of the roll out loud in about nine feet of lane outside the room four hundred yards from that gate at about six, to about four people, from the step, with the sheet in his hand, and a clerk entered that the man who reads a thing out loud in a lane is not a man who holds it, and that a step is a step and is not a threshold and is not a counter. The man of about thirty-seven who puts tables up for anybody who will use them was at the foot of the east wall with his hand-cart against it and the tent still rolled on the back of it, and a clerk entered that a tent rolled on a hand-cart has not gone up on any day since the thirtieth of the sixth month and is not a shelter and is not a lean-to and is not a sixth of the five protected things.

What had not moved on the sixteenth of the eighth month went in at the end of the day in the same hand and not as a paragraph. Nobody has put a line on a board on any of the hundred and thirty-eighth days since the first of the fourth month, counting the first day in, and the word practise is not a line on a board. The refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given did not move at about ten in the morning on the sixteenth of the eighth month, and a man or a woman who keeps a house is not a clerk of a house. The rival record has been in force seventy-six days and has been read out in that yard seven times and its eighth reading was refused out loud with a reason on the twenty-fourth of the sixth month, and it is unanswered. Three documents this district does not own, and a rehearsal is not a document and did not become a fourth of them. Five protected things and no sixth, and a rehearsal is not a sixth and a stick about four feet long is not a sixth and a bucket is not a sixth.

The day came down about half past eight and the first day of the eighth month was fifteen days past, and the figure on the sheet at that gatepost was still four hundred and eleven, and there were nine feet of brick at the near end of the lane behind that bank with two buckets of water and a stool and a sheet of print on a bench in it.

**AND ON THE SIXTEENTH OF THE EIGHTH MONTH SOMEBODY SAID THE WORD OUT LOUD IN THAT YARD THAT THE THING HAD TO BE PRACTISED, AND THE ANSWER WAS THAT IT CANNOT BE PRACTISED WITH PEOPLE WHO HAVE NOT BEEN ASKED, AND THE CHILD OF ABOUT EIGHT WAS NOT ASKED AND THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-SIX WHO KEEPS A SCALE WAS NOT ASKED AND THE NINTH OF THE ELEVEN HOUSES WAS NOT ASKED, AND NONE OF THE THREE IS A FIGURE AND NONE OF THE THREE IS A REFUSAL, AND THE FIGURE ON THE SHEET AT THAT GATEPOST WAS STILL FOUR HUNDRED AND ELEVEN.**
