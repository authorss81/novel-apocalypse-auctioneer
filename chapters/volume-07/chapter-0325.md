# Chapter 325: Eleven Words

---

About nineteen people were in the yard of Lot Seventeen by ten on the fourteenth of the eighth month, and the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing had the three figures on the end of the trestle table and the man of fifty-six read the board out over his shoulder and got the other two right. The man of about sixty-four was at the foot of the low wall along the lane with his coat folded beside him for the twenty-fifth night, and the light was still on the top of that wall and not yet on the ground.

"**Two hundred and thirty-three days on the board and five hundred and forty-nine days on the train, and the days nobody has entered since the twenty-fourth of November stand at two hundred and sixty-three, and two hundred and twenty-four days separate the second of January and this morning, and the first day of the eighth month is thirteen days past this morning, and the ninth of the nine printed nights is a hundred and sixteen days back, and the pool of refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given last moved on the twenty-fourth of the third month, which is four months back and a hundred and forty-three days, and the table against that wall stood up for the thirty-ninth time this morning, and the chair at the end of this table was sat in once, on the tenth of this month, and one is not a figure and is not a habit.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the length of the eighth month is not stated in this district and was not used this morning, and that thirteen days past is a figure and is not a term. A man of about sixty-four has been in this district twenty-four days and has slept on that low wall on twenty-four of the twenty-five nights of that run, and nobody asked him about the one he did not and the record about the not asking says not asked.

---

The man of about fifty-six asked her one question at about eleven in the ordinary voice in front of about nine people, and the question was whether she would put her name under the word NOT ASKED in the roll inside the building with two doors, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a question is not a form and a form is not a consent and that the word over that column was ruled on the tenth of the eighth month and that nobody has been put under it and nobody is to be.

She came in by the gap in the wall about four feet wide and went round the ditch at the end of the bank with her weight on the outside of her left boot, and she was a woman of about thirty-one who keeps a route for people who are not in any book and is not the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps the room with nothing over the door and is not the woman of about thirty-six who keeps a scale and is not the woman of about thirty-four who keeps a goat and is not the woman of fifty-eight, and she said no in about four seconds, and the reason was her own, and a man of about nineteen counted what she said and got a hundred and sixty-eight, and it went in the minute in her own words.

"**No, and I will tell you what I would be doing and it is not going to be the thing you think. If I put my name under that word then I am in a column, and the column is in a book, and in nine years somebody who has never met me will read that I was asked and I said no, and that is the whole of what is left of me, and it is a line with my name on it and a rule under it. I am not in any book. I have kept that for eleven years by not putting a hand on a piece of paper, and a piece of paper is the easy way and I am not going to take it because a man in this yard has been careful. Ask the man who keeps the books what it is to have a line with your name on it in front of a stranger. He has not been asked either.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a woman who keeps a route for people who are not in any book is not a clerk of a house and is not a keeper of a roll and is not a keeper of anything, and that the two refusals given in that yard on the fifth and the sixth of the eighth month and this one are three refusals on three days of three different things asked of one person by three different men, and that three is a figure about three refusals and is not a figure about her, and that this one is not a tenth refusal of eleven coppers a week and is not a ninth refusal with no reason and is not an eighth about the ninth holding, and that the nine and the eight and the seven are three figures and are not added together.

She was not in that yard again that afternoon. A clerk of nineteen years entered that she was not, and that nobody went after her, and that a man who convenes nine settlements did not go after her and did not send anybody after her, and that a person who has gone has not refused to be found and cannot be counted either way.

---

He was in a room about nine feet by nine at the top of that lane, past the third house from the scale, with a stool by the wall and a window about a foot and a half square that had a board nailed across the outside of it, and he came in from the top of the lane at about four in the afternoon with his left hand in a cloth because the cold gets into it, and she was already in the room, standing at the bench with nothing in her hands.

Nothing went into that room. She said so before he had the door shut and she said it once, and a clerk of nineteen years entered the whole of that afternoon as one line and not as a paragraph: a woman of about thirty-one who keeps a route for people who are not in any book went into a room about nine feet by nine at the top of that lane at about four in the afternoon on the fourteenth of the eighth month with nothing in her hands, and nothing came out of that room in her hands, and nobody wrote anything down in that room, and she chose the man she said the thing to, and the three people in this district she had reason to say it to were the man who reads things out loud in that yard, the man of about fifty-six, and the man the figure of twenty-one years is against, and she did not choose the first of the three by accident.

The man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said one thing out loud in that yard at about six, in the ordinary voice, to about nineteen people, and a man of about nineteen counted what he said and got a hundred and thirteen, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**She said eleven words to me in a room this afternoon and I am not going to say what they were and nobody in this yard is going to make me. It was a condition and not a promise, and it was not about her and it was not about what she is going to do, and I have been turning it over for about two hours and I have not found the end of it. I am not going to ask her to say it again. If anybody in this yard wants the words, they can ask her, and I have already decided what I am going to do if they do.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that eleven is a figure about eleven words and is not a figure about a person, and that a thing said once in a room is not a document and is not a copy of a document, and that a clause is a document and eleven words in a room are not a document and did not become one, and that the eleven words carry no name and no place and no figure and no body, and that a condition is not a promise, and that they do not say what she will do, and that she did not say them a second time to anybody, and that nobody asked her to say them again and the record about the not asking says not asked.

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against was at the bank about two hundred yards down that lane from about ten until about six and could not write at all on the fourteenth of the eighth month, and had the clerk of nineteen years come down the lane from that building and write what he said in her own hand instead and did not read it back, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a man whose hand has stopped is not a man who has stopped and is not a refusal to write and cannot be counted either way, and that the hand of the registrar of this district's records office is not the hand of the fencer and is not the hand of the man of about twenty-three who copies for nothing and is not the arm of the man of about thirty-one who digs loam, and that four hands and an arm in this district are five different things and are not added together.

The man of about thirty-two who puts tables up for anybody who will use them came up that lane at about two in the afternoon with nothing in his hands, which is not what he usually brings, and stood at the table with four legs against the outside wall of that building for about forty minutes and looked at the two lines of print on it without going into the building, and said one thing out loud at about half past two to about nine people and it was not counted, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that it was not counted, and that what he said was that a sheet of print that is out where a person walks past it is worth more than a sheet of print that is in a room, and that he is not going to be the man who says which of those two is right in front of a yard. A clerk entered that a man of about thirty-seven has lost the use of both hands and has not been asked to say anything about that, and that a man who has said out loud that he is not going to say which of two things is right has not refused to say and cannot be counted either way.

A room about nine feet by nine with a board nailed across the outside of its window is not a sixth of the five protected things and is not a shelter and is not a document. Nobody has put a line on a board on any of the hundred and thirty-sixth days since the first of the fourth month, counting the first day in, and eleven words said in a room are not a line on a board. The six households of the man of about forty-eight who keeps a tally have not moved on any of the hundred and fifteenth days since the twenty-first of the fourth month, entered out of a ladder and not asked for, and he has said of himself that he is fifty-one. The refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given did not move at about ten in the morning. The rival record has been in force seventy-four days and is unanswered. Three documents this district does not own, and eleven words in a room are not a fourth of them. Four columns of not-askings, and a column with the word NOT ASKED over it and nothing under it is not a fifth of them and is not the same figure as the four.

The day came down about half past eight and the first day of the eighth month was thirteen days past, and eleven words had been said once in a room at the top of that lane and were not written down anywhere in this district, and the figure on the sheet at that gatepost was still four hundred and eleven.

**AND ON THE FOURTEENTH OF THE EIGHTH MONTH A WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE WHO KEEPS A ROUTE FOR PEOPLE WHO ARE NOT IN ANY BOOK REFUSED ANOTHER THING IN ABOUT FOUR SECONDS WITH A REASON OF HER OWN AND THEN CHOSE ONE PERSON OUT OF THREE AND GAVE HIM ELEVEN WORDS ONCE IN A ROOM, AND THEY ARE NOT WRITTEN DOWN ANYWHERE IN THIS DISTRICT AND SHE DID NOT REPEAT THEM AND NOBODY ASKED HER TO, AND A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS ENTERED THAT A CLAUSE IS A DOCUMENT AND THAT ELEVEN WORDS IN A ROOM ARE NOT A DOCUMENT AND DID NOT BECOME ONE. THE FIGURE ON THE SHEET AT THAT GATEPOST WAS STILL FOUR HUNDRED AND ELEVEN.**
