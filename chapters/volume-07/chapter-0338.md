# Chapter 338: Twenty-Six Days Past

---

The afternoon of the twenty-seventh of the eighth month was the afternoon after two words had been said in that yard, and about nineteen people were in the yard of Lot Seventeen by ten and the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing had the three figures on the end of the trestle table and the man of fifty-six read the board out over his shoulder and got the other two right, and the man of about sixty-four was at the foot of the low wall along the lane with his coat folded beside him for the thirty-eighth night, and the figure on the sheet at that gatepost was four hundred and eleven and the figure on the low wall was a man and the two are not added together.

"**Five hundred and sixty-two days on the train, and the first day of the eighth month is twenty-six days past this morning. The pool of refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given last moved on the twenty-fourth of the third month, which is four months back and a hundred and fifty-six days.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that twenty-six days past is a figure about a ladder and is not a term, and that a man of about sixty-four has been in this district thirty-seven days and has slept on that low wall on thirty-six of the thirty-seven nights of that run, and that nobody asked him about the one he did not and the record about the not asking says not asked. She entered that the refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given did not move at about ten in the morning, and that a man or a woman who keeps a house is nobody's clerk of a house.

---

The second line of the sheet of five lines of print that came off the top of a stake in that gatepost on the seventh of this month is still unanswered on the twenty-seventh of this month, and a clerk of nineteen years entered the whole of that in one line and not as a paragraph, and entered that the sheet names the second book four hundred yards from that gate and says that the figure is to be completed in it, and that it carries the word COMPLETED BY over a ruled line with nothing on it, and that a document that names a job that does not exist has found nobody, and that the one person in this district who may write in that book has not been asked anything about that line and has not been asked anything about the five lines of this month, and the record about the not asking says not asked.

The man of about thirty-one who digs loam said one thing out loud at about eleven in the ordinary voice, in front of about nineteen people, and a man of about nineteen counted what he said and got two hundred and nineteen, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**I want to say the part nobody wants said. That line of the second term holds in this district and it cannot hold anywhere else, and both of those are the same fact and not two facts. It holds here because it is nailed up in a yard a stranger can walk into and there are about nineteen people who have read it off that table with their own eyes in the last ten days, and one of those nineteen is a man who read the whole of it back from the top and got nothing wrong. It cannot hold anywhere else because nobody outside this basin has heard of it and there is nothing on it but two lines of type and a stone about the size of a fist holding the corner down. I have been in nine things in this basin that I did not agree to and I am not in a tenth, and I am not standing in this yard telling anybody that we are all right. We are not all right. We have got one line of type and about nineteen readers and that is the whole of the protection and I would rather say that out loud this morning than hear in three weeks that this district believed something had been done.**"

She entered that a term that holds for this district and cannot hold for anybody else is entered as holding and as not holding and the two entries are of one term and are not two terms, and that the standing of it is not a success, and that a clerk who could have made a finding that a term is not enforced and did not is not a clerk who has found nothing, and that a man of about thirty-one who digs loam is not a keeper of a term and has never been asked to be one.

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against put his right hand out at about half past eleven and picked the stone about the size of a fist up off the near corner of the two sheets on that table and carried it about four inches and set it down on the other corner, and it was the first time since the tenth of this month that that hand had held anything at all, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a hand that has not closed since the tenth of this month closed about a stone on the twenty-seventh of it, and that a hand is a figure about a hand and is not a figure about a document, and that the two sheets of print did not say anything about it and could not.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the one book in this district he would want to write in is the second of the two in the room four hundred yards from that gate, and that the second book has been open since the sixth of the sixth month with nothing entered in it, and that a hand that can hold a stone is not a reason to go up that bank, and that the record about the not going says not asked, and that he did not go up that bank on the twenty-seventh of this month and has not gone up it on any day of this month.

He said one thing out loud at about one, in the ordinary voice, to about nine people, and a man of about nineteen counted what he said and got two hundred and twelve, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**I want the twenty-second of this month put down in one line, because nobody is going to be able to work out later what it cost. I left this district at about eight in the evening on the twenty-first and I walked eleven miles up that road to the wall about four feet high where a table a man cannot set up has gone up fifty-two mornings running, and I sat at it until about the middle of the night, and I walked the eleven miles back in the dark and was in a bed at about two this morning and slept until about four, and my right hand has not closed since the tenth of this month, and I did not hear the bell. A sound that goes eleven miles did not wake me four hundred yards off it, and I came out of that gate at about ten past four into a yard with about nineteen people standing in it who had been standing in it since two in the morning, and I was the man in this district who was not in it. That is what it cost and it is not a lesson and I am not going to stand in a yard and turn it into one.**"

She entered that a man who has said out loud in a yard what a night cost him has not made a finding and cannot be counted either way, and that eleven miles up a road in the dark and eleven miles down it is a figure about a man's legs and is not a figure about a bell, and that nobody has asked him whether he could do it again and he has not said, and that the eleven miles a man who keeps a road brought back in the seventh month and the eleven miles this man walked in the dark are the same length and are two figures and are not added together, and that nobody put either of them there.

---

The eight lines of the roll were read out loud once in that yard at about half past five by the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing and were read back from the top by the man of about twenty-nine who drives a cart, and nothing was wrong with either reading, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a document read out in that yard on the twenty-third of this month and again on the twenty-seventh of it is two readings and is not a habit and is not a finding, and that a list of about nineteen names read out on the twenty-third of this month was not read out again and has not been read out again, and that the man who keeps a road was at the foot of that low wall from about six until the light went and did not come into that yard at all, and that nobody in that yard has asked him one question about the eleven miles.

The old charter in the book four hundred yards from that gate is not corrected by anything said in that yard this week, is not replaced by it, is not superseded by it and is not taken out by it, and the fifth line of it is still wrong on its face in a book a stranger may walk up to, and a wrong count is not struck by writing a correct one beside it. A clerk of nineteen years entered that the figure on the sheet at that gatepost is four hundred and eleven, and that a name was said out loud in that yard on the twenty-sixth of this month, and that the four hundred and eleven has not moved and is not struck, and that the reason is underneath this entry and is that a name is not a figure and a name said out loud is a consent the person it belongs to did not give, and that nobody in that yard was asked whether they wanted to be in a list on the twenty-seventh of this month and that nobody has been asked to be in one on any day of this month.

Three documents this district does not own, and a name said in a yard and a list read out in a yard and a sound that came back are not a fourth of them and did not become one. Five protected things and no sixth, and a name is not a sixth and a hand is not a sixth and a stone is not a sixth. The third column of a covenant in a book a stranger may walk up to is still ruled and still empty, and a ninth charter's line and the ninth place on a page out of a cave are still ruled and still empty, and the three of them are not filled by two words said in a yard. Nine refusals of eleven coppers a week, eight refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given and seven refusals about the ninth holding, and the three are three figures and are not added together. A table against a wall about four feet high eleven miles off was standing against that wall on the fifty-second morning of it and nobody was going to put it up.

The light went off the far wall at about half past eight and the first day of the eighth month was twenty-six days past. The second line of that sheet of five lines of print was still unanswered, a stone about the size of a fist was holding the corner of two other sheets about four inches further along than it had been this morning, and the figure on the sheet at that gatepost was still four hundred and eleven.

**AND ON THE TWENTY-SEVENTH OF THE EIGHTH MONTH THE FIGURE ON THE SHEET AT THAT GATEPOST WAS STILL FOUR HUNDRED AND ELEVEN AND THE SHEET WAS STILL ON THE GATEPOST AND THE SECOND LINE OF THE SHEET OF FIVE LINES OF PRINT WAS STILL UNANSWERED, AND THE TERM ABOUT A SOUND HOLDS FOR THIS DISTRICT AND CANNOT HOLD FOR ANYBODY ELSE AND IS ENTERED AS HOLDING, AND A WRONG COUNT IS NOT STRUCK, AND THE FOUR HUNDRED AND ELEVEN GOES IN THE SAME ENTRY AS THE NAME WITH THE REASON UNDERNEATH. FOUR HUNDRED AND ELEVEN IS WHAT THAT SHEET SAID ON THE TWENTY-SEVENTH OF THE EIGHTH MONTH AND IT IS WHAT IT SAID ON THE TWENTY-SIXTH.**
