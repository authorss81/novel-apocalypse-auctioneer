# Chapter 323: The Sixth of the Same Shape

---

The twelfth of the eighth month came in about the same, and the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing had the three figures on the end of the trestle table and the man of fifty-six read the board out over his shoulder and got the other two right, and there were about nineteen people in that yard by ten and a sheet of print on a chair in a building at the near end of the lane behind the bank, and the man of about sixty-four was at the foot of the low wall along the lane with his coat folded beside him for the twenty-third night.

"**Two hundred and thirty-one days on the board and five hundred and forty-seven days on the train, and the days nobody has entered since the twenty-fourth of November stand at two hundred and sixty-one, and two hundred and twenty-two days separate the second of January and this morning, and the first day of the eighth month is eleven days past this morning, and the ninth of the nine printed nights is a hundred and fourteen days back, and the pool of refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given last moved on the twenty-fourth of the third month, which is four months back and a hundred and forty-one days, and the table against that wall stood up for the thirty-seventh time this morning, and the chair at the end of this table was sat in once, on the tenth of this month, and had stood a hundred and nine days before that, and one is not a figure and is not a habit.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that eleven days past is eleven days past and is not a term in a ladder, and that the length of the eighth month is not stated in this district and was not used this morning, and that a man of about sixty-four has been in this district twenty-two days and has slept on that low wall on twenty-two of the twenty-three nights of that run, and that nobody asked him about the one he did not sleep on it and the record about the not asking says not asked.

---

She entered the six in the same hand and not as a paragraph, because it is the only thing on the page this morning that is a set of six. **One: the printed sheet nailed to a gatepost, which carries a word over a column and has nothing under it and a number under that. Two: the third column of a covenant in a book a stranger may walk up to, ruled and empty on its face, and empty on every day since the tenth of the sixth month. Three: a line under a heading on the ninth of the nine charters in nine rooms, and nobody has been put under it. Four: the ninth place on a page that came out of a cave, ruled, and empty. Five: the sheet that came off a stake in that gatepost on the seventh of the eighth, with a word over a line and nothing on the line. Six: the column ruled yesterday inside the building with two doors, with the word NOT ASKED over it and nothing under it.** A clerk of nineteen years entered that the six are the same shape of thing, and that none of the six is a person, and that the six are not added together, and that a column of a thing with a word over it is not a column of people, and that the sixth of them is not a sixth of the five protected things in this district, and that the third column of a covenant in a book a stranger may walk up to is a different column from the sixth and is not that column, and that it was ruled and left empty in this district and is still empty this morning, and that it is not filled by a new column of the same shape being ruled in a different building.

The man of about thirty-four who digs loam said one thing at about half past ten about the sixth of them, out loud, in the ordinary voice, to about nineteen people, and a man of about nineteen counted what he said and got a hundred and thirty-eight, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**Six of them, and yours is the sixth. I am not saying it is worse than the other five, because I do not know that and I have got an arm that has not gone above my shoulder since the first of the seventh month and I am not going to be the man who is wrong about six things in one morning. I am saying the word over yours says NOT ASKED, and the word over the one on that gatepost is a job nobody in this district has, and both of them are a word printed over a line that nobody is to write on, and I would put them in the same drawer. That is all. I have got a bad arm and I am not going to make it into an argument about handwriting.**"

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against said one thing back at about eleven, in the ordinary voice, to about nineteen people, and a man of about nineteen counted what he said and got a hundred and seventy-two, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**He has put them in the same drawer and I am going to say the opposite out loud in this yard this morning because it is in me and because I would rather be wrong in daylight than right in a book later. I think that column is worth more than the other five because it is the only one that has nothing to do with me. Every one of those five is a thing somebody wanted filled in. Mine is a column nobody is to be put in. That is not more honest. It is less, and I have had it wrong since yesterday afternoon and he has just taken the word out of it. A document that cannot be wrong about anybody cannot be right about anybody. It is a drawer. I am not going to have it in a drawer, so it goes in a building and a stranger walks up to it, and that is all the difference there is and I know it is a small one.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man who has said out loud in a yard that he had a thing wrong and has said what is wrong with it in the same breath has not withdrawn the thing and cannot be counted either way, and that six shapes of a thing in this district are six shapes and not six people and not a sixth of the five protected things, and that a document which names a job that does not exist has found nobody, and that a column nobody is to be put in has found nobody, and that finding nobody is a figure about a document and is not a figure about anybody.

---

The rest of that day went over the sheet and the six. A woman of about thirty-four who keeps a goat came down to the foot of that gatepost at about two and read the two sheets on that post from about four feet off and said nothing about either of them and went back up the lane to the third house from the scale. The man of about thirty-four who keeps a road was at the end of that trestle table from about ten until about six with his leg out in front of him under it and did not stand up once. The man of about forty with a cart of his own came up the lane as far as that gate at about half past four, looked up the eleven mile road for about a minute and a half, and said nothing at all to anybody, and a clerk entered that a man who has said nothing and has not been asked has not refused to speak and cannot be counted either way.

The man of about twenty-three who sells nothing and copies for nothing was at the end of that trestle table at about half past two and asked the man the figure of twenty-one years is against for a piece of paper and a stub of chalk, and was given both, and copied the eight lines of that charter out between about half past two and about five, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a copy is not a document and is not a fourth of the three documents this district does not own, and that a copy of a document this district made is not a copy anybody asked for, and that nobody asked him to do it and the record about the not asking says not asked. She entered that his hand has not closed since the thirteenth of the sixth month and that the man the figure of twenty-one years is against has a hand that has not closed properly since about four yesterday afternoon, and that those are two figures about two men and are not added together and neither of them is the fencer's left hand.

The man of about thirty-four who mends fencing went up that bank at about half past four and stood at the step of two stones outside the door of the room four hundred yards from that gate, and looked at the sheet of print the man of about sixty-four laid flat on the top of it on the ninth of the eighth month, and put his hand on the wood beside it and took it away again, and did not knock, and came back down about eleven minutes later. A clerk of nineteen years entered that the sheet of print is on the top step of two stones outside that door on the twelfth of the eighth month and that where a sheet is is not an answer to anything, and that nobody in this district has been asked whether anybody will read it and the record about the not asking says not asked, and that a sheet on a step is a state and not a place.

The man of about thirty-seven who puts tables up for anybody who will use them was at the foot of the east wall of that yard at about five with his hand-cart against it and the tent still rolled on the back of it, and a man of about forty-five who digs and fills in holes for anybody who asks him came up that lane at about six with a bar of iron in one hand and nothing in the other and said one thing out loud to nobody, and a clerk entered that what he said was that somebody ought to be able to say where that water in the ditch behind the building with two doors goes in the spring, and that nobody asked him and the record about the not asking says not asked.

What had not moved on the twelfth of the eighth month went in at the end of the day in the same hand and not as a paragraph. Nobody has put a line on a board on any of the hundred and thirty-fourth days since the first of the fourth month, counting the first day in, and a column ruled on a sheet is not a line on a board. The refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given did not move at about ten in the morning on the twelfth of the eighth month, and a man or a woman who keeps a house is not a clerk of a house. The rival record has been in force seventy-two days and has been read out in that yard seven times and its eighth reading was refused out loud with a reason on the twenty-fourth of the sixth month, and it is unanswered, and a sixth of a shape of thing is not an answer to it. Documents nobody owns are three, and a charter that registers only those who choose to enter is not a fourth of them and a column with a word over it is not a document that registers anybody. The instruments this district has built and not named are six, and a charter is a document and a document is not an instrument and did not become a seventh of them, and a term is a line and a line is not an instrument either.

The day came down about half past eight and the first day of the eighth month was eleven days past, and the third column of a covenant in a book a stranger may walk up to was still ruled and still empty, and the figure on the sheet at that gatepost was still four hundred and eleven.

**AND ON THE TWELFTH OF THE EIGHTH MONTH A CLERK OF NINETEEN YEARS ENTERED THAT A COLUMN OF A THING WITH A WORD OVER IT AND NOTHING UNDER IT IS THE SIXTH OF THE SAME SHAPE OF THING IN THIS DISTRICT, THAT NONE OF THE SIX IS A PERSON, THAT THE SIX ARE NOT ADDED TOGETHER, THAT A SIXTH OF A SHAPE OF THING IS NOT A SIXTH OF THE FIVE PROTECTED THINGS AND IS NOT A SEVENTH OF THE SIX INSTRUMENTS, AND THAT A COLUMN OF THE SAME SHAPE IS NOT THE SAME COLUMN AND THE THIRD COLUMN OF THAT COVENANT STAYS RULED AND EMPTY AND IS NOT FILLED, AND THE FIGURE ON THE SHEET AT THAT GATEPOST WAS STILL FOUR HUNDRED AND ELEVEN.**
