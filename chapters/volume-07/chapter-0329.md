# Chapter 329: From the Top

---

On the eighteenth of the eighth month about nineteen people were in that yard by ten and the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing had the three figures on the end of the trestle table and the man of fifty-six read the board out over his shoulder and got the other two right. The man of about sixty-four was at the foot of the low wall along the lane with his coat folded beside him for the twenty-ninth night, and the wind had got up off the low ground behind the bank and was going through that yard at about the height of a table.

"**Two hundred and thirty-seven days on the board and five hundred and fifty-three days on the train, and the days nobody has entered since the twenty-fourth of November stand at two hundred and sixty-seven, and two hundred and twenty-eight days separate the second of January and this morning, and the first day of the eighth month is seventeen days past this morning, and the ninth of the nine printed nights is a hundred and twenty days back, and the pool of refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given last moved on the twenty-fourth of the third month, which is four months back and a hundred and forty-seven days, and the table against that wall stood up for the forty-third time this morning, and one sitting in the chair at the end of that trestle table since the tenth of this month is a figure about a chair and is not a figure about a person.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the length of the eighth month is not stated in this district and was not used this morning, and that seventeen days past is a figure and is not a term. A man of about sixty-four has been in this district twenty-eight days and has slept on that low wall on twenty-eight of the twenty-nine nights of that run, and nobody asked him about the one he did not and the record about the not asking says not asked.

---

The rule of the counter is that a document is introduced as a thing somebody reads out and is read back afterwards, and it was applied on the eighteenth of the eighth month in about nine feet of yard in front of about nineteen people, and neither reading was counted, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that neither reading was counted, and that a document read out in daylight in a yard a stranger can walk into and read back from the top is a document that has been introduced and not a document that has been proved, and that the two are not the same thing.

The roll was carried out of the building with two doors at about ten in the morning and laid on the top of the table with four legs against its outside wall, and the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing read it out from the top, in the ordinary voice, from about nine feet off it, and it is eight lines and a name at the foot of it in chalk, and the two terms he read out after it, from the top of that as well.

> **THIS IS THE ROLL OF THE BUILDING WITH TWO DOORS AT THE NEAR END OF THE LANE BEHIND THE BANK.**
>
> **A PERSON GOES IN THIS ROLL BY WRITING THEIR OWN NAME ON A LINE OF THEIR OWN, IN THIS ROOM, IN DAYLIGHT. NOBODY MAY WRITE A NAME FOR ANOTHER PERSON AND NOBODY MAY ASK A PERSON TO WRITE.**
>
> **NOBODY IS ASKED TO GO IN THIS ROLL. NOBODY IS REFUSED FOR NOT GOING IN IT. A PERSON WHO HAS NOT BEEN ASKED HAS NOT REFUSED AND CANNOT BE COUNTED EITHER WAY, AND THE RECORD ABOUT THE NOT ASKING SAYS NOT ASKED.**
>
> **NOT ASKED**
>
> **______________________________**
>
> **THE LINES UNDER THAT WORD ARE RULED AND THEY ARE LEFT AS THEY ARE. NOBODY IS TO BE PUT UNDER THAT WORD AND NOBODY IS TO BE ASKED WHETHER THEY WOULD LIKE TO BE.**
>
> **THIS ROLL GIVES NO FIGURE OF ANYBODY. THE FIGURE ON A SHEET AT A GATEPOST IS NOT ENTERED IN IT, IS NOT TO BE ENTERED TWICE, AND IS NOT TO BE ASKED FOR A SECOND TIME.**
>
> **THIS ROLL IS IN A ROOM A STRANGER MAY WALK UP TO AND READ. IT SAYS NOTHING ABOUT HOW A PERSON LEAVES IT, BECAUSE A PERSON LEAVES IT WHEN THEY GO.**

> **A PERSON MAY TAKE WATER, SHELTER AND MEDICINE IN THIS DISTRICT AND IS NOT ASKED FOR A PUBLIC IDENTITY IN RETURN, AND A PUBLIC IDENTITY IS NOT GIVEN UP BY TAKING ANY OF THE THREE.**
>
> **NO PERSON IN THIS DISTRICT IS TO BE ENTERED IN ANY RECORD OF IT BY HAVING HEARD A SOUND, AND NOBODY IS TO BE ASKED WHETHER THEY HEARD ONE.**

The man of about twenty-nine who drives a cart read all of it back from the top in the ordinary voice to about nine people and got nothing wrong, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a thing read back wrong is read back again from the top and not from the word, and that it was read back right the first time, and that getting it right once is not a finding and is not a habit and is not a figure about the man who read it, and that a term with nobody's name against it binds nobody and was entered as binding nobody on the thirteenth of the eighth month and stands anyway, and that the standing of it is not a success.

A woman of about thirty-four who keeps a goat asked one question at about half past four, in the ordinary voice, to about nine people, which was what happens to the lane past the third house if it comes in, and a man of about nineteen counted what she said and got a hundred and seventy-one, and it went in the minute in her own words.

"**I am not asking to be on it. I have said that twice this month and I am not going to say it again. I am asking what it says about the lane. I have read it from about four feet off twice this afternoon and the last line of the first sheet says a person leaves it when they go, and there is nothing in eight lines and two terms about where you go to, or what is out there, or whether anybody has to come out of a door and stand in a lane in the morning. I am not saying that is wrong. I am saying I have been looking for it in two places and it is not in either, and if I am the one who has to stand in that lane in the morning then I would like there to be a line about it, and there is not one, and you did not put one there on purpose, and I know you did not.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that the routes of the people who are not in any book are not written down anywhere in this district and are not given up and are not in that roll and are not in the two terms, and that the roll says nothing about the way out of it, and that a stranger may walk up to that roll and read that there is no way out of it written on it, and that that is what the roll is for.

The man of about thirty-four who mends fencing said one thing about the last line of it at about five, in the ordinary voice, to about nine people, and a man of about nineteen counted what he said and got a hundred and sixty-five, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**I read that last line out this afternoon to about nineteen people and nobody said one word about it, and I have read things out loud in this yard for five months and I have never had that. A thing that says a person leaves it when they go is not a promise and it is not a door and it is not a rule. It is the absence of a rule, and it is the only kind of line in those eight that I would be willing to be one side of. I have been putting my name to things since I was nineteen years old that I could not get out of, and a roll that will not tell me how to get out of it is not a cage, and I do not know what it is, and I am not going to stand in this yard and give it a name so that the rest of you feel better about it.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man who reads things out loud in yards is not a keeper of a roll and is not a bearer of anything and has never been asked to be a keeper of anything, and that a man who has said out loud in a yard that he does not know what a document is has not made a finding and cannot be counted either way, and that a document that will not tell a person how to get out of it is not a cage and is not a freedom and is not a third thing, and that a clerk of nineteen years is not going to call it a third thing.

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against was at the end of that trestle table for both readings and did not read either of them and did not speak, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the man who wrote those eight lines did not read them out on the eighteenth of the eighth month, and that a man who has not read his own document out loud in a yard has not refused to read it and cannot be counted either way, and that the rule of the counter is that a document is read out by somebody and is not read out by the man who made it as though that were a different kind of reading, and that the two are the same reading and neither of them is a proof.

---

The rest of that day went the way the days of this month have gone. The man of about thirty-seven who puts tables up for anybody who will use them was at the foot of the east wall of that yard with his hand-cart against it and the tent still rolled on the back of it at about half past six, and a clerk entered that a tent that has not gone up on any day since the thirtieth of the sixth month is not a figure about that roll and is not a sixth of the five protected things. A table against a wall about four feet high eleven miles off was standing against that wall on the forty-third morning of it and nobody was going to put it up, and a clerk entered that a table eleven miles off is not a figure about a roll in a building and is not a shelter and is not a seventh of the six instruments, and that the two things are in the same sentence in the same hand and are not added together.

A document read out in a yard is not a line on a board, and nobody has put one on any of the hundred and forty days since the first of the fourth month, counting the first day in. The refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given did not move at about ten in the morning, and a man or a woman who keeps a house is not a clerk of a house, and the third column of that covenant is not one of the eight either. The rival record has been in force seventy-eight days and is unanswered, and a roll read out from the top is not an eighth reading of it and is not a ninth. Three documents this district does not own, and a roll this district made in a building at the end of a lane is not a fourth of them and is not a document nobody owns. Five protected things and no sixth, and a reading is not a sixth and a lane is not a sixth and a tent is not a sixth.

The day came down about half past eight and the first day of the eighth month was seventeen days past, and the figure on the sheet at that gatepost was still four hundred and eleven, and eight lines and two terms were in about nine feet of yard in the open air and a name in chalk was at the foot of one of them.

**AND ON THE EIGHTEENTH OF THE EIGHTH MONTH A DOCUMENT WAS READ OUT IN DAYLIGHT IN A YARD A STRANGER CAN WALK INTO AND READ BACK FROM THE TOP AND NOTHING WAS WRONG WITH EITHER READING, AND THE TERM WITH NOBODY'S NAME AGAINST IT BINDS NOBODY AND STANDS ANYWAY, AND THE ROUTES OF THE PEOPLE WHO ARE NOT IN ANY BOOK ARE NOT WRITTEN DOWN ANYWHERE AND ARE NOT GIVEN UP, AND THE DOCUMENT SAYS NOTHING ABOUT THE WAY OUT OF IT. THE FIGURE ON THE SHEET AT THAT GATEPOST WAS STILL FOUR HUNDRED AND ELEVEN.**
