# Chapter 330: Eighteen Days Past

---

The nineteenth of the eighth month came in about the same, and about nineteen people were in the yard of Lot Seventeen by ten, and the man of about thirty-four who mends fencing had the three figures on the end of the trestle table and the man of fifty-six read the board out over his shoulder and got the other two right, and the man of about sixty-four was at the foot of the low wall along the lane with his coat folded beside him for the thirtieth night and had been in this district twenty-nine days.

"**Two hundred and thirty-eight days on the board and five hundred and fifty-four days on the train, and the days nobody has entered since the twenty-fourth of November stand at two hundred and sixty-eight, and two hundred and twenty-nine days separate the second of January and this morning, and the first day of the eighth month is eighteen days past this morning, and the ninth of the nine printed nights is a hundred and twenty-one days back, and the pool of refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given last moved on the twenty-fourth of the third month, which is four months back and a hundred and forty-eight days, and the table against that wall stood up for the forty-fourth time this morning, and the chair at the end of this table was sat in once, on the tenth of this month, and had stood a hundred and nine days before that, and one is not a figure and is not a habit.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that eighteen days past is eighteen days past and is not a term in a ladder, and that a ladder that stood at nine on the tenth of this month has stood at eighteen this morning and is a figure about a ladder and is not about anybody and is not a finding, and that the length of the eighth month is not stated in this district and was not used this morning, and that a man of about sixty-four has been in this district twenty-nine days and has slept on that low wall on twenty-nine of the thirty nights of that run, and that nobody asked him about the one he did not sleep on it and the record about the not asking says not asked.

---

The two sheets lay on that table from about ten yesterday morning until about half past ten this morning, the roll of eight lines and the two terms with the stone still weighed down on the near corner of it, and about half past ten on the nineteenth the man the figure of twenty-one years is against took the two of them and laid them flat side by side on the top of the table with four legs and put the stone on the corner of both of them, because the near corner of the roll had begun to lift at about four joints in the dark, and he used his left hand for the whole of it because his right one has not closed properly since the tenth of this month and there was a clerk of nineteen years standing about nine feet off him who did not offer to do it and did not say anything about it.

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man laid two sheets of print on a table outside a building with his left hand and that a document is not lighter for being carried, and that the stone about the size of a fist is a stone and is not a seal, and that a table with four legs outside a door is a table with four legs and is not a counter and is not a shelter and is not a sixth of the five protected things, and that there are nine feet of brick at the near end of that lane and a bench and a stool and a curtain on a nail and two buckets of water and no window in it and that is what a building is and is not what a shelter is.

The woman who keeps the books in the room four hundred yards from that gate came down that bank at about half past four in the afternoon, on her own two feet, and nobody went with her and nobody met her at the bottom of it, and she came across the yard and stopped about nine feet off the end of that trestle table, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that the one person in this district who may write in those two books was outside the room they are in on the nineteenth of the eighth month, and that the room has a shut door and a handle and a fanlight over it that lights nothing, and that she was not asked to come out of it and the record about the not asking says not asked.

She said one thing out loud at about half past four, in the ordinary voice, to about nineteen people, and a man of about nineteen counted what she said and got a hundred and forty-nine, and it went in the minute in her own words.

"**I have read the sheet that was on my own step off the top, because it was on my step, and I have not read the eight lines, and I am not going to tell either of you why and nobody asked me. I came down that bank this afternoon to say that I have said in this basin that there is going to be one line in that book because of something I did, and I have not written it, and there is not going to be one in either of those two sheets, and I did not write either of those two sheets and I was not asked to and I am not going to be. That is the whole of it and I am going back up that bank now and I am not going to stand in this yard and be asked a second thing.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a woman who has said out loud in a yard that there is not going to be a line in a document because of something she did has not entered anything in it and cannot be counted either way, and that the record about the not asking says not asked, and that the man the figure of twenty-one years is against did not ask her one question this afternoon and did not ask anybody else to ask her one and did not go after her and sent nobody, and that a clerk entered that he asked for nothing and that that is entered and is not a finding and is not a refusal.

The man the figure of twenty-one years is against said one thing at about five, in the ordinary voice, to about nineteen people, and a man of about nineteen counted what he said and got two hundred and seventeen, and it went in the minute in his own words.

"**She came out of that room. I have walked up that bank about twenty times in a month and I have not knocked once and I have not been asked to by anybody and I did not ask anybody to, and this afternoon she came down it and stood nine feet off that table, which is the distance I stand at, and she is not a favour and I am not going to write it down as one. There is a building with two doors at the end of that lane and it is not a shelter and nobody in this district has said it is. There are two terms on a table outside it and one of them binds nobody and both of them can be read by anybody, and that is all they are, and I have not found a third thing about them. And there are eleven words in a room at the top of that lane that are not a document and I am not going to be told what they are. I have got a proof I cannot get and three people I have not asked and a document I signed anyway. I am going to sit down on that step for about nine minutes and I am not going to knock.**"

A clerk of nineteen years entered that a man who has said out loud in a yard what he has got and what he has not got has made a list and not a finding, and that a list of three things and a list of three other things are two lists and are not added together, and that a man who says out loud that a person who came out of a room is not a favour has not been given a favour and cannot be counted either way, and that a man who has said out loud in a yard that he is not going to knock has not refused to knock and cannot be counted either way, and that the record about the not knocking says not asked.

---

The rest of that evening went the way the evenings of this month have gone. The man of about thirty-four who mends fencing read the eight lines out loud once in that yard at about half past six and the man of about twenty-nine who drives a cart read them back from the top and got nothing wrong, and a clerk of nineteen years entered that a document read out in that yard and read back in that yard on the eighteenth of this month and again on the nineteenth of it is two readings and is not a habit and is not a finding. The man of about thirty-four who keeps a road was at the foot of that low wall and did not come into that yard, and the man of about thirty-seven who puts tables up for anybody who will use them was at the foot of the east wall with his hand-cart against it and the tent still rolled on the back of it, and a clerk entered that neither of them is a figure about the two sheets on that table and that the tent is not a shelter and a table eleven miles off is not a shelter and that a building with two doors is not a shelter and that all three of them are still standing where they were this morning.

What had not moved on the nineteenth of the eighth month went in at the end of the day in the same hand and not as a paragraph. Nobody has put a line on a board on any of the hundred and forty-first days since the first of the fourth month, counting the first day in, and a document is not a line on a board. The chair at the end of that trestle table was sat in once, on the tenth of the eighth month, by a man who had not sat in it and had not been asked to, and had stood a hundred and nine days before that, and one sitting is not a figure about anybody. The six households of the man of about forty-eight who keeps a tally have not moved on any of the hundred and twenty days since the twenty-first of the fourth month, entered out of a ladder and not asked for. The rival record has been in force seventy-nine days and has been read out in that yard seven times and its eighth reading was refused out loud with a reason on the twenty-fourth of the sixth month, and it is unanswered. Three documents this district does not own, and a roll this district made and a pair of terms this district made are not a fourth of them. Five protected things and no sixth, and a signature in chalk is not a sixth and a stone is not a sixth and a bucket is not a sixth. Four conditions with no end on it and no fifth was proposed on any day of this month. Nine refusals to read, the departure is unspent, no date in any ledger carries the next reading, and the fourth line of that offer was not read out loud on any day of this month. Four columns of not-askings and she did not rule one, and a column with the word NOT ASKED over it and nothing under it is not a fifth of them. Nine refusals of eleven coppers a week, eight refusals with no reason a clerk of a house has given and seven refusals about the ninth holding, and the three are three figures and are not added together, and none of the three moved on any day of this month. The third column of a covenant in a book a stranger may walk up to is still ruled and still empty, and a ninth charter's line and the ninth place on a page out of a cave are still ruled and still empty, and the three of them are not filled by a column ruled in a building at the end of a lane, and a sixth of a shape of thing is not added to a fifth of a shape of thing.

The day came down about half past eight and the first day of the eighth month was eighteen days past, and the figure on the sheet at that gatepost was still four hundred and eleven, and the ninth of the nine printed nights was a hundred and twenty-one days back and nobody in this district can say whether that night happened, and a plank does not keep nights.

**AND ON THE NINETEENTH OF THE EIGHTH MONTH THERE IS A BUILDING WITH TWO DOORS AT THE END OF A LANE AND IT IS NOT A SHELTER, AND THERE ARE TWO TERMS ON A TABLE OUTSIDE IT AND ONE OF THEM BINDS NOBODY AND BOTH OF THEM CAN BE READ BY ANYBODY, AND THERE ARE ELEVEN WORDS IN A ROOM AT THE TOP OF THAT LANE THAT ARE NOT A DOCUMENT, AND THE COST OF ALL THREE IS A PROOF THAT NOBODY CAN GET, THREE PEOPLE WHO HAVE NOT BEEN ASKED, AND A DOCUMENT BEING SIGNED ANYWAY, AND THE FIGURE ON THE SHEET AT THAT GATEPOST WAS STILL FOUR HUNDRED AND ELEVEN.**
